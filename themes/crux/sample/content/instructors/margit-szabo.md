Margit runs the Over-50s Bouldering mornings on Monday and Thursday and a
lunchtime mobility session on Tuesdays that a surprising number of
twenty-somethings now come to.

The Over-50s group climbs on the low wall first, with tea afterwards. Plenty
of its regulars had never touched a climbing hold before their first
session.

She started climbing at fifty-two, after her grandchildren's birthday party
was held at the wall.
