Dev runs Strength & Fingerboard twice a week for climbers on V4 and above
who want a plan instead of another evening of the same three problems.

Every session starts with a twenty-minute warm-up that nobody is allowed to
skip, then hangboard sets, campus-board drills or core work, depending on
the week. He keeps a notebook on every regular.

Before coaching he was a physiotherapy assistant, and he will happily tell
you what your elbows are trying to say.
