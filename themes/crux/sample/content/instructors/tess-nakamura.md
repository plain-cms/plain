Tess runs the Thursday evening Adaptive Climbing session for climbers with a
disability or a long-term condition, with a second coach and a carer welcome
at no charge.

On Tuesdays she hosts the Women's Bouldering Social: a short warm-up, an hour
of climbing in pairs, and nobody keeping score.

She is a paraclimbing coach and happy to talk through any session before you
book. Ask at the desk or email her.
