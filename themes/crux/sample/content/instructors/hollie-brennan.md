Hollie runs Saturday's Little Climbers for four- and five-year-olds and the
Monday and Thursday Kids' Club for ages six to eight.

Her sessions are mostly games: traverse tag, colour hunts, sticker problems
set at knee height. Parents of Little Climbers stay on the bench by the mats;
everyone else is collected at the desk.

She has a Foundation Coach qualification, a first-aid certificate she renews
every year, and the loudest whistle in the building.
