Rhys runs the Tuesday and Friday Kids' Club for ages nine to twelve and the
Wednesday morning Home-School Climbing session.

His groups work through a challenge board of problems that he resets every
half term, and the kids who finish it get to name the next one. Parents can
watch from the café gallery.

Rhys was a junior member here himself before he qualified as a coach.
