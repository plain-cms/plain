Ana teaches all three of the week's Intro to Bouldering sessions and the
Wednesday Footwork Clinic, which is where people who have plateaued at V2
quietly turn into V4 climbers.

Her intros start on the mats, not the wall: ten minutes of learning to fall
and roll before anyone leaves the ground. Nobody has ever been made to climb
something they didn't want to.

She came to climbing from contemporary dance, which explains the footwork
and the way she counts moves out loud.
