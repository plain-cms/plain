Kwame looks after the ten-metre top-rope wall upstairs and teaches Learn to
Belay on Tuesday and Friday evenings, in groups of no more than six.

By the end of the session you can tie in with a figure-of-eight, belay a
partner and lower them smoothly — and you will have done each of those
several times while he watched.

He has been climbing outdoors on gritstone for twenty years and still thinks
a top rope is the best place to learn to trust your feet.
