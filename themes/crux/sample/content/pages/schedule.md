Taught sessions last an hour; Kids' Club and Youth Squad run for an hour and
a quarter. Come ten minutes early to sign in and pick up shoes. Holiday
changes go up on this page and on the board by the desk.

## What the levels mean

- **Beginner** — no climbing needed, just trainers you can move in.
- **All levels** — every problem has an easier and a harder way up.
- **V1–V3** — you can climb the yellow and green circuits already.
- **V4+** — you are working on the blue circuit and above.
- **Ages** — kids' sessions are grouped by age, not grade.

## Term dates

Kids' Club, Little Climbers, Youth Squad and Home-School Climbing run in
terms; adult sessions run all year.

- **Autumn term:** Monday 7 September to Saturday 12 December 2026, with
  half term from Monday 26 October to Saturday 31 October.
- **Spring term:** Monday 11 January to Saturday 27 March 2027, with half
  term from Monday 15 February to Saturday 20 February.
- **Summer term:** Monday 19 April to Saturday 10 July 2027, with half term
  from Monday 31 May to Saturday 5 June.

Places are booked for the whole term at the desk or by phone — see
[contact](/contact/).
