## Your first visit

Book Intro to Bouldering by phone or at the desk, arrive ten minutes early,
and fill in the climber's form at the counter. Wear clothes you can stretch
in; we lend shoes and chalk. After the session you are signed off to climb on
your own whenever we're open.

Children under 16 climb with an adult who has been signed off, or in a
kids' session.

## Where we are

**Tanyard Bouldering**, Unit 6, Tanyard Road, Calderbrook — the brick
building with the tall chimney, behind the canal basin. The 592 bus stops on
Mill Lane, four minutes' walk away. There are bike racks by the door and
twenty parking spaces in the yard.
