## How it works here

The main hall is 380 square metres of bouldering wall over deep matting,
with a ten-metre top-rope wall in the old drying loft. You can come in and
climb whenever we're open — the sessions on the timetable are the taught
part: a coach, a small group and a plan for the hour.

Nobody needs a partner, a harness or any kit of their own. Shoes and chalk
are for hire at the desk, and the walls are set from the easiest grade up,
so there is always something your size.

## Where to start

If you have never climbed, book [Intro to Bouldering](/contact/). It is an
hour on how to fall, how to read the colours and how to move your feet, and
at the end your coach signs you off to climb on your own.

Children start in Little Climbers or Kids' Club, which run in school terms.
The [coaches](/instructors/) are all qualified and DBS-checked, and most of
them started out on this wall.
