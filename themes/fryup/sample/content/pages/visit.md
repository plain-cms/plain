The grill goes off half an hour before we close. We shut for the last week of July and between Christmas and New Year.

## Find us

41 Station Parade, Ashby Cross, AC4 2JD — the green door between Hallam’s the butcher and the launderette, two minutes’ walk from the station forecourt. Buses 14 and 37 stop outside the post office opposite.

There is half an hour’s free parking along the Parade and a pay-and-display behind the library on Mill Lane. Vans fit in the lay-by outside before eight. The door is level with the pavement, and the table by the window has room for a wheelchair or a pram.

## Collection

Ring [01632 960147](tel:+441632960147) and we will have it boxed and on the counter in ten minutes. We take orders by phone from opening until half an hour before closing. Cash or card; no minimum.

For a works breakfast for eight or more, give us a day’s notice by phone or write to [hello@dotscafe.example](mailto:hello@dotscafe.example).
