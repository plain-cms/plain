Everything is cooked to order on the flat-top behind the hatch: bacon and sausages from Hallam’s three doors down, chips cut here every morning, bread from the bakery on Mill Lane. Have a look at [the whole menu](/menu/), or see [where we are and when](/visit/).

## This week

Thursday’s dinner is steak and kidney pie and it is usually gone by one o’clock, so come early or ring and we will put a plate by. The scaffolders from the station job have discovered black pudding: swap it for the tomato on any breakfast, at no charge.

## Takeaway

Anything on the menu goes in a box. Ring ten minutes ahead and it will be on the counter, wrapped, when you walk in.
