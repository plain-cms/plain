Owen Pryce's version keeps the play in 1890 but cuts the cast to six and the running time to two hours, with no interval.

## In the round

Saskia Lund staged it with the audience on four sides and no furniture but the piano and the stove. Hedda never leaves the stage, so the part is two hours of being watched from behind as much as from the front.

We rehearsed for five weeks, the first of them around a table with the translator in the room.
