**Available from January 2027.** Based in Manchester, represented by Pellow Associates, and happy to tour.

I trained at the Northern School of Drama and have worked since 2019, mostly in the north of England and in Scotland: classical parts in the round, new plays in studio theatres, and returning crime and hospital drama on television.

What directors cast me for:

- **Classical leads.** Hedda, Rosalind and Masha, with a voice that carries 640 seats in the round.
- **New writing.** In the room from the first workshop, as on *Undertow* in Glasgow.
- **Screen.** A series regular and a recurring part in returning drama, and self-tapes turned round in a day.

Every credit is on the [CV](/experience/), which prints on one sheet of A4. For availability and fees, [get in touch with my agent or with me](/contact/).
