The Owl & Lantern has a bay window four metres wide and a tradition of painting it every December. This was my turn.

## The brief

The window had to say "books" from the far end of the street, leave a clear view of the display table inside, and come off with warm water in January.

## The roughs

A winter street runs along the bottom third of the glass, with every lit window in it a different book's cover in miniature; the top two thirds stay clear. The lettering and the street were painted in reverse from inside the shop over two evenings after closing.

## The finished work

The window stayed up from the first of December to the sixth of January. The shop sold out of the books in the painted windows twice.
