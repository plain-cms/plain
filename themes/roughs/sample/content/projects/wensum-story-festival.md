The Wensum Story Festival is a week of free readings, workshops and drawing clubs in the city's libraries every February half-term. This was the fifth festival and the first with a single picture across everything.

## The brief

One picture had to work as an A2 poster, a programme cover, a library banner three metres long and a square for the festival's newsletter — and the dates had to be readable from the top deck of a bus.

## The roughs

The answer was a long, thin procession of readers walking along the river with the festival's name carried overhead like a banner. Cropped, it makes a poster; stretched, it makes a banner; the lettering is drawn so it can be moved without redrawing the picture.

## The finished work

Six hundred posters, eight thousand programmes and four banners. The festival reported its busiest week so far, with 3,400 children at 62 events.
