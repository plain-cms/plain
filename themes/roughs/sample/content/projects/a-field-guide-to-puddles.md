Written by Hal Brereton, a pond ecologist, for children of five to eight. Forty pages, hardback, with a fold-out spread in the middle.

## The brief

Every creature in the text had to be drawn accurately enough for the author to sign off, and simply enough for a five-year-old to find and count on the page.

## The roughs

The book is built around one puddle seen through a year. Each spread shows the same puddle from the same spot, with a cut-away below the waterline; only the season, the weather and the visitors change.

## The finished work

Thirty-eight illustrations and the fold-out. The book is now used by two county school-library services for a spring science term.
