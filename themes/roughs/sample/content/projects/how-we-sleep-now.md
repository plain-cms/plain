A 4,000-word feature on how people sleep in one-bedroom flats, box rooms and house-shares, for the winter issue of Hearthside. Opener across pages 46–47, five spots through the article.

## The brief

The art director wanted the opener to show one building with every window lit differently — a cross-section, but warm rather than technical — and the spots to pick out the article's sleepers one by one.

## The roughs

Monday: three thumbnails. Tuesday: one rough at size, with the headline space kept clear across the left-hand page. Wednesday to Friday: finished art in pencil and gouache, with each of the five spots lifted from a window in the opener, so the article reads as one walk around the building.

## The finished work

Delivered on the Friday at 4pm. The opener was later used on the magazine's subscription postcard.
