Mole has everything a mole could want except a window. So she digs one — upwards, through the floor of a rabbit's kitchen, a fox's wardrobe and a badger's bath, until she comes out under the open sky. Hardback, 32 pages, 270 × 230 mm.

## The brief

The book began as a single drawing on the back of a dummy sheet for another job. Bramble & Finch saw it in my folder and asked for a story to go with it.

## The roughs

Each home Mole breaks into is a page turn, and each is drawn from the same low angle, so the reader always knows which way is up. The text was cut from 900 words to 310 across eleven dummies; the pictures took over the rest.

## The finished work

Published in spring 2025, reprinted twice in its first year, and shortlisted for a regional picture-book prize. Mole now has her own story-time pack for libraries.
