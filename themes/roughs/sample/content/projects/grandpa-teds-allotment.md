A book I made for myself, about a boy who spends a year helping his grandfather on an allotment that is slowly becoming his. A finished dummy of 32 pages, with two spreads taken to finished art.

## The brief

None — which is the hard part. I gave myself the rules a publisher would: 32 pages, fewer than 500 words, and a reason to turn every page.

## The roughs

The seasons do the pacing: spring is busy and crowded, summer is one enormous spread, autumn is a harvest across a double page, and winter is almost empty. The two finished spreads are the first frost and the first potato.

## The finished work

The dummy is the book I show publishers when they ask how I think about a whole book rather than a single picture. It has not been published.
