Saltmarsh Fen is a reed-bed reserve of 140 acres with one circular path, two hides and a great many birds that visitors walk past without seeing.

## The brief

The trust wanted one map that a family could follow without reading, that would tell them what to look for at each of six stopping places, and that would stand up to five years of weather on a lectern board.

## The roughs

The path is drawn as a bird's-eye loop with the reeds rising around it, and each stopping place gets a small window showing what lives there — a bittern standing very still, an eel, the dragonflies in June.

## The finished work

Twenty thousand leaflets and six lectern boards, printed on outdoor laminate. The same drawings are now on the trust's welcome sign at the car park.
