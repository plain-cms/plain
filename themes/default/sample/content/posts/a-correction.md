The parish path map I published in October shows the footbridge at Hareshaw on the west side of the weir. It is on the east side. A reader followed the map, found no bridge, and waded.

The file has been corrected and the printer has the new version. If you have one of the first two hundred copies, the bridge is forty paces downstream of where I put it. I am sorry about the boots.

— Ines
