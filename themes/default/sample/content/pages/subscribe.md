New entries arrive by e-mail on the morning they are published: about one a month, never two in a week. No announcements, no offers, and your address goes nowhere else.

To join, write to [ines@callowaymaps.example](mailto:ines@callowaymaps.example) with the word *subscribe*. I add every name by hand, so allow me a day. To leave, write the word *stop* to the same address.

Prefer a feed reader? The [RSS feed](/blog/rss.xml) carries every entry the moment it is published.
