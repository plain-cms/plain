I’m Ines Calloway. I draw maps for a living — walking guides, estate plans, the endpapers of other people’s books — from a converted wash-house in Hebden Bridge. This is where I write down what the work teaches me, about once a month, in entries short enough to read over a cup of tea.

If you are new here, three to start with:

- [North Is a Decision](/blog/north-is-a-decision/) — why a map does not have to point the way you expect.
- [What to Leave Off](/blog/what-to-leave-off/) — the entry other map-makers argue with.
- [The Client Who Wanted Every Tree](/blog/the-client-who-wanted-every-tree/) — a commission that went wrong, and then right.

Everything else is in the [archive](/blog/), newest first. New entries can come to you by [e-mail](/subscribe/), and the [about page](/about/) says who I am and how to reach me.
