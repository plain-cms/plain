I’m Ines Calloway, a freelance cartographer. I trained as a surveyor, spent nine years drawing maps for a national agency, and have worked for myself since 2019 from a converted wash-house in Hebden Bridge, West Yorkshire.

## What I draw

Mostly maps for people on foot: walking guides, parish path maps, the plan a country estate hands its visitors at the gate. Now and then a map for a book, which is the work I like best.

## What I write here

One entry a month, more or less, each about a single thing the work has taught me. I name my tools and my mistakes. I do not name clients unless they have said I may.

## Writing to me

Every entry takes replies, and I answer all of them: [ines@callowaymaps.example](mailto:ines@callowaymaps.example). For a commission, the telephone is quicker: [01632 960142](tel:+441632960142), weekdays from nine to five.
