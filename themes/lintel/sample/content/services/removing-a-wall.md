Most knock-throughs in a terrace or a semi need one steel beam and two padstones. The fee depends on what the wall is holding up, which is why we look before we quote a final figure.

## How it runs

We visit, lift a floorboard or two upstairs if we need to, and measure the opening. The calculations and sketch follow within ten working days. If your builder finds something different when the wall is opened up, ring us: a change to the beam is free while the job is still on site.
