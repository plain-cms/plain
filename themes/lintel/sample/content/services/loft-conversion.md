A loft conversion moves the weight of the roof onto new beams and puts a floor where there were only ceiling joists. We work out both, and check that the house underneath can take it.

## What to send first

The plans from your architect or loft company, if you have them. If not, we can work from the measurements we take on the visit and a sketch of what you want.
