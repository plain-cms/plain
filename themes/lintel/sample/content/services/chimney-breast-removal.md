Taking out a breast downstairs leaves the brickwork above it hanging. It has to be carried on something, and building control will ask to see how.

## What it costs

£295 for one breast on one floor, and £95 for each further breast in the same stack. Gallows brackets are the cheaper answer where the wall allows them; we will say if it does not.
