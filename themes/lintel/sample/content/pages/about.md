Ruth Pennick spent fourteen years with a consulting firm in the city, on schools, footbridges and the odd listed mill. She set up on her own in Holmbrook in 2015, when friends kept asking who could size a beam for their kitchen.

## How we work

Every job starts with a visit: we look at what the wall or floor actually carries before we draw a line. Calculations are to the current Eurocodes and are checked by a second engineer before they leave the office. You get them as a PDF with a plain-English summary on the first page.

## Builders we work with

About half our work comes from local builders who send us a job each week. We keep to the size of steel that is easiest to get from the stockholders in Sallowfield, and we come back to site when the builder finds something nobody expected.

## Membership and insurance

Ruth is a Chartered Member of the Institution of Structural Engineers and follows its code of conduct. The practice holds professional indemnity insurance of £2 million for each claim; a copy of the certificate is sent with every fee letter.
