## What to have ready

- The address of the property and the name of your builder, if you have one
- Any plans you already have, from an architect or from an earlier extension
- For a house you are buying, the surveyor's report and the page that mentions the defect

We visit almost every job in person, so there is no need to come to the office unless you want to.

## Finding the office

Fearnley Yard is off Bridge Street, behind the timber merchant, about five minutes' walk from Holmbrook station. Unit 3 is the brick building on the left with the green door.

## Parking and access

There are two visitor spaces in the yard; ask at the gate if they are full. The office is on the ground floor with a level entrance, and the meeting room has space for a wheelchair.
