## Where we work

Houses and small blocks of flats within about 25 miles of Holmbrook: Sallowfield, Cawden, Netherthorpe, Brindle Moor and the villages of Lunsdale. Most of our work is in terraces and semis built between 1880 and 1970, so we know what is usually inside their walls.

## What we do not take on

Commercial buildings over three storeys, new basements and party wall awards. We will give you the name of an engineer or surveyor who does that work.

## After the first visit

You get a fee letter the same week, setting out what will be calculated, the fixed fee and the date the calculations will be ready. That is usually ten working days from your go-ahead. They come with a sketch your builder can price from, and we answer building control's questions on them at no extra charge.
