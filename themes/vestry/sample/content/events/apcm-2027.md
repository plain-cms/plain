The meeting follows the 10.30 am Parish Eucharist, with a bring-and-share lunch. The annual report is at the back of church from 11 April.

## Agenda

1. Election of churchwardens
2. The vicar’s report
3. The accounts for 2026
4. Election of three members of the parochial church council
5. Any other business
