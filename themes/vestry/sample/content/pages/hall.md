The hall is free for parish groups and is hired by the hour to anyone else for parties, meetings, classes and wakes. We do not take bookings after 11 pm.

| Booking | Weekdays | Weekends |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main hall, per hour | £16 | £20 |
| Main hall and kitchen, per hour | £20 | £26 |
| Children’s party, three hours | £45 | £55 |
| Regular weekly class, per hour | £13 | — |

A deposit of £50 is returned when the hall is left as you found it. Tables and chairs are stacked in the store by the stage; please put them back.

To check a date, write to the hall bookings secretary at [hall@stbotolphskelsingham.example](mailto:hall@stbotolphskelsingham.example) or ring [01632 960342](tel:01632960342).
