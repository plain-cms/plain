St Botolph’s has stood at the top of Church Lane for more than eight hundred years. We are a small, mixed congregation of about sixty on a Sunday: families from the new houses on Saxby Road, farmers, retired teachers, and a good many people who simply like to sit somewhere quiet.

You do not need to know the words or when to stand. The service books are on the table by the door, and somebody will find you a seat. Children are welcome in every service, and there is a box of toys and picture books at the back of the north aisle.

The church is open daily for anyone who wants to look round, light a candle or leave a prayer request in the book by the font. If you would like a baptism, wedding or funeral here, or simply someone to talk to, ring the parish office.
