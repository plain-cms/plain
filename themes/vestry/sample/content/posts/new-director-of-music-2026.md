Peter has led the choir for twenty-six years and trained more than a hundred junior choristers. He will conduct for the last time at Evensong on 23 August, with tea in the hall afterwards. Everyone who has sung under him is invited.

Anna plans to start a monthly come-and-sing session for adults who have not sung since school. Details will follow in the autumn.
