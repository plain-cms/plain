The appeal raised £86,400 over three years, from a national heritage grant, the county ringing association and more than four hundred people in and around Kelsingham.

The old oak frame of 1780 had split in two places, and the tenor could no longer be rung safely. The new frame sits lower in the tower and the bells sound noticeably clearer from the village green.

Thank you to everyone who gave, baked, ran or sat in the stocks at last year’s fête. The names of every donor are written in the book on the table by the font.
