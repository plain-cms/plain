Arthur Holroyd took the lease on 27 Kirkgate in 1954 with a block, a bandsaw and a bicycle for deliveries. His son Geoff put in the cold room in 1979. Today the shop is run by Geoff's children, Karen and Neil Holroyd, with Dev Patel on the block and Marjorie on the till on Saturdays.

## What we buy

We buy whole animals from four farms, all within twenty miles, and we have shaken hands with every one of the farmers.

| Farm | What it sends us |
| --- | --- |
| Height Farm, Slack Top | Hereford cross beef |
| Slack Top Moor | Swaledale and mule lamb |
| Lumb Bank | Outdoor-reared pork |
| Cragg Farm | Chickens, and bronze turkeys in December |

## What we make

Sausages are made on Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday. Bacon is dry-cured in the back shop, and the pies are baked every morning before we open. The dripping comes from the same carcasses, because nothing we pay a farmer for should go in the bin.

## Ask us

If you are not sure what to buy, say what you are cooking and for how many. We would rather sell you the right half-kilo than the wrong joint.
