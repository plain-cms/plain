## At the counter

Whole carcasses come in on Tuesday and Thursday and we break them down ourselves, so you can have any cut at any thickness: a single chop, a kilo of mince, a rib of beef for twelve. Tell us how many you are feeding and we will tell you the weight.

## From farms we can name

The beef is Hereford cross from the Akroyds at Height Farm, hung here for twenty-eight days. Lamb comes off the moor at Slack Top, and the pork from outdoor-reared pigs at Lumb Bank. Each week's farms are chalked on the board by the door.

## For the weekend

Ring by Thursday and your Sunday joint is boned, rolled and tied with your name on it by Saturday morning. Christmas turkeys can be ordered from the first of November.
