The shop is halfway up Kirkgate on the left, between the chemist and the bank, under the blue and white awning. The 590 bus stops at the bottom of the street.

## Parking and access

There is half an hour of free parking on Kirkgate itself and a pay-and-display car park behind the Market Hall, two minutes away on foot. The door is level with the pavement and wide enough for a pushchair or a wheelchair. If the queue is out of the door on a Saturday, it moves quickly.

## Paying

Cash, card and contactless. Regular customers can run a monthly account; ask Karen.

## Collecting an order

Orders are kept in the cold room under your surname. Collect any time we are open, and tell us if someone else is coming for it. We hold an order until we close on the following day.

## Friday deliveries

The van goes out at one o'clock on Fridays. Delivery is £3, or free on orders over £40, and we need the order by four o'clock on Thursday on [01632 960317](tel:01632960317).
