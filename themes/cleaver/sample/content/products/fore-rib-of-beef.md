The joint for a big Sunday. We cut it to the number of ribs you ask for, chine the backbone so the carver is not fighting it, and tie it with a cap of its own fat.

## How much to ask for

A two-rib joint feeds five or six. For more than ten people take four ribs and give it an extra half hour.

## To have it for the weekend

Ring by Thursday afternoon. Ribs cut on Friday sit overnight in the cold room and are ready from seven on Saturday.
