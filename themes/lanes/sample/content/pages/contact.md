## Your first visit

Every new swimmer starts with a free 15-minute assessment swim on a Saturday
morning, between 09:00 and 11:30, in the end lane beside the lessons. A
teacher watches what you can already do and suggests a stage and a slot.
Bring a swimming costume, a towel and goggles if you have them; long hair
tied back. Arrive fifteen minutes early to change.

## Where we are

**Tanners Lane Baths**, 14 Tanners Lane, Elderton — the red-brick building
with *Public Baths 1936* carved over the door, opposite the library. Buses
22 and 36 stop outside the library. The pay-and-display car park on Wharf
Street is three minutes' walk; two blue-badge spaces are by the entrance.
Pushchairs can be left in the foyer.
