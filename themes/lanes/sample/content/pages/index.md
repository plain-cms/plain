## How lessons work

Children learn in groups of six or fewer, one teacher to a lane, through
Stages 1 to 6. Each stage is a short list of things a swimmer can do — float
on their own, swim 10 metres, tread water in the deep end — and a child moves
up when they can do them, not when the term ends. Every swimmer takes a
report card home on the last week of term.

Adult beginners is for anyone over sixteen who never learned, or who learned
once and lost their nerve. It runs in the shallow end with a teacher in the
water beside you, three times a week, and nobody else is watching: the pool
is ours alone during lessons.

The water is kept at 30 °C for teaching, and the 1936 building has been
properly warm since the boiler was replaced.

## Where to start

Every new swimmer, aged three or sixty-three, starts with a free assessment
swim on a Saturday morning so we can find the right stage. [Book one through
the desk](/contact/), or [meet the teachers](/instructors/) first.
