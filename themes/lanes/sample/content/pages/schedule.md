Splashers and Stages 1 to 6 last 30 minutes. Junior squad, Lifesaving club
and every adult lesson last 45 minutes. Private lessons are booked in
30-minute slots. The changing rooms open 15 minutes before your lesson;
please be on the poolside five minutes before it starts.

On Sundays the council runs public swimming and there are no lessons.
There are no lessons in half-term weeks or on bank holidays either. Any
other change is posted on this page and on the noticeboard by the pool
doors.

## What the stages mean

- **Splashers** — ages 3 and 4, with a grown-up in the water. Songs,
  splashing and getting a face wet without fuss.
- **Stage 1** — non-swimmers. Getting in safely, blowing bubbles, floating
  with a woggle.
- **Stage 2** — can float without help. Kicking on front and back.
- **Stage 3** — swims 5 m on front and back.
- **Stage 4** — swims 10 m; front crawl with breathing to the side.
- **Stage 5** — swims 25 m; breaststroke and treading water.
- **Stage 6** — swims 50 m in two strokes, and works in the deep end.
- **Junior squad** — ages 9 to 14 who have finished Stage 6: fitness, all
  four strokes, tumble turns.
- **Lifesaving club** — Stage 6 passed: rescues, deep-water skills and the
  water-safety badges.
- **Adult beginners** — from age 16, from nothing. **Adult improvers** can
  already swim 25 m and want to swim further or better.

## Term dates

- **Autumn term 2026:** Monday 7 September to Saturday 12 December, with no
  lessons from Monday 26 to Saturday 31 October
- **Spring term 2027:** Monday 4 January to Saturday 27 March, with no
  lessons from Monday 15 to Saturday 20 February
- **Summer term 2027:** Monday 12 April to Saturday 17 July, with no
  lessons from Monday 31 May to Saturday 5 June

Children's places are booked by the term, and a swimmer keeps the same day
and time from one term to the next. New swimmers join after an assessment
swim — [book one at the desk](/contact/). Adults can book a whole term or pay
lesson by lesson.
