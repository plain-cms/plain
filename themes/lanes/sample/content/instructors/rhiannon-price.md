Rhiannon coaches the Junior squad on Tuesdays and Thursdays: lengths, all
four strokes, tumble turns and racing starts from the blocks at the deep
end. Squad swimmers race in the town gala each summer if they want to.

On Fridays she runs the Lifesaving club — rescues with poles and floats,
treading water in clothes, and the water-safety badges.

She is studying sports science and still trains at 6 a.m. twice a week.
