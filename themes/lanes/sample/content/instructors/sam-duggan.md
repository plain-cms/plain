Sam teaches Adult beginners on Monday and Thursday evenings and on Saturday
mornings. Most people who come have never swum, or were put off as
children, and many have not been in a pool for twenty years.

The first lesson is in the shallow end, standing up, and nobody is asked to
put their face in until they want to. Sam is in the water the whole time.

Sam learned to swim at thirty-four, in this pool, in the same lesson.
