Tomasz takes Adult improvers on Tuesday and Friday evenings and the
Wednesday Stroke clinic, which looks at one stroke in detail each half of
term.

Improvers is for anyone who can swim 25 metres but stops at the wall out of
breath. Most people leave the term swimming 400 metres without stopping.
