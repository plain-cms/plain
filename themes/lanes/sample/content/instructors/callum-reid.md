Callum takes Stages 4, 5 and 6 through the week and on Saturday mornings.
These are the lessons where a child who can swim becomes a child who swims
well: breathing to the side, a proper breaststroke kick, and the first
length that ends in the deep end.

He is very patient with tumble-turn requests and makes children earn them.
