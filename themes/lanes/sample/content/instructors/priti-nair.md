Priti teaches all three Splashers sessions — Monday afternoon, Wednesday
morning and Saturday — plus Stage 1 on Mondays and Fridays and Stage 2 on
Thursdays.

Splashers is about being happy in water: songs, kicking from the side,
faces in for a count of three. The grown-up stays in the water the whole
time, and Priti shows them how to hold a child so that the child feels
safe and the grown-up's back survives.
