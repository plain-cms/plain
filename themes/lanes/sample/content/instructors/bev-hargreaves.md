Bev teaches Stage 1 and Stage 3 on Wednesday afternoons and Saturday
mornings, and the Monday Stage 3. She also does most of the assessment
swims, so she has probably met your child before anyone else.

Her Stage 1 lessons start on the steps and stay there until every child is
happy to let go of the rail. Nobody is ever lifted in.

She swam for the county as a teenager and taught at the leisure centre for
twelve years before starting the school in the old baths.
