Jo teaches Stage 2 and Stage 4 on Tuesdays, Stage 2 on Saturday mornings,
and the private lessons on Thursday afternoons.

Most private lessons are for someone who froze in a group: a child who
needs a few weeks with one teacher, or a grown-up who wants to learn
without anyone watching. Jo gets in the water for every one of them.

Before teaching, Jo was a pool lifeguard for eight years and still holds
the lifesaving qualifications the Lifesaving club works towards.
