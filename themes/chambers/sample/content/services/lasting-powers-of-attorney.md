There are two kinds: one for property and money, one for health and care. Most people make both, and most couples make them for each other at the same meeting.

## What it costs

£350 plus VAT for each document, or £600 plus VAT for both made by the same person. The Office of the Public Guardian's registration fee is paid on top and passed on at cost.
