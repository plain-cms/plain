Gareth Lowe handles most sales and purchases, with Jane Tremlett where a property is unusual: a farm, a listed building, or land with a right of way across it.

## What to bring

The estate agent's memorandum of sale, your mortgage offer if you have one, and for a sale the deeds or the name of the lender who holds them.
