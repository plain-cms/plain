Executors can do all of this themselves, and some do. We are happy to take on only the part you would rather not, such as the inheritance tax account or the sale of the house.

## How the fee is set

We charge for the time spent, at the rates below, and give you a written estimate after the first meeting. Most straightforward estates are settled for between £2,500 and £4,500 plus VAT.
