## What to bring

- Photographic identification, such as a passport or driving licence
- A bank statement or council tax bill from the last three months, showing your address
- Any letters or documents about the matter: a will, a contract of employment, a letter from the other side
- For a house sale or purchase, the estate agent's memorandum of sale

The law asks us to check who you are before we act, which is why we need the first two even for a will.

## Finding the door

Number 12 is the blue door opposite the war memorial, between the chemist and the old corn chandler's. Ring the bell marked Tremlett & Fry; reception is on the ground floor.

## Parking and access

The Riverside car park on Mill Lane is three minutes' walk, and the first two hours cost £1.60. There is a single step at the front door and a portable ramp behind it: say when you ring and it will be down when you arrive. The meeting room on the ground floor has a hearing loop.

## Sending documents

Post, or the letterbox at number 12 at any hour. Please do not send passports or bank statements by ordinary e-mail; ask for the secure upload link instead.
