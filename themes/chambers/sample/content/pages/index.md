## Who we act for

Private clients in Hatherby, Kelsall Cross, Upper Brayford and the villages along the Hather: people buying a first home, making a will, winding up a parent's estate, separating, or in difficulty at work. When a client cannot come to Bridge Street, we go to them, at home or in hospital.

## What we do not take on

Criminal cases, immigration, personal injury and company work. We will give you the name of a firm that does, and there is no charge for the call.

## After the first meeting

Within five working days you receive a letter setting out the work, who will do it, the fee and how long it should take. Nothing is charged until you have signed and returned it.
