## How the firm began

Margaret Tremlett and David Fry opened an office of two rooms above the bank on Bridge Street in 1987, to do the work the town's families brought them: houses, wills, the occasional divorce. The firm moved across the road to number 12 in 1999. Jane Tremlett joined in 2001 and became senior partner when her mother retired; Owen Fry, David's nephew, has led the family work since 2010.

## How we work

One solicitor runs your matter from the first meeting to the last letter, and you have their direct line. Letters and e-mails are answered within two working days. Every fee is fixed or estimated in writing before work starts, and we tell you before it would change.

## Membership and insurance

The firm is accredited under the Law Society's conveyancing quality scheme, and Owen Fry is a member of Resolution, the family lawyers' association that asks its members to settle disputes without a fight wherever they can. We carry professional indemnity insurance of £3 million for any one claim.

## If something goes wrong

Tell the solicitor running your matter first. If that does not put it right, write to Jane Tremlett, who will reply within fourteen days. You may then take a complaint to the Legal Ombudsman, normally within a year of the problem.
