## How the school works

Children start with Baby Ballet at three, move through Pre-Primary and
Primary, and then work up the grades — one a year or so, never in a hurry.
Each class is about the age group first and the grade second, so nobody is
the tallest in the room for long. Exams are offered, never required.

Classes are capped at fourteen, and there is always a teacher at the front
who knows every child’s name by the second week. Tap, jazz and contemporary
run alongside the ballet for anyone who wants a second style.

## Where to begin

For a child, choose the class that matches their age on the timetable and
[book a free first class](/contact/). For an adult who has never danced,
the Tuesday or Saturday beginners’ class with Peggy is the place to start —
nobody there expects you to know a plié from a pirouette.

Meet the [teachers](/instructors/) before you come, or simply turn up ten
minutes early and say hello in the foyer.

## The summer show

Every pupil who wants to dances in the summer show, on Saturday 10 July 2027
in a school hall two streets away. Rehearsals happen inside
normal class time; costumes are made and lent by the school.
