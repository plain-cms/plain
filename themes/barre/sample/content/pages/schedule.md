## Ages and grades

- **Baby Ballet** (ages 3–4) is half an hour of music, marching and
  pointed toes, with a grown-up waiting just outside the door.
- **Pre-Primary and Primary** (ages 5–7) lay the foundations: posture,
  the five positions, skips and gallops.
- **Grades 1 to 5** follow the graded syllabus. Most children spend about a
  year in each grade; Sofia will tell you when it is time to move up.
- **Intermediate and Pointe Preparation** are for older pupils who come at
  least twice a week. Pointe work starts only when Yuki says the feet are
  ready.
- **Adult Beginners’ Ballet** assumes nothing. **Adult Improvers** is for
  anyone with about a year of classes behind them.

Children’s classes last thirty minutes to an hour and a quarter; the
timetable shows each one’s start and finish. The doors open fifteen minutes
before the first class of the day, and parents wait in the foyer.

first class through the [office](/contact/); a place is kept for the rest
of the term once the term’s fees are paid.
