## Your first class

Every new pupil’s first class is free. Ring or e-mail the office with the
child’s age, or tell us you are an adult beginner, and we will book a place
in the right class. Come ten minutes early in anything comfortable — leggings
or shorts and a T-shirt, bare feet or socks. Uniform and ballet shoes can
wait until you have decided to stay.

## Fees

Fees are paid per term and depend on the length of the class. The autumn
term 2026 has thirteen weeks of classes.

| Class length               | Per week | Autumn term 2026 |
| -------------------------- | -------- | ---------------- |
| 30 minutes (Baby Ballet)   | £6.50    | £84.50           |
| 45 minutes                 | £8.00    | £104.00          |
| 1 hour                     | £9.50    | £123.50          |
| 1 hour 15 minutes          | £11.00   | £143.00          |

Adult classes are £12 each, or £100 for a card of ten that lasts a year.
Brothers and sisters, and a second class in the same week, are 10% off.

## Where we are

**Linnet Ballet School**, The Old Drill Hall, 22 Hatfield Road, St Albans
AL1 3RT. It is a twelve-minute walk from the railway station, and buses stop
at the end of Hatfield Road. Parking outside is for drop-off only; the public
car park on Grange Street is three minutes away on foot.

The office in the foyer is open Monday to Friday from 3.30 to 9.00pm and on
Saturday from 8.30am to 3.30pm. We are closed on Sundays.
