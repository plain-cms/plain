Harriet teaches every Baby Ballet class, Pre-Primary on Tuesdays and
Saturdays, and Primary on Monday afternoons.

Her classes are built from stories and songs: a three-year-old learns to
point their toes by being a cat on a fence. Parents wait in the foyer and
are invited in to watch on the last Saturday of each term.

She trained as a primary-school teacher before she trained as a dance
teacher, and she can calm a room of twelve four-year-olds with one quiet
clap.
