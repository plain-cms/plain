Clementine teaches Adult Barre & Stretch on Wednesday evenings, the Thursday
Adult Improvers’ class and the Saturday Repertoire Workshop, where the older
pupils learn the solos for the summer show.

She danced for eleven years in a touring company before she opened the
school in a hired church hall with six pupils. She still runs the office on
Saturdays and answers most of the e-mails herself.

A first class with her is calm and quick: a slow barre, a little centre
work, and one correction for each dancer that they can take home.
