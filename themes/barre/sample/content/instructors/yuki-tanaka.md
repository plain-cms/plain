Yuki teaches Intermediate Ballet on Monday and Thursday evenings and the
Wednesday Pointe Preparation class.

She danced as a soloist for nine years and still takes class every morning.
Her pupils learn to work on their own: she gives the exercise once, and
then watches.

Nobody goes up on pointe until Yuki has checked their feet and ankles, and
a pupil’s first pair of shoes is always fitted with her in the shop.
