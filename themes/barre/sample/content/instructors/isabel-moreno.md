Isabel teaches Tap for children from eight and the Friday Jazz class for
ages nine to thirteen.

She spent ten years in musical theatre, in the ensemble and then as dance
captain, and her classes feel like a rehearsal room: counts, a short
routine, and a run-through at the end so everyone goes home having
performed something.

Tap shoes can be borrowed for the first few weeks.
