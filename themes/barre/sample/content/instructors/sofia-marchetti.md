Sofia teaches the graded ballet classes through the week, Primary on
Saturday mornings, and Grade 4 straight after it.

She trained in Milan and has taught graded ballet for fifteen years. She
keeps a notebook for every class, so she knows when a child is ready to
move up long before they do.

Exams are offered each spring; Sofia talks to parents first, and no child
is entered who does not want to be.
