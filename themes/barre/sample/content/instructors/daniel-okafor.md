Daniel teaches Junior Contemporary on Tuesdays, Boys’ Ballet on Thursdays
and Senior Contemporary on Friday evenings.

He trained in contemporary dance and spent six years in a dance-theatre
company before he came back to teaching. Boys who arrive sure that ballet
is not for them usually stay because of the jumps.

Expect bare feet, a warm-up on the floor and something new to try every
week. He choreographs the contemporary pieces for the summer show.
