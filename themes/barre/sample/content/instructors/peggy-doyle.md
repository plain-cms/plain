Peggy teaches both Adult Beginners’ Ballet classes: Tuesday at 7.45pm and
Saturday at 2.15pm.

Her pupils range from twenty to seventy-five, and most of them have never
danced before. She explains every step in plain words first and in French
second, and she never asks anyone to do anything in the middle of the room
on their own until they want to.

She started ballet herself at forty-one, which she mentions whenever
someone says they are too old.
