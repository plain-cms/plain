## What comes out of the kiln

Every piece is thrown on the wheel by Ruth Calloway from a grey stoneware clay that fires with a freckle of iron. There is one glaze, a pale celadon mixed in the studio, and one shape of each thing. All of it is made to be used every day.

## Small batches

The gas kiln holds about ninety pieces and is fired every three weeks. When a firing has sold, that is it until the next one, so the shelves are fullest on the Saturday the kiln is opened.

## This season

Bulb bowls are back from September for hyacinths and paperwhites. Orders for a dinner service in time for Christmas close on the last Saturday of October.
