The pottery is in the old dye house at the bottom of Mill Lane, the last building before the footbridge over the beck. Look for the grey door with a row of glaze tiles nailed beside it. The 31 bus from Malton stops at Alderbeck green, five minutes away on foot.

## Parking and access

There is room for three cars in the yard and more on the green. The showroom is on the ground floor, with one shallow step at the door; a ramp is kept inside, so knock and it will be put down. The floor is stone flags and the aisles are wide enough for a wheelchair or a pushchair.

## Paying

Card, contactless and cash. For an order by phone, a payment link is sent by e-mail once the piece has been put by.

## Collecting

Pieces put by are wrapped and kept on the shelf by the door under your surname for four weeks. Collect whenever the studio is open, or ring [01632 960284](tel:01632960284) to arrange another time; someone is usually in on making days.

## By post

Parcels go by tracked courier on Mondays, double-boxed and packed in wood wool.

| Parcel | Cost | What fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small | £6 | Up to four mugs or two bowls |
| Large | £11 | A set of plates or one serving bowl |
| Dinner service | Included | Sent in two boxes, insured |

If anything arrives broken, send a photograph and a replacement goes out from the next firing.

## Kiln openings

The kiln is unpacked at ten o'clock on the Saturday after each firing and visitors are welcome to watch. The date of the next one is chalked on the door.
