We're Spoil Tip: Mags on vocals, Dev on guitar, Tash on bass and Col on
drums. Four people, one long-wheelbase van and a merch box that weighs more
than the drum kit. **Coalface**, our second record, is out on 1 May on
vinyl, tape and everywhere that streams.

## On the road

Eight dates this spring, from a railway arch in London to Thrash in the
Yard in Leeds. The release show at the Miners' Welfare and the Glasgow
night have already sold out. Every patch on the [shows page](/shows/)
has the room, the support and the ticket link.

## Hear it first

Start with *Winding Gear*, the opening track, then go straight to the
closer. Both records and the first demo tape are on the
[music page](/music/).

## Promoters and press

Bios, the backline we bring and who to write to are on the
