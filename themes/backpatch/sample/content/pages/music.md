## Coalface (2027)

Ten songs in twenty-six minutes, recorded live in four days with the amps
turned up too far. Out on 1 May. Start with *Winding Gear*.

- [Bandcamp](https://example.bandcamp.com) — buying here pays the band most
- [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com) · [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com)
- 12-inch vinyl (£18) and tapes (£7) from the merch box at every show

## Dead Air (2025)

Eight songs, eighteen minutes. The one with *Lamp Room* on it, which is
still the last song most nights.

- [Bandcamp](https://example.bandcamp.com) — vinyl is down to the last box
- [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com) · [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com)

## Ashworth demo (2023)
