## In one line

Spoil Tip are a four-piece hardcore band from Ashworth.

## In fifty words

Spoil Tip play short, fast hardcore about the towns the pits left behind.
Formed in Ashworth in 2022, they have put out a demo tape and *Dead Air*
(2025); the second record, *Coalface*, is out on 1 May 2027. They play
around forty shows a year, mostly in rooms that hold fewer than two hundred
people.

## Photos

## Technical

- We bring our own amp heads, cabs, pedals, snare and cymbals
- On all-dayers we are happy to share a drum kit
- One vocal microphone for Mags and two for backing vocals
- Set: thirty minutes. Changeover: fifteen minutes
- One long-wheelbase van to park near the load-in
