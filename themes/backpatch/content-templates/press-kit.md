## In one line

Who the band are and where they are from, in one sentence.

## In fifty words

What the band sounds like, when they started, what they have put out and
how often they play. Write only what is true; leave out what you do not
know yet.

## Photos

Press photos are sent by email on request.

## Technical

- What the band brings: amps, cabs, pedals, cymbals
- What the band needs from the venue: microphones, a drum kit, power
- Set length and changeover time
