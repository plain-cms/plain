We met under the cedar at Elmswold Court, at Clemmie’s thirtieth, and it seemed only right to go back. We would love you to be there.

The ceremony is in the walled garden and the party carries on in the old stables until midnight. Children are welcome all day. [Getting there](/getting-there/) has the train, the minibus and the rooms we have held at the Wheatsheaf.
