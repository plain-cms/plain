The times are as near as we can keep them. The ceremony starts on time, so please be seated by quarter past two.

## If it rains

The ceremony moves into the orangery, which seats everyone. Drinks and games move under the stable arches, and the photographs are taken in the long gallery instead of under the cedar.

## During the ceremony

Please keep your phones in your pockets until we have walked back down the aisle. Our photographer, Imogen Hale, will share her pictures with everyone by the end of September.

## Children

Children are welcome all day. There is a quiet room off the orangery with books, crayons and a sofa, and a children’s supper at five.

## Food and drink

The wedding breakfast is three courses, with a choice of lamb or a summer vegetable risotto. Tell us which when you reply. The bar in the stables stays open until midnight.
