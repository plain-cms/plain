Elmswold Court is at the top of Church Lane in Upper Elmswold, four miles north of Winchcombe. The gates are opposite the church; follow the drive past the lodge to the front of the house.

## By train

The nearest station is Cheltenham Spa, twenty-five minutes away by road. On the day, a minibus waits outside the main entrance from 1.15 pm and leaves at 1.35 pm sharp. Tell us when you reply if you would like a seat.

At midnight the same minibus goes to the Wheatsheaf and then to Cheltenham Spa station, for anyone staying in town.

Taxis must be booked ahead: Elmswold Cars, [01632 960318](tel:+441632960318).

## By car

From the B4632, take the lane signed Upper Elmswold and turn left at the church. There is parking for sixty cars in the paddock beside the drive. Cars may stay overnight and be collected by noon on Sunday.

## Without steps

The walled garden, the orangery and the stables are all on one level, reached by a gravel path from the car park. Four parking spaces are kept by the orangery door; tell Clemmie when you reply and she will save you one.
