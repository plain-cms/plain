If you are coming by train, a minibus will wait outside the main entrance of Cheltenham Spa station from 1.15 pm on Saturday 4 September, and leaves at 1.35 pm sharp.

At midnight it leaves the front drive of Elmswold Court for the Wheatsheaf and then the station. There are sixteen seats; tell us when you reply if you would like one.
