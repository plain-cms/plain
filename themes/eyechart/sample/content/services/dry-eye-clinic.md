Dry eye is common and often feels like the opposite: eyes that water, sting or tire by the afternoon. Priya runs the clinic on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

## What happens

Priya asks about your eyes, your work and your medicines, then looks closely at your eyelids, the oil glands along them and the tear film, using a dye that glows under blue light. You leave with a written plan: which drops, how often, warm compresses, and lid cleaning.

## What it costs

The assessment is £45. A follow-up after six weeks is £25. A reusable heated eye mask is £25 if you would like one; drops are sold at the counter.

## Afterwards

Most plans take a few weeks to make a difference. If your eyes become red and painful, rather than gritty, call us — that needs a different look.
