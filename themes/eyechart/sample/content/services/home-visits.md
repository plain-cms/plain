If you cannot leave home without help — because of illness, disability or caring for someone — we come to you. The visit is the same full eye test as in the shop, with portable equipment.

## What happens

Ruth and Tom come together, usually on a Wednesday afternoon. The test takes about forty-five minutes; if you need glasses, you choose frames from a case we bring, and Tom brings them back and fits them.

## What it costs

The eye test is free on the NHS if you qualify for a home visit, and most people who do also get a voucher towards glasses. Ask us on the phone and we will check with you.
