Short sight that starts young tends to keep increasing through childhood. Special glasses lenses or contact lenses can slow that down. Ruth runs this care, from age six.

## What happens

The first visit measures your child's prescription with drops and the length of each eye with a scanner. Ruth explains the options — myopia-control glasses lenses, or daily contact lenses for older children — and the measurements are repeated every six months to see how they are working.
