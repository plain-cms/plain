An eye test is a health check as well as a check of your vision. Most adults need one every two years, and once a year from seventy or if you have diabetes.

## What happens

Ruth or Priya asks about your eyes, your health and how you use them, then checks how well you see at distance and close up, measures the pressure in your eyes and looks at the back of each eye with a camera. You see the pictures, and anything worth watching is explained in plain words.

## What it costs

| | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Eye examination | £35 |
| Eye examination with an OCT scan of the back of the eye | £55 |
| NHS eye test, if you qualify | Free |

The NHS pays for your test if you are under 16, under 19 and in full-time education, 60 or over, have diabetes or glaucoma, are 40 or over with a parent, brother, sister or child with glaucoma, or receive certain benefits. Ask us if you are not sure.

## Afterwards

You get your prescription to take away. If we find something that needs a doctor, we write the letter that day.
