Most people who want contact lenses can wear them, including people with astigmatism and people who need reading glasses. Megan fits them, teaches you to put them in and take them out, and checks your eyes while you get used to them.

## What happens

The first fitting takes about forty minutes. Megan measures the front of your eye, chooses a lens, and lets it settle while you wait. If it fits well, you learn to handle it before you go home with a week of trial lenses. A short check-up a week later makes sure everything is comfortable.

## Afterwards

Wear the lenses for a few hours on the first days and build up. If an eye becomes red or sore, take the lens out and call us the same day.
