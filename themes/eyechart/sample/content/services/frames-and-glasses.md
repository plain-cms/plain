Choose with as much time and as many opinions as you like. Tom or Ruth measures you for the lenses, and every pair is fitted to your face when you collect it.

| | Price |

NHS vouchers are taken off the price. Glasses are usually ready in seven to ten days; we call you when they are.

## Afterwards

Adjustments are free for as long as you have the glasses, wherever you bought them. Varifocals take a week or two to get used to; if they still feel wrong after that, come back and we look again.
