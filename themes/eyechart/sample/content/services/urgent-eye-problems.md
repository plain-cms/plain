We are part of the local NHS urgent eye care service, so we can see many eye problems that would otherwise mean a wait at the doctor's or in A&E.

## When to call us

- A red, sore or sticky eye
- New flashes of light, or new floaters
- Something in your eye that will not wash out
- A sudden blur that has not cleared

## When not to wait for us

For a chemical splash, a blow to the eye or a sudden loss of sight, go straight to A&E or call 999. When we are closed, NHS 111 will tell you where to go.

## What happens

Priya or Ruth examines your eye, and treats it, prescribes drops or refers you to the eye hospital the same day if that is what it needs.

## What it costs

Free on the NHS.
