When glasses alone are not enough, the right magnifier and the right light can bring back reading, sewing or the crossword. We keep a range to try.

## What happens

The appointment is an hour. We try different magnifications on the things you want to see, look at light at home, and talk about larger print and phone settings.

## What it costs

The assessment is free. Hand magnifiers start at £18, stand magnifiers at £35 and daylight reading lamps at £45, and you can take one home for a week before you decide.
