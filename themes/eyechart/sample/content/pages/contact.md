## Book or ask

Call **[01632-960418](tel:01632-960418)** whenever we are open, and Sue or whoever is nearest the desk will answer. You can also write to [eyes@ferrandandhale.example](mailto:eyes@ferrandandhale.example) — we reply the same working day — or come in and book at the desk.

## Something wrong with your eyes?

A red or painful eye, new flashes or floaters, or something stuck in your eye: **call us first** rather than walking in. We keep urgent appointments free every day and will see you the same day or the next. See [urgent eye problems](/care/urgent-eye-problems/).

**Sudden change in your sight while we are closed?** Call NHS **[111](tel:111)**. For a chemical splash, a blow to the eye or sudden loss of sight, go straight to A&E or call **[999](tel:999)**.

## Where we are

**41 Sheep Street, Wellingham** — the green door between Dunmore's Bakery and the bookshop, opposite the war memorial. The 22 and 31 buses stop at the Market Cross, a minute's walk away.

## Parking

The Bell Lane car park is two minutes' walk behind the shop (pay and display, free after 6 pm). There are two blue-badge bays on Sheep Street right outside our door.

## Access

- Step-free entrance at the front door; the testing room, the frames and the desk are all on the ground floor
- A hearing loop at the front desk
- Large-print price lists and aftercare leaflets — ask at the desk
- If you would like a longer appointment, or a quieter time of day, just say when you book
