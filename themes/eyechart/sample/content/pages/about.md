Peter Ferrand opened the practice at 41 Sheep Street in 1987, in what had been a watch repairer's shop. His daughter Ruth trained as an optometrist and joined him in 1999; Tom Hale came as a dispensing optician in 2004 and became a partner in 2015, the year Peter retired. He still drops in on Saturdays to tell us the window display is crooked.

## How we work

- **Time first.** Eye tests are booked thirty minutes apart, so nobody is rushed out of the chair.
- **Your prescription is yours.** You get a copy after every test, whether or not you buy glasses here.
- **Prices on the wall.** Every frame carries its price, and lenses are priced before you choose.
- **No upselling.** If your old frames are fine, we put new lenses in them.

## Who you will meet

- **Ruth Ferrand**, optometrist and partner. Does most of the eye tests and all of the myopia care for children. Has tested three generations of some Wellingham families.
- **Tom Hale**, dispensing optician and partner. Fits frames, measures varifocals and does the repairs at the bench in the back. Can usually fix it while you wait.
- **Dr Priya Nandra**, optometrist with an independent prescribing qualification. Runs the dry eye clinic and sees most urgent eye problems, on Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays.
- **Megan Price**, contact lens optician. Teaches first-time wearers to put lenses in and take them out, which takes most people less time than they fear.
- **Sue Bellamy**, at the front desk. Books appointments, hands over glasses and answers the phone.

## Home visits

Ruth and Tom test eyes at home for people who cannot get to Sheep Street on their own. See [home visits](/care/home-visits/).
