Ferrand & Hale is a small, independent optician at 41 Sheep Street: two optometrists, a dispensing optician, a contact lens optician and a front desk that knows most people by name. An eye test here takes as long as it takes, and you hear what we found in plain words before anyone shows you a frame.

## Most asked for

- [Eye examinations](/care/eye-examinations/) — about thirty minutes, free on the NHS for many people
- [Contact lenses](/care/contact-lenses/) — fitted, taught and checked, with a monthly plan if you want one
- [Myopia care for children](/care/myopia-care-for-children/) — for children whose short sight keeps getting worse
- [Repairs & adjustments](/care/repairs-and-adjustments/) — screws, nose pads and wonky arms, mostly while you wait
- [Urgent eye problems](/care/urgent-eye-problems/) — a red or painful eye, flashes or floaters: call us first

## Your first eye test

Bring your current glasses, any contact lenses you wear and a list of medicines you take. Ruth or Priya will ask about your work, your screens and your driving, then check your vision, the pressure in your eyes and the health of the back of the eye. Most first tests take thirty minutes. You leave with your prescription in your hand, whether or not you buy glasses from us.
