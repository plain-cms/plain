Masonry paint fails from behind. If the wall is green, powdery or cracked when the paint goes on, it will be flaking within three years however good the tin was.

## How the job goes

Day one is washing and treating. The wall then needs two dry days. We fill and patch, prime the repairs, and put on two full coats by roller and brush. A semi-detached house takes about a week, and we work between April and October.

## What decides the price

The from-price covers the front of a two-storey terraced house. A full semi is usually between £2,400 and £3,200 depending on the render and how much of it is sound. You get one written figure after we have been round with a ladder.
