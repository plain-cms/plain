Woodwork takes the knocks: hoovers, shoes, dogs. It is also where a room shows its age first, because old oil-based gloss turns yellow.

## How the job goes

Rub down, fill and caulk on the first visit, undercoat when the filler is hard, top coat the next day. We use water-based trade finishes that dry in hours and do not yellow, so doors can go back on their latches the same evening.

## What decides the price

The from-price covers one room: a door and frame, the skirting, and one window board. Panelled doors, picture rails and built-in cupboards are counted separately in the written quote.
