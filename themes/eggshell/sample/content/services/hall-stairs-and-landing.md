Most people paint their own bedrooms and then stop at the landing, because the wall above the stairs is four metres high and there is nowhere to stand a ladder.

## How the job goes

We build a platform over the stairs each morning and take it down each afternoon, so you are never stuck on one floor. Ceilings first, then walls from the top down, then woodwork. Three to four days for most houses.

## What decides the price

The from-price covers a two-storey house with a straight flight, walls and ceilings only. Painted spindles add about a day, as there are usually forty of them and each has four sides.
