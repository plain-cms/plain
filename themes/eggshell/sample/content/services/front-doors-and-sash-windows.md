A front door is the one piece of paintwork every visitor touches. Done in a day with the furniture off and the edges painted, it changes the look of the whole house.

## Doors

We take off the knocker, letter plate and numbers, rub down, fill and prime, and put on two top coats. The door is dry enough to close by teatime and we leave a card through it saying which parts are still tacky.

## Sash windows

Sashes are painted with both halves moved so nothing is missed and nothing is painted shut. If the cords are frayed or the putty has gone we will say so before we start, and can deal with either.

## What decides the price

The from-price is one front door, both faces. Sash windows start at £95 each when we are doing four or more.
