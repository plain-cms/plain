A colour chosen from a chip the size of a stamp, under shop lighting, is a guess. The same grey can read blue in a north-facing bedroom and beige in a kitchen with a west window.

## How the visit goes

Carys brings the large cards and looks at what is staying: the floor, the sofa, the worktops. You narrow it down together, and she paints the shortlist onto lining paper you can move around the room for a few days.

## What you get

A one-page schedule listing every surface with its colour and finish, matt for ceilings, eggshell for woodwork and so on. Any decorator could work from it, though we would rather it was us.
