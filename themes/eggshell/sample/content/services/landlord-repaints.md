An empty property costs money every day. We keep two days a fortnight free for landlords, so a repaint rarely waits more than a week.

## How the job goes

We collect the keys, work with the place empty, and send photos of each room when it is done. A one-bedroom flat takes two days and a three-bedroom house four.

## What decides the price

The from-price is a one-bedroom flat, walls and ceilings in one colour. Full gloss work, nicotine staining and anything that needs replastering are quoted from photos or a visit. Agents with several properties are invoiced monthly.
