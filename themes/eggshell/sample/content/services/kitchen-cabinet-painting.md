If the carcasses are solid and the layout works, the kitchen does not need replacing. It needs a colour from this decade.

## How the job goes

We take the doors and drawer fronts on a Monday and you keep using the kitchen without them. Carys sprays them at the workshop while Alun paints the frames in place. Everything is back and hung by Friday.

## What decides the price

The from-price covers up to twelve doors and drawer fronts. Solid wood, laminate and vinyl-wrapped doors can all be painted, though peeling vinyl has to come off first. A larger kitchen with an island is usually around £1,100.
