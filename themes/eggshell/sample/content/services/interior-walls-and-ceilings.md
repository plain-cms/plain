Two coats on a clean, filled wall will look good for eight years. Two coats over cracks, grease and old drips will look like exactly that by Christmas.

## How the job goes

Sheets down first, then filler, then a pause while it dries. We sand, dust off, spot-prime the repairs and paint the ceiling before the walls. An ordinary bedroom is a day and a half. New plaster gets a thinned mist coat before anything else.

## What decides the price

The from-price is a room up to four metres square with walls and ceiling in sound condition. Rooms with a lot of repair, very dark colours to cover, or ceilings over 2.7 metres are priced on sight.
