Good paper hung badly is an expensive mistake you look at every day. Carys has hung everything from lining paper to hand-printed rolls at £180 each.

## Before we start

Tell us the paper and the room size and we will tell you how many rolls to order, allowing for the repeat. Old paper has to come off and the wall underneath has to be sound. Stripping is charged at £25 an hour.

## What decides the price

The from-price is per roll hung, for standard paste-the-wall or paste-the-paper rolls. Wide-width, mural panels and grasscloth cost more to hang because they are slower and cannot be wiped if paste gets on the face.
