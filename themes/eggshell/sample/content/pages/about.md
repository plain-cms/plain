Alun Pryce served his time with a firm of church decorators and went out on his own in 1996 with a set of steps and a borrowed estate car. Carys grew up holding the other end of the dust sheet, did her own apprenticeship, and joined properly in 2014. She hangs the paper and runs the spray gun. Alun still cuts in a ceiling line freehand faster than most people can tape one.

Most of our work is for people we have painted for before, or for whoever bought their house.

## What we promise

- **A written price, room by room.** Paint, filler and making good are in it. It changes only if the job does.
- **The dates we give are the dates we come.** We book one job at a time and stay until it is finished.
- **Preparation you could inspect.** Filled, sanded, caulked and primed before any colour goes on. That is two thirds of the job and the part that lasts.
- **A clean house every evening.** Sheets lifted, brushes washed outside, furniture back where you can use it.
- **A snagging visit.** We come back after a fortnight, in daylight, and put right anything you have spotted. No charge.

## Qualifications and insurance

- **City & Guilds Level 3** in painting and decorating, both of us.
- **Members of the Painting and Decorating Association** since 2003.
- **PASMA trained** for tower scaffolds, so most exteriors need no scaffolding firm.
- **DBS checked**, for work in schools, surgeries and occupied care homes.
- **£2 million public liability insurance.** Ask and we will email the certificate.

## The paint

We use trade paint, bought by the ten-litre tin from the merchant on Station Road. It covers better and wears longer than the retail tin of the same name. Bring us any colour from any brand's chart and we will have it mixed. Whatever is left over stays with you, labelled with the room it came from.
