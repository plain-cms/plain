## What to have ready

- Which rooms, and roughly how big they are
- Whether it is walls only, or ceilings and woodwork as well
- What is on the walls now: paint, paper, or bare plaster
- When you would like it done. We are usually booked three weeks ahead.

## Where we work

Wrenbury Cross and about twelve miles around: Coldharbour, Tanners Lane, Marlstone and the ridge villages.

The workshop is at Unit 2, The Old Maltings, Station Road, Wrenbury Cross. It is a store rather than a shop, so ring before calling in.
