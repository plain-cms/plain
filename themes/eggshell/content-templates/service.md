Timber in a garden goes grey, then green, then soft. Paint or stain put on in a dry week stops that for about five years.

## How the job goes

We wash everything down on the first day and let it dry, then treat any bare timber and put on two coats. Most gardens take two or three days.

## What decides the price

The from-price covers six standard fence panels, both sides. Sheds and garden rooms are priced by size after a look, and you have the figure in writing before we book.
