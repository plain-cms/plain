## Find the door

9 Tanner Row, Ashby Quay. Look for the brass lamp beside the old ticket office; our door is the green one to its left, and the bar is down fourteen steps. There is no lift, we are sorry to say — if the stairs are a problem, ring ahead and we will bring a table up to the lobby.

Over-eighteens only. No bookings needed at the bar, and no standing: when the seats are full we take your number and ring you when one comes free.

## Getting home

The last tram leaves Quay Street, three minutes’ walk away, at 12:40 am. There is a taxi rank outside the Corn Exchange until two, and we are happy to call one for you.
