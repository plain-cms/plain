We keep eight drinks on the list and make them the same way every night: good ice, cold glasses, the right bottle rather than the expensive one. If none of the eight is what you came for, ask for the Dealer’s Choice. See [the whole list](/menu/), or [how to find the door](/visit/).

## Winter on the list

The Night Porter is back for the winter — dark rum, cold-brew coffee and a syrup we make from the stout brewed two streets away. The Irish Coffee goes on the moment the clocks go back, and stays until they go forward.

## Stools and booths

The ten stools at the bar are first come, first served. The two booths seat six each and can be booked for the evening; ring after three and whoever is cutting ice will answer.
