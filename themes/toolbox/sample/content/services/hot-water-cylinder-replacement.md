Cylinders rarely fail politely. If yours is past fifteen years old, runs lukewarm, or has started leaving a rusty ring on the airing cupboard floor, it's cheaper to replace it on your schedule than on its own.

## How we size it

Bigger isn't better. An oversized cylinder costs you money every day it heats water nobody uses. We ask how many people are in the house, how many bathrooms, and when everyone showers, then size it to that. You get two or three options with firm all-in prices: cylinder, fitting and disposal as one number.

## The swap

Most straight swaps are done in a day. We drain and disconnect the old cylinder, fit and commission the new one, check every joint under pressure, and take the old one away. You're back in hot water by teatime.
