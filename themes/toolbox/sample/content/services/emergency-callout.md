A burst pipe doesn't wait for opening hours, so neither do we. Call the main number any time. After hours it rings through to whoever is on duty.

## What happens when you call

First we get the water under control. We'll talk you to your stop tap over the phone, which usually turns a flood into a puddle before we've left the yard. Then we come and make it safe: stopped, capped or contained.

## What it costs

The call-out covers getting to you and making the problem safe. If the permanent fix is quick, we usually do it there and then inside the same price. If it's bigger, say a corroded run that needs replacing or a cylinder on its way out, you get a firm written quote before we go any further. No work starts on a guess.
