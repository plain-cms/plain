Heating jobs vary too much for a from-price to mean anything, so we don't print one. We look first, usually for free if you're in Calderbrook, and then give you a firm figure.

## Common jobs

Radiators that are cold at the bottom, new radiators and towel rails, thermostatic valves, a pump that hums but doesn't push, and moving a radiator so the sofa finally fits.

## Power flushing

Ten years of sludge will choke any system. A power flush clears it, and we fit a magnetic filter afterwards so it stays clear. For a three-bedroom house, allow most of a day.
