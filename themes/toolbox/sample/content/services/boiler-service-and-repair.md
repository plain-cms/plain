The cheapest boiler repair is the one you never need. An annual service catches the tired parts in September, when it's a planned visit. In January it's an emergency, and half the street's boilers went down in the same cold snap.

## Servicing

A proper service is a strip-down and inspection, not a vacuum and a new sticker. We check the burner, heat exchanger, seals and safety devices, take flue readings, and leave you the numbers in writing. It takes about an hour.

## Repairs

Whatever make you have, we've probably had it apart. If a part is needed you get the price, parts and labour as one number, before we order it. If the honest answer is that the boiler owes you nothing and a repair is throwing money at it, we'll say that too.

Both of our heating engineers are Gas Safe registered. Ask to see the card.
