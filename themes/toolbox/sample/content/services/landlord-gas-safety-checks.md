If you let a property with gas in it, the law asks for a gas safety check every twelve months and a record you can show. We do several hundred a year for landlords and letting agents around Calderbrook.

## What we check

The boiler, the hob, any gas fire, the flues, the ventilation, and the pipework we can see. We test for leaks at the meter and make sure each appliance is burning as it should.

## Booking around tenants

Give us the tenant's number and we arrange the visit with them directly, including early mornings and Saturdays. If we can't get in after two attempts we tell you in writing, which is the paper trail you need.

## If something fails

You get a phone call before we leave the property, a plain explanation, and a price for putting it right. Anything unsafe is isolated on the day.
