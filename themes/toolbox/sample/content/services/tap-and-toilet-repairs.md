A dripping tap wastes a bath of water a week and nobody enjoys listening to it. These are the small jobs we like: one visit, one price, done.

## What we fix

Dripping and stiff taps, mixer cartridges, cisterns that run on, weak flushes, loose toilet seats and pans, leaking wastes under the sink, and isolating valves that have seized open.

## Got a few small jobs?

Save them up. The price covers the first half hour, and most taps take less than that. Anything else on your list is charged by the quarter hour after it, so three small jobs in one visit cost a lot less than three visits.
