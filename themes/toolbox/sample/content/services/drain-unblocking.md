A blocked drain is one of the few jobs where the price is nearly always the price. Most blockages clear on the first visit with rods or the machine, inside the standard rate.

## What we clear

Kitchen sinks, basins, baths and showers, toilets, and outside gullies. Grease, limescale, wipes (they are never flushable, whatever the packet says), and the occasional toy dinosaur.

## When it keeps coming back

A drain that blocks every few months isn't unlucky. It's telling you something: usually a sagging pipe, a poor fall, or roots finding their way in. If that's what we think you've got, we'll say so and quote the proper fix separately. We won't just keep taking the call-out fee.
