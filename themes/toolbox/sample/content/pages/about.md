Dennis Hartley started the firm in 2008 with one van and a second-hand pipe bender. His son Mark joined four years later, and Priya served her apprenticeship with us and never left. About half of every month's work comes from people we've worked for before, or from their neighbours. We reckon that's the only review that matters.

## What we promise

- **A firm price before work starts.** The number we quote is the number you pay. If we open something up and find a bigger problem, we stop and talk to you before going on.
- **We turn up when we say.** If we're running late you get a call, not silence.
- **Tidy work.** Boot covers on, floors sheeted, old parts and packaging taken away.
- **A written guarantee.** Twelve months on workmanship, on paper, every job.

## Qualifications and insurance

- **Gas Safe registered.** Dennis and Mark both carry their Gas Safe ID cards. Ask to see them. We'd rather you did.
- **City & Guilds qualified** in plumbing and domestic heating, all three of us.
- **WaterSafe approved** for work on your mains water supply.
- **£2 million public liability insurance.** The certificate lives in the van and we're happy to email you a copy.

## The vans

Two white Transits with the name on the side, stocked every Friday so that most repairs are finished on the first visit. If we do have to order a part, we tell you what it costs and when it lands before we leave.
