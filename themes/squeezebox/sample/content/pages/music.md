## Hay Time (2026)

Fourteen sets recorded over three days in the Victoria Hall with the
chairs stacked against the wall, so you can hear the room. Start with
*The Low Garth Reels* — it's the set we finish every barn dance with.

- [Buy the download](https://shop.barleyfold.example/hay-time) — this pays the band most
- [Listen online](https://listen.barleyfold.example/hay-time)
- CDs £12 at every dance, or £20 for both records

## The Lantern Sets (2023)

Our first record: ten sets, most of them learned at the Packhorse
session, plus Annie singing *The Ripon Carrier*. Rougher than Hay Time,
and quicker.

- [Buy the download](https://shop.barleyfold.example/lantern-sets)
- [Listen online](https://listen.barleyfold.example/lantern-sets)
- CDs £10 at every dance

## Dances we call

Annie chooses the dances on the night to suit the room. The ones that
come up most:

| Dance | Formation | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strip the Willow | Longways sets | Ending the night loudly |
| Circassian Circle | Big circle | Getting everyone up first |
| Gay Gordons | Couples round the room | Weddings and mixed ages |
| Cumberland Square Eight | Squares of four couples | Halls with a good floor |
| Galopede | Longways sets | Children and grandparents together |
