We're Jess on melodeon, Tom on fiddle, Pete on double bass and Annie on
guitar and calling. Most Saturdays we're in a hall somewhere with the
chairs pushed back; the rest of the time we're learning tunes off each
other in Jess's kitchen.

## New this autumn

**Hay Time**, our second record, is fourteen sets of the tunes we
actually dance to — jigs, reels, a few polkas and two waltzes for the
end of the night. Hear it on the [music page](/music/), or buy a CD at
any dance.

## Come and dance

The Christmas Ceilidh at the Victoria Hall has gone, but there's plenty
of room at Burns Night in Skelderby and the spring barn dance at Low
Garth Farm. Every date we've got is on the [dates page](/shows/).

On the first Thursday of every month we lead the tune session in the
back room of the Packhorse on Hebbleton Market Place, from half past
eight. Bring an instrument, or just a pint.

## Weddings, parties and promoters

We play weddings, birthdays and fundraisers as well as public dances —
the [press kit](/press/) has our story in three lengths, what we need
from a venue, and how to reach us.
