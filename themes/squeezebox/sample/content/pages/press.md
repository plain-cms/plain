## In one line

Barley Fold are a four-piece ceilidh band from Hebbleton, with a caller
who gets a whole hall dancing.

## In fifty words

Barley Fold play English and Scottish dance music on melodeon, fiddle,
guitar and double bass, with Annie Marsh calling. They formed at the
Packhorse session in Hebbleton in 2019, play around forty dances a
year from village halls to festival tents, and released their second
record, *Hay Time*, in 2026.

## In two hundred words

Barley Fold started in 2019 as the four people who stayed latest at the
Packhorse tune session in Hebbleton. Jess Ollerton plays a two-row
melodeon she bought at fifteen; Tom Rigby learned fiddle from his
grandmother; Pete Hallam brought a double bass because the session
needed a bottom end; and Annie Marsh plays guitar and calls.

The calling is what makes the night. Annie walks every dance through
before the music starts, chooses the next one by watching the floor,
and has never yet left anyone sitting down. A typical evening is two
sets of an hour and a quarter, from a Circassian Circle to get everyone
up to a Strip the Willow to finish.

The band have released two records, *The Lantern Sets* (2023) and *Hay
Time* (2026). They play around forty dances a year: weddings, village
halls, fundraisers and festival ceilidh tents across the Dales, and the
Christmas Ceilidh at Hebbleton's Victoria Hall, which sells out every
year.

## What we need

- A clear floor: about one square metre for every dancer
- A space 4 m wide and 3 m deep for the band, and one 13 A socket within 10 m
- We bring our own sound system for rooms up to 250 people
- Ninety minutes to load in and set up before the doors open
