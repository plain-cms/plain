We're on in the ceilidh tent from 21:00 to midnight. A festival day or weekend ticket covers it; there's no separate ticket for the dance.

Riverside Meadow is a ten-minute walk from Ashwell Bridge station, and the festival runs a shuttle bus from the town car park.
