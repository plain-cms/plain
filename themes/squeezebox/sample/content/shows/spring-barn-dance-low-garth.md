Pay on the door: £10 for adults, £4 for under-16s, and every penny goes to the Fell Rescue team. Doors at 19:00, dancing until 23:00. There's a bar, a hog roast from 19:30, and straw bales to sit on between dances.

The barn isn't heated. Park in the top field; the farm track is signed from the Ellerby road.
