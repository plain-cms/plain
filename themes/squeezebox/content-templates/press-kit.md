## In one line

Who the band are, what they play and where they are from.

## In fifty words

The line-up, the kind of night you play, how long you have played together and the latest recording. Write only what is true.

## Photos

Press photos are sent by e-mail on request.

## What we need

- Floor space for the band, and a power socket near the stage
- Setup time, and whether you bring your own sound system
