Ellison & Okafor was founded in 2021 by Mara Ellison and Tomas Okafor — a graphic designer and a photographer who kept borrowing each other's tools until it made more sense to share a room. We are deliberately small: every project is led by the two of us, start to finish.

## How we work

We take on a handful of projects a year, and we take them whole — research, design, and production. We would rather do the packaging *and* the photography than hand either to someone who wasn't in the room when the idea happened.

Every case study on this site follows the same honest shape: the challenge as the client described it, the approach we actually took, and the outcome as it can be measured. No retouched mockups, no invented metrics.

## What we do

- Identity and naming
- Packaging and print
- Books, reports and catalogues
- Wayfinding and signage
- Photography — portrait, product and documentary
- Art direction

## Who for

Corvid Coffee Roasters, The Tidemill Hotel, Harbourside Maritime Trust, Ninebark River Trust, Fenn Street Bakery, The Lumen Archive, Kiln Row Ceramics, Salt Theatre and Pilcrow Books.

## Start a project

A project usually starts with a one-hour conversation and ends somewhere between six weeks and six months later. If you have something that needs a careful eye and an unhurried process, a paragraph about what you're making is the perfect introduction.
