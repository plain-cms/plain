You do not need to have climbed anything. Most of our members started on the Wednesday training walks, and the leaders will match you to walks that suit you.

## What it costs

| Membership | A year | What it includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adult | £32 | Every walk, club nights, the hut at members' rates |
| Joint, two adults at one address | £50 | The same, for both |
| Student or under 25 | £16 | The same |
| Junior, 10 to 15, with a member | Free | Sunday walks and the climbing wall |

Hut nights at Blea Ghyll are £9 for members and £14 for guests. Climbing wall sessions are paid at the door.

## How to start

1. Come on a Wednesday training walk or a Sunday club walk as a guest. Tell the leader it is your first time.
2. After two walks, ask the membership secretary for a form, or pick one up in the Reading Room.
3. Pay by bank transfer or at a club night, and you are on the list for the next hut weekend.

## Before your first walk

Bring walking boots, a waterproof jacket and trousers, a warm layer, lunch and a drink. From October to March bring a head torch to the Wednesday walk.
