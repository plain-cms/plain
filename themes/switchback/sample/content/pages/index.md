We walk every Wednesday evening and every Sunday, all year round, and spend a weekend a month at Blea Ghyll Hut. Some of us are out for the high tops and the Alpine meet; plenty come for a steady day and the tea afterwards.

Guests are welcome on any walk marked open to all, and on the Sunday walks. Come with boots and waterproofs, and say hello to the leader at the start.
