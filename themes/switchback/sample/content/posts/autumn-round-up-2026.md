The best-attended day was the Raven Crag ridge in August, with 31 walkers and two dogs. The wettest was the September Wednesday when the bus shelter flooded.

The new routes, over Thornsgill Edge and round Grisedale Tarn, will be walked again in the spring.

The left boot found at Wythe Pass on 20 September is at the Reading Room. Size nine, brown, much loved.
