The old range had been smoking since 2009. The new stove was paid for by the hut fund and a grant from the parish council, and fitted by Ruth Nakamura-Dixon's team over two weekends in October.

Please split wood before you leave, and bring a bag of kindling when you come. The Christmas weekend in December will be its first full test.
