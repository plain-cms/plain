A walk that ends in the dark is not an emergency if everyone has a torch. From November the leaders will ask to see these before we set off:

- a head torch with fresh batteries, and spare batteries
- a hat, gloves and a spare warm layer, even on a mild morning
- waterproof jacket and overtrousers
- a map of the area and a whistle
- a flask or two litres of water, and more food than you think you need

Microspikes are worth having for the Sunday walks after a frost. The club has six pairs to lend; ask Owen Pryce.
