The route follows the Stubai high trail from Neustift, with six nights in mountain huts and a rest day at the Sulzenau hut. Most days are five to seven hours of walking on marked paths; one optional day crosses a glacier on a rope with a guide.

You need to have walked at least three club days this year. Talk to Joe Satterthwaite if you are unsure whether the meet is for you.
