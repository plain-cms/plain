description: Two days of walks from the hut door, with the club's Christmas dinner cooked on the new stove on Saturday night.
example: true
---

Twelve bunks, first come first served. Book with the hut warden by 27 November and say whether you will eat on Saturday.

- Hut nights: £9 for members, £14 for a guest you bring
- Dinner: £12, paid on the night
- Bring a sleeping bag, slippers and something for the raffle

Saturday's walk goes over Blea Pike and down the ghyll, about 14 km. Sunday is shorter and ends at the cars by noon.
