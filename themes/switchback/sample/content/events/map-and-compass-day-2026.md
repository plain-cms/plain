No experience needed. Bring boots, waterproofs, a packed lunch and a compass if you have one; we lend maps and spare compasses.

The day is free for members and £5 for guests. Tell Owen Pryce you are coming so that we have enough leaders: [training@harrowdalefac.example](mailto:training@harrowdalefac.example).
