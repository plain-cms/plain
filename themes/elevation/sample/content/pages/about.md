Ruth Ostler and Daniel Wren set up the practice in 2018, after twelve years between them in larger offices in Leeds and Manchester. Both of us are registered architects. Every project is drawn by one of us and checked by the other, from the first site visit to the last snagging list.

## How we work

We start on site, not at a desk. The first visit is free: an hour walking the building or the plot with you, then a short letter saying what we think is possible, what it might cost and what we would charge to get you there.

We draw by hand first and on screen second, and we build a card model of almost everything. Planning officers, builders and neighbours understand a model in a way they rarely understand a drawing.

## What we do

- New houses and extensions
  - Infill plots and garden plots in conservation areas
  - Extensions to stone terraces and farmhouses
- Second lives for old buildings
  - Mills, barns and chapels
  - Listed building consent
- School and community buildings
- Interiors and joinery for shops and cafés

## Fees

We charge a fixed fee for each stage, agreed in writing before the stage begins.

| Stage | What you get | Typical fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Feasibility | Site visit, measured survey, two or three options sketched and costed | £1,200–£2,500 |
| Planning | Drawings, design statement and the application, with every conversation with the planners | 4–6% of build cost |
| Detailed design and site | Building regulations drawings, tender, and a fortnightly visit while it is built | 6–8% of build cost |

## Who for
