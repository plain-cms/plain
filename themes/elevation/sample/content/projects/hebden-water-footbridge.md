The trust asked for a new footbridge to replace stepping stones that flood several times every winter. Span 21 metres; budget £380,000.

## The challenge

The bridge had to clear the highest recorded flood of 2015 and still sit low enough in the woods not to be seen from the crags above.

## The approach

One shallow arch of weathering steel, springing from the existing stone abutments of an older bridge, with a deck of oak felled in the valley. The handrail is a single steel flat at elbow height; there is nothing above it.

## The outcome

The jury placed the scheme second. The winning design was built in 2025, a hundred metres downstream.
