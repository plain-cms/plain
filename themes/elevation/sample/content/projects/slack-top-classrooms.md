A cross-laminated timber extension of 160 m² for a school that had been teaching its oldest children in a mobile classroom since 2009. Contract value £610,000.

## The challenge

The building had to go up between the last day of the summer term and the first day of the autumn one: six and a half weeks, on a site reached by a single-track lane.

## The approach

The walls and roof were made in panels off site and arrived on four lorries in one week. Everything heavy was finished before the holidays began; everything noisy was finished before the staff came back.

## The outcome

Year 5 and Year 6 started the autumn term in their new rooms. The covered outdoor room between them is now where the whole school eats lunch when it rains.
