A shelter for eight people at the last stop before the moor road. Built from the stone of a fallen field wall for £7,800, most of it the roof.

## The challenge

The old shelter faced the road and the weather came from behind it. The new one had to keep out a westerly and still let the driver see who was waiting.

## The approach

A curved back wall of drystone to the west, open to the east, and a window slot at standing height looking down the road. The bench is a single oak plank, 3.2 metres long.

## The outcome

Twenty-two volunteers built it over six Saturdays with a waller from Heptonstall. The village holds its summer meeting in it.
