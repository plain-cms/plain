A laithe is a farmhouse and barn under one roof. The house half had been lived in for two centuries; the barn half had held hay, then cars, then nothing. 210 m² once joined.

## The challenge

The barn had four openings in its walls and the planners wanted no more. A house needs light.

## The approach

The cart door, five metres high, became one window lighting a double-height hall. Rooflights run along the back slope where they cannot be seen from the lane, and the bedrooms sit in the old hayloft under them.

## The outcome

The family moved in for Christmas 2022. The barn's old owl hole, high in the gable, is now the window of the youngest child's room.
