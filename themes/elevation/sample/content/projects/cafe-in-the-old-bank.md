A banking hall of 1904, 140 m², with a plaster ceiling worth keeping and a strongroom nobody could get into. Fit-out cost £185,000.

## The challenge

The bakers needed ovens, a proving room and a dishwasher in a building with no extract, no drains in the right place and a ceiling the conservation officer had asked us to leave alone.

## The approach

The bakery went into the strongroom, once a locksmith had opened it. Its walls hold the heat and its old vent took the extract. The new counter is oak and runs the length of the hall on the line of the old one.

## The outcome

The café opened in October 2023. The strongroom door stands open every morning from six.
