A single room, 3.6 by 4.2 metres, clad in Siberian larch and lined in birch ply shelving from floor to ceiling on three walls. Built for £46,000.

## The challenge

The garden climbs nine metres from the back door to the top wall, and nothing could be brought up it bigger than two people could carry.

## The approach

Everything was sized to be carried: a frame of short glulam posts, floor cassettes made in a joiner's shop in Mytholmroyd, and shelving in panels no longer than 1.8 metres. The one large window faces back down the hill to the house.

## The outcome

The builder put it up in eleven working days. The books moved in over a fortnight, a box at a time.
