Salem Chapel is a galleried chapel of 1868 with seating for 600. The collective had a lease offer, a list of forty artists waiting for space and a grant bid due in eight weeks.

## The challenge

Twelve studios had to fit without touching the gallery, the pulpit or the painted ceiling, all three of which carried the chapel's listing.

## The approach

The studios stood on the ground floor as twelve timber rooms, open at the top, set between the old pew lines. The gallery stayed as it was and became the exhibition space, looking down over the studios.

## The outcome

Planning and listed building consent were granted, but the grant bid was not. The chapel was sold in 2022 and converted into a private house.
