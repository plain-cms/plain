A new house of 96 m² on a plot the owners had used as a vegetable garden for thirty years. Stone below, dark-stained timber above, and a stone slate roof.

## The challenge

Two earlier applications on the plot had been refused. Both had tried to look like the cottages next door and stood taller than the chapel wall, which the conservation officer considered the most important thing on the street.

## The approach

We kept the house below the top of the wall on the street side and let it rise only at the back, where the land falls away. From the lane you see the wall, a new oak gate and a roof; from the valley you see two storeys of glass.

## The outcome

Planning was granted at the first attempt, with no objections from the neighbours. Joan and Martin moved in eighteen months later and now rent out the family house up the hill.
