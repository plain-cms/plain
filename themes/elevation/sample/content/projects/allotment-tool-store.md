A timber store of 9 m² with a green roof that waters four butts below it. Materials cost £2,100, raised at two plant sales.

## The challenge

Twelve plot holders, one padlock and no budget. Tools were being carried up from home in wheelbarrows.

## The approach

We drew it so that anyone could build it: a post-and-beam frame with no joint harder than a bolt, cladding from offcuts given by a sawmill in Hebden Bridge, and a sedum roof grown on the plots the year before.

## The outcome

Built in two weekends. It was our first project as a practice, and the one we are still asked about most.
