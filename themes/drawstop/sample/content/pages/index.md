I have been Organist of St Mary's Priory, Wexcombe, since 2014, and I play
around thirty recitals a year away from it: big symphonic instruments in
town halls, cathedral organs, and village organs that someone has just
spent ten years raising the money to restore.

## New for winter 2026

On 5 December I play all nine movements of Messiaen's *La Nativité du
Seigneur* at Easterby Minster, and the Priory Christmas recital on
19 December has already sold out. The full list, with tickets, is on the
[dates page](/shows/).

*Fanfares and Fantasias*, recorded on the Merrow Cathedral organ, came out
in September. Where to hear it and buy it is on the [music page](/music/).

## Booking a recital

I play on any organ with two manuals and pedals or more, and I am happy to
build a programme around an anniversary, an appeal or a newly restored
instrument. Programmes, biographies and what I need from a venue are on
the [press page](/press/).
