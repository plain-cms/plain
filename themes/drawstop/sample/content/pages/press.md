## In one line

Imogen Thwaite is a concert organist and the Organist of St Mary's Priory, Wexcombe.

## In fifty words

Imogen Thwaite is a concert organist based in Wexcombe, where she has been
Organist of St Mary's Priory since 2014. She plays around thirty recitals a
year in cathedrals, parish churches and town halls, and is known for
complete cycles (Bach's *Clavier-Übung III*, Messiaen's *Nativité*) and for
improvising on themes from the audience.

## In two hundred words

Imogen Thwaite grew up in Saltholme and learned the organ at St Nicholas
Church on Quay Street, where she now plays every spring. She studied music
in Merrow, was organ scholar at Merrow Cathedral, and spent four years as
assistant organist at Easterby Minster before coming to Wexcombe in 2014.

At the Priory she plays for services, directs the Priory Singers and runs
the lunchtime recital series. Away from home she plays around thirty
recitals a year, in cathedrals, on the big Victorian concert organs of
northern town halls, and at the reopening of restored village instruments,
which she counts among the best evenings of any year.

Her programmes are often whole cycles played in one sitting: Bach's
*Clavier-Übung III*, Messiaen's *La Nativité du Seigneur* and the Vierne
symphonies. Most recitals end with an improvisation on a theme handed in
by the audience.

She has made three recordings: *Bach at Wexcombe* (2019), *Messiaen: La
Nativité du Seigneur* (2023) and *Fanfares and Fantasias* (2026), recorded
on the organ of Merrow Cathedral.

## Photographs

Print-quality photographs, at the console and portrait, are sent by e-mail
on request.

## What a recital needs

- Three hours alone with the organ the day before, or on the morning, to set the registrations
- A page turner for the longer programmes; Imogen can bring one
- In a building of more than three hundred seats, a camera on the console and a screen, if the venue has them
- The organ tuned within the month, and the reeds touched up on the day
