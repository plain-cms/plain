## Fanfares and Fantasias (2026)

Ceremonial and festive music on the four-manual organ of Merrow Cathedral:
Lemmens, Guilmant, Bairstow and Mathias, ending with Widor's Toccata.
Start with the Bairstow *Prelude on "Vexilla regis"*.

- [Buy the CD](https://shop.example.com/fanfares-and-fantasias) — £12, with a booklet on the organ
- [Download or stream](https://listen.example.com/thwaite/fanfares-and-fantasias)
- Signed CDs at every recital, from the table by the door

## Messiaen: La Nativité du Seigneur (2023)

The complete cycle, recorded over two winter nights at Easterby Minster.
Start with *Dieu parmi nous*, the last movement.

- [Buy the CD](https://shop.example.com/messiaen-nativite)
- [Download or stream](https://listen.example.com/thwaite/messiaen-nativite)

## Bach at Wexcombe (2019)

Preludes, fugues and chorale preludes on the Priory organ at home,
including the *Passacaglia in C minor*.

- [Download or stream](https://listen.example.com/thwaite/bach-at-wexcombe)
- CDs from the Priory shop, or by post from the Priory office
