Wednesday at 12:30, forty minutes. Free, with a retiring collection; sandwiches are welcome in the pews.

The programme repeats the music played at the organ's opening in March 1877, including the Mendelssohn Sonata No. 1 and a set of variations by the Priory's first organist.
