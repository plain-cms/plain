Doors at 18:45, recital at 19:30, about seventy-five minutes with no interval. Unreserved seating in the nave; a big screen in the crossing shows the console.

Programme: Bach, Prelude and Fugue in E flat, BWV 552; Franck, Choral No. 3 in A minor; Vierne, Symphony No. 3. Merrow station is a ten-minute walk down Abbey Hill.
