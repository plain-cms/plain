Tickets go on sale on 1 March 2027 from the Ludworth Organ Society, at the town library or by post. One ticket covers all four recitals.

Imogen plays the last recital of the day, at 17:00 at St Peter's: English music from Stanford to Howells, and Elgar's Sonata in G.
