## In one line

Who you are and what you play, in one sentence.

## In fifty words

Where you are based, any post you hold, how many recitals you play and what you are known for. Only facts you can stand behind.

## In two hundred words

Where you learned, where you have played, the programmes and recordings you are proud of. Leave this section out until there is enough to fill it.

## Photographs

Say how to get them, for example: sent by e-mail on request.

## What a recital needs

- Practice time alone with the organ before the recital
- A page turner or registrant, if you need one
- A console camera and a screen in a large building, if the venue has them
