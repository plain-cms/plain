Panels last thirty years and inverters about twelve. If the display is blank, or the app has shown nothing for a week, the inverter is the likely cause, and every sunny day without it is money lost.

## What we check first

Half of the inverters we are called to are not dead. A tripped isolator, a failed fan or a lost internet connection looks the same from the sofa. The first visit is a system health check, and if the fix is a switch that is all you pay for.

## What decides the price

The from-price is a like-for-like swap of a single inverter up to 4 kW, fitted in the same place. If you are thinking about a battery, say so: a hybrid inverter costs more now and saves buying a second one later.
