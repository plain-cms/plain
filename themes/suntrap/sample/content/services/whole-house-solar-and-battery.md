Fitting both at once saves a second visit, a second set of paperwork and a second inverter. On most houses that is between £900 and £1,400.

## How we size it

We ask for a year of electricity bills, or half-hourly readings if your meter is a smart one. The panels are sized to the roof and the battery to your evenings. Bigger is not always better: a battery that never fills is money on the wall.

## What it should do

On an ordinary three-bedroom house the starting system covers about 70 per cent of a year's electricity. The quote gives the figure for your house, month by month, and we stand by it.
