A full charge from the grid at the day rate costs about £17. From your own roof in June it costs nothing, and the charger can be told to wait for the sun.

## How the visit goes

Most chargers take half a day. The power is off for about an hour. We set the charger up on your phone, show you the solar-only mode, and leave you with the certificate.

## What decides the price

The from-price is a charger on the house wall within ten metres of the consumer unit. A longer run, a charger on a post, or a consumer unit that has no room for another circuit are priced before we start.
