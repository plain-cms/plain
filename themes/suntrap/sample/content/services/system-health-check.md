Panels fail quietly. One poor connector can take a third off a system's output and nobody notices until the yearly statement. We look after systems we did not fit, including those whose installer has stopped trading.

## What you get

A two-page report: what the system should be making, what it is making, and what, if anything, to do about it. Often the answer is nothing.

## What it costs

Fixed price for a house system of up to sixteen panels that can be reached from a ladder. If the roof needs a tower or scaffolding, we tell you when you ring.
