Without a battery, a house uses about a third of what its panels make. With one, about three quarters. The rest of the saving comes from filling the battery overnight on a cheap tariff in winter.

## Where it goes

Garages and utility rooms are best. Lofts are possible if the joists are sound and there is a fixed ladder. We do not fit batteries in bedrooms or on escape routes, and we will say so at the survey.

## What decides the price

The from-price is a 5 kWh battery added to a system whose inverter can already take one. A 10 kWh battery, a new inverter, or a backup circuit that keeps the lights on in a power cut are each priced separately on the quote.
