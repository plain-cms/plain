The gap under a solar array is warm, dry and safe from cats, which is why pigeons like it. The noise at five in the morning is the first sign. Chewed cable is the second.

## How the visit goes

We work from a tower or from roof ladders, never from the panels themselves. Mesh is clipped to the panel frames, so nothing is drilled and no warranty is affected. A ten-panel array takes about three hours.

## What decides the price

The from-price covers one array of up to ten panels on a two-storey house. Nesting birds are protected by law between March and August: if there are eggs or chicks, we wait, and it costs you nothing extra.
