A typical three-bedroom house takes ten panels and makes about 3,800 units of electricity a year, a little more than it uses. What you do not use is sold back to your supplier.

## How the job goes

The survey takes an hour: we measure the roof, look in the loft and check the consumer unit. The quote follows within three working days. Scaffolding goes up on a Monday, the panels on Tuesday, the electrics on Wednesday, and the scaffolding is gone by Friday.

## What decides the price

The from-price is eight panels on one face of a tiled roof, two storeys high. More panels, slate, a second roof face or a three-storey house add to it. Every one of those is on the quote before you say yes.
