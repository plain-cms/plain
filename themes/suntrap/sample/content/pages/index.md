## Who turns up

Priya Tallis designs the system and Daniel Rowe leads the fitting. There are five of us, all employed, with no subcontractors and no salesmen: the person who measures your roof is the one who comes back a year later. We are MCS certified, so your panels qualify for export payments from the first day.

## Where we work

Hexley and about twenty-five miles around it: up the Wend valley to Dunmore and Aske Bridge, and east as far as Thorley. For a whole system we will come a little further.

## Is your roof any good?

East, west, part shaded, slate or tile: most roofs are better than their owners think, and the survey costs nothing. Call [01632-960418](tel:01632-960418), Monday to Friday from eight, Saturday until one.
