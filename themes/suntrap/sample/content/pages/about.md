## Who we are

Priya Tallis is an electrician and Daniel Rowe is a roofer. They met on a new-build site in 2011, fitted one system together on Daniel's mother's bungalow, and have fitted a little over 1,400 since. Marcus and Jo fit with Daniel. Bethan runs the office, books the scaffolding and deals with the network operator so that you never have to.

## What we promise

- **One price.** The figure on the quote is the figure on the invoice. If we find rotten battens once the tiles are up, we show you a photograph and agree the cost before touching them.
- **An honest forecast.** Every quote says how much the system should generate in a year, worked out from your roof and not from a brochure. If it falls more than a tenth short in the first two years, we come back and find out why at our cost.
- **Our own people.** Nobody is paid on commission, and nobody you have not met turns up to do the work.
- **A tidy roof.** Cables are run inside wherever they can be, tiles are replaced and not siliconed, and the scaffolding is down within three working days.

## Guarantees

Panels carry a 25-year maker's warranty and batteries ten years. Our own workmanship is guaranteed for ten years and backed by insurance, so it stands even if we do not.

## Paperwork
