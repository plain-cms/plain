On a bright day the panels make more than the house is using. A diverter notices the surplus and heats the hot water with it, so the boiler stays off from about April to September.

## How the visit goes

It takes a morning. The power is off for about an hour while we fit the unit beside the hot water cylinder and the clamp at the meter. Before we go we show you the one button you may ever need: the boost.

## What it costs

The from-price covers a house that already has panels, a hot water cylinder and a working immersion heater. A new immersion heater, or a cable run of more than ten metres, is priced before we start.
