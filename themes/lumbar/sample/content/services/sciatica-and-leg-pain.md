Sciatica means pain along the path of the sciatic nerve, usually from irritation near the lower back. It can be very painful and often improves over weeks to a few months.

## What happens

We test the strength, feeling and reflexes in your legs to see how the nerve is working. Treatment aims to ease the pain and keep you moving. If anything in the examination worries us, we refer you to your GP that day and tell you why.

## What it costs

A first visit is an hour, £64. Follow-ups are forty minutes, £50.

## Afterwards

Numbness between the legs, new problems passing urine or controlling your bowels, or weakness in both legs need urgent care: call 999 or go to A&E.
