Back and pelvic pain are common in pregnancy as your posture and ligaments change. Treatment is gentle and adapted at every stage, and it never replaces your midwife's care.

## What happens

Ruth sees all our pregnancy appointments. After the usual questions, treatment takes place lying on your side or sitting, with pillows to support your bump. You leave with positions for sleeping and getting out of bed that take the strain off.

## What it costs

A first visit is an hour, £64. Follow-ups are forty minutes, £50.

## Afterwards

If pain suddenly gets worse, or comes with bleeding, tightenings or a fever, contact your midwife or maternity unit straight away rather than us.
