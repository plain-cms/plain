Most sudden back pain is frightening and not dangerous. A muscle or joint has been strained, the muscles around it have locked tight to protect it, and every movement hurts. It usually settles within days to a few weeks — the aim is to get you moving sooner and more comfortably while it does.

## What happens

We ask how it started and check for the few signs that mean you need a doctor instead. Then we treat gently — no forceful manipulation on a back that is in spasm — and show you two or three positions and movements that ease it. You leave with a plan for the next 48 hours: what to do, what to avoid, and when to call us back.

## What it costs

A same-day appointment is a follow-up length visit: forty minutes, £50. If it is your first time with us, it is booked as a first appointment, an hour for £64.

## Afterwards

Keep moving little and often, even if it is only walking round the house. Most people feel a clear change within a week. If the pain spreads down both legs, or you notice numbness or bladder changes, call 999 or go to A&E.
