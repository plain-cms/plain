## What happens

We go at your pace. Treatment is gentle — no quick manipulations — and combines hands-on work with simple exercises you can do in a chair or holding the kitchen counter.

## What it costs

A first visit is an hour, £64. Follow-ups are forty minutes, £50.

## Afterwards

Many people come back every six to eight weeks to keep things moving. There is no obligation to book again until you want to.
