A sore knee is often working hard for a stiff hip, or the other way round. So we look at the whole leg, and at how you walk.

## What happens

We watch you walk, squat and climb a step, then examine both joints and the muscles around them. Treatment eases stiffness and pain, and we give you a short set of exercises to build strength where it is missing. If we suspect wear in the joint that needs an X-ray, we write to your GP.

## What it costs

A first visit is an hour, £64. Follow-ups are forty minutes, £50.

## Afterwards

Strength work takes six to eight weeks to make a real difference, so we usually see you a few times over that period rather than every week.
