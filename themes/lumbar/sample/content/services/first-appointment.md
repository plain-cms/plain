Everyone starts here, whatever brought you in. The first appointment is the longest because the listening matters most.

## What happens

We ask about the pain and your health in general, then watch how you move and examine the area that hurts. We explain in plain words what we think is happening and what we would suggest. If treatment makes sense and you are happy to go ahead, it starts in the same appointment. If it doesn't, we tell you who to see instead.

## What it costs

An hour, £64. We take cards and most health insurance; ask when you book.

## Afterwards

You may feel a little sore for a day or two, as after exercise. Most people come back for one to three follow-up visits; some need only this one.
