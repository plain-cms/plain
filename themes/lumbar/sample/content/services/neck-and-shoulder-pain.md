## What happens

We check how far your neck turns and tilts, how your shoulders move, and how the nerves to your arms are working. Treatment is mostly soft-tissue work and gentle mobilisation. Manipulation of the neck is only ever offered, explained and agreed first — never sprung on you.

## What it costs

A first visit is an hour, £64. Follow-ups are forty minutes, £50.

## Afterwards

Your neck may feel tired for a day. Keep turning your head as normal, and do the two exercises we show you twice a day.
