## What happens

Femi asks about your training as much as your pain: what changed in the weeks before it started, how far, how often, on what. He examines the injured area and how you move, treats it, and writes you a return plan with distances and checkpoints.

## What it costs

A first visit is an hour, £64. Follow-ups are forty minutes, £50.

## Afterwards

Most people keep training in some form while they recover. The plan tells you what to keep, what to drop for now, and when to build back up.
