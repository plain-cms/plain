There is no single perfect posture. The bodies that cope best with desk work are the ones that move often. This appointment is about finding the two or three things in your working day that matter most for you.

## What happens

We look at your photos, then at how you sit, stand and turn your head. We check your neck, shoulders and back, treat anything that is stiff or sore, and agree on a few small changes: the height of a screen, a reminder to stand, one stretch you will actually do.

## What it costs

Forty minutes, £50.

## Afterwards

Try the changes for two weeks before judging them. If something still aches, a follow-up appointment picks up where we left off.
