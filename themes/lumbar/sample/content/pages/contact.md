## Book or ask

Call **[01632-960412](tel:01632-960412)** while we are open, or write to [hello@brindleyard-osteo.example](mailto:hello@brindleyard-osteo.example) and we will reply the same working day. Tell us roughly what hurts and since when; it helps us give you the right length of appointment.

If you need to cancel, please give us a day's notice so someone else can have the time.

## Back gone? Same-day line

If your back has gone and you can barely move, call the same-day line on **[07700-900318](tel:07700-900318)**. It is Ruth's practice mobile, answered on the days we are open. We keep one slot free each morning for exactly this and will see you that day if it is still free.

**Some symptoms cannot wait for an osteopath.** Numbness between your legs or around your back passage, new trouble passing urine or controlling your bowels, or weakness in both legs: call 999 or go to A&E straight away.

## Where we are

**Unit 3, Brindle Yard, Tanner Street, Elmsford.** Go through the brick arch next to the hardware shop on Tanner Street. Our green door is the second on the left, under the old saddler's sign.

## Parking

Two marked spaces in the yard belong to the practice; take either. If both are full, the Mill Lane car park is a four-minute walk and free after 6 pm.

## Access

The door and the treatment room are on the ground floor, with a level threshold and no steps from the yard. The couch lowers to knee height, and there is a chair with arms in the room. The toilet is on the same floor but is not wide enough for a wheelchair; tell us if that matters and we will plan around it.
