We are a small osteopathy practice in an old saddler's workshop at the back of Brindle Yard: one treatment room, two osteopaths and a phone that a person answers. Most people come with a sore back or a stiff neck. Some come because a knee or a hip has started to complain on the stairs. All of them get the same thing first — time to tell us what is going on.

## What people come to us for

- [Acute back pain](/care/acute-back-pain/) — when your back has gone this morning; ring the same-day line
- [First appointment](/care/first-appointment/) — an hour to listen, examine and usually treat
- [Neck and shoulder pain](/care/neck-and-shoulder-pain/) — from desks, driving or a bad night's sleep
- [Sciatica and leg pain](/care/sciatica-and-leg-pain/) — assessed carefully, and referred on when it should be
- [Running and sports injuries](/care/running-and-sports-injuries/) — and a sensible way back to training

[Everything we treat, and what each visit costs](/care/)

## Your first visit

Ruth or Femi will ask about the pain, your work, your sleep and anything you have already tried. Then we look at how you move — standing, bending, lying down — and tell you in plain words what we think is happening. If treatment makes sense, it usually starts in the same hour. If it doesn't, we say so and tell you who to see instead.

You may be asked to undress to your underwear so we can see how your back and hips move. Shorts and a vest top are fine if you would rather. A chaperone is welcome at every appointment.

## Prices

A first appointment is £64 for an hour. Follow-up treatment is £50 for forty minutes. We take cards and most health insurance; ask when you book.
