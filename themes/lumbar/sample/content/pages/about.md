Ruth Calloway opened the practice in 2015, after nine years working in a busy sports clinic where appointments were twenty minutes long. She wanted a room where nobody had to watch the clock. Brindle Yard is that room.

## The people

- **Ruth Calloway**, registered osteopath — Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday mornings. Backs, necks and pregnancy back care.
- **Femi Adeyemi**, registered osteopath — Tuesday and Thursday, until 8 pm. Runners, cyclists and the knees and hips of people who used to be both.
- **Tom Ashby** looks after the phone and the diary on weekday mornings. If you call and Tom answers, he can book you, move you or find out who is free today.

## How we work

Every appointment starts with a conversation and ends with a plan you can follow at home. Treatment is hands-on: soft-tissue work, stretching, joint mobilisation and, only if you agree to it first, manipulation — the quick movement that sometimes clicks. Plenty of people never have it and do just as well.

We would rather see you three times and send you away with exercises than see you every week for a year. If we think your pain needs a scan, a GP or a specialist, we write the letter and tell you why.

## The room

One room on the ground floor, with a couch that lowers to knee height, a chair with arms, and a screen to change behind. It is quiet, warm, and has a window onto the yard.
