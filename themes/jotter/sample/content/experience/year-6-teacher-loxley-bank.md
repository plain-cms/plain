Loxley Bank is a two-form-entry school on the edge of the city, with a mixed
intake and a staff who have mostly been there a long time. When I arrived,
every year group taught maths its own way.

## Maths across the school

We spent a year agreeing one approach: small steps, the same pictures and
models from Reception upwards, and ten minutes of retrieval every morning. I
taught demonstration lessons in each year group and ran six twilight sessions
for the whole staff.

## Year 6

My class has 30 children and two teaching assistants in the mornings. I keep
the routines the same all year, including the weeks of the tests, so that May
feels like any other month.
