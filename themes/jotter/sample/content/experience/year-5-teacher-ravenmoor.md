Ravenmoor is a one-form-entry school with a large garden and a small budget.
I took on computing in my first term because nobody else wanted it, and found
that the children who struggled to write could often explain a program
perfectly well.

In spring 2020 I taught from my kitchen table. Nine families had no device at
home, so I delivered a paper pack to each of them every Monday until the
school reopened.
