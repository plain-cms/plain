On Tuesday morning there was a tea chest at the top of the stairs with a note from Mrs Ackroyd of Mill Row: her mother’s willow pattern, forty-one plates, nine cups, eleven saucers and a gravy boat, “to be used and not looked at”.

They are washed and on the shelves. The gravy boat now holds the custard.

> If you have china that deserves an outing, bring it in. We pay in cake.
