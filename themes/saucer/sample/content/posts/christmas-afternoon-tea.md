From Wednesday 2 December until Wednesday 23 December the afternoon tea changes for Christmas: turkey and cranberry sandwiches join the egg and cress, the scones are spiced with orange, and the top tier carries a mince pie, a slice of Yule log and a square of Christmas cake with Wensleydale.

The price is £27.50 each, with a pot of tea or a glass of mulled apple juice. As ever, we need a day’s notice: ring [01632 960418](tel:+441632960418).

## Christmas hours

- Last day before Christmas: Wednesday 23 December, closing at 16:00
- Closed from 24 December to 5 January
- Open again on Wednesday 6 January at 10:00
