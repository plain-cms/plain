The scones go in at eight and the first sponge is cooling by nine. Tea is loose leaf, made in a pot and left alone for four minutes. See [the whole menu](/menu/), or [how to find the stairs](/visit/).

## This week

The damson jam is finished and the first jars of marmalade are open. From December the cake stand turns festive: [Christmas afternoon tea](/blog/christmas-afternoon-tea/) can be booked now.

## The china

None of it matches. The plates came from house clearances, church sales and customers’ attics, and the willow pattern outnumbers everything else three to one. If you break a cup, you are in good company.
