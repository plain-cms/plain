Last orders are half an hour before closing. We shut for a fortnight over Christmas and New Year.

## Find us

6 Buttermarket, Alderbeck, on the first floor above the old butter market. The Dales bus stops at the Market Cross, a minute’s walk away, and the long-stay car park is on Tannery Lane behind the church.

The tea room is up fourteen stairs with a handrail on both sides, and there is no lift. If the stairs are a difficulty, ring ahead and we will set a table for you in the courtyard or pack a cream tea to take away.

## Book a table

Tables for tea and cake are first come, first served. Afternoon tea needs a day’s notice so that we can bake for you: ring [01632 960418](tel:+441632960418) during opening hours, or write to [tea@oddsaucer.example](mailto:tea@oddsaucer.example) and we will answer by the next morning.

## Parties

The whole room seats twenty-four and can be yours on a Monday or Tuesday for a christening, a birthday or a book group that has got out of hand.
