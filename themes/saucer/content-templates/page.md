We bake for the whole party at once: sandwiches, scones and three cakes on every stand, and as many pots of tea as you can drink. Tell us about allergies when you book and we will plan around them.

## How to book

Write to [tea@example.com](mailto:tea@example.com) with the date and the number of guests. We hold the room for three days while you gather numbers.
