The kitchen takes last orders half an hour before closing. We shut for the first two weeks of January.

## Find us

14 Market Lane, in the old print works behind the square. The nearest car park is on Cooper Street; the bus stops at Market Cross, two minutes’ walk. There is one step at the door and a ramp we’re glad to put down — just knock.

## Book a table

Call us on [020 912 3456](tel:+353209123456) between 10:00 and 17:00, or email [table@example.com](mailto:table@example.com). For groups of six or more, please give us a day’s notice so we can set the long table.
