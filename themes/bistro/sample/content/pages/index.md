We cook what the market gives us and pour wine we’d happily drink ourselves. The bread is baked here every morning, the trout is smoked out the back, and the half roast chicken has been on since the day we opened. See [the full menu](/menu/), or [find your way to the door](/visit/).

## This week

The first wild mushrooms are in, so the tagliatelle is back. The pears came from an orchard twenty minutes up the road and go into the pot with the same red we pour by the glass. Friday and Saturday fill up early — ring before you set out.

## The long table

One table seats fourteen. It is yours for a birthday, a leaving do or a Sunday that got out of hand; give us a day’s notice and we’ll cook for the whole table at once.
