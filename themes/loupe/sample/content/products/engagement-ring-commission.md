We start with what the ring has to live through: gardening, a keyboard, a toddler. That decides the setting before anything else does. Then we draw, and a week later you try on a wax model in the size and shape of the finished ring.

## If it is a surprise

Borrow a ring that fits the right finger and bring it in; we can measure it in a minute and nobody need know. The finished ring is resized once without charge.

## Stones

Tell us what you want to spend on the stone and we will have two or three sent on approval to look at in daylight. Stones from a family ring are welcome, and we will tell you honestly whether they are sound enough to set.
