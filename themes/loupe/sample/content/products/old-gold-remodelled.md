Old gold can be melted, rolled and made into something new, or the stones can be lifted and re-set in a new mount. Which is better depends on the carat, the solder in the old piece and what you want to keep of it.

Bring everything, including the broken chain and the single earring. What we do not use is weighed and handed back, or its value is taken off the bill.
