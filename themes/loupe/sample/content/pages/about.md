Morwenna Penhale served her apprenticeship with a manufacturing jeweller in Birmingham's Jewellery Quarter; Tobias Wray trained as a silversmith in Sheffield and spent six years mounting stones for the trade. They took the old saddler's shop on Tanners Row in 2009, put two benches under the north window and a counter in front of them, and have worked there since.

## How we work

Nothing is bought in finished. Wire is drawn, sheet is rolled and stones are set here, and a customer at the counter can watch it happen. Castings, such as the acorn cups and the bark rings, are poured for us by a family foundry in Sheffield from waxes we carve ourselves.

## What we work in

| Metal | Stamp | What it suits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sterling silver | 925 | Everyday rings, chains, earrings |
| 9 carat gold | 375 | Signets and hard-wearing bands |
| 18 carat gold | 750 | Wedding and engagement rings |
| Platinum | 950 | Settings for diamonds |

All our gold and silver is recycled, refined in Birmingham and bought as grain. Diamonds and coloured stones come from two dealers we have used since we opened, and each arrives with its paperwork.

## The hallmark

> A hallmark is the oldest consumer protection there is: someone other than the maker has tested the metal and says it is what the maker claims.

Our maker's mark has been registered at the Sheffield Assay Office since 2009. Anything of ours above the legal weight is sent there before it is sold, which is why a new commission can never be ready the same week.
