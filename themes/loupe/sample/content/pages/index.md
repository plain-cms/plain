## At the counter

The shop and the workshop are one room. Everything in the cases was made at the two benches behind the counter, in sterling silver or in 9 and 18 carat gold, and can be made again in your size, usually within three weeks.

## Hallmarked in Sheffield

Every piece carries our maker's mark, P&W, and goes to the Sheffield Assay Office to be tested and struck with its fineness, the Yorkshire rose and the year's date letter. Ask for the loupe and we will show you where to look.

## This winter

The acorn cup earrings are being cast from this autumn's acorns, and will be made until the cups run out. Wedding bands ordered by the end of February are ready for an Easter wedding.
