## Getting here

From the Market Cross, walk down Tanners Row towards the river: we are the fourth door on the left. The lane is too narrow for cars. The nearest parking is the pay-and-display at Mill Green, three minutes away on foot, and the 31 bus from Malton stops at the Cross on the hour.

## Steps and access

There is one step up at the door. A ramp is kept just inside; ring the bell and we will put it down. The counter has a lowered end with a chair, a mirror and good daylight.

## Fittings and commissions

Call in while we are open to try a ring or leave a repair. For a commission, book a time on [01632 960418](tel:01632960418) so that one of us can leave the bench for three quarters of an hour and give you our whole attention.

## Paying

- Card, cash or bank transfer
- Commissions: half when you approve the design, the rest on collection
- Repairs: paid on collection, never before

## By post

We send finished pieces by tracked next-day post, insured and signed for. Repairs can come to us the same way: write first, pack the piece in a small box inside a padded envelope, and do not write "jewellery" on the outside.
