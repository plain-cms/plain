Tanner Row runs from the Market Place down to the river. We are number 9, on the left as you walk downhill, between the cobbler and the old bank. The door is the one with the 45 adapter for a handle.

## Getting here

- The station is eight minutes on foot: out of the main entrance, across the Market Place and down the Row.
- Buses 4 and 27 stop at the Market Place.
- Tanner Row is closed to cars from ten until four. Park at Foundry Yard, two minutes away, £1.20 an hour and free after six.
- There are cycle hoops outside the cobbler.

## Steps and access

There is one shallow step at the door and a ramp behind the counter; ask and it is down in a moment. The racks are at wheelchair height apart from the top row of the jazz wall, which we will gladly go through for you. The listening deck has a stool and a pair of headphones that reach it.

## Paying

Cash, card and contactless. We take the Kelderby pound. Shop credit from records you have sold us never runs out.

## Collection and post

A record held by phone or e-mail waits behind the counter with your name on it for three days. If you would rather have it posted, we pack on Mondays and Thursdays and send it tracked. Up to four LPs travel in one mailer for the same £4.50. We do not post outside the UK, because we cannot stand behind a grade after three weeks in a hold.
