## In the racks

About four thousand LPs and as many singles, filed by genre and then by surname. Second-hand records sit beside new ones, so the 1971 pressing and this year's reissue of the same album are in the same row and you can choose with both in your hands.

## How we grade

Every second-hand record is cleaned and played on the shop deck before it gets a sticker. The grade on the sleeve is what we heard, not what we hoped: NM, VG+, VG or G, first for the vinyl and then for the cover. If it plays worse at home than the grade says, bring it back.

A collection of two hundred Blue Note and Prestige albums came in from a house in Sandsend and goes out to the jazz racks a crate at a time. The dub and roots section has moved to the wall by the listening deck.
