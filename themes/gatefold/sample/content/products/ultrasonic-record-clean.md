Each record has ten minutes in the ultrasonic bath, a rinse in distilled water and an hour on the drying rack. It will not mend a scratch, but it takes out the crackle that is only dirt, which is most of it.

We cannot clean shellac 78s or records with a paper label that is already lifting.
