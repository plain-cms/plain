# Theme catalogue

100 planned themes in 14 families, beside the 15 themes already shipped. A **family** is a structural archetype with **one content contract** — the same collections, fields, data files, navigation preset and template names for every theme in it — so a site can switch between two themes of one family with no content change. Within a family, themes differ only in how they look: each is for a particular trade or person, is built around one real-world object from that trade, and spends its boldness on one signature element (cms-spec.md §10.1).

This is Stage 1 of the bulk theme build: a plan, not code. The family contracts (Stage 2) are in `families/<family>.md` at the repo root — not inside `themes/`, where every folder is treated as a theme.

## Decisions taken and known weak spots

The rows went through three review rounds (218, then 92, then 47 findings, each fixed, replaced or dropped), and again when each family's contract was written: a row whose signature the contract could not carry was reworded to fit it.

### How the hundred came out

All hundred rows were built and none was dropped. Each theme went through the same steps: built to its row and its family contract. Two independent checks followed: a design review that saw only `screenshot.png` and the row, and a technical check against the contract and the theme rules. Up to two rounds of fixes, each followed by a fresh check, came next, and last a polish pass over the checker's small findings.

- **Final design scores:** 84 themes at 8 out of 10 and 16 at 7, the pass mark: `loupe`, `dewclaw`, `crux`, `roughs`, `reel`, `linocut`, `dugout`, `advent`, `tenancy`, `seaview`, `hideaway`, `showhome`, `backpatch`, `chambers`, `datestamp`, `doublebill`. They were kept because each carries its row's object and nothing in them is broken, but they are the first candidates for a second design pass.
- **First reviews:** three themes failed theirs, all at 6: `loupe`, `souschef` and `advent`. Each passed after fixes. In all, 32 themes needed no fix round, 61 needed one and 7 needed two.
- **Dark-first screenshots:** the 17 themes designed dark first (`advent`, `alehouse`, `bandstand`, `chisel`, `crux`, `drawstop`, `elevation`, `eyechart`, `gasket`, `hideaway`, `loupe`, `rivet`, `souschef`, `suntrap`, `slurp`, `vigil`, `wristband`) were reviewed from screenshots of their light scheme. Their `screenshot.png` was retaken afterwards in their own dark scheme and checked by eye, not by a fresh review.
- **Limits every theme shares:** the template language cannot slice a list, so a home page that shows a collection shows all of it (themes cap it with CSS or a fade). A page whose slug has one or two letters counts as the home page in themes that test `page.url.4`.

### Decisions the family contracts rest on

- **`property` is one family.** `keys`' `status` select gained To let, Let agreed, Available and Booked after its three sale values. The contract groups the seven into three states (open, pending, closed), and a theme styles states, not values. There is still no availability calendar and no sleeps or amenities field.
- **`restaurant` has ten sections.** `bistro`'s `section` select gained Breakfast, Bread, Pastries, Sides, Hot drinks and Snacks. The service order is fixed in the contract and every theme seats the sections the same way.
- **Past dates stay on the list.** The build cannot know today's date, so an events or shows list includes whatever is in the collection, and no theme says "upcoming" in its own words.
- **Contact is a link.** `trades`, `clinic` and `writing` each gained an optional `email` key in their data file, so a theme can print a `mailto:` link where there is no phone.
- **Every rendered item can carry a description.** `classes`, `personal`, `property`, `music` and `news` each gained an optional `description` field that the shipped themes already read but the schema did not declare.
- **`writing` holds three shipped themes.** `letters` is the contract. `default` keeps its landing layout and `terminal` its `github-stars` plugin as named extras in `families/writing.md`; no new theme may add either.
- **Interface words in eight languages** live in each theme's `strings.json`: English keys at the top level (all the engine reads) and one object each for `de`, `fr`, `it`, `ro`, `es`, `pt`, `nl`, which a site copies into `data/strings.<lang>.json`.

### Rows that are still a stretch

- `restaurant/crumb` — the site title repeated across a band is a fixed run of decorative copies, clipped; a very long title shows fewer of them.
- `music/mixtape` — two sides of five lines; an eleventh show runs on under side B.
- `clinic/eyechart` — the stepped first letters follow alphabetical order, not importance.
- `portfolio/linocut` — a word wider than the block is still broken mid-word.
- `nonprofit/hustings` — an election candidate is a loose fit for programs, an impact number and a donate button.

### Families whose themes are still close

- **`clinic`** — several themes turn the service list into a chart.
- **`property`** — `tenancy` and `buzzer` were moved apart, as were `hideaway` and `seaview`, but each pair shares a persona.
- **`restaurant`** — half the signatures are the masthead, not the menu.
- **Across families** — typewriter or mono on a card or label recurs in about ten rows; white ground, black ink and one colour is the recipe for about a third of the light rows.

### Names

- Ten names are two words run together: `dualcontrol`, `taxreturn`, `souschef`, `doublebill`, `backissue`, `showhome`, `seaview`, `fieldgate`, `fryup`, `dropin`.
- `chisel` (a stone letter-cutter's portfolio) reads as a carpentry theme.
