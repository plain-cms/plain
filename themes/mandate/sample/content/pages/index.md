**Available for an interim or turnaround assignment from January 2027.** Anywhere in the north of England, on site four days a week.

I am a chartered accountant with eighteen years in finance and restructuring, the last five as interim finance director, chief restructuring officer or chief executive of businesses that were short of cash, of time, or of both. Boards, lenders and owners bring me in when the numbers have stopped adding up and somebody has to stand in the room.

- **Cash first.** A thirteen-week cash flow in the first fortnight, and lenders who know what it says.
- **A plan the board can sign.** Costed, dated and owned by named people, not by a slide.
