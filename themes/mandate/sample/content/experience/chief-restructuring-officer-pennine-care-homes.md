A care home cannot close for a refit or turn away its residents while the numbers are fixed, so a turnaround there is done with the doors open. The group had grown by buying homes faster than it could staff them, and agency nurses were costing more than the fees brought in.

We recruited a permanent bank of staff across the group, paid the same rate whichever home they worked in, and sold six homes that were never going to be full to operators who could fill them. Each sale was agreed with the local authority first, and no resident had to move.

The group refinanced in February 2024 with a smaller estate and a lower debt, and I stepped down when the new chair took office.
