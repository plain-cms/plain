Calder Valley Mouldings makes injection-moulded parts for the automotive and white-goods trades from two sites in Halifax. When I arrived the business had breached its banking covenants twice in a year, the founder had stepped back, and the bank had asked for an independent business review.

## The first ninety days

A thirteen-week cash flow, reviewed with the bank every Friday, bought the time for everything else. We repriced the twenty largest contracts, most of which had not moved since resin prices doubled, and closed the second shift on the older of the two sites. Nobody was made redundant who wanted to stay; the night shift moved to the main site.

## Handing over

The refinancing closed in the summer of 2026 with the same lender, on longer terms. The permanent chief executive, recruited from the board's own shortlist, shadowed me for six weeks before I left in October.
