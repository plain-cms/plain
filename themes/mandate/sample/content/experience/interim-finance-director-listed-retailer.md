The finance director left with two weeks' notice, three weeks before a trading update that turned out to be a profit warning. The board wanted someone who had sat in front of lenders and analysts before and could start on a Monday.

Most of the year went on the estate. We closed eighteen stores at lease break and renegotiated rent on sixty-four more, store by store, with the landlords' own figures on the table. The rest went on the audit, which moved to a new firm that year and still signed on the original date.

I handed over to the permanent finance director in April 2025, after the results announcement.
