I'm Ruth Kavanagh. I direct, shoot and edit documentaries and short films, and make commissioned films for charities, mills, galleries and festivals across Yorkshire and the North. Most projects are me and one camera; bigger ones bring in a sound recordist and a colourist I have worked with for years.
