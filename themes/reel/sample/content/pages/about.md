I started as a camera assistant on regional news, spent six years cutting other people's documentaries, and have made my own films since 2019. I work from a shared studio by the canal in Leeds and travel for most shoots.

## How I work

Small crews, long access. I would rather film a place for twelve Thursdays than for two long days, and I show the people in a film a cut before anyone else sees it.

## What I do

- Short and feature-length documentary
- Commissioned films for charities, makers and arts organisations
- Interview and portrait films
- Edit and colour on other directors' projects

## Who for

Calder & Aire Canal Society, Marsden & Hey Worsted, Wharfedale Arts Trust, Leeds Tenants' Advice Network, Dales Line Railway, the Pennine Peat Partnership and Chapel Allerton Open Studios.

## Start a project

Tell me what the film is for, who will watch it and when you need it. A first conversation costs nothing, and I will say plainly if I am not the right person.
