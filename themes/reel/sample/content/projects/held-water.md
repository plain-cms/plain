Documentary, 24 minutes, shot between November and March on the locks above Hebden Bridge.

## The brief

The society wanted a film for its fiftieth year that new volunteers would actually sit through — not a history, but a reason to turn up on a wet Saturday with a windlass.

## The making

I filmed one lock-keeping crew of six through a whole winter, on the days the canal was shut as much as the days it was open. There is no narration: the crew's own radio chatter and the sound of the paddles carry it.

## What came of it

The film premiered at the society's AGM in a canal-side chapel and now opens every volunteer induction. Forty-one new volunteers signed up in the six weeks after the first screening.
