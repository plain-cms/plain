Twelve films of one minute each, in vertical and widescreen versions, all subtitled.

## The brief

Visitors chose which studios to walk to from a map. The organisers wanted every artist to get the same minute, whatever they made.

## The making

One morning per studio, one question per artist: what are you making this week? Every film was subtitled so it could play silently in the shared gallery.

## What came of it

Visitor numbers were the highest in the weekend's eight years, and five of the artists now use their minute on their own websites.
