Commissioned film, 3 minutes, no dialogue, cut to loop silently on a stand.

## The brief

The mill had never exhibited abroad. Buyers would pass the stand at walking pace, so the film had to make sense from any frame, with the sound off.

## The making

Two days in the mill: the yarn store at dawn, the looms at full speed, the menders' table under north light. Every shot is locked off and held for at least six seconds, so a buyer who stops for one shot sees a whole one.

## What came of it

The mill came home with nine new trade accounts and now uses the film, re-cut at thirty seconds, on its website and in sales meetings.
