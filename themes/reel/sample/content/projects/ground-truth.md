Documentary, 40 minutes, filmed on twenty-two days between October and March.

## The brief

The partnership needed to explain to landowners and funders why restoring peat takes decades, without a single graph on screen.

## The making

Three scientists, one long season of rain. I filmed them blocking drainage grips by hand, carrying sphagnum up the moor in buckets, and measuring the water table every fortnight in the same eleven holes.

## What came of it

Ground Truth has been shown at forty screenings in village halls and at two conferences. A shorter cut is part of the partnership's funding applications.
