Fiction short, 10 minutes, self-funded, shot in one night in a closed ward of a hospital outside Wakefield.

## The idea

My father was a hospital porter for thirty-one years. The film is the shift he described to me most often: nothing happens, and everyone he passes says goodbye without saying it.

## The making

A cast of four, a crew of five and eleven hours of darkness. We rehearsed in a car park for two weekends so that on the night every scene could be shot in two takes at most.

## What came of it

Night Porter screened at five festivals and won the audience award at a regional short-film night in Sheffield.
