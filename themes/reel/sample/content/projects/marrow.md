Short documentary, 8 minutes, self-funded, filmed over one summer of growing and one Saturday of judging.

## The idea

I went to film the vegetables and stayed for the men who grow them: three neighbours who have competed against each other for thirty years and share every seed.

## The making

Allotments at six in the morning, a church hall on show day, and no interviews at all — just the growers talking to their marrows and to each other.

## What came of it

Marrow was my first film to play at festivals, and it is the one people still ask me about.
