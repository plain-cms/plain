Documentary, 12 minutes, shot in the ringing chamber of one village church over twelve months.

## The brief

The trust funds one film a year about a tradition in the dale that might not last. The band had eight ringers and needed ten.

## The making

The chamber is three metres across, so the camera stayed on a single fixed mount in the corner all year. I recorded the bells from the churchyard, the ropes from inside, and let the ringers explain the method to each other rather than to me.

## What came of it

The trust screened it in the church itself, bells and all. Two of the people in that audience now ring with the band on Thursdays.
