Commissioned film, 90 seconds, for cinema advertising and the railway's centenary weekend.

## The brief

One minute and a half to say a hundred years, to be shown before the main feature in two local cinemas.

## The making

We shot a single day in August, beginning at 4.30am in the shed as the fire was lit. The aerial shots of the viaduct were flown with the railway's permission and a licensed operator beside me.

## What came of it

The centenary weekend sold out both days. The railway still runs the film before its autumn timetable is announced.
