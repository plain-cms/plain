Six films of three to five minutes each, for the charity's campaign on damp and disrepair.

## The brief

The network wanted tenants to speak for themselves, without being shown as victims and without anyone being identifiable to their landlord unless they chose to be.

## The making

Every interview was filmed at the tenant's kitchen table, with one small camera and no lights of my own. Four people chose to show their faces; two are filmed by their hands and their kitchens, and their voices are their own.

## What came of it

The films were shown to the city council's housing scrutiny board and shared widely online. Each tenant approved their own cut before it was published.
