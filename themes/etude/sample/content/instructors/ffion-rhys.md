Ffion gives singing lessons on Wednesday afternoons and leads two choirs:
Youth Voices for ages 11 to 16, and Little Voices on Saturday mornings for
ages 5 to 8.

A first lesson starts with breathing and a few silly sounds, then moves to
a song the pupil chooses. Musical theatre is welcome; so is folk, pop, and
anything in Welsh.

She grew up singing in chapel in Carmarthenshire and sang with a touring
opera chorus before coming to Frome.
