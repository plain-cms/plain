Clara teaches piano on Monday and Thursday afternoons and runs Tuesday's
Theory Club for Grades 1 to 5. Most of her pupils are between six and
sixteen; a few started with her as children and now bring their own.

A first lesson with Clara is half an hour at the grand piano in the front
room: finding middle C, a tune with both hands by the end, and a short
list of things to practise that fits on one page of a notebook.

She trained at a northern conservatoire, spent ten years accompanying
choirs, and still marks every pupil's fingering in pencil, never pen.
