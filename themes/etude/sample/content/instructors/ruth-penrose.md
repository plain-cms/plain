Ruth leads Music Bugs three mornings a week. Each session is forty minutes
for children from two to four, with a parent, grandparent or carer sitting
on the floor beside them.

There are songs with actions, a parachute, egg shakers and a real cello
for the children to touch at the end. Nobody has to join in until they
are ready, and some children only watch for the first few weeks.

Ruth trained as a primary teacher, and has run early years music groups
around Somerset for nine years.
