Daniel teaches piano on Thursdays and Saturday mornings, mostly to
grown-ups: returners who stopped at Grade 3, retired engineers, and one
farmer who learned to read music at sixty-eight.

His Tuesday group class, Adult Beginners' Piano, runs on eight digital
pianos with headphones, so you can make mistakes in private and play
together at the end of the hour.

Before teaching he played keyboards in theatre pits for twelve years. He
will happily teach you to read chord symbols as well as the dots.
