Tomasz teaches violin and viola on Tuesday afternoons. He runs the two
children's string groups — First Strings for ages 5 to 7 and Junior
Strings for ages 7 to 11 — and conducts the Saturday Orchestra with
Isabel.

Beginners start with a violin from the school's loan cupboard, and with
pizzicato tunes before the bow. Adults are welcome; he has taught a
seventy-year-old to play her late husband's viola.

He studied in Kraków and played in a regional orchestra in Poland before
moving to Somerset. He tunes every child's violin before every lesson,
which is why the lessons always start on time.
