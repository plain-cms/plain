Isabel teaches cello on Wednesday afternoons and runs the Strings Ensemble
that follows, a chamber group for violin, viola, cello and double bass
players of Grade 4 and above. On Saturdays she sits beside Tomasz at the
Saturday Orchestra.

Cellos come in small sizes, and the school has five to lend for a first
term. Most new pupils play an open-string tune in the first lesson and a
proper one by half term.

She has played in a string quartet for twenty years and repairs bows as a
sideline, so expect a short lecture on rosin.
