Helen gives singing lessons on Friday afternoons and conducts the school's
two adult choirs: the Chamber Choir on Mondays, which sings in four parts
from scores, and Sing for Pleasure on Thursdays, which learns everything by
ear and needs no audition.

Lessons with Helen are for adults at any stage, from first lesson to
diploma repertoire. She is particularly good with voices that have been
told, at some point, that they cannot sing.

She studied at a London conservatoire and sang as a soprano soloist for
fifteen years. She still warms up every rehearsal with the same three
scales.
