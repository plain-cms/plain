## How lessons work

Most pupils come once a week for a private lesson of thirty, forty-five or
sixty minutes, at a time agreed with their teacher inside the windows on
the timetable. Group classes — choirs, string groups, Theory Club and
Music Bugs for the very small — meet at fixed times and are booked for a
whole term.

We teach piano, violin, viola, cello and singing, from first notes to
Grade 8 and beyond. Exams are welcome and never compulsory: about half our
pupils take them, the other half just play.

## Where to start

Children under five start with **Music Bugs**. From five, most begin with
a free trial lesson on the instrument they are curious about, and many join
**First Strings** or **Little Voices** alongside. Adults who have never
played usually start with Daniel's group piano class or with **Sing for
Pleasure**, which needs no audition and no reading.
