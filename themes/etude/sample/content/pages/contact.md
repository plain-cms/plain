## Your free trial lesson

Ring or email the office with the instrument, the age of the pupil, and the
afternoons that suit you. We will offer a half-hour trial with a teacher
that week, free and with no commitment. Bring nothing: we lend violins,
violas and small cellos for the first term, and every room has a piano.

## Fees

Lessons are paid by the term, in advance; a term is eleven to thirteen
weeks of lessons.

| Lesson or class            | Each week | Notes                              |
| -------------------------- | --------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Private lesson, 30 minutes | £19       | Best for under-tens and beginners  |
| Private lesson, 45 minutes | £27       | Most pupils from Grade 3           |
| Private lesson, 60 minutes | £35       | Grade 6 and above, adults          |
| Music Bugs                 | £6        | One child and one grown-up         |
| String groups and choirs   | £7        | Free with any private lesson       |
| Theory Club                | £7        | Workbooks included                 |

Brothers and sisters get ten per cent off the second private lesson.
Instrument loans are free for the first term, then £30 a term.

## Finding us

**Tanner Street Music School**, The Old Chapel, 7 Tanner Street, Frome —
the grey stone chapel with the green door, halfway down the hill from the
market place. There is a short-stay car park on Market Yard, and the train
station is a twelve-minute walk.

The building has a ramp at the side entrance and a lift to the first-floor
rooms.
