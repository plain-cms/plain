Lessons marked *By arrangement* are private lessons: you and your teacher
agree a regular slot inside that window, of thirty, forty-five or sixty
minutes. Group classes last an hour, except Music Bugs, which lasts forty
minutes. The building opens fifteen minutes before the first lesson of the
day, and the waiting room has a kettle.

## What the levels mean

- **Ages** — group classes for children are grouped by age, not by
  ability. A child who is between groups can try both.
- **Grades** — the graded music exams (Grade 1 is about a year of lessons,
  Grade 8 is the top). Your teacher will tell you roughly where you are.
- **Adults** — eighteen and over, and nobody asks how much over.

## Term dates

- **Autumn term 2026** — Monday 7 September to Saturday 12 December 2026;
  half term Monday 26 to Saturday 31 October.
- **Spring term 2027** — Monday 4 January to Saturday 27 March 2027; half
  term Monday 15 to Saturday 20 February.
- **Summer term 2027** — Monday 12 April to Saturday 17 July 2027; half
  term Monday 31 May to Saturday 5 June.

There are no lessons in the half terms or between terms. Changes for snow
or illness are posted on this page and sent to every family by email.

## Enrolling

New pupils can start in any week of term; group classes are charged from
the week you join. Book a free trial lesson through the
[office](/contact/), and we will send a place and a term invoice after it.
