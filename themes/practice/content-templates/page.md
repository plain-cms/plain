Every price is told to you before the work starts, and written down if you
would like to take it home and think.

## What visits cost

| Visit | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Check-up & cleaning | Fixed price, told when you book |
| Urgent visit | Flat fee, told on the phone |
| Treatment | Quoted in writing first |

## Paying

We take cards and cash at the front desk on the day. For larger treatment
plans, ask about spreading the cost over a few months — there is no charge
for doing so.

## Insurance

We work with most dental plans and send the claim for you. Bring your card
to the first visit and we will check what is covered before anything begins.
