We're a small practice at 14 Alder Street: two dentists, one hygienist, and a front desk that answers the phone. We keep appointments unhurried, we explain everything before we do it, and we never start work without telling you what it costs.

## How we can help

- [Check-ups & cleaning](/care/check-ups-and-cleaning/) — thirty unhurried minutes, once a year for most people
- [Fillings](/care/fillings/) — tooth-colored, and the numbing gets time to work
- [Children's dentistry](/care/childrens-dentistry/) — first visits that are mostly a ride in the chair
- [Dentures](/care/dentures/) — made to fit your mouth, adjusted until they do
- [Urgent & after-hours care](/care/urgent-and-after-hours/) — in pain today? Call first; we hold same-day slots every morning

[Everything we do, and what each visit costs](/care/)

## Your first visit

You'll sit down with a dentist, not a form. We look, we listen, and we talk through what we find in plain words — no jargon, no pressure. Most first visits take about forty minutes, and you leave knowing exactly what (if anything) needs doing and what it would cost.

If it's been years since you saw a dentist, that's fine — it's most of the new patients we meet. Nobody here will lecture you.

## Nervous about the dentist?

Say so when you book — it changes how we plan your visit. We go at your pace: we explain each step before it happens, we agree on a stop signal, and nothing happens without your say-so. Many of our regulars once dreaded coming. They'll tell you it passes.

## Find us

14 Alder Street, Fairview — the blue-green door between the pharmacy and the bakery, two minutes' walk from the Market Square bus stop. There's free parking behind the building and a step-free entrance at the back door.
