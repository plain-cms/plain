We would always rather save a tooth. When one is too broken or too loose to
keep, taking it out properly ends the pain and protects the teeth beside it.

## What happens

We numb the area and wait until you tell us it is completely numb. You will
feel pressure, not pain — if that changes, raise a hand and we stop. Most
teeth are out within twenty minutes. Before you leave we talk through what
could fill the gap later, with no decision needed on the day.

## What it costs

A fixed price for a simple removal and a higher fixed price for a surgical
one, such as a wisdom tooth below the gum. You'll know which yours is, and
the figure, before we start.

## Afterwards

Bite on the gauze for half an hour, skip hot drinks and hard exercise for a
day, and don't rinse until tomorrow. Some oozing and an ache are normal.
Pain that gets worse after the third day isn't — call us and we'll see you
that day.
