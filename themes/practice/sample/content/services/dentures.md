A denture replaces some or all of your teeth and comes out for cleaning.
A good one is quiet: it stays put when you talk, and you stop thinking about
it by the second week.

## What happens

Four short visits over about a month. We take impressions, check your bite
with a wax model, try the teeth in so you can see them before anything is
final, and then fit the finished denture. You choose the shade and shape
with a mirror in your hand.

## What it costs

A fixed price for a full denture and a fixed price for a partial, in writing
at the first visit. Adjustments in the first three months are included —
most new dentures need one or two.

## Afterwards

Expect a few sore spots in the first week; call and we'll ease them the same
week. Dentures need relining every few years as your gums change, and a
cracked or dropped denture can usually be repaired within two working days.
