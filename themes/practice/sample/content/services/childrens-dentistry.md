We like to meet children early — around the first birthday, or when the
first teeth are through. Nothing much happens at that visit, and that is the
point: the chair goes up and down, the mirror gets passed around, and the
dentist counts teeth out loud.

## What happens

A parent stays in the room, always. For the youngest children the check-up
happens on your lap. We look at every tooth, check how the bite is coming
together, and paint on fluoride varnish if it will help. If your child says
no, we stop and try again another day — a calm second visit beats a
frightening first one.

## What it costs

A child's check-up is one fixed price, lower than an adult's, told to you
when you book. Fillings and sealants are quoted in writing first, the same as
for grown-ups.

## How often

Every six months until the adult teeth are settled, usually around age
twelve. We'll tell you if your child can safely come less often.
