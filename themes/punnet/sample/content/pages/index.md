## On the stall

Three stalls' length of fruit and veg under the green awning by the Buttercross, weighed on the scales in front of you. Buy one carrot or a sack of potatoes; nobody minds. If you want it for jam, for juicing or for the freezer, say so and we will pick it out to suit.

## Straight off the fen

Most of the vegetables are grown within fifteen miles of the Market Place and were in the ground the day before. Callum is at the growers' gates by five; what he cannot get locally comes from the wholesale market at Spalding the same morning.

## Through the year

Asparagus from St George's Day, strawberries from the end of May, plums in August and Bramleys from September. Whatever has just come in is on the front of the stall with the biggest star on it.
