We are on the north side of the Market Place, the three stalls nearest the Buttercross, under a plain green awning with the name along the front. On Saturdays the queue starts at the egg end.

## Parking and access

The Cattle Market car park on Sluice Road is two minutes away on foot and free for the first hour. Buses 37 and 505 stop at the Corn Exchange. The Market Place is flat and cobbled only at the edges; there is room at the stall for a wheelchair, a pushchair or a shopping trolley, and we will carry a heavy order to your car.

## Paying

Cash, card and contactless, and Healthy Start cards for fruit, veg and eggs. Bring your own bags and egg boxes if you can; we have paper bags if you cannot.

## Collecting an order

Orders are bagged and kept behind the stall under your surname. Collect any time the market is on, and tell us if someone else is coming for it. We keep an order until the end of the next market day.

## Friday veg box rounds

The van leaves when the stall is set up, at about nine. Delivery is £2 a box, and we need the order by four o'clock on Thursday on [01632 960174](tel:01632960174).

Leave last week's box out and we will take it back. If nobody is in, the box goes wherever you have told us is safe from the sun and the dog.
