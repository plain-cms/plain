---
title: Norfolk asparagus
category: Greens and salad
status: Seasonal
description: Cut at Terrington Marsh the afternoon before and sold from St George's Day to the longest day. The price is chalked up each market day.
details:
  - Bundle: about 250 g
  - Sprue and kitchen grades: loose, by weight
example: true
---

The price follows the weather, so it changes from one market to the next. Sprue, the thin spears, is cheapest and best for soup and omelettes.
