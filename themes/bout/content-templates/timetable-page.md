Anything visitors should know about the week: when the doors open, how
long a class lasts, what the levels mean, where holiday changes are posted.

## What the levels mean

- **All levels** — every round has an easier and a harder version.
- **Beginner** — assumes nothing.
- **Intermediate** — a few months of regular classes.
