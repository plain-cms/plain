## How the gym works

One ring, fourteen heavy bags, a wall of skipping ropes and a timetable that
starts at half past six in the morning. Nobody is too unfit, too old or too
nervous to walk in: most of our members had never thrown a punch before
their first Fundamentals class, and plenty still haven’t sparred. Sparring
is earned, never expected.

There is no joining fee and no contract. Pay per session or buy a card at
the desk — the [prices and directions are on the contact page](/contact/).
Gloves, wraps and gumshields to borrow are in the box by the ring.

## Where to start

Adults start with **Fundamentals** on Monday, Wednesday or Saturday: stance,
guard, the jab and how to stand back up after a round on the bags. Over
forty and not sure? Frank’s Thursday class was built for you. Children from
eight start in **Juniors**, and teenagers in **Youth Squad**. Every coach
on the [coaches page](/instructors/) is qualified, first-aid trained and
background-checked.
