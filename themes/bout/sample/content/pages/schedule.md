Doors open fifteen minutes before each class. Most classes run an hour;
Early Bell Boxfit and Juniors run forty-five minutes. Bank holiday changes
go on the board by the door and on this page a week ahead. Junior and
youth classes carry on through the school holidays.

## What the levels mean

- **All levels** — every round has an easier and a harder version, and you
  choose round by round.
- **Beginner** — assumes nothing. Never worn gloves? This is your class.
- **Intermediate** — about three months of regular classes: you know your
  combinations and can hold pads for a partner.
- **Experienced** — sparring. Ask Tomasz or Delroy before your first one;
  they will have watched you on the bags already.
- **Carded boxers** — the competition squad, for members registered to box
  in shows.

## Juniors and youth

Juniors (ages 8–11) and Youth Squad (12–16) are non-contact unless a coach
says otherwise and a parent has signed the form at the desk. A parent or
carer stays in the building for Juniors. Family Pads on Saturday morning is
one adult and one child together — no experience needed for either.
