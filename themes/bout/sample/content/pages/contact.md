## Your first session is free

Pick any class marked *Beginner* or *All levels* on the
[timetable](/schedule/), come fifteen minutes early and tell the desk it is
your first time. You will fill in a short health form, borrow gloves and
wraps, and a coach will show you how to put them on. Wear trainers and
something you can move in. Juniors need a parent or carer to sign them in.

separate; Tomasz will talk you through them.

## Where we are

**Foundry Lane Boxing Club**, Unit 4, Foundry Lane Trading Estate,
Smethwick B66 2LP — the green roller door at the far end of the yard, with
the bell painted on it. Buses 87 and 89 stop on the High Street, five
minutes’ walk. There is free parking in the yard after 5 p.m. and all day
Saturday.
