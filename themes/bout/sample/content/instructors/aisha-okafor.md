Aisha takes the 06:30 Early Bell Boxfit on Monday, Wednesday and Friday.
It is boxing training without the contact: skipping, bagwork, pads with a
partner and a finisher that she changes every week.

First-timers stand at the back, nobody minds, and by the third round
you have forgotten to be self-conscious. She calls every combination out
loud and counts down the last ten seconds of every round.

She came to the gym to get fit for a half-marathon and stayed for the
skipping, which she can now do faster than anyone in the building.
