Sunita coaches Juniors on Monday and Thursday at 17:00, and Family Pads on
Saturday at 09:30, where a parent or carer and a child train together on
the pads.

Juniors is forty-five minutes of balance, coordination and basic boxing,
taught through games. Nobody spars. Children leave tired, and usually ask
when they can come back.

She is the club’s welfare officer, so any question about a child’s safety
or wellbeing comes straight to her.
