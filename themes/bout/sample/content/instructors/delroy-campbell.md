Delroy teaches Fundamentals on Monday and Wednesday evenings and Saturday
morning, and runs Wednesday’s Sparring Night with Tomasz. If you join the
club, he is almost certainly the first coach you will meet.

Fundamentals is stance, footwork, the jab, the guard, and doing all four
when you are tired. He will correct your hands every single round until
you stop dropping them.

He boxed for the club as an amateur for eleven years and has the same rule
for everyone: you spar when he says so, and not a session before.
