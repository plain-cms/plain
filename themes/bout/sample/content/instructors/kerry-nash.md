Kerry runs Women’s Boxing on Tuesday at 18:15: an all-levels class for
women who want to learn to box with other women, from complete beginners
to members on the competition squad.

Stay on for the Conditioning Circuit at 19:30 if you have anything left.
It is open to everyone, and it is exactly as hard as it sounds.

She won two national novice titles before a shoulder injury moved her to
the coaching side of the ropes.
