Liam trains the Youth Squad on Tuesday at 17:00 and teaches Ring Craft on
Thursday evenings. Youth Squad is technique, fitness and respect for the
gym, and it is non-contact until a coach and a parent both say otherwise.

Ring Craft is for members who know their combinations and want to learn
where to stand: cutting off the ring, getting off the ropes, and turning
an opponent.

He joined the club at fourteen himself, and still holds the record for
the most rounds skipped without a trip.
