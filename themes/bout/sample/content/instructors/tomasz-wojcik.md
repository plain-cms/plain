Tomasz coaches Bagwork & Pads on Monday at 19:30, Sparring Night on
Wednesday with Delroy and on Friday on his own, and the Competition Squad
on Saturday at noon.

Sparring Night is controlled, supervised and matched by weight and
experience. Bring your own gumshield and headguard once you are sparring
regularly; until then, the club lends them.

He boxed in Poland and in the Midlands before coaching, and works the
corner for every member of the squad on show nights.
