## Who we are

Dan Fenner started with one second-hand Luton van and a borrowed sack truck in 1994, moving students in and out of the terraces behind the station. Ruth joined in 2011, straight from a logistics course, and put the diary on a screen. Dan still carries the heavy end.

Today there are nine of us. Everyone on the vans is on the payroll, trained by us, and has been with the firm at least two years. We do not use day labour, and we do not pass your job to another firm.

## What we promise

- **One price.** We look round, we write it down, and that is what you pay. Stairs, parking and a late set of keys are our problem.
- **On the day we said.** If a van breaks down we send another. In thirty years we have never cancelled a move.
- **Nothing left in pieces.** Beds, wardrobes and cots are rebuilt before we go, and the boxes go in the rooms written on them.
- **Put right, not argued about.** If we mark it, we mend it or pay for it. You deal with Ruth, not a claims line.
