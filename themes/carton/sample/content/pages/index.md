## Who turns up

Dan Fenner bought his first Luton van in 1994. His daughter Ruth runs the diary now, and between them they have four vans, a crew of seven and a dry store on Canal Wharf. Nobody is hired in for the day: the crew that looks round your house is the crew that carries it. Blankets on everything, runners on your carpets, and the beds put back together before we leave.
