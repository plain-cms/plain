A business cannot stop for a week to move. We do it while you are closed.

## How it goes

A fortnight ahead we walk both buildings with you and mark up a floor plan. Lidded crates arrive a week before, each one numbered to a desk. On the night we move in plan order, so the first desks in are the first people at work on Monday.

## What decides the price

The from-price covers an office of up to six desks moving within Tarnside in one evening. Larger offices, shop fittings and anything that needs an electrician are priced on the visit.
