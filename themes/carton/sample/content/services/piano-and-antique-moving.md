A piano weighs as much as four people and has no handles. It wants a crew who move one every week.

## How we move it

Uprights go on a piano shoe and are strapped upright in the van. Grands come off their legs, go on their side on a padded skid board, and are rebuilt at the other end. Clocks travel with the movement and weights packed separately.

## What decides the price

The from-price covers an upright piano, ground floor to ground floor, within Tarnside. Stairs, grands and anything that has to come out through a window are priced after we have seen it.
