A house move is one long day done in the right order. We have done a little over four thousand of them.

## Before the day

Ruth or Dan comes round, opens every cupboard, and looks at the loft and the shed. You get a written price within two days. A week before the move we drop off wardrobe boxes and mattress bags at no charge.

## On the day

The crew arrives at half past seven. Carpets get runners, banisters and door frames get guards. The kettle, the cat basket and the box marked FIRST go on last. At the new house we ask where each room is, once, and then you can stop answering questions.

## What decides the price

The from-price covers a two-bedroom house moving within fifteen miles. More rooms, a longer drive, a piano or a packed loft are counted on the visit, and the figure we write down is the figure on the invoice.
