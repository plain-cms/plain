Not everything is a house move. If it fits in a van and takes an afternoon, this is the cheap way to do it properly.

## How it works

Tell us what is going and where. We give you a time, turn up, and the clock starts when we knock on the first door and stops when the last thing is in. The minimum is two hours.

## A second pair of hands

One mover can carry most things if you take the other end. If you would rather not, a second mover is £22 an hour.
