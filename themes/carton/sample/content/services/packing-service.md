Packing is the part everybody underestimates. Two of our packers do in a day what takes most households three weekends.

## How the day goes

We start with the rooms you use least and finish with the kitchen. Each box is labelled with the room it came from and what is in it. Tell us what you need on the first night and it goes in one box, marked, loaded last.

## What decides the price

The from-price covers a two-bedroom flat. A three-bedroom house is usually two packers for a day and a half. We can also pack just the fragile things, or just the kitchen.
