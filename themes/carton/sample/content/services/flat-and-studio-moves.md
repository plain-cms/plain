Most flats fit in one Luton van and take a morning. You pay for that, not for a lorry and a day you do not need.

## How the morning goes

We arrive at eight, walk round with you, and load the heavy things first. Boxes go last so they come off first. At the other end everything goes into the room written on the box, and the bed is back together before we leave.

## What decides the price

The from-price covers a one-bedroom flat moving within Tarnside, up to the third floor at either end. A second bedroom, a fourth floor without a lift, or a longer drive is priced when we look round.
