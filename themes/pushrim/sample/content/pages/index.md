## What we do

When we started in 2012, the nearest wheelchair basketball session was
forty minutes away by car, and most of the children we met had spent PE
lessons keeping score from the side. Now there is a session somewhere in
town four days a week, in school halls that would otherwise stand empty
in the evening.

## How it works

Come to any session and play. The first four are free, after that it is
£3 a time (£2 for juniors), and nobody is turned away because they cannot
pay that week. We lend sports chairs, eyeshades and boccia ramps, and our
minibus collects players for three sessions a week. Every way to take part is on
[our programs page](/programs/).

## Where the money goes

Coaches and drivers give their time. A session still costs us about £15
a player: hall hire, chair repairs, insurance and diesel. The
[donate page](/donate/) shows what each gift pays for, down to the goalballs.
