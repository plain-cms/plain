Coaches and drivers give the hours. Your money keeps the hall booked and
the chairs rolling.

| Gift | What it pays for |
| --- | --- |
| £3 | A junior's session when the family cannot pay that week |
| £15 | One player's session: court time, chair, coach and insurance |
| £38 | An hour of the sports hall at Haverleigh High School |
| £68 | A new goalball, with the bells inside |
| £2,400 | A basketball sports chair, built to the player's measurements |

## How to give

**By bank transfer or standing order:** e-mail
[hello@haverleighability.org.uk](mailto:hello@haverleighability.org.uk) or
call [01632 960418](tel:+441632960418) and we send the details the same day.

**By cheque:** payable to Haverleigh Ability Sport and posted to us at
Haverleigh High School, Moor Road, Haverleigh HV4 7RT.

**At a session:** the tin is on the table by the door. Ask for a receipt
and you get one.

If you pay UK income tax, tick Gift Aid when we send your receipt and we
can claim another 25p on every pound.

## Other ways that count the same

A regular £5 a month does more than most one-off gifts: it is what lets us
book a hall for a whole school year. If money is tight, give an evening
instead. [Volunteering at a session](/programs/volunteer-at-a-session/)
needs no sports knowledge at all.
