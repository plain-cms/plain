title: Lift to the hall
description: Our twelve-seat minibus with a tail lift collects players from home for the Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday sessions, driven by volunteers.
example: true
---

Getting to the hall is often harder than playing once you are there. A taxi
that takes a wheelchair can cost more than a week's sport, so we drive.

**Who it serves:** any player who cannot get to a session another way.
Ring or e-mail by the day before and we add you to the round.

**A typical week:** three rounds a week, before the Tuesday boccia, the
Thursday adult basketball and the Saturday junior session. Six volunteer
drivers, each trained on the tail lift.

**What it costs us:** about £22 a round in diesel, plus the minibus's
insurance and its yearly service.
