Every session we run needs at least three volunteers. Most of ours had never
seen wheelchair basketball before their first evening.

**Who it serves:** players first, but volunteers tell us they get as much
out of it. You need to be 16 or over; we arrange the safeguarding check.

**A typical week:** two hours at the session of your choice. Boccia
assistants, minibus drivers and someone handy with a spanner are always
in demand.

**What it costs us:** about £45 per volunteer for training and the check,
once. Your time is the gift.
