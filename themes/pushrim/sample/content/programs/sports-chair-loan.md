A sports chair is not an everyday wheelchair: the wheels are angled for
turning and the frame is built to take a collision. A new one costs £2,400,
which is why most players start in one of ours.

**Who it serves:** every player at a basketball session, and players who
are training for a junior or league team.

**A typical week:** chairs come out of the store at the start of each
session and are checked on the way back in. Take-home loans run for a
school term, and a volunteer adjusts each chair to the player.

**What it costs us:** tyres, bearings and straps come to about £600 a year
across the fleet. A new chair, built to measure, is £2,400.
