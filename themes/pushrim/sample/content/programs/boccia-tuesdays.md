Boccia is a target-ball game, close to bowls, played seated. It was made for
players with the most severe impairments, and it is where many of our
oldest and youngest players start.

**Who it serves:** players from eight to ninety. Players who cannot throw
use a ramp and an assistant who aims it on their instructions, exactly as
at the Paralympics.

**A typical week:** Tuesday, 6pm to 8pm, in the hall at St Wilfrid's Primary
School, Church Walk. Six courts taped on the floor, short games, a lot of
tea.

**What it costs us:** a boccia set costs £250 and lasts for years; most of
the cost is the hall and the minibus that brings half of the players.
