Goalball was invented for blind players and has no sighted version. Teams
of three take turns rolling a ball with bells inside at the other team's
goal, and stop it with their bodies. The hall has to be silent for the
game to work, which makes Sunday morning the right time.

**Who it serves:** blind and partially sighted players aged 10 and over.
Sighted family and friends can play too: everyone wears eyeshades, so
everyone is equal.

**A typical week:** Sunday, 10am to 12.30pm, in the sports hall at Holm Lane
Academy. Court lines are taped over string, so players can feel them.

**What it costs us:** a goalball costs £68 and wears out in about a season.
Eyeshades, knee pads and the hall come to about £12 a player each week.
