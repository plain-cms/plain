Wheelchair basketball is fast, loud and very hard to play badly for long.
Our adult session is where the club began, and it is still the busiest
night of the week.

**Who it serves:** anyone aged 16 or over. Disabled and non-disabled players
play together, as the sport allows, and nobody needs their own chair.

**A typical week:** Thursday, 7pm to 9pm, in the sports hall at Haverleigh
High School, Moor Road. Half an hour of chair skills, then games. Players
who want more travel with the team to league fixtures about once a month.

**What it costs us:** about £15 a player each week: two hours of the hall,
chair repairs and insurance. Players pay £3, after four free sessions.
