For a lot of our juniors this is the first sport they have played rather
than watched. Within a term most can turn, stop and shoot at a lowered
basket, and some are playing junior league fixtures within a year.

**Who it serves:** children and young people aged 7 to 17 with any
impairment that makes running sport hard, and their brothers and sisters.

**A typical week:** Saturday, 10am to 12 noon, in the sports hall at
Haverleigh High School, Moor Road. Two coaches, three helpers, small
chairs for small players.

**What it costs us:** about £15 a player each week. Juniors pay £2, and
families who cannot pay that week do not.
