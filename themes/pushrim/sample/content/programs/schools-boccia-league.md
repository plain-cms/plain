title: Schools boccia league
description: A league for nine local schools, played at a festival each term in the hall at Brookfield School, where every pupil on the team gets a game.
example: true
---

The league started because teachers told us their pupils had never once
represented their school. Now nine schools enter teams of four, and the
festival each term is the loudest day in Brookfield School's calendar.

**Who it serves:** pupils aged 9 to 16 at mainstream and special schools
in and around Haverleigh. Schools enter a team; we bring the kit and the
referees.

**A typical week:** one Friday each term, 10am to 2.30pm, in the hall at
Brookfield School. Between festivals our coaches visit schools to run a
practice session.

**What it costs us:** about £400 a festival for kit, referees' travel and
the medals, and nothing at all for the schools.
