## Who we are

Dave Hobday started with a second-hand roller mower and four customers on Church Row in 1998. Three of those gardens are still on the round. His daughter Ellie spent her school holidays raking up behind him, went away to study horticulture, and came back in 2019 to take over the borders and the planting plans.

There are only two of us, so the person who prices your garden is the person who turns up to do it.

## What we promise

- The price is agreed on the walk-round and does not change on the day
- We come on the day we said, and text the evening before if rain moves it
- Clippings, prunings and rubble leave with us, and the paths are blown clean
- If turf or a plant we put in fails in its first season, we replace it
