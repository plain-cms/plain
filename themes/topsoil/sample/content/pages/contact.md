## Call us

[01632-960417](tel:01632-960417)

If the mower is running we will not hear it. Leave a message and Ellie rings back at lunch or by six.

## Write to us

[hello@hobdaygardeners.example](mailto:hello@hobdaygardeners.example)

A photo of the garden helps. We answer in the evening.

## When we answer

| Day | Hours |
|:--|:--|
| Monday to Friday | 8am to 5pm |
| Saturday | 8am to 1pm |
| Sunday | Closed |

From November to February we stop at four, when the light goes.

## Before you ring

- The size of the lawn or the length of the hedge, roughly, in paces
- Whether we can get a mower round the side of the house
- If you have a green bin, or want the cuttings taken away

## Where we work

Aldermere, Low Fenton, Hoxby, Sutton Wick and the villages within about eight miles. The yard is at 6 Mill Lane, Aldermere. It is not open to callers, but post finds us there.
