Most fences fail at the post, not the panel. We dig every hole to two feet and set the post in concrete, so the next gale does not undo the work.

## What we put up

Six-foot closeboard for boundaries, lap panels where the budget is tight, picket for front gardens, and post-and-rail for paddocks. Gates are framed, ledged and braced, on galvanised hinges.

## What it costs

The from-price is one six-foot lap panel on a concrete post with a gravel board, fitted. Closeboard is £120 a bay. A side gate, hung and latched, is from £240.
