Ellie looks after the borders. She will tell you what is already doing well, what is sulking, and what would fill the gap by the fence.

## Regular care

Most borders want a visit every three or four weeks from March to October: weeding, dead-heading, tying in, and a mulch of composted bark in spring.

## New planting

We test the soil, watch where the sun falls, and write a list with prices. Plants come from the nursery at Sutton Wick and are charged at what we pay.

## What it costs

Border work is by the hour, with a two-hour minimum. Plants, compost and mulch are extra and itemised.
