New turf takes in about three weeks if it is laid on the day it is cut and watered every evening. We only lay from September to April, when it has a fair chance.

## Repairs

Scarifying and moss treatment in autumn, hollow-tine aeration on clay, and overseeding thin patches with a shade mix. Bare goalmouths get a square of turf let in.

## What it costs

The from-price is supplied and laid on ground we have prepared, with a minimum of twenty square metres. Scarifying and overseeding a tired lawn is from £90.
