Some gardens need rescuing before they can be gardened. We clear in a day what would take most people a month of weekends.

## How the visit goes

We walk round first and mark anything worth saving: an old rose, a decent apple tree under the ivy. Then two of us work from the house outwards. You get the garden back by the end of the day.

## What it costs

The from-price is half a day for two people with one trailer load taken away. A full day with two loads is £340. Soil, rubble and old sheds are priced by the load.
