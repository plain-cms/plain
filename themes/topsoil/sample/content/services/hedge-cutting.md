A hedge cut twice a year keeps its shape. A hedge left three years needs taking back in stages, and we will tell you which one yours is.

## When we cut

Beech and hornbeam in August. Privet and laurel in June and again in September. Nothing with a nest in it between March and the end of July, which is the law as well as good manners.

## What it costs

The from-price covers ten metres of hedge up to two metres high, reached from the ground. Taller hedges need a platform and a second pair of hands, and are priced when we have seen them.
