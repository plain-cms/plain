No two gardens start level, so we measure up, draw the job on squared paper, and give you one figure for the lot before a slab is ordered.

## What we lay

Indian sandstone and reclaimed York stone for patios, clay pavers for paths, and gravel over a membrane where the budget or the tree roots say so. A mowing strip along the lawn saves trimming the edge for ever after.

## How long it takes

A patio of twenty square metres is about four days: one to dig, one for the base, two to lay and point. We do not start one job until the last is finished.

## What it costs

The from-price is a square metre of clay pavers or gravel on level ground with easy access for a barrow. Sandstone, steps, slopes and old concrete to break out are priced on the drawing.
