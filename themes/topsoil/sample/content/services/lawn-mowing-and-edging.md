A stripe is only grass leaning two ways. It takes a mower with a roller behind the blade, a straight first pass and the patience to turn at the same spot each time.

## How the visit goes

We check the lawn for stones and toys, cut it in straight runs, then go round the edge with shears. A lawn of sixty square metres takes about forty minutes.

## What it costs

The from-price is one visit to a lawn of up to sixty square metres on a weekly or fortnightly round. Larger lawns are priced on the walk-round. A first cut of long grass is charged once at double.
