Overspill comes out twice a year, in April and October. Subscribers get one e-mail each time: a list of the new pieces, with a link to each. Nothing in between, and your address goes to nobody else.

To be added, write to [post@overspill.example](mailto:post@overspill.example) with the word *subscribe*. We add every name by hand. To leave, reply to any of our e-mails with the word *stop*.

## Printed copies

Printed copies of each issue are £3, posted anywhere in the UK, while they last. Ask for the issue by number in the same e-mail; we send the bank details back with your copy.

Prefer a feed reader? The [RSS feed](/blog/rss.xml) lists every piece the moment it goes up.
