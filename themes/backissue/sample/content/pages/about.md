Overspill is made by Priya Nandra and Dom Keeley, who both grew up on the Harlow estates and still live within a mile of the Water Gardens. Priya teaches English at a sixth-form college; Dom repairs bicycles. Neither of us is paid for this, and neither is anyone else, which is why the zine costs what the paper and ink cost.

## What goes in

Writing about Britain’s new towns by the people who live in them, or used to: essays, memories, interviews with older neighbours, arguments about car parks, letters found in archives. Each issue holds eight to twelve pieces, and each piece runs to about a thousand words or fewer.

## The rules

- Write about a real place, and name it.
- No estate agents, no councillors in office, no developers.
- Nostalgia is allowed, but it has to be specific.

## Sending us something

The deadline for each issue is the last day of January or July. Send the piece itself, not a pitch, to [post@overspill.example](mailto:post@overspill.example). We answer everything, usually within a fortnight.

You can also ring Priya on [01632 960418](tel:+441632960418) in the evenings, or post things to us at 17 Kiln Croft, Harlow CM20 3QP.
