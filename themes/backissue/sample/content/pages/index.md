We make a zine about the towns that were built from nothing after the war: Harlow, Stevenage, Runcorn, Skelmersdale, and the rest of the list. Every April and October we print an issue on a stencil duplicator in a garage in Harlow, and the week it goes to press every piece in it goes up here, so the back numbers never go out of print.

Three to start with:

- [Why Overspill](/blog/why-overspill/) — the first piece we printed, and where the name comes from.
- [Moving Out, 1958](/blog/moving-out-1958/) — Joan Pettit on her first night in a house with a garden.
- [The Machine in the Garage](/blog/the-machine-in-the-garage/) — how a 1974 duplicator makes every copy, one pass of ink at a time.

Every piece from every issue is in the [archive](/blog/), newest first, or by issue: [Issue 1](/blog/tag/issue-1/), [Issue 2](/blog/tag/issue-2/), [Issue 3](/blog/tag/issue-3/), [Issue 4](/blog/tag/issue-4/). To hear when the next one is printed, [subscribe](/subscribe/).
