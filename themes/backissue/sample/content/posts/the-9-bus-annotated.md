The 9 goes round Skelmersdale in a loop, so you can get on anywhere and, if you are patient, end up where you started. I rode it all the way round on a wet Tuesday in February with a notebook. It took fifty-two minutes. I was, for most of it, the only passenger.

## What the timetable does not say

A loop is a strange shape for a bus route. It makes no promise of arriving anywhere; it only promises to come back. The planners drew it that way because the town was meant to be complete in itself, everything you needed within one circle.

The driver, a woman called Pauline, has driven the 9 for nineteen years. She told me the route has been cut back twice and the timetable thinned from every ten minutes to every half hour. She knows most of her passengers by name. When I got off at the bus station for the second time she asked if I had enjoyed the tour.

I had. I am going to do it again in summer, with my mother.
