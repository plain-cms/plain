*This is the mission page, normally the home page (`content/pages/index.md`).
`hero: true` turns on the hero: the headline, this description, and the
cheque made out to your organisation, whose amount box is the Donate link.
The figure on the cheque's counterfoil lives in `data/impact.json`
(`stat.number`, `stat.unit`, `stat.verb`); keep the number to seven
characters or fewer and write millions as 1.2M. Without a number the
counterfoil is left off. `donatePrompt` is written on the amount-in-words
line, so phrase it as a sum: "Ten pounds buys …". A photograph is optional;
set `heroImage` to one person mid-task, or leave it as it is.*
