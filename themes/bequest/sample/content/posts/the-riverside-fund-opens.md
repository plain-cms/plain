Harold Venn's gift to the river is ready to spend. The [Riverside
Fund](/programs/the-riverside-fund/) opens this year with £9,000 to hand on
in grants of up to £2,000.

Applications close on Friday 27 March 2026. The panel meets in April and
every group hears by the end of that month.
