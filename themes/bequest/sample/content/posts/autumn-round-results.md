The September panel read twenty-three applications and funded seventeen.
Among them:

- **Wendle Valley Brass**, £640 for music stands and a new tuba case
- **Ince Repair Café**, £400 start-up grant
- **Mill Lane Toddlers**, £900 for a term of hall hire
- **Haddleton Food Bank**, £1,500 towards a chest freezer

The next round closes on Friday 27 November.
