title: The Riverside Fund
description: A named fund left by a local boatbuilder for the Wendle, its banks and the town's green spaces, open to groups who care for them.
example: true
---

When Harold Venn died in 2019 he left the foundation his boatyard on the
condition that the money was spent on the river. The Riverside Fund is the
result.

**Who it serves:** groups that clean, plant, restore or open up the Wendle
and the town's parks, allotments and footpaths.

**A typical year:** one round each spring, decided by the main panel with
two members of the angling club and the canoe club.

**What it costs us:** grants of up to £2,000 come from the fund's own
interest, so the fund itself stays whole for the river.
