example: true

Most groups in this town are not short of volunteers. They are short of the
few hundred pounds that pays for the hall, the insurance or the broken
kit, and that money is hardest to raise.

**Who it serves:** any group run by volunteers for people in Haddleton,
Ince and the villages along the Wendle: clubs, societies, tenants'
associations, faith groups running something open to all.

**A typical round:** applications close on the last Friday of February,
May, August and November. The panel meets the following month, reads every
one, and phones each group with its answer.

**What it costs us:** the average grant last year was £792. Panel evenings
and postage come out of the 8p in every pound that runs the office.
