---
title: Leave a gift in your will
description: A gift in a will goes into the endowment, where only the interest is spent, so it keeps paying for Haddleton groups every year.
example: true
---

A third of the foundation's endowment came from people who remembered the
town in their wills. Their gifts are still paying for grants every year.

**Who it serves:** every group we fund, every year, for as long as the
foundation lasts.

**What happens:** you tell your solicitor you want to leave a sum or a share
of your estate to Haddleton Community Foundation. If you wish, you can name
a cause, a street or a person, and we will open a named fund in their
memory.

**What it costs us:** nothing. We never charge for a named fund opened from
a legacy, and we are happy to talk it through at the office first, with no
obligation.
