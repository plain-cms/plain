Every grant we make is decided by residents, not by staff. That is the
whole point of a community foundation, and it only works if new people keep
joining.

**Who it serves:** the groups who apply, who are judged by neighbours who
know the town.

**A typical evening:** eight or nine panel members around the table at the
Old Corn Exchange, a pack of applications read beforehand, tea, and two
hours of honest argument. Tom takes the notes and phones the groups.

**What it needs:** four evenings a year and the willingness to say no
kindly. Write to the volunteer address on this page to come and sit in on
one panel first.
