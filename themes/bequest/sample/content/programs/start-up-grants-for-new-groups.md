title: Start-up grants for new groups
description: Up to £400 for a group in its first year, with help writing a simple constitution and opening a bank account in the group's own name.
example: true
---

A new group needs a bank account before anyone will fund it, and a
constitution before a bank will open one. This grant breaks that circle.

**Who it serves:** three or more residents who have started something and
want to keep it going: a parents' walking group, a repair café, a choir
that has outgrown someone's front room.

**A typical week:** Tom, our grants officer, meets the group once at the
office, goes through a two-page model constitution with them, and signs the
bank's form as a referee.

**What it costs us:** an average of £320 per group, mostly public liability
insurance and the first term of room bookings.
