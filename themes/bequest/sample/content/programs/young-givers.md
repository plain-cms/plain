People who have sat on a grants panel at seventeen tend to give, volunteer
and stand for things for the rest of their lives.

**Who it serves:** students aged sixteen to eighteen at Haddleton High and
St Aidan's. Twelve places a year, chosen by the schools.

**A typical week:** six evening meetings between January and April. The
panel writes its own criteria, visits shortlisted groups, and presents the
cheques at the town hall in May.

**What it costs us:** the £3,000 pot, given each year by firms on the
Wendle Road estate, plus pizza.
