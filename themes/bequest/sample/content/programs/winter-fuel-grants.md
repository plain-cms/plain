---
title: Winter fuel grants
description: A one-off top-up of £50 to £150 for a household that cannot heat its home, paid straight to the supplier and arranged through a referral.
example: true
---

Every winter some households in Haddleton choose between heating and
eating. A single top-up often gets them to spring.

**Who it serves:** households referred by the GP surgery on Mill Lane, the
advice centre on Bridge Street, the food bank, or a school's family
support worker. We never ask the household to apply themselves.

**A typical week:** between November and March, referrals arrive by e-mail
and are paid within two working days, straight onto the meter or the
account, so no cash changes hands.

**What it costs us:** £50 tops up a prepayment meter for about three weeks
of a cold January.
