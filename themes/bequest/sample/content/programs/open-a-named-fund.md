title: Open a named fund
description: Families, firms and clubs can open a fund in their own name from £5,000 and choose the kind of group it helps. We do the paperwork.
example: true
---

Some donors want their giving to carry a name: a parent, a business that
has traded on Sheep Street for sixty years, a team that has disbanded and
has money left in the kitty.

**Who it serves:** whichever groups the fund's founder chooses, within the
foundation's area: young people, older people, sport, music, the river.

**A typical year:** we send the founder a short report each spring listing
every grant the fund made, with a line from each group.

**What it costs us:** five per cent of each year's grants from the fund
covers the panel and the reporting. The rest is handed on in full.
