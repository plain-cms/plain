Haddleton Community Foundation began in 2003, when Margaret Pell, then
manager of the bank on Sheep Street, noticed how many small groups came in
asking for a loan they could never repay. She persuaded the town's two service
clubs and the town council to put £20,000 into one pot and give it away in small
pieces. The first grant, £350, bought a new urn for the Tuesday lunch club
at St Mary's hall. The urn still works.

Since then we have handed on 1,214 small grants. The panels that decide them
are made up of residents who give four evenings a year.

## Accountability

Our accounts are filed every year and summarised on one page in the
annual review. Of every pound we were given last year:

| Share | Where it went |
| --- | --- |
| 88p | Grants handed on to local groups |
| 8p | Running the office and the grants panels |
| 4p | Raising the next pound |

Haddleton Community Foundation is a registered charity and a company limited
by guarantee. Both numbers are printed on every receipt and on the front
page of the accounts, which we post to anyone who asks.
