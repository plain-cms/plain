Every gift goes into one pot. Four times a year a panel of residents hands
it on to the groups that asked.

| Gift | What it pays for, once handed on |
| --- | --- |
| £15 | A week's hall hire for a lunch club |
| £50 | A winter fuel top-up for one household |
| £400 | The largest start-up grant: insurance, a bank account and the first room bookings for a new group |
| £1,500 | The largest small grant: a minibus repair, a kitchen refit, a season of coaching |

## How to give

**Online:** through [our giving page](https://give.example.org/haddleton),
which takes card payments and monthly gifts.

**By cheque:** made payable to Haddleton Community Foundation and posted to
The Old Corn Exchange, 9 Sheep Street, Haddleton.

**In person:** at the office, Tuesday to Thursday, 10am to 3pm.

**By standing order or bank transfer:** e-mail
[hello@example.org](mailto:hello@example.org) or call
[01632 960418](tel:+441632960418) and we send the details the same day.

If you pay UK income tax, tick the Gift Aid box or tell us, and every pound
becomes £1.25 at no cost to you.

## Other ways to give

You can [leave a gift in your will](/programs/leave-a-gift-in-your-will/),
[open a named fund](/programs/open-a-named-fund/) for your family or firm,
or give four evenings a year by [joining a grants
panel](/programs/join-a-grants-panel/).
