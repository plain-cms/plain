## What we do

Haddleton has more than two hundred volunteer-run groups: lunch clubs, a
food bank, five football teams, a brass band, two toddler groups and a
river-cleaning crew. Most of them need a few hundred pounds a year more than
they can raise from a tombola. We find it for them. Last year that was 61
grants and £48,300, and the average grant was under £800.

## How it works

A group fills in one side of A4 and sends it to the office in the Old Corn
Exchange. Four times a year a panel of residents reads every application
and decides. Groups hear within a fortnight of the panel, and the money is
in their account the week after. See [our programs](/programs/) for every
grant we make and every way to take part.

## Where the money goes

Gifts come from residents, from firms on the Wendle Road estate, and from
people who remembered the town in their wills. Of every pound, 88p goes
straight out as grants; the rest runs the office and the panels. The
[donate page](/donate/) shows what each sum pays for.
