The work took three weeks in September and cost £14,200. The town council’s community grant paid £9,000, the summer fair raised £2,650, and the rest came from the hut fund.

The new roof is insulated, so the hall should be warmer on winter evenings and cheaper to heat. Thank you to everyone who baked, ran a stall or bought a raffle ticket.
