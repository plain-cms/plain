The troop arrived at Brackenwood on 25 July and pitched camp in the dry, which turned out to be the last dry evening for three days. The patrols cooked every meal on open fires all the same.

Highlights were the canoe trip down the lake, the night hike to the ridge and the raft race, won by the Kestrel patrol on a raft made of four barrels and a great deal of rope. Between them the Scouts finished 21 badges during the week.

Thank you to the six leaders who gave up a week of their holidays, and to the parents who drove the kit trailer.
