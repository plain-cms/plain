The posting boxes are in the library, the newsagent on Bridge Street and the hut porch from Saturday 21 November until Saturday 12 December. Stamps are 40p each from the same places, or ten for £3.50.

Every section helps to sort and deliver. Last year we delivered 6,180 cards and raised £2,290 towards the summer camps.

Cards for addresses outside the three villages go back to the sender with the stamp money, so please check the list of streets in each box.
