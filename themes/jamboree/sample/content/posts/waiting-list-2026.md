On 1 November we had 41 names on the list: 27 for Beavers, 10 for Cubs and 4 for Scouts. A section can only grow when it has enough adults, at one adult for every six Beavers or eight Cubs and Scouts.

Places are offered in the order names were added, with brothers and sisters of members first. Joanne writes to every family on the list each September to check they still want a place.

The quickest way to shorten the list is more helpers. Two more adults in the Beaver colony would mean twelve more places on Monday evenings.
