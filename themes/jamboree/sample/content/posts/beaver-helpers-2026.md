You do not need to have been a Beaver or a Scout, and you do not need to come every week. A rota of helpers who each come once or twice a month works just as well.

Helpers run games, sit with a lodge during crafts and help with the occasional outing. We arrange the background check and the short online safeguarding course. Sarah is happy to talk it through: [beavers@3rdkettlebyscouts.example](mailto:beavers@3rdkettlebyscouts.example).
