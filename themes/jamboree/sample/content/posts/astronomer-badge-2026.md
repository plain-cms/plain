After four weeks of learning the constellations indoors, the sky finally cleared on the last Tuesday of the term. The Cubs found the Plough, the Pole Star and Cassiopeia by eye, and Saturn and its rings through the club’s telescopes.

Thank you to the astronomy club for bringing their kit, and to the parents who stayed late with flasks of hot chocolate. The badges will be presented at the next parents’ evening.
