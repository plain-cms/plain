Every week about ninety young people from Kettleby and the villages around it come to our hut to learn knots, cook on fires, earn badges and get muddy. Each section camps at least once a year, and the Scouts spend a week under canvas every summer.

Places are in demand, so every section keeps a waiting list: put your child’s name down early. If you can spare an evening a month, we would love to hear from you too, because more adult helpers means more places.

Our neckerchief is teal with a gold border. You will see it at the Christmas fair, on the green at carol time and marching to St Wilfrid’s on St George’s Day.
