Each section takes new members as places come free, in the order names went on the list. Brothers and sisters of members, and children moving up from our own Beavers and Cubs, come first. At the moment most families wait between six months and a year for Beavers, and less for Cubs and Scouts.

## How to start

1. Send Joanne your child’s name, date of birth and the section you would like. She will confirm by e-mail that the name is on the list.
2. When a place comes up, the section leader invites your child to three trial evenings.
3. After the trial evenings you fill in the joining form and your child makes the Promise at an investiture.

## What it costs

| What | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Subscriptions, all sections | £48 a term |
| Second child in the family | £40 a term |
| Group neckerchief and slide ring | £8, once |
| Camps and sleepovers | from £12, on top of subscriptions |

Subscriptions pay for the hut, the national membership fee and the badges. If the cost is a worry, speak to Helen in confidence: no young person has ever left the group because of money.

## Adults: help us shorten the list

Every extra pair of hands lets a section take more children. You do not need to have been a Scout. You could help once a month on a section night, keep the kit store, drive the minibus or join the committee. We arrange the checks and the training.

Write to Joanne at [waitinglist@3rdkettlebyscouts.example](mailto:waitinglist@3rdkettlebyscouts.example) for the waiting list, or come to any meeting night.
