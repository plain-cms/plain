The 3rd Kettleby Scout Group was registered in March 1926 by the curate of St Wilfrid’s, who gathered twelve boys in a borrowed barn on Mill Hill. Girls have been members since 1991. Today the group has three sections and about ninety young members.

## The sections

- **Beavers**, ages 6 to 8: games, crafts and first nights away, on Monday evenings.
- **Cubs**, ages 8 to 10½: the pack works towards its badges and camps twice a year, on Tuesday evenings.
- **Scouts**, ages 10½ to 14: patrols, hikes, pioneering and the summer camp, on Thursday evenings.

At 14 our Scouts move up to the Kettleby Explorer unit, which meets in the town.

## The hut

The hut on Orchard Road was built by parents in 1958 on land left to the group by a former leader. It has a main hall, a kitchen, a kit store and an acre of field behind it, where every section lights fires and pitches tents. The roof was replaced in September 2026.

The hut is for hire on Saturday afternoons and Sundays for children’s parties and meetings: £18 an hour. Ask Graham for dates.

## Badges and awards

In 2025 our young people earned 214 activity badges, and four Scouts gained the Chief Scout’s Gold Award. Badge work happens at meetings and at camp; nothing has to be bought, and no young person is held back by cost.

## Keeping everyone safe

Every leader and helper has a criminal record check and safeguarding training before they work with young people, and there are always at least two adults at every meeting.
