We charge nothing for the drop-in and £3 a day for holiday club, which is
free for anyone on free school meals. Everything else comes from grants and
from people like you.

| Gift | What it buys |
| --- | --- |
| £5 | Toast, fruit and squash for ten young people at the drop-in |
| £14 | A child's day at holiday club, lunch included |
| £45 | A youth worker on shift for one drop-in evening |
| £120 | A month of Saturday pitch hire at Calderbrook Rec |

If you pay UK income tax, tick the Gift Aid box on the giving page and the
government adds 25p to every pound.

## Other ways to give

**By cheque:** made out to Corner Pocket Youth Centre and posted to The Old
Bath House, 22 Wharf Street, Calderbrook.

**By bank transfer:** e-mail
[hello@cornerpocket.example.org](mailto:hello@cornerpocket.example.org) or
call [01632 960418](tel:+441632960418) and we send the details the same day.

**In person:** cash or a card at the front desk during any session.

## Give time instead

Volunteers run the tuck shop, help with homework and coach on Saturdays.
See [volunteer with us](/programs/volunteer-with-us/).
