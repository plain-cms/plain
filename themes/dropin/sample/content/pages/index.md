## What we do

Calderbrook lost its council youth service in 2011. Since then the nearest
thing to a youth club has been the bus shelter outside the chip shop. We
give young people a warm room, a kettle, a pool table, and two youth workers
who are there every week.

## How it works

The doors open at six on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday for the
[drop-in](/programs/drop-in-evenings/). No booking, no forms on your first
night — just your first name. There is homework help before, girls' night on
Wednesday, football on Saturday, and holiday club whenever the schools shut.
Everything is listed under [what's on](/programs/).

## Where the money goes

Most of it pays for people: a youth worker on every shift, and the holiday
club team. The rest buys food, heating and the odd new pool cue. The
[donate page](/donate/) shows what each gift buys.
