Corner Pocket began in 2009 as two evenings a week in the back room of
St Anne's church hall, with a borrowed pool table and a tin of biscuits. In
2014 the town council leased us the empty Old Bath House on Wharf Street for
a peppercorn rent, and volunteers spent a summer painting it.

The pool table came with us. It is still the most popular thing in the
building.

## Accountability

Our accounts are filed with the Charity Commission every year. Of every
pound we spent in 2025:

| Share | Where it went |
| --- | --- |
| 74p | Sessions: youth workers on shift, food, kit and holiday club |
| 15p | The building: rent, heating, insurance and repairs |
| 11p | Running the charity and raising money |

Corner Pocket Youth Centre is a charitable incorporated organisation
registered in England and Wales.
