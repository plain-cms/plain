The table is back. It has new green cloth, new cushion rubber on the bottom
rail, and a strict no-drinks rule on the chalkboard.

Thank you to everyone at the quiz night who raised the £320 it cost. The
pool league, which spent a fortnight on the table-tennis table, picks up its
season again on Monday 12 October.
