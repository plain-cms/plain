Summer holiday club ran from Monday 27 July to Thursday 27 August, Monday
to Thursday as always. We had an average of 58 children a day, and 31 of
them came for free on free school meals.

> I didn't know you could make bread. I'm doing it at home now.

Thank you to the eleven volunteers who gave a week each, and to everyone
whose £14 paid for a day.
