Plenty of the young people who come on a Thursday have not had a hot meal
since school dinner. Some have never cooked one.

**Who it serves:** ages 11 to 18, eight places a week. Put your name on the
board by the kitchen door on Monday or Tuesday.

**A typical week:** Thursday, 5 to 6pm. Priya picks a recipe that costs
under £2 a head — chilli, a curry, a tray bake — and the group shops the
list from the cupboard, cooks, lays the table and washes up. Whatever is left
goes out to the drop-in at six.

**What it costs us:** about £16 a week in food, and the gas.
