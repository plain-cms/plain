The pool table is never free, so in 2011 we made it fair: a league on
Monday nights, and a chalkboard that settles every argument.

**Who it serves:** anyone aged 11 to 18 who comes to the Monday drop-in.
Sign up on the board in September, or join as a reserve at any time.

**A typical week:** Monday from 6.30pm, during the drop-in. Two divisions,
best of three frames, eight-ball rules. The final is in June, and the
winner's name goes on the trophy by the door.

**What it costs us:** new cloth every few years, cues and tips — about £400
a year.
