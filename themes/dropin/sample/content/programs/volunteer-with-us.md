Twenty volunteers keep Corner Pocket open. Most started by giving one
evening a week, and most are still here.

**Who it serves:** anyone over 18 who is reliable and kind. Former members
make the best volunteers.

**A typical week:** one regular session — the tuck shop at drop-in,
homework club, Saturday football or a week of holiday club. You will meet
Dee for a cup of tea first, then do safeguarding training and an enhanced
DBS check before your first session.

**What it costs us:** about £60 per volunteer for the check and the
training, and every penny comes back in hours.
