Some things are hard to say across a pool table. A one-to-one is an hour
in the quiet room with a youth worker you already know.

**Who it serves:** anyone who comes to Corner Pocket, aged 11 to 18. Ask
any youth worker, or a teacher or social worker can call us.

**A typical week:** Monday to Thursday afternoons, by appointment, for as
many weeks as it helps. What you say stays between you and the youth
worker, unless someone is in danger.

**What it costs us:** about £25 an hour of a youth worker's time.
