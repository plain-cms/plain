**Who it serves:** ages 11 to 16, any ability. Girls and boys play
together.

**A typical week:** Saturday, 10am to noon, on the all-weather pitch at
Calderbrook Rec. Warm-up, small-sided games, and squash afterwards. Liam
coaches with two volunteers; there is a box of boots and shin pads to
borrow.

**What it costs us:** £30 a session in pitch hire. Players pay £1 when they
can.
