On a mixed night the boys hold the pool table and the girls hold the sofa.
Wednesday is the evening the building, the table and the music room belong
to girls.

**Who it serves:** girls and young women aged 11 to 18. Bring a friend.

**A typical week:** Wednesday, 6 to 8.30pm. Some weeks there is a plan — a
self-defence session, a visit from the sexual-health nurse, nails — and
some weeks there is just pool and talking.

**What it costs us:** about £95 an evening for two youth workers and food.
