The school holidays are the hungriest weeks of the year for some families.
Holiday club gives children somewhere to go and a hot lunch every day.

**Who it serves:** children aged 8 to 13. Book a place at the front desk or
on the phone; places on free school meals are kept back first.

**A typical week:** 10am to 3pm, Monday to Thursday. Games, crafts and
cooking in the morning, a hot lunch at twelve, and an afternoon outside —
the park, the canal path, or one trip a week on the minibus.

**What it costs us:** about £14 per child per day, lunch included. We charge
£3, and nothing to families on free school meals.
