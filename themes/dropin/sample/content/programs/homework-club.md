Plenty of homework now needs a laptop and a printer, and plenty of homes
do not have either.

**Who it serves:** secondary school pupils aged 11 to 16. Just turn up.

**A typical week:** Monday and Wednesday, 4 to 5.30pm, in the back room
with the door shut. Four laptops, a printer, squash and biscuits, and two
volunteers — one of them a retired maths teacher.

**What it costs us:** about £10 a session in printing and snacks.
