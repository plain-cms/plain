The younger ones see their brothers and sisters coming here and want to
come too. Junior club is their hour and a half.

**Who it serves:** children aged 8 to 11. A parent or carer signs a form on
the first visit and collects them at six.

**A typical week:** Tuesday, 4.30 to 6pm. Parachute games, crafts, the
table-tennis table, and a turn each on the pool table with the short cue.

**What it costs us:** about £60 a session for a youth worker, a volunteer
and snacks.
