This is what Corner Pocket was started for: somewhere to be in the evening
that is warm, free and not the street.

**Who it serves:** anyone aged 11 to 18. On your first night we ask your
first name and your age, and that is all.

**A typical week:** three evenings, 6 to 9pm. The pool table has a list on
the chalkboard and the list is always full. There is a music corner, a sofa
by the radiator, table tennis, board games and a tuck shop selling toast for
20p. Two youth workers are on every shift, and they are the reason most
people come back.

**What it costs us:** about £150 an evening — two youth workers, heating,
and the food the tuck shop sells at a loss.
