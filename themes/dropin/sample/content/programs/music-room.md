The music room was a storeroom until 2019. Now it is the second thing,
after the pool table, that people ask about on their first night.

**Who it serves:** ages 11 to 18, beginners included. Saturday sessions
are booked at the front desk, two hours at a time.

**A typical week:** open during Thursday drop-in, 6 to 9pm, and Saturday,
1 to 4pm, with Josh, who plays drums and can show you three chords.

**What it costs us:** about £300 a year in strings, sticks and repairs.
