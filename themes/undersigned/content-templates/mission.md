*This is the mission page, normally the home page (`content/pages/index.md`).
`hero: true` prints it as a petition sheet: the title as the demand in heavy
capitals, the description under it, and numbered lines that lead to the ways
to help. The running total in the top corner comes from `data/impact.json`
(`stat.number`, `stat.unit`, `stat.verb`, read as one phrase: "2,400 names on
the petition since May"). Keep the number to seven characters or fewer. Leave
it out and the box goes with it.*

*The first numbered line is the volunteer e-mail from `data/impact.json`, when
there is one; the next is Donate, with `donatePrompt` beside it. A photograph
is optional and is printed in black and white, like a photocopy. Delete these
notes and set `draft: false` to publish.*
