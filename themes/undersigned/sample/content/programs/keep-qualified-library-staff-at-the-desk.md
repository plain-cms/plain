title: Keep qualified library staff at the desk
description: The council's fallback plan is a volunteer-run community hub. We are asking for the two paid posts at Holme Lane to stay.
example: true
---

If the closure is dropped, the council has said it may hand the building to
volunteers instead. We do not want to run it, and we cannot.

**What it changes:** a trained member of staff helps with job applications,
benefit forms and the computers, runs the rhyme-time mornings, and knows
which child needs which book. Volunteers cannot sign up new borrowers to the
county catalogue, and nobody is responsible when the rota is short.

**What happens next:** we are asking every ward councillor to vote against
any budget line that turns Holme Lane into a volunteer-only hub.

**What it needs:** the two posts cost £58,000 a year, less than a third of
the saving the council gives for closing the branch.
