title: Publish the 2025 building survey in full
description: The council says the roof and boiler need £410,000 of work. We have asked to read the survey that says so, and been refused.
example: true
---

The repair bill is the council's main reason for choosing Holme Lane. Nobody
outside the council has seen the figures behind it.

**What it changes:** if the survey shows the work can be phased, or that
part of the bill is routine maintenance, the case for closing this branch
rather than another falls apart.

**What happens next:** we asked for the survey under the Freedom of
Information Act on Monday 14 September 2026. The council refused on Monday
12 October, saying it would be published "in due course". We have asked for
an internal review.

**What it needs:** one councillor to ask for the survey to be put before
full council before the debate on 10 December.
