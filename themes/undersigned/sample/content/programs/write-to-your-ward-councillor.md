---
title: Write to your ward councillor
description: Holme ward has three councillors and each of them votes on the budget. A short letter in your own words is read; a copied one is counted.
example: true
---

Councillors keep a tally of letters from their own ward. Ten letters on one
subject is a lot.

**What it changes:** a councillor who has had forty letters about Holme Lane
will not vote for the closure quietly.

**What happens next:** the budget vote is on Thursday 25 February 2027.
Letters before the debate on 10 December count twice: once then, once in
February.

**What it needs:** your name and address, so they know you are a voter in
the ward, and three sentences on what the library does for you. The
councillors' addresses are on the council's website under "Your
councillors", and printed on the back of our leaflet.
