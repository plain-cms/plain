Numbers make the case. Stories are what councillors remember when they vote.

**What it changes:** the booklet goes to all 51 councillors a week before
the debate, so the people voting have read who uses Holme Lane and why.

**What happens next:** stories received by Saturday 28 November 2026 go in
the booklet. We print your first name and street only, and only if you say
we may.

**What it needs:** fifty words or five hundred. A grandparent reading to a
grandchild, a first job applied for on the library computer, an afternoon
out of the cold.

> I learned to read English at the Thursday evening group. Now I read to my
> daughter there.
