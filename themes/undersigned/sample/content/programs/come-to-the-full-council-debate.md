Councillors vote differently when the gallery is full and they can see who
is watching.

**What it changes:** a full gallery is the picture in the local paper the
next morning, and it is the councillors' own voters looking down at them.

**What happens next:** the doors open at 6pm. Margaret Oduya has five minutes
to present the petition, then councillors debate it for up to thirty minutes
and vote on a motion to remove the closure from the budget.

**What it needs:** 120 people in their seats by 6.15pm. Bring a library book
and hold it up when the vote is called. Step-free access is by the side door
on Bridge Street.
