---
title: Deliver leaflets on your street
description: Collect a bundle of 100 from the stall and put one through every door on your street. A round takes about an hour.
example: true
---

Most people in the ward still have not heard that the library is on the
list. A leaflet on the mat reaches them; a post online mostly does not.

**What it changes:** after every round the stall gets new signatures from
that street the following Saturday. Gethin Morris marks the streets done on
the ward map, so no door gets two.

**What happens next:** the next print run is 4,000 leaflets, collected from
the stall from Saturday 14 November 2026.

**What it needs:** about thirty people, one hour each. Tell Gethin which
street is yours when you collect your bundle.
