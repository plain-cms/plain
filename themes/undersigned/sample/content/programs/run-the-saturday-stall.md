The stall is where most of the 4,817 names came from. It only works when
someone is behind it.

**What it changes:** a staffed stall collects between 150 and 300 names on a
Saturday. An unstaffed one collects none.

**What happens next:** the rota is filled four weeks ahead. Write to us with
the Saturdays you can do and whether you can carry the table from the shed
behind St Aidan's.

**What it needs:** two people per Saturday, ideally one who has done it
before. Bring a warm coat. The flask and the sheets are already packed.
