example: true
---

The council counts names, not opinions. A name on a sheet is the one thing
every councillor has to read out loud.

**What it changes:** past 1,500 names the council must debate the closure in
public at full council. We passed that in October. Every name since then is
counted again on the night, and the total goes in the minutes.

**What happens next:** the sheets are handed to the mayor at the start of the
full council debate on Thursday 10 December 2026. Sheets in shop windows are
collected on Saturday 5 December.

**What it needs:** your name, your street and postcode, and a signature. No
e-mail address and no phone number. Priya Lal keeps the sheets in a locked
box and destroys the copies once the count is accepted.

The council's scheme does not accept online names for a debate, so there is
no web form. Paper only.
