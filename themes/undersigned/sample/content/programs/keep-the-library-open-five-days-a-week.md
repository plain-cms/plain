title: Keep the library open five days a week
description: Tuesday to Saturday, 9.30am to 5pm and Thursdays until 7pm, as it is now. A fortnightly mobile library in the car park is not the same service.
example: true
---

A mobile library parked for two hours every other Wednesday has no
computers, no toilets, no rhyme time and no warm room to sit in.

**What it changes:** the library stays where people can reach it on foot,
five days a week, including the Thursday evening when people who work can
come.

**What happens next:** the budget is set at full council on Thursday
25 February 2027. The closure only goes ahead if that budget includes it.

**What it needs:** councillors to move the £186,000 saving to another line
of the budget, and enough names on the petition to make that the easier
vote.
