## What the council plans

In its budget consultation, Kettlewick Borough Council proposes to close
Holme Lane and two other branch libraries on 31 March 2027 and send a mobile
library to the car park once a fortnight instead. The saving it gives is
£186,000 a year. Last year Holme Lane lent 38,400 books, ran 52 rhyme-time
mornings and logged 6,100 hours on its public computers, many of them by
people applying for jobs and benefits with no internet at home.

## How the petition works

Under the council's own petition scheme, 1,500 names from people who live,
work or study in the borough force a debate at full council. We passed that
in five weeks. The debate is on Thursday 10 December 2026 at 6.30pm in the
council chamber at the Town Hall, and every name added before then goes in
the box we hand to the mayor. [Our demands and every way to join
in](/programs/) are set out one at a time.

## Where the money goes

Nobody is paid. Money buys paper and toner, the £18 Saturday pitch on Market
Row and the hall for public meetings, and what is left is saved towards a
barrister's opinion on whether the closure can be challenged. The [donate
page](/donate/) shows what each sum buys, and every penny spent so far is in
the table on the [about page](/about/).
