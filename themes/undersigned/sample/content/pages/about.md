The Friends of Holme Lane Library met for the first time on Thursday
3 September 2026, in St Aidan's church hall, two weeks after the council
published its budget consultation. Ninety people came. Sheets went round the
room that night, and the first Saturday stall on Market Row was two days
later.

The library itself opened in 1931 in the red-brick building on the corner of
Holme Lane and Cross Street. It is the only public building in the ward with
a step-free entrance, free toilets and free computers.

## The people

## The money

We are an unincorporated residents' association with a written constitution,
not a registered charity, so gifts cannot carry Gift Aid. The account is in
the group's name and every payment needs two signatures. Of the £1,936 given
by 7 November 2026:

| Sum | Where it went |
| --- | --- |
| £1,120 | Printing: leaflets, posters and petition sheets |
| £180 | Ten Saturday pitches on Market Row at £18 each |
| £135 | Three public meetings at St Aidan's hall at £45 each |
| £501 | Held in the account towards a barrister's opinion |

The cash book and the bank statements are on the stall table every Saturday.
Ask to see them.
