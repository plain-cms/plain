We take cash and cheques at the stall and bank transfers by arrangement.

| Gift | What it buys |
| --- | --- |
| £5 | 400 leaflets, enough for one street round |
| £18 | One Saturday pitch for the stall on Market Row |
| £45 | St Aidan's hall for one public meeting |
| £600 | Our share of a barrister's written opinion on a legal challenge |

## How to give

**At the stall:** cash or a cheque, any Saturday from 10am to 1pm on Market
Row. Cheques are payable to Friends of Holme Lane Library. Every gift is
written in the cash book in front of you, and you get a receipt.

**By bank transfer:** e-mail
[hello@saveholmelane.example](mailto:hello@saveholmelane.example) or ring Tom
Brannigan, our treasurer, on [01632 960427](tel:+441632960427), and he sends
the account details.

We are not a registered charity, so we cannot claim Gift Aid on what you give.

## Ways that count as much as money

A name on the petition costs nothing and matters most. If you have not
signed yet, [add your name](/programs/add-your-name-to-the-petition/). An
hour on the stall or a [street round of leaflets](/programs/deliver-leaflets-on-your-street/)
saves us the same as a gift.
