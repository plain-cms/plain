## Find us

62 High Street, Hexby HB1 4TN — between the chemist and the old bank, opposite the war memorial. The 14 and 22 buses stop outside. Park free after six in the Market Place car park, two minutes’ walk away.

There is a low step at the front door and a ramp behind the bar: ask and we will put it down. The toilets are on the ground floor.

## Book a table

Call [01632 960147](tel:+441632960147) any day after four, or email [tables@padmatandoori.example](mailto:tables@padmatandoori.example) and we will reply the same evening. We hold a table for fifteen minutes. For ten people or more, ask for the back room.

## Collection

Ring your order through and it will be ready in about twenty-five minutes; forty on a Friday or Saturday. Collected orders over £20 come with poppadoms and a pickle tray.

## Delivery

We deliver within three miles of the restaurant — all of Hexby, Calder Vale and Ashbrook — from 17:30 until half an hour before closing. The minimum order is £15, and delivery is £2, or free over £30. Pay the driver by card or cash.
