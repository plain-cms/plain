Everything is cooked to order by Rafiq and the kitchen he trained: tikka and naan from the tandoor, the mixed grill brought to the table still sizzling, and a dal that takes all afternoon. Read [the full menu](/menu/), or see [how to find us and order a takeaway](/visit/).

## This week

Kulfi is back on the desserts — pistachio, mango or malai. Fridays fill up by eight, so ring before six if you want a table.

## Christmas

We are open on Christmas Eve and closed on Christmas Day — the dates are in [Christmas and New Year opening](/blog/christmas-opening/). Parties of ten or more can book the back room.
