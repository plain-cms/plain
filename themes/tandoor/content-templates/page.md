The banquet is poppadoms and pickles, a mixed starter, mains and sides chosen for the table, rice, naan and coffee. Tell us about allergies when you book and we will plan around them.

## How to book

Email [bookings@example.com](mailto:bookings@example.com) with the date and the number of guests. We ask for a deposit per person, taken off the bill on the night.
