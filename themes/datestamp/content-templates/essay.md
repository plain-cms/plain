Start with the book in your hands: how you came to borrow it, and what you expected before page one.

Then what it is: the story or the argument, in a paragraph, without giving away the ending.

## What works

The part you would read aloud to someone. Quote a line if it earns its place.

> A short quotation from the book.

## What doesn’t

Be fair and specific: one or two things, and why.

End with your verdict in a single line.
