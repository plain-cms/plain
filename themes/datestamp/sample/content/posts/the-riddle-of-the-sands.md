Carruthers, a bored young man from the Foreign Office, accepts an invitation to go duck-shooting on a friend’s yacht in the Baltic, expecting something smart. He gets the *Dulcibella*, a converted lifeboat with a leaking deck, and Davies, a quiet sailor who is sure the Germans are up to something among the sandbanks of the Frisian coast.

For long stretches nothing happens except navigation: soundings, channels, the times of the tides. I did not expect to enjoy that, and I enjoyed it more than the spying. Childers knew those waters and it shows on every page.

The plot, when it arrives, is thin, and there is a girl in it who exists to be rescued. But the fog chapters are as good as anything I have read this year.

There are maps in the front. Keep a finger in them.

**Verdict:** return it on time.
