I’m Gwen Harries. For thirty-one years I stamped other people’s books at a library counter in Carmarthenshire. Now I borrow one at a time, read it before it is due back, and write it up here on the Saturday morning I return it.

Three to start with:

- [Middlemarch, by George Eliot](/blog/middlemarch/) — the long one, and worth the fine.
- [Three Men in a Boat, by Jerome K. Jerome](/blog/three-men-in-a-boat/) — the one I laughed at on the bus.
- [Lark Rise, by Flora Thompson](/blog/lark-rise/) — the one readers write to me about.

Every review is in the [archive](/blog/), newest first. New ones arrive by [e-mail](/subscribe/) the morning the book goes back.
