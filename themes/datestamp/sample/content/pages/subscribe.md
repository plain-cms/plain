A new review arrives by e-mail every three weeks, on the Saturday morning I return the book. That is all you will get: no digests, no reading lists, no news about me.

To be added, write to [gwen@lastdatestamped.example](mailto:gwen@lastdatestamped.example) with the word *subscribe*. I add every name by hand, usually the same evening. To leave, write one line back and you will be off the list before the next due date.

Prefer a feed reader? The [RSS feed](/blog/rss.xml) carries every review the moment it goes up.
