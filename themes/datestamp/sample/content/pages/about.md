I’m Gwen Harries. I worked the counter of a county library for thirty-one years, the last twelve of them on the mobile library, and I retired to Llandeilo with a ticket that lets me borrow twelve books at once. I borrow one.

## The rules

1. One book at a time, borrowed, never bought. If the library doesn’t have it, I ask them to order it and wait.
2. The review is written while the book is on loan and goes up the Saturday morning it goes back. A loan is three weeks, so that is how often you hear from me.
3. I never renew. If three weeks is not enough, that goes into the verdict.
4. I say who wrote the book in the title and what I thought of it in the last line.

The verdict is advice to you, in the library’s own terms. It runs from *return it early*, through *return it on time* and *renew it*, to *renew it twice and pay the fine*. Only two books have earned the fine so far.
