*This is the mission page — normally the home page, `content/pages/index.md`.
`hero: true` turns on the hero: one number, what it counts, your mission, and
the Donate button. The number lives in `data/impact.json` (`stat.number`,
`stat.unit`, `stat.verb` — "1,142", "adults", "taught to read since 2011");
`donateUrl` there points every Donate button at your giving page. Keep the
number to seven characters or fewer (248,300 or $12,480) and write millions
as 1.2M — a longer figure breaks onto two lines on a phone or beside a
photograph. Up to five characters (1,142) sits beside the mission; a longer
one takes a row of its own.*

*A photograph is optional. If you have one, set `heroImage` to a single
person mid-task, not a group shot; without one the number stands alone.
Delete these notes and set `draft: false` to publish.*
