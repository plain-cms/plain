Tutors give the hours. Your money buys everything around the table.

| Gift | What it buys |
| --- | --- |
| $10 | A learner's first three books — theirs to keep |
| $25 | Childcare for one session, the number-one reason learners stay |
| $60 | A neighborhood room for a month of evenings |
| $150 | Training for one new tutor, good for about 100 learner-hours a year |

## How to give

**By check:** made out to Page & Chapter and mailed to 14 Library Row,
Millbrook — or handed to any tutor, who will bring back your receipt.

**By bank transfer, stock, or a donor-advised fund:** email
[hello@example.org](mailto:hello@example.org) and we send the details the
same day.

Page & Chapter is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, so gifts are tax-deductible, and
every gift gets a written receipt.

## Other ways that count the same

A monthly $5 beats most one-off gifts — it's what lets us promise a learner
eighteen months. Employers often match donations; ask yours. And if money
is tight, [give an hour a week instead](/programs/volunteer-as-a-tutor/) —
four evenings of training and you're someone's Tuesday.
