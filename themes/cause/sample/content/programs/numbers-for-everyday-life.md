Half the reading an adult does is really arithmetic: a pay stub, a utility
bill, a dose on a medicine label, a "30% off" sign. Learners told us they
could now read the words and still not trust the numbers.

Numbers for Everyday Life runs alongside tutoring, in blocks of eight weeks.
Each block takes one piece of paper from real life and stays with it until
it holds no surprises — the pay stub block ends with every learner checking
their own deductions, and more than one has found an error in their favor.

**Who it serves:** any current learner, and adults who read well but were
never taught to handle figures. There is no test to get in; we ask which
piece of paper worries you most and start there.

**A typical session:** one hour a week in groups of four, mornings and
evenings, with a tutor who has had an extra evening of numeracy training.

**What it costs us:** $4 per learner-hour, the same as tutoring. Calculators
are lent, and usually given.
