Finishing a first book is a private triumph, and for most of our learners
there is nobody at home to tell who would understand what it cost. So once a
month we fill a room with people who do.

The club reads one short book a month, chosen by the learners from a shelf
of titles written for adults — real stories about work, love, and money in
plain language, not children's books with the pictures taken out. Tutors
come as readers, not teachers: nobody is corrected, and anyone can pass when
it is their turn to read aloud.

**Who it serves:** learners who have been with a tutor for six months or
more, and graduates who miss the table. About forty people come in a typical
month.

**A typical evening:** the first Wednesday of the month, seven to half past
eight, in the library's meeting room. Coffee, twenty minutes of reading
aloud, and an argument about the ending.

**What it costs us:** $9 per member per month — a copy of the book to keep,
and the coffee.
