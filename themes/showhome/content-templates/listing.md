What the place is, in a sentence or two.

**The facts:** the rooms, the heating, the parking, the tenure, any yearly charges.

**The garden or outside space:** its size and which way it faces.

**When it is ready:** the build stage, or when it is free.

**The drawback:** anything a buyer or tenant should know before a viewing.
