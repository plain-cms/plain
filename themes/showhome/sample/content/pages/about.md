Ruth runs the sales cabin and the paperwork; Tom runs the site. Between them and a site team of eight, they have built forty-one homes in the district, and most of the people who bought them still live in them.

## How a plot is sold

1. **Visit.** Walk round the show home and look at the plans for the plot you like. Every released plot has one price, written on the list in the cabin and on this site.
2. **Reserve.** A reservation fee of £500 takes the plot off the market for 28 days while your mortgage and your solicitor get going. The fee comes off the price when you complete.
3. **Exchange.** Contracts are exchanged with a 10% deposit. From then the plot is sold.
4. **Choose.** While the house is still being built, you choose the kitchen, the tiles and the flooring from our standard range at no extra cost.
5. **Move in.** Tom walks you round the finished house two weeks before completion, and again six weeks after you move in to put right anything that has shown up.

## What we will not do

We do not raise a price once a plot is on the list, and we do not take a second offer on a reserved plot. If a reservation falls through, the plot goes back on the list at the same price.

## After you move in

Every house comes with a ten-year structural warranty. For the first two years, anything we built that goes wrong is ours to fix: call the cabin number and Tom will come and look.
