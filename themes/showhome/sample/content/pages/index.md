We build a few homes at a time and sell them ourselves, plot by plot, at a fixed price: no bidding, no sealed offers. When a plot is reserved or sold, the [price list](/listings/) says so the same day.

## What every house has

An air-source heat pump with underfloor heating downstairs, solar panels, a car-charging point, a turfed and fenced garden, and a ten-year structural warranty. The kitchen, the tiles and the flooring are yours to choose while the house is still being built.
