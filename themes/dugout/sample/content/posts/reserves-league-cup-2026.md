Ferris Lane led 2–1 at half-time on a heavy pitch. Liam Byrne changed nothing but the shape, Jordan Pike scored twice in ten minutes, and Callum Reeve saved a penalty with four minutes left before Sam Okafor ran the length of the pitch for the fourth.

The quarter-final draw is on 17 November. The date will go on the card as soon as the league confirms it.
