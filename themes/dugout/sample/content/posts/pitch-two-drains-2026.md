Pitch 2 has flooded every winter since the Wharf Road houses were built. Contractors laid 400 metres of new drains in September, and the grass has had six weeks to settle.

Thank you to everyone who sponsored a horse in March: the race night raised £2,340 of the £9,000 it cost. The county grant paid the rest.

Please keep dogs off the pitches, and use the path by the hedge.
