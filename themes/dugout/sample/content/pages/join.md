Senior players start by coming to training. The managers pick from everyone who trains, and nobody is turned away for not being good enough: the reserves exist so that everyone gets a game.

## What it costs

| Membership | Per season | Or monthly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Senior player | £120 | £12 for ten months |
| Youth player, Under-11s to Under-15s | £90 | £9 for ten months |
| Mini-soccer, ages 5 to 8 | £45 | — |
| Social member | £15 | — |

Senior players also pay £5 a match towards referees and kit washing. Brothers and sisters in youth sides pay half.

## For youth players

Youth places are kept by age group, and some groups are full. Write to the youth secretary with your child’s name and date of birth, and she will tell you which session to come to.

## For everyone else

We always need coaches, referees, linesmen, groundstaff and people for the tea bar. The club pays for coaching and refereeing courses for anyone who helps a youth side for a season.
