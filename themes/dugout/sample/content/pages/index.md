We play in claret and sky blue at the Osier Ground, between the canal and the Academy fields. The first team are in the Mid-Shires League Division One, the reserves in the Reserve Division, and about 140 children play for our youth sides every weekend.

Players of any standard are welcome at training, and so is anyone who would rather run the line, coach a youth side or pour the teas. The clubhouse bar is open to everyone on Friday nights.
