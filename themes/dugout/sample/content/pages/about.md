The club was started in 1908 by workers from the Osier basket works, who played on the field behind the wharf where the ground still is. The first clubhouse was a railway carriage. The present one was built by members in 1972 and extended in 2011.

## What we run

- **First team**, Mid-Shires League Division One
- **Reserves**, Mid-Shires Reserve Division
- **Youth sides** from Under-7s to Under-15s, girls and boys, in the Pellow and District Youth League
- **Mini-soccer** for ages 5 to 8 on Saturday mornings

## The ground

The Osier Ground has three pitches: the main pitch with a covered stand for 120, pitch 2 for youth games, and pitch 3 for mini-soccer. The main pitch has training floodlights; league games kick off in daylight.

## The clubhouse

The bar, function room and kitchen are run by volunteers, and every penny goes back into the club. The function room seats 80 and can be hired for parties and meetings: ask the clubhouse manager.

## Safeguarding

Every youth coach holds an FA coaching badge and a current DBS check. Any worry about a child, however small, goes to our welfare officer.
