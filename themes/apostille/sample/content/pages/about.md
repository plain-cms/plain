## How it started

Ruth Penhallow qualified as a solicitor in Truro and took her notarial examinations in 2008, when the nearest notary was an hour away by road. She opened the office in Chapel Yard in 2011, above what was then the harbour master's store. Marek Vane joined her in 2016 and became a partner in 2020.

## How we work

A notary acts for the transaction, not for one side of it, and keeps a permanent record of everything signed in front of them. That is why we ask to see your passport even when we have known you for years, and why every act we prepare is numbered, bound with cord and sealed before it leaves the office.

We read every document before you sign it, in English, French, Spanish, Polish or German, and tell you if the foreign office receiving it is likely to want something more.

## Regulation and insurance

Both notaries hold a practising certificate from the Faculty Office, renewed every November, and are members of the Notaries Society. The practice carries professional indemnity insurance of £2 million for any one claim, and is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office for the records it keeps.

## Complaints

If something has gone wrong, write to Ruth Penhallow and she will reply within ten working days. If you are not satisfied with the answer, the Legal Ombudsman can look at it.
