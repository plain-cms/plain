## Who we act for

Private clients, families and small companies in Fallowmouth, Penwithy and the villages of the Fal estuary who need a document accepted by a foreign bank, court, land registry or consulate. We also act for the solicitors and accountants of the town when their clients' papers are going overseas.

## What a notary does not do

We do not give advice on foreign law, and we cannot tell you what a document means for you in Spain or Poland. Your lawyer there can; we make sure that what you sign here will be accepted there.

## After the appointment

Most documents leave with you the same day, signed, sealed and bound. Where an apostille or a consulate's stamp is needed we send the papers off ourselves and post them to you, or to the country, when they come back.
