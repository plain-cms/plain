## What to bring

- Your passport or photocard driving licence, in date
- A utility bill or bank statement from the last three months with your address
- The document itself, unsigned: please do not sign it before you come
- Any instructions from the person or office abroad who asked for it

Signing for a company, bring the certificate of incorporation and the board resolution that authorises you, or tell us the company number and we will look it up.

## Finding the door

Chapel Yard is the cobbled lane between the Methodist chapel and the fish market on Quay Street. Number 3 has a blue door and a brass plate; the office is on the first floor. Ring the bell marked Penhallow & Vane and we will come down.

## Parking and access

The Harbour car park is three minutes' walk, and there are two short-stay bays on Quay Street. There are eighteen stairs and no lift: say so when you book and we will see you in the ground-floor room at the town library, or at your home.

## Sending papers from abroad

Post them to the office address by a tracked service and send us an e-mail on the day they go. For urgent papers during the week, the out-of-hours line is [01632 960249](tel:01632960249).
