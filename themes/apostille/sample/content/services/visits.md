The notarial fee is the same as at the office. Travel is £60 within Fallowmouth and Penwithy and £90 elsewhere within twenty miles.

## Before the visit

We need to be satisfied that the person signing understands the document. Where there is any doubt, we may ask for a short letter from their doctor.
