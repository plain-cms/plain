Most of our powers of attorney are for houses in France, Spain and Portugal, and for estates in Poland. Your lawyer there usually sends a draft; if they have not, we can prepare one in the form the country expects.

## What to bring

The draft, the full names, dates of birth and addresses of the person you are appointing, and the address of the property or the reference of the estate.
