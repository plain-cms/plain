An apostille is a certificate, issued by the government, that confirms the notary's signature and seal are genuine. Most countries ask for one; a few, outside the Hague Convention, ask for their own consulate's stamp instead.

## How long it takes

The standard service usually takes about a week from the day we send the papers. A same-day service is available for an extra government fee; ask when you book.
