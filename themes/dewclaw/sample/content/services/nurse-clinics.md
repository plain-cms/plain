Not everything needs a vet. Our nurses run clinics every weekday afternoon for the small jobs and the regular checks, and they will tell you straight away if your pet should see a vet instead.

## What we do

- Weight and diet checks, with a plan for pets who need to lose a little
- Nail clipping for dogs, cats, rabbits and guinea pigs
- Microchipping, and updating the details on an existing chip
- Stitch removal and checks after an operation
- Flea and worm advice, with a year's plan for your pet
- Help giving tablets or eye drops, for owners who find it hard

## What it costs

| Clinic | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Weight and diet check | Free |
| Nail clip | £14 |
| Microchip | £22 |
| Checks and stitch removal after an operation with us | Free |

## Booking

Nurse clinics run Monday to Friday, 2 pm to 5 pm, in twenty-minute slots. Book through the desk like any other appointment.
