Most visits start here: a vet examines your pet nose to tail, listens to what you have seen at home, and explains what they find and what they suggest, in plain words.

## What happens

You are called in at your booked time. The vet checks your pet over, weighs them and talks you through the options, including the option of waiting to see. If a test is worth doing, you hear what it is for and what it costs before anything is taken.

Book a double appointment for more than one problem, or for an older pet with a lot going on.

## Afterwards

If medicine is prescribed, you take the first course home from the desk. Call us if things are not improving in the time the vet gave you.
