X-rays show bones, chests and swallowed objects; ultrasound shows the organs of the belly and the heart. Tomasz Kowalczyk does most of our imaging, and both machines are in the building.

## What happens

Most pets need a sedative to lie still and comfortable for X-rays. An ultrasound scan often needs only a patch of fur clipped. The vet sits down with you, on the screen or on the phone, to show you the pictures and say what they mean.

## Afterwards

Your pet may be sleepy for the evening after sedation. If the pictures need a specialist's opinion, we send them the same day and phone you when the report is back.
