Neutering prevents unplanned litters and some cancers and infections later in life. We talk through the best age for your pet at their vaccinations, and a free pre-op check with a nurse settles the date.

## What happens

A vet examines your pet on the morning of the operation and answers any last questions. Your pet has a general anaesthetic, and a nurse stays with them from going to sleep until they are awake and warm in the ward. We phone you when they have woken up, with a time to collect them.

## Afterwards

Keep your pet quiet and indoors for ten days, with short lead walks only for dogs. Stitch checks at three and ten days are free.
