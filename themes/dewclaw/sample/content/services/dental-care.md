Bad breath, dropping food and chewing on one side are often a sore mouth. Most dogs and cats over three need a proper clean at some point, and it can only be done thoroughly while they are asleep.

## What happens

Your pet has a check and a sedative on arrival and then a general anaesthetic. We clean above and below the gum line, polish, and check and chart every tooth. If a tooth is loose or broken, we phone you with what we have found and the price before taking it out.

Your pet goes home the same afternoon, usually after 4 pm.

## Afterwards

Soft food for a few days if teeth were taken out. A free nurse check after ten days, and a word on brushing if you would like to try.
