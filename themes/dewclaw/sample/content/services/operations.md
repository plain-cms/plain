Our theatre is in the same building as the ward, so your pet is never moved between sites. Most operations are day cases: in in the morning, home in the late afternoon.

## What happens

You have a consultation first, where the vet explains the operation, the risks and the alternatives. You leave with a written estimate. On the day, a vet checks your pet again before the anaesthetic, a nurse monitors them throughout, and we phone you as soon as they are awake.

If your pet needs to stay the night, a nurse or vet checks on them through the evening and the vet on call is in the building nearby.

## What it costs

Every operation is priced in writing before admission, and we phone you before doing anything that would change the figure. Pre-operative blood tests are offered for older pets and are on the estimate.

## Afterwards

You go home with written instructions and any medicine. Free checks at three and ten days. If you are worried at night, the out-of-hours line is on the instructions sheet.
