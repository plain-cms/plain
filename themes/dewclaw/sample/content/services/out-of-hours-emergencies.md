Our vets share the nights and weekends on a rota. When you call the out-of-hours line, you speak to a vet from this practice who can see your pet's notes.

## When to call straight away

- Breathing trouble, or gums that are pale, grey or blue
- Bleeding that will not stop with pressure
- A road accident or a fall, even if your pet seems fine
- Suspected poisoning: chocolate, rat bait, lilies for cats, antifreeze, painkillers for people
- A swollen belly with retching that brings nothing up
- A cat straining in the litter tray without passing urine
- A fit that lasts more than five minutes, or several in a day

## What happens

The vet asks a few questions, tells you whether your pet needs to be seen now, and if so meets you at the surgery, usually within half an hour.
