When the time comes, call the desk and say why you are phoning. We give you the last appointment of the day or a visit at home, so nobody is waiting outside and nothing is rushed.

## What happens

The vet explains each step before it happens. Your pet is given a sedative first so they are calm and sleepy, and you can stay with them throughout or say goodbye beforehand — either is right. Take as long as you need, before and after.

We settle the bill and the paperwork afterwards, by phone, never on the day.

A clay paw print is free; ask on the day or before.

## Afterwards

Ashes come back to the surgery within about two weeks and we phone you when they are here. If you would like to talk to someone in the weeks after, Hannah keeps a list of local pet bereavement services.
