Every vaccination appointment is also a full health check with a vet, so it is the visit where we spot early problems with teeth, weight, heart and skin.

## What happens

The vet examines your pet, gives the injection under the scruff — most pets barely notice — and fills in the card. A first course is two visits, two to four weeks apart.

## Afterwards

Your pet may be a little quiet for a day. Puppies can go out to walk a week after the second injection. We send a reminder when the next booster is due.
