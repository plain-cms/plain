Rabbits and guinea pigs hide illness well, so small changes matter. Priya Nair sees most of our small-pet patients, and the ward has a quiet room for them away from dogs and cats.

## What happens

A consultation includes a check of the teeth, which keep growing all their lives and are the most common thing we treat. We weigh your pet and talk through hay, greens and housing. Rabbits can be vaccinated at the same visit.

## When to call the same day

A rabbit or guinea pig that has stopped eating or has passed no droppings for half a day needs to be seen that day. Call us, or the out-of-hours line after we close.
