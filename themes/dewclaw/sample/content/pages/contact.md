## Book or ask

Call **[01632-960418](tel:01632-960418)** while we are open, or write to [desk@brindlestreetvets.example](mailto:desk@brindlestreetvets.example) and the desk will reply the same working day. Repeat prescriptions can be ordered by e-mail; give us two working days.

## If it cannot wait

**Ill or injured after we close?** Call **[01632-960990](tel:01632-960990)**. One of our own vets answers, talks it through with you and, if your pet needs to be seen, meets you at the surgery. Please always phone first — the building is locked at night and nobody can see you if you just arrive.

Call straight away for breathing trouble, bleeding that will not stop, a road accident, suspected poisoning, a dog with a swollen belly retching but bringing nothing up, or a cat straining to pass urine.

## Where we are

**27 Brindle Street, Ashby Moor, AM4 2LT.** The green door next to the launderette, five minutes' walk from Ashby Moor station.

## Parking and access

Free parking for six cars in the yard behind the building; the yard gate is on Tanner's Row. The front entrance has a ramp, the consulting rooms are all on the ground floor, and we can lend a trolley for a large dog who cannot walk.
