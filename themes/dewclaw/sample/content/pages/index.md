We are a small-animal practice at 27 Brindle Street: three vets, two nurses and a front desk that picks up the phone. We see dogs, cats, rabbits, guinea pigs and the odd ferret. We tell you the price before we start, and when a pet stays in with us you get a call from the ward, not a wait by the phone.

## What most people come in for

- [Vaccinations](/care/vaccinations/) — boosters and first courses, with a full health check each time
- [Consultations](/care/consultations/) — fifteen minutes with a vet, and longer when it needs it
- [Neutering](/care/neutering/) — in by half past eight, home the same afternoon
- [Dental care](/care/dental-care/) — a proper clean under anaesthetic, and a phone call before any tooth comes out
- [Out-of-hours emergencies](/care/out-of-hours-emergencies/) — ill or injured at night? Call 01632-960990 first

[Everything we do, and what each visit costs](/care/)

## Your first visit

Bring your pet on a lead or in a carrier, and any vaccination card or notes from a previous vet. A first consultation is a nose-to-tail check and a talk about food, fleas, worms and anything that has been worrying you. It takes about fifteen minutes; book a double slot if there is a lot to go through.

Nervous dogs can wait in the car — tell the desk and we call you in when the room is free. Cats have their own side of the waiting room, away from the dogs.
