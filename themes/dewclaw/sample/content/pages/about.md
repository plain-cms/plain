Ruth Ashworth opened Brindle Street Vets in 2011 in what used to be the corner dairy. It is still independent: no group head office, and the vet who sees your pet is the one who decides what happens next.

## How we work

- **One vet for each case.** When your pet comes back for the same problem, you see the same vet whenever their rota allows.
- **Prices before treatment.** A consultation has one fixed price. Anything more comes with an estimate first, in writing for operations.
- **Our own nights.** We do not hand you to a call centre. After we close, the phone goes to one of our own vets, who can meet you at the surgery.
- **A quiet ward.** Twelve kennels for dogs, a separate cat ward with hiding boxes, and a small room for rabbits and guinea pigs, away from barking.

## Insurance

We fill in the vet's part of a claim form for any insurer and can send it straight to them. Ask at the desk if you are not sure what your policy covers; Lorraine has read most of them.
