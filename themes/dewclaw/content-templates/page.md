You hear the price of a visit before it starts, and every operation comes
with a written estimate first.

## What visits cost

| Visit | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Consultation | Fixed price, told when you book |
| Booster vaccination | Fixed price per species |
| Operation | Written estimate before admission |

## Paying

We take cards and cash at the desk on the day you collect your pet. Ask about
spreading the cost of a larger bill.

## Insurance

Bring your policy details to the first visit. We fill in the vet's part of
the claim form and can send it straight to your insurer.
