Calder is a ground engineering consultancy of sixty people with offices in
Bristol and Swansea. My team takes a scheme from the desk study to the last
settlement reading, so the engineer who scopes the boreholes is the one who
signs off the foundations.

## The hospital extension

The first design put 380 driven piles through soft alluvium beside a working
ward block. Vibration limits would have kept the rig to four hours a day. A
second look at the borehole logs showed a gravel layer at six metres that
could carry the load if the clay above it was stiffened, so we proposed stone
columns instead, trialled twelve, and load-tested three before the client
agreed.

## Bringing people on

Four of the eleven joined as graduates. Each spends their first three months
logging on site before they write a report, because a report is only as good
as the logger's description of the ground.
