The old bank stood on up to eighteen metres of soft grey clay. Raising it in
one go would have pushed the clay out sideways, so the fill went on in four
stages, each held until the drains had let enough water out of the clay for
the next.

I read the piezometers and settlement plates every week, and the decision to
place each stage was mine. Twice the readings said wait, and we waited.
