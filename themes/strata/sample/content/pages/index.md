**Open to a principal or associate role from March 2027.** Bristol, Cardiff or the South West, with site travel.

I am a chartered geotechnical engineer. I have logged boreholes in Pennine cuttings, run a site laboratory beside a tailings dam in Western Australia, and raised six kilometres of flood bank over estuary clay you could push a finger into.

What clients and contractors bring me in for:

- **Soft ground.** Embankments, staged filling and settlement monitoring on alluvium and peat.
- **Investigations that answer the question.** Scoped to the foundation decision, not to a standard schedule of boreholes.
- **Savings below ground.** Pile and ground-improvement options costed side by side before the design is fixed.
