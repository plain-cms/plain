Lower Hartwell Farm is three miles north of Ledbury, at the end of Bosbury Lane. Follow the yellow festival signs from the A417; the satnav postcode takes you to the farm gate, not the car park.

## By train and shuttle bus

Ledbury station has trains from Worcester, Hereford and Birmingham. A shuttle bus meets every train from 11 am to 8 pm on Friday and from 9 am to 6 pm on Saturday. It goes back to the station from 5 pm to 9 pm on Sunday and from 8 am to 10.30 am on Monday. Tickets are £3 each way, cash or card on the bus.

## By car

Parking is £10 a car for the weekend, paid at the car park gate. Campervans park in the Top Field and need a campervan pass (see [Join us](/join/)). Please do not park on Bosbury Lane: it is a single-track road and the bus needs it.

## By bike

There is a locked bike pen beside the box office. Bosbury Lane is quiet but has no lights after dark.

## Without steps

The accessible campsite is next to the car park, on level ground with its own toilets and a water point. A raised viewing platform stands to the left of the Meadow stage. Tell the box office when you arrive and a steward will take you round by the farm track.

## Taxis

Hartwell Cars waits at the box office from 10 pm on Friday and Saturday. Book a ride on [01632 960218](tel:+441632960218).

## Places to stay

The Bell at Hartwell Cross has six rooms and is a twenty-minute walk across the fields. Ledbury has more, ten minutes away on the shuttle bus.
