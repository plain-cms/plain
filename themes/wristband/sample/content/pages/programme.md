Set times can move by a few minutes on the day. The chalkboard by the bar always has the latest running order.

## If it rains

The Meadow stage has a roof and the Orchard tent has sides, so the music carries on. Only lightning stops a set. Bring boots: the Long Meadow is on clay and holds water.

## Quiet hours

Music ends at 1 am on Friday and Saturday. The Quiet Field, next to the car park, is for families and early sleepers and stays quiet from midnight.

## Children

Under-12s come free with a paying adult. The craft tent is open from 10 am to 5 pm on Saturday and Sunday, and there are baby-changing tables in the toilet block by the Orchard tent.

## With thanks

The bar is run by Bosbury Lane Cider and the food stalls are all from within twenty miles. The Meadow stage is lent by the Hartwell Cross Young Farmers.
