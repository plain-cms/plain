Hartwell Meadows began as a barn dance for the farm’s neighbours. It is still run from the farmhouse kitchen, and it is still small on purpose: two thousand tickets, two stages and one long field of tents beside the brook.

A weekend ticket covers camping from Friday noon until ten on Monday morning. [Getting there](/getting-there/) has the shuttle bus from Ledbury station, and [Join us](/join/) has every ticket and price.
