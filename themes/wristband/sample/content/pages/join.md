Everyone, babies included, needs a ticket. Book online and swap it at the box office, at the top of the car park, for a woven wristband. Keep it on until you go home: it is your way in and out all weekend.

| Ticket | Price | What it includes |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Weekend | £95 | Friday noon to Monday 10 am, with camping |
| Saturday or Sunday | £45 | 9 am to the last set, no camping |
| Under-12s | Free | With a paying adult; collect a wristband at the box office |
| Campervan pass | £30 | A pitch in the Top Field, added to a weekend ticket |

Lost wristbands cannot be replaced, so do not cut yours off on Saturday night.

## Questions

Write to [box@hartwellmeadows.example](mailto:box@hartwellmeadows.example) or ring the farm on [01632 960473](tel:+441632960473), weekdays from 9 to 5.

## Volunteer

Work three four-hour shifts, on the gate, the bar or the car park, and your weekend ticket is free. Write to the address above with the days you can do.

## Traders

We have room for eight food stalls and four craft stalls, all from within twenty miles. Pitches are £180 for the weekend, with power.
