Odette Marsh will play the last set of the weekend, at 4 pm on Sunday on the Meadow stage, with a string trio she brings down from the Fens.

That fills the bill. The other new names are Fen & Furrow, Mabel Thorne, Rosa Quintero, Dandelion Clock and DJ Sorrel. The full running order is on the [programme](/programme/).
