The Long Meadow is on clay. Whatever the forecast says, pack boots.

## Bring

- A torch: there are no lights between the tents
- A refillable bottle; the water points are by both toilet blocks
- Something warm for after dark, when the mist comes off the brook
- A little cash for the smaller stalls

## Leave at home

- Glass of any kind
- Gas barbecues and open fires; the firepit in the Long Meadow is the only fire on site
- Fireworks and sky lanterns, which frighten the cattle in the next field
