Last year the lane was full of cars by Friday teatime. This year a shuttle bus meets every train at Ledbury station, from 11 am to 8 pm on Friday and from 9 am to 6 pm on Saturday.

It runs back to the station from 5 pm to 9 pm on Sunday and from 8 am to 10.30 am on Monday. Tickets are £3 each way on the bus. All the times are on [Getting there](/getting-there/).
