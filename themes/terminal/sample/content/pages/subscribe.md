New posts arrive by e-mail about twice a month: the whole post in plain text, code included, so you never have to click through. No digests, no launch announcements, no tracking pixels.

To be added to the list, write to [dana@whitlock.example](mailto:dana@whitlock.example) with the word *subscribe*. I add every address by hand, which is slower than a form and means the list lives in one text file that nobody else has seen.

Leaving is one line back to me, and it takes effect before the next post.

## Or use a feed reader

The [RSS feed](/blog/rss.xml) lists every new post the moment it is published. Paste its address into any reader; nothing to sign up for, nothing to leave.
