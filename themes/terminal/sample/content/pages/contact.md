## Me

[dana@whitlock.example](mailto:dana@whitlock.example) — for anything about a post, a correction, a question about running a small software business, or to say a tool was useful. Corrections get fixed and credited.

I don't take sponsored posts or link swaps, so those won't get a reply.

## Copperline support

Customers should write to [support@copperline.example](mailto:support@copperline.example) instead. It reaches the same person, but it lands next to your account, so I can see your checks while I read your message.

## Security reports

If you've found a vulnerability in Copperline, jotfile or pgq-worker, please report it privately before publishing. The details are in the usual place:

```text
Contact: mailto:security@copperline.example
Preferred-Languages: en
Policy: reports acknowledged within 24 hours, fixed before disclosure
Expires: 2027-06-30T00:00:00Z
```

## Code

Bugs and feature requests for the open-source tools belong on their issue trackers, where other people can find the answer too. The [projects page](/projects/) lists them.
