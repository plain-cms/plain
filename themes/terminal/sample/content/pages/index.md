I'm Dana Whitlock. I run [Copperline](/projects/), a one-person uptime and status-page service, and this site is the logbook: what broke, what shipped, what I'd do differently.

```sh
$ copperline stats --since 2023-09-01
checks run            212,480,113
paying teams          412
unplanned downtime    4m 12s
people on call        1
```

Some posts are deep technical dives with real query plans in them. Some are essays about running a business with a team size of one. Both are written for the same reader — a developer who might do this someday.
