Everyone on the site told me two courgette plants would be plenty for one person. I raised six from seed and planted four, because the other two looked so healthy it seemed unkind to put them on the compost.

In the first week of August they began to crop, and they have not stopped. I pick every other day, small — fifteen centimetres or so, when they are firm and sweet — and still there are more than I can cook. The neighbours on 22 and 24 have stopped answering the gate.

## The one that got away

One fruit grew in the shade of a leaf the size of an umbrella, and when I found it today it was forty-three centimetres long and weighed two and a half kilos. Des says stuff it with mince. I have put it in the shed until I am braver.

Next year: two plants. I am writing it down here so I cannot pretend I was not told.
