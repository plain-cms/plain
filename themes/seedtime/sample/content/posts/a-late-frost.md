The potatoes went into trenches at the start of the month, the chitted shoots handled like eggs. By this weekend the first ‘Charlotte’ leaves were through, a bright crinkled green in two neat lines.

Then the sky cleared on the Saturday evening and the temperature dropped to minus two. By morning the leaves were black and soft at the tips. Des, who had watched the forecast, had drawn soil up over every one of his shoots with a draw hoe and lost nothing.

## What happens next

Frosted potato tops are rarely the end. The plant has food in the tuber and will send up new shoots in a week or two; the crop is a little later and a little smaller, and that is all. I earthed up what was left, and keep a roll of fleece in the shed now, for nights like that one.

The parsnip row shows nothing yet. The radishes in the same drill are up and doing their job.

— Hilary
