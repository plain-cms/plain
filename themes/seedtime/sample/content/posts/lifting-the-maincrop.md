The haulm had died back at the start of the month. I cut it off at ground level and left the tubers in the dry soil for a fortnight, so the skins would set and they would keep through the winter.

Then the digging: a fork pushed in well away from each plant and levered up, the tubers picked out by hand. ‘Pink Fir Apple’ grows in long tangles, so every plant took longer than I expected, and I still put a fork through the biggest one in the row.

## The weights

| Variety | Seed planted | Lifted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ‘Charlotte’ (lifted in July) | 1 kg | 11.5 kg |
| ‘Pink Fir Apple’ | 1 kg | 19 kg |

The potatoes are drying on newspaper in the shed and will go into paper sacks, in the dark, once the soil on them has fallen away.

The parsnips came up after all, in their own good time. The row is green from one end to the other.

— Hilary
