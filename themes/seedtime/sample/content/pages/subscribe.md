Each entry arrives by e-mail the evening I write it up: about once a month, more often in spring, hardly at all in December. No adverts, no seed offers, and your address goes nowhere else.

To be added, write to [hilary@fiverods.example](mailto:hilary@fiverods.example?subject=Subscribe) with the word *subscribe*. I add every name to the list by hand, so it may take a day or two. To leave, reply to any entry and say so.

If you use a feed reader, the [RSS feed](/blog/rss.xml) has every entry as soon as it goes up.
