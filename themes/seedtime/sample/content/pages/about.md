I’m Hilary Brennan, a retired school librarian, and this is the diary of plot 23B on the Wharf Lane allotments in Belgrave, Leicester: a half-plot of about five rods, between the canal path and the site’s one tap.

I had never grown anything bigger than a tomato in a grow bag before. The diary began as a way of remembering what I had done, so I could stop making the same mistakes twice. It has turned into a way of making new ones in public.

## The rules

- One entry a month, more if something happens, none if nothing does.
- Dates are the days I was on the plot, not the days I wrote it up.
- Varieties are named, because that is what other growers ask about. Shops are not.
- Failures go in. They are most of the useful bits.

## Writing back

I would much rather hear what grows on heavy clay in your part of the country than be told I am doing it wrong — but I will take either. Write to [hilary@fiverods.example](mailto:hilary@fiverods.example).

— Hilary
