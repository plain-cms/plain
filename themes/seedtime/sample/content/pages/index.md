I’m Hilary Brennan. In November 2025 I took on half-plot 23B at Wharf Lane — five rods of clay, couch grass and one sound water butt — and I write up what happens on it, roughly once a month, usually at the kitchen table with soil still under my nails.

If you’re new, three to start with:

- [Taking on half a plot](/blog/taking-on-half-a-plot/) — the first entry, and the state of the ground.
- [The broad beans go in](/blog/the-broad-beans-go-in/) — the first sowing that came to anything.
- [Lifting the maincrop](/blog/lifting-the-maincrop/) — the potato harvest, weighed.

Every entry is in the [archive](/blog/), newest first. New ones can come to you by [e-mail](/subscribe/).
