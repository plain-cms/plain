The smallest room in the house and the quietest: a single bed, a chest of drawers, hooks for wet coats, and a window looking up Rope Walk to the chapel. The shower room is the room's own, two steps along the landing.

**For walkers:** the coast path is at the end of the road, we dry boots overnight in the boiler room, and breakfast can be packed as a lunch if you are off before eight.

The price includes breakfast.
