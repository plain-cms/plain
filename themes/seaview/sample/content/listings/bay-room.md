The best room in the house, and the one with the board in its window. It sleeps two in a king-size bed, and the three windows of the bay take in the whole of Hollins Bay from the harbour wall to the Point.

**In the room:** a window seat in the bay, a wardrobe and an armchair, a tea tray with proper leaf tea, and a bathroom of its own with a roll-top bath and a separate shower.

**Breakfast** is included, 8:00 to 9:30 in the front parlour downstairs.
