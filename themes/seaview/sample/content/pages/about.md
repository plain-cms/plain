Rockpool House was built in 1874 as a captain's house, and it has taken guests since the 1950s. We bought it from Mrs Arkwright, who ran it for thirty-one years and left us her breakfast menu, which we still use.

## A stay, from arrival to departure

- **Arrival** is between 15:00 and 19:00. If you will be later, ring and we will leave a key in the safe by the door.
- **Breakfast** is from 8:00 to 9:30 in the front parlour: a full cooked breakfast, kippers from the smokehouse on Quay Street, porridge, or eggs any way. Tell us about any diet when you book.
- **Departure** is by 10:30. You can leave bags in the hall for the day.

## Terms

- From May to September, a stay that includes a Friday or a Saturday is two nights at least.
- We ask for one night's price as a deposit when you book. It is returned in full if you cancel seven days or more before you arrive.
- Children are welcome in the Family Room. Dogs are welcome in the Garden Room only, at £10 per dog per night.

## Getting here

The station is a six-minute walk, downhill all the way. If you drive, we have two parking permits for the bays on Marine Terrace; otherwise the long-stay car park by the station is £6 a day.

There is no lift. The Garden Room is the only room with no stairs.
