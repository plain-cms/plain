## Ring or write

Call [01632-960418](tel:01632-960418), any day between 8:00 and 20:00, or write to [stay@rockpoolhouse.example](mailto:stay@rockpoolhouse.example). Through the winter, when the house is closed to guests, we still answer both.

## Booking a room

Tell us the nights, the room and how many of you there are. We will say straight away whether it is free, and hold it for 48 hours. To confirm, send a deposit of one night's price by bank transfer; we send the details with our reply.
