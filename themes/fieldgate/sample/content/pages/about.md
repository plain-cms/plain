## Who we are

**Hannah Pennock** grew up on a dairy farm at Thorpe and has sold land
in the valley for eighteen years. She values farms, runs the tenders and
answers most of the phone calls.

**Daniel Rowe** looks after lettings: grazing licences, farm business
tenancies and the cottages that come with them. He also measures and
draws every sale plan we publish.

**Ruth Ackroyd** runs the office, the diary and the keys.

## How we sell

We sell by private treaty, by informal tender or, for larger farms, at
auction in the Kelderby Mart sale room. Where a farm will sell better in
pieces, we offer it in lots as well as a whole and tell you which way the
offers came in.

Every listing gives the area in hectares and acres, the guide price, the
tenure, the rights of way and the drawback: the wet corner, the steep
bank, the footpath across the middle.

## Our fees

| Service | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Sale of a farm or land over £250,000 | 1.25% of the price, plus VAT |
| Sale of land under £250,000 | 1.75% of the price, plus VAT, minimum £1,500 |
| Grazing licence or farm tenancy | 10% of the first year’s rent, plus VAT |
| Valuation for probate or a family partnership | Quoted in writing before we start |

No sale, no fee. If you withdraw after the plan and particulars are
printed, we charge the cost of the plan and nothing else.
