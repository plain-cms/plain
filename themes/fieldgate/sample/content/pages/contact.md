## Ring or write

Call [01632-960274](tel:01632-960274), Monday to Friday 8:30 to 17:00.
Write to [office@pennockrowe.example](mailto:office@pennockrowe.example)
and you will have an answer the same working day. Tell us the name of
the lot you are asking about.

## Viewing land

Most bare land can be walked on foot with the particulars in hand, any
day in daylight; each listing says so, and where to park. Please close
every gate and keep dogs on a lead where there is stock. Houses,
cottages and farm buildings are viewed with one of us, by appointment.
