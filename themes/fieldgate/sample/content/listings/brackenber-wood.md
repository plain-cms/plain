An old wood on the south side of the valley, mostly oak and ash over
hazel, with a stand of Scots pine at the top and a pond near the gate.

**The wood:** 14.20 hectares (35.09 acres), of which about 9 hectares
is ancient semi-natural woodland. Thinned in 2018 under a felling licence
that has now run out. Bluebells along the lower edge in May.

**Access:** a stone track from Brackenber Lane runs up through the wood
to a timber store at the top. A bridleway follows the western edge.

**Tenure:** freehold, with vacant possession. The sporting rights are
included and are not let.

**The drawback:** ash dieback is in the wood. About a fifth of the trees
are ash, and the ones beside the track will need felling within a few
years.

**Viewing:** on foot, any day in daylight. Park at the gate on
Brackenber Lane.
