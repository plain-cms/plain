Grazing on a licence from 1 April to 31 October 2027 for cattle or
sheep, let by the owner each year.

**The land:** four fields of permanent pasture totalling 11.30 hectares
(27.92 acres), stone walls in good order, mains water to a trough in
each field and a handling pen at the bottom gate.

**Terms:** a grazing licence, not a tenancy. The licensee keeps the
walls stock-proof and tops the thistles in July. No fertiliser without
the owner's agreement.

**The drawback:** the top field is steep and holds sheep better than
cattle.

**How to apply:** ring or write to Daniel Rowe with the stock you would
put on it. The owner chooses by the end of January.
