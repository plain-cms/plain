The largest field of Low Garth Farm and the second lot of three: one
level field of Grade 3 land, cut for hay every July and grazed in the
autumn.

**The land:** 9.12 hectares (22.54 acres) of permanent grassland, last
reseeded in 2014. Stone walls on three sides and a hawthorn hedge
along the beck. Mains water to a trough at the top gate.

**Access:** two field gates onto the Kelder Moor road, one wide enough
for a modern baler.

**Tenure:** freehold, with vacant possession on completion. No public
rights of way. The land is in a Countryside Stewardship agreement until
December 2027, which a buyer would take over.

**The drawback:** the bottom corner by the beck floods for a day or two
most winters.

**Viewing:** on foot with these particulars, any day in daylight. Park
in the gateway, not on the verge.
