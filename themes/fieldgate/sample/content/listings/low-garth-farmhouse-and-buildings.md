The house and steading of Low Garth Farm, which the Metcalfe family have
farmed since 1931. The farm is offered as a whole or in three lots; this
is the first, and the other two are [Long Meadow](/listings/long-meadow-low-garth/)
and [Beck Ings](/listings/beck-ings-low-garth/).

**The house:** a double-fronted farmhouse in sandstone under a stone
slate roof. Four bedrooms, two bathrooms, a farmhouse kitchen with an oil
range, a sitting room and an office. Oil central heating, mains
electricity, water from a private spring with a UV filter. Septic tank,
replaced in 2019.

**The buildings:** a stone threshing barn of about 240 m² with
permission in principle for conversion, a range of stone byres, and a
five-bay steel portal frame shed of 1998 (27 × 18 m).

**The land:** two level paddocks behind the house, in permanent pasture,
with water troughs on the spring supply.

**Tenure:** freehold, with vacant possession on completion. A public
footpath crosses the yard between the house and the barn.

**The drawback:** the lane up from the road is shared with Garth
Cottage and is the farm's to maintain.

**Sale:** by informal tender, in writing, by 12 noon on Friday
4 December 2026. Viewing of the house by appointment only.
