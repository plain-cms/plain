Three level arable fields on the valley floor below Thorpe, in winter
wheat and spring barley for the last six years.

**The land:** 32.60 hectares (80.56 acres) of Grade 2 sandy loam, under
drained in the 1980s. Field gates onto the Thorpe road.

**Terms:** a farm business tenancy of ten years from Lady Day, 25 March
2027, with a rent review in the fifth year. The tenant has been chosen
from eleven tenders and the agreement is signed.

**The drawback:** the drains in the bottom field need attention, and the
tenancy makes the landlord responsible for them.
