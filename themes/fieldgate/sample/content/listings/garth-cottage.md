A former farm worker's cottage of about 1850, at the foot of the lane
to Low Garth. It stays in the owner's hands whichever way the farm
sells.

**The cottage:** two bedrooms, a bathroom with a shower over the bath,
a kitchen with a back door to the garden, and a sitting room with a
multi-fuel stove. Oil central heating; EPC rating E.

**Terms:** an assured shorthold tenancy of twelve months, then month to
month. Deposit £835. Pets considered; no smokers.

**The drawback:** no mobile signal indoors on most networks, and the
broadband is the copper line.
