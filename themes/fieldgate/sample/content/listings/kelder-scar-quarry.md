A limestone quarry last worked in 1962, now a flooded pool ringed by
scrub woodland, with a level hardstanding yard by the road.

**The land:** 4.62 hectares (11.42 acres) in all: the pool of about
1.1 hectares, the quarry faces and the woodland around them, and a
yard of about 0.3 hectares.

**Terms:** the owner will sell the freehold or grant a lease of up to
25 years, as a whole. Uses so far discussed include diving training and
a nature reserve; any of them needs planning permission.

**The drawback:** the faces are up to 18 metres high and the pool is
deep. The fencing is sound but anyone taking it on takes on its care.

**Viewing:** with us only, by appointment.
