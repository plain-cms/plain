Two or three sentences on what this is and where: the lane it is reached
from, what lies around it, and how it is offered (as a whole or in lots).

**The land:** the area in hectares and acres, the grade, how it is used
now, the boundaries and the water supply. Delete `beds` and `baths` above
for bare land or woodland.

**Tenure and rights:** freehold or let, vacant possession or not, any
rights of way, wayleaves, sporting or mineral rights, and the basic
payment or stewardship position.

**The drawback:** the wet corner, the steep bank, the footpath across the
middle. Say it here before a viewing does.

**Viewing:** on foot with these particulars, or with us by appointment.
