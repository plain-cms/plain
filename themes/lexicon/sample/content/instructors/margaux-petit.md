Margaux teaches French Beginners on Tuesday evenings and the French Film
Club on Thursdays, for anyone at B1 or above.

Beginners start with sounds before spelling, so nobody is embarrassed by
the letters French leaves silent. In Film Club you watch at home, with
French subtitles, and come in ready to disagree.

She worked in subtitling in Paris before she taught, and she will pause
any scene to show you the joke the English subtitles missed.
