Dominic teaches Tuesday's IELTS Preparation and Thursday's English
Conversation. Most of his IELTS students need a band score for a
university place or a visa, and he plans the term backwards from their
test dates.

You will write one timed essay a week and get it back marked against the
real band descriptors, with two full mock exams in the term. The Thursday
group is looser: newspapers, podcasts, and whatever happened at work.

He taught in Valencia and Busan before coming home to Bristol, and he
remembers what it is like to be the one who doesn't understand the joke.
