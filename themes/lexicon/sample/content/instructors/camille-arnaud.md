Camille has the Wednesday French Elementary group, the DELF B1 Exam Class
straight after it, and the Saturday morning French beginners.

Her beginners' lessons are built around one real situation an evening —
the bakery, the station, the doctor's — and end with you doing it out
loud. The exam class is different: past papers, a timer on the table and
written feedback on every essay, returned the following week.

Before Wordhouse she examined for the DELF in Lyon for eight years, so she
knows exactly where the marks go.
