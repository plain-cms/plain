Priya teaches Everyday English at Entry 1 on Mondays and at Entry 2 on
Wednesdays, both at 18:00, and places every new English student herself.

Lessons cover what the week actually needs: phone calls to the surgery,
school letters, job forms, the bus. You can bring a letter you don't
understand and the group will work through it together.

She has taught English to speakers of other languages for twelve years
and speaks Tamil, Hindi and enough Arabic to say hello properly.
