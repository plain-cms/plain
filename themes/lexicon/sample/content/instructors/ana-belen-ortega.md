Ana Belén teaches the Monday 20:00 Spanish Conversation group and the
Saturday morning beginners, which she says keeps her honest: in one room
everybody argues about the news, in the other everybody is still learning
to say where they live.

Expect to speak within the first five minutes, in pairs, and to swap
partners often. Mistakes are corrected after you finish your sentence,
never in the middle of it.

She grew up in Salamanca, worked as a tour guide in Seville for six years,
and still explains the subjunctive with a walking route.
