Jean-Baptiste has the Monday French Improvers at B1 and the Tuesday 20:00
French Conversation for B2 and above.

His groups read one short article at home each week and spend the lesson
arguing about it. He keeps a list of the mistakes he hears and works
through them in the last quarter of an hour, without saying whose they
were.

He came to Bristol from Nantes to study engineering, stayed for the
cider, and has taught here since 2018.
