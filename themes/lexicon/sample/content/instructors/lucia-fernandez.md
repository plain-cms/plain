Lucía teaches Spanish Beginners on Monday and on Wednesday at 18:30; the
two groups follow the same course, so you can swap evenings for a week if
work gets in the way.

The first lesson is almost all listening and pointing, and by the end you
will have introduced yourself to everyone in the room. She sends a short
recording after each lesson for the bus home.

She is from Oviedo, has a degree in teaching Spanish as a foreign
language, and will happily tell you which part of Asturias to visit first.
