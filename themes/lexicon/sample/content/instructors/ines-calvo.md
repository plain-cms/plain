Inés teaches Spanish Improvers on Tuesdays, Spanish Elementary on
Thursdays, and the Thursday 20:00 Grammar Clinic, which students from any
group may join for the term.

Her lessons have a plan you can see on the board, a short piece of
homework every week, and a five-minute quiz nobody fails. The Clinic is
built from the questions you bring in on paper.

She trained as a secondary teacher in Zaragoza and has taught adults in
Bristol since 2015. Her rule: one new tense a fortnight, never more.
