Classes last ninety minutes: the 18:00 groups finish at 19:30, the 18:30
groups at 20:00, and the 20:00 groups at 21:30. The door opens fifteen
minutes before the first class. There are no classes on Fridays or
Sundays. Any change for snow or illness is posted on this page first, and
your teacher texts the group.

## What the levels mean

Spanish and French levels follow the European framework, the same letters
you will see on exam certificates.

- **A1** assumes nothing. You learn to introduce yourself, order, ask the
  way and understand the answer.
- **A2** is for people who finished a year of A1, or who can still get by
  on holiday.
- **B1** means you can hold a slow conversation on most everyday subjects.
- **B2–C1** is discussion, news and film, with grammar where it trips you up.
- **Entry 1** and **Entry 2** are the first two levels of English for
  speakers of other languages. Priya places every new student herself.

Conversation Café on Saturdays is informal: three tables, one per
language, tea included, no booking.

## How to enrol

You can join any course in its first three weeks, and later if the
teacher agrees and there is a seat. [Contact the desk](/contact/) to book
a placement chat or a trial lesson; you pay only when you decide to stay.
