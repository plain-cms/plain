## Your first lesson is free

Tell us which language and which evening, and we will book you into one
lesson of the course you are thinking of. If you have studied before, we
start with a twenty-minute placement chat — in person at the desk or on
the phone — so you land in the right group. Bring a pen. Books are lent
for the term.

## Prices

| Course | Price | Good to know |
| ------ | ----- | ------------ |
| Placement chat and trial lesson | Free | Once per language |
| Term course, evening or Saturday | £20 a lesson | £240 in autumn (12 weeks), £200 in spring and summer (10 weeks) |
| IELTS Preparation | £290 a term | Includes two timed mock exams with written feedback |
| DELF B1 Exam Class | £290 a term | The exam fee is paid to the exam centre, not to us |
| Everyday English (ESOL) | £60 a term | Free if you receive Universal Credit or are seeking asylum |
| Conversation Café | £5 | Pay on the day; tea included |

You can pay the term in two halves. A second course in the same term is
a quarter off.

## Where we are

**Wordhouse Languages**, first floor, 41 Gloucester Road, Bristol BS7 8AD —
the green door beside the bookshop, up one flight of stairs. Buses along
Gloucester Road stop at Berkeley Road, a minute away; Montpelier station
is a ten-minute walk. There is a bike rack in the yard behind the
building. The stairs have a handrail but no lift; tell us if you need a
ground-floor room and we will arrange one.
