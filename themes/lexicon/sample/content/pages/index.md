## How it works

Every course meets once a week for ninety minutes and runs for a whole
term. You stay with the same group and the same teacher from September to
December, so by half-term the room knows your name and your excuses. The
lesson is taught in the language from the first evening — slowly, with a
lot of pointing — and everyone speaks in every lesson.

We teach Spanish and French from complete beginner to exam level, and
English to adults who live and work here: everyday English from Entry 1,
and IELTS for university and visa applications.

## Where to start

If you have never studied the language, choose any course marked **A1**
on the timetable: Monday or Wednesday evening for Spanish, Tuesday evening
for French, or Saturday morning for either. If you learned some at school
and it has gone quiet, [book a placement chat](/contact/) first — twenty
minutes, free, mostly in English.

Read about [the teachers](/instructors/) before you choose; most students
pick the evening, then stay for the person.
