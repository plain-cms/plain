## How it works

We are seven people who teach what we love and rent Mill Lane Hall to do it.
The hall is ours from six in the evening, Monday to Thursday, and all day on
Saturdays; on Fridays the badminton club has it. Every class runs every week
of term, and you can pay by the evening or buy a ten-class card — prices are
on the [contact page](/contact/).

Materials are included in every price: paper and charcoal, clay and firing,
wood and the loan of a knife, ink and lino. Bring an apron and something to
carry your work home in.

## Where to start

If you have never drawn from life, come to Thursday's quick poses — short
poses, lots of paper, nobody looking over your shoulder. If you want to speak
French on your next holiday, start with Hélène on a Tuesday. Not sure? Read
about [the teachers](/instructors/), then ring Ruth, who keeps the bookings
and will tell you honestly which class suits you.
