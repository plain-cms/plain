Each class shows its start and finish. The door to the hall opens fifteen
minutes before the first class of the evening; please don't arrive earlier,
as the hall is let to other groups until then. If a class is cancelled — a
teacher's illness, or snow — we post it here and on the hall noticeboard by
four o'clock that afternoon.

## What the levels mean

- **All levels** — complete beginners and old hands in the same room; the
  teacher sets each person their own next step.
- **Beginners** and **Beginners welcome** — assume you have never done it
  before. Quick-pose life drawing is the gentlest way in.
- **Improvers** — French conversation is for people who can order a meal and
  ask the way, and want to get past that.

## Term dates

- **Autumn term:** Monday 7 September to Saturday 12 December 2026; no

- **Spring term:** Monday 11 January to Saturday 27 March 2027; no classes

To join part-way through a term, see the [contact page](/contact/) — you are
welcome any week there is a free place.
