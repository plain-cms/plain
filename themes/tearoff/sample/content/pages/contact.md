## Your first evening

Ring or email to save a place, then come to the side door of the hall a few
minutes before the start. Your first class is half price, whichever you
choose, and if it is not for you there is no card to buy and nothing to
cancel.

## Prices

All materials are included. Pay on the evening by card or cash, or buy a
ten-class card to use on any ten evenings of that class within one term.

| Class                     | Price                        |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| Life drawing (either)     | £12, or £100 for ten         |
| Clay hand-building        | £18, or £160 for ten         |
| French or Italian         | £10, or £85 for ten          |
| Watercolour               | £14, or £120 for ten         |
| Spoon carving (Saturday)  | £30 a morning                |
| Lino printing (Saturday)  | £22 an afternoon             |

The life drawing price includes the model's fee. Students and anyone on
benefits pay half; just say so, no questions asked.

## Finding the hall

**Mill Lane Hall**, 3 Mill Lane, Ashcombe — the brick hall behind the
chapel, side door off the yard. It is five minutes' walk from the Market
Place; buses 3 and 53 stop on Bath Street. The hall has no car park, but
the Station Road car park is free after six.

There is a step at the side door; ring ahead and we will open the front
ramp. Toilets and a kitchen with a kettle are inside.
