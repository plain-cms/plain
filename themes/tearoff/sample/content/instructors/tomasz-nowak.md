Tomasz brings the clay, the tools and the boards on Wednesday evenings and
takes everything back to his studio to fire. Pieces come back glazed two
weeks later.

There is no wheel: everything is made by hand, which is the best way to
learn what clay will and won't do. Beginners usually make a pinch pot and a
small coil vase in their first few weeks.

Wear clothes you don't mind getting grey.
