Devika's Thursday class works from still life in winter and from photographs
of the town in summer, with one evening a term outside if it's dry.

Paints, brushes and paper are provided. Each evening opens with a short
demonstration and then everyone paints; she goes round the room twice.

She illustrates walking guides for a living and carries a paintbox wherever
she goes.
