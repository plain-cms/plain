Carla's Monday class is built around a holiday: by the end of term you can
book a room, order a meal, buy a train ticket and understand most of the
answer.

There is no textbook and no homework you have to do. Each evening has a
short handout, a lot of speaking in pairs, and five minutes of grammar only
when someone asks why.

She grew up in Bergamo and has taught in Ashcombe since 2015.
