Gwen's Saturday afternoon class covers the whole job: drawing a design,
transferring it, cutting the lino and printing an edition of your own.

She has a small press, but most printing is done by hand with a baren or the
back of a spoon, so you can carry on at home with a kitchen table.

She made the posters for the hall's summer fair for eleven years running.
