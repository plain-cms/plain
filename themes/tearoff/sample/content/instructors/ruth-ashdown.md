Ruth runs Monday's long-pose session, where the model holds one pose for most
of the evening, and Thursday's quick poses, which move from one-minute
gestures to twenty minutes by the end.

On your first evening she will set you up with a board, cartridge paper and
willow charcoal, show you how to measure with a pencil, and then leave you
alone. She only comes round when you put your hand up.

She trained as a scenery painter and still thinks in big shapes first.
