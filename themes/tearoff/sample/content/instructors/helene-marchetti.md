Hélène teaches French from scratch at six on Tuesdays and the conversation
class straight after. Plenty of people stay for both.

Beginners start with the sounds before the spelling, so you can say what you
read. The conversation class has a topic each week — the news, a recipe, an
argument — and English is allowed only for emergencies.

She comes from Lyon, moved here for a year in 2009, and stayed.
