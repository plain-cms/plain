Joe's Saturday class starts with a split log and ends, three hours later,
with a spoon. Most people finish one on their first morning.

He brings the wood, the axes and the knives, and teaches the safe grips
before anyone cuts anything. Gloves are provided for the hand that holds.

He is a tree surgeon on weekdays, which is where the wood comes from.
