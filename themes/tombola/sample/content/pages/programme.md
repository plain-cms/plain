Times in the ring are as near as we can keep them. The dog show starts when the dogs are ready.

## If it rains

The stalls move into the church and the church hall, and the band plays in the nave. The dog show, the races and the tug of war go ahead unless the green is under water. A notice goes up on the church gate by ten on the morning.

## The dog show

Six classes at £1 a class, judged by Hilary Fenwick of Kirkby Wold Vets. There is a rosette for every dog placed.

1. Waggiest tail
2. Best puppy, under a year
3. Golden oldie, eight and over
4. Best trick
5. Dog most like its owner
6. Best in show

## With thanks

The raffle prizes, the marquee and the public address are given by Hallowby Stores, the Plough, Garside & Daughter Butchers and Wold Edge Nurseries.
