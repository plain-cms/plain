Nobody needs a ticket and nobody needs to reply. To take part, talk to Margaret Thwaite, the fair secretary: [fair@hallowby.example](mailto:fair@hallowby.example) or [01632 960147](tel:+441632960147), any evening before nine.

## Have a stall

Book by Friday 4 June 2027. You bring the cloth and the float; we put out the table.

| Stall | Cost | What you get |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Village groups and charities | Free | One six-foot trestle |
| Crafts, plants and produce | £10 | One six-foot trestle |
| Hot food and vans | £25 | A pitch on the long side, no power |

Setting up is from ten. Cars must be off the green by half past eleven.

## Give a cake

The tea tent gets through sixty cakes. Bring yours to the church hall by eleven on the day, on a plate you do not mind losing, with a note of what is in it.

## Give a raffle prize

Prizes can be left at Hallowby Stores until Thursday 17 June. Bottles, tins and vouchers all go; please, no more bath salts.

## Enter the dog show

No booking. Bring the dog and a pound to the ring from 1.30.

## Lend a hand

We need twelve people to put up tables on Friday evening at six, and the same twelve to take them down at five on Saturday. Tea and a bacon roll are provided.
