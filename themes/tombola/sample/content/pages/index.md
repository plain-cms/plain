Hallowby has held a fair on the green on the third Saturday of June since 1951. Everything on it is run by people who live here, and every penny goes to the roof of St Wilfrid’s.

Come at noon for the band and stay for the raffle at four. [Getting there](/getting-there/) has the bus and the car park. If you would like a stall, or have a cake to give, see [Join us](/join/).
