The green is in the middle of the village, between St Wilfrid’s and the Plough. If you can walk, walk: Church Lane is closed to cars from eleven until six.

## By bus

The 31 from Kirkby Wold market place stops at the church gate. It leaves at ten past the hour and takes eighteen minutes. The last bus back is at 5.40.

## By car

Follow the yellow signs from the Kirkby Wold road to the Glebe Field, behind the church. Parking is £2 a car, paid to the scouts on the gate. It is a three-minute walk to the green through the churchyard.

Please do not park on Church Lane or in front of the cottages on Beck Row.

## Without steps

The churchyard path has four steps. For a level way in, ask at the Glebe Field gate and you will be sent round by Beck Row, which is tarmac all the way. Six blue-badge spaces are kept beside the church hall; tell the steward at the top of Church Lane.

## Toilets and babies

Toilets, one of them accessible, are in the church hall. There is a changing table in the vestry.

## Dogs

Dogs on leads are welcome everywhere except the tea tent. There are water bowls by the bandstand.
