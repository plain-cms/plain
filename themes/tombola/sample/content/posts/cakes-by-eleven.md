Last year the tea tent ran out at ten to three, with the dog show still in the ring. This year Priya Nair is asking for sixty cakes, and has promises of forty-one.

Anything that cuts into slices is welcome: sponges, loaf cakes, tray bakes, a fruit cake if you have the patience. Scones are made on the morning in the hall kitchen.

Please write the main ingredients on a card and tape it to the plate. Nuts, eggs, milk and wheat matter most.

To promise a cake, ring Priya on [01632 960163](tel:+441632960163).
