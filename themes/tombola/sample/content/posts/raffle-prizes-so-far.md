Tickets are £1 a strip and go on sale at Hallowby Stores on Monday 14 June. They are also sold on the gate until a quarter to four.

The top of the list so far:

1. A hamper from Hallowby Stores
2. Sunday lunch for four at the Plough
3. A trailer load of seasoned logs, delivered within five miles
4. A leg of lamb from Garside & Daughter
5. Two hanging baskets from Wold Edge Nurseries

The raffle is drawn at four at the bandstand. You need not be there to win: write a phone number on the back of the stub.
