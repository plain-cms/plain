The cricket club is reseeding the outfield, so the car park moves to the Glebe Field, behind St Wilfrid’s. Follow the yellow signs from the Kirkby Wold road.

It is £2 a car, paid to the scouts on the gate, and three minutes on foot to the green through the churchyard.

Church Lane is closed to cars from eleven until six. Blue-badge holders are waved through to the spaces by the church hall.
