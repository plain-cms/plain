Hearing changes slowly, and hearing aids wear. A short check twice a year
keeps both in step.

## What happens

We clean the aids, change tubes, domes and filters, check the battery or
charger, and listen to each aid. If your hearing has changed, we retest and
retune the aids before you leave.

## What it costs

Free for aids we fitted. £35 for aids fitted elsewhere.
