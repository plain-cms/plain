Sometimes the answer is not a stronger hearing aid but a small box by the
television, or a phone with a louder, clearer earpiece.

## What happens

We set up the devices in the shop so you can try them with your own aids.
If you buy one, we pair it with your aids and show you how it works.

## What it costs

From £60 for a phone amplifier to £250 for a television streamer. Ask us
before you buy one elsewhere; some don't work with every aid.
