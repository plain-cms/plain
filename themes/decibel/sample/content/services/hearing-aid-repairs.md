Most hearing aids that go quiet have a blocked wax filter or a split tube.
Both take a few minutes to fix.

## What happens

Come to the repairs counter during opening hours — no appointment needed.
Bev cleans and checks the aid, replaces what is worn, and listens to it
before handing it back. If it needs the maker, we send it away and lend
you a spare aid in the meantime where we can.

## What it costs

Free for aids we fitted. £10 for tubes, domes and filters on aids from
elsewhere. Repairs sent to the maker are quoted before they go, usually
between £95 and £180.

## Afterwards

Sent-away repairs come back in about ten days. We call you when it's in.
