Plugs made to your ears stay in, don't ache, and last for years.

## What happens

Owen takes a soft impression of each ear; it feels cool and takes about
five minutes. The plugs come back from the lab in about three weeks, and we
check the fit with you.

## What it costs

Swimming or sleeping plugs: £85 a pair. Musicians' plugs with filters that
lower every pitch evenly: £180 a pair.
