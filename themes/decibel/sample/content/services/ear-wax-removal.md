Wax is healthy, but when it blocks the ear it muffles everything and stops
hearing aids from working.

## What happens

Owen looks into your ear with a magnifying camera and shows you on the
screen. A thin, gentle suction tube lifts the wax out. You hear a whistling
noise; it doesn't hurt. We show you the clear ear at the end.

## What it costs

£60 for both ears, £45 for one. If the wax won't come out at the first
visit, the second visit is free.

## Afterwards

Sounds may seem bright for a day or two. Some people need wax removed every
six months; we'll tell you if you are one of them.
