We aren't tied to one maker, so we choose aids for your hearing, your
hands and your budget — and we show you the cheapest pair that would do
the job.

## What happens

At the fitting we set the aids to your audiogram, then fine-tune them while
you listen to speech, music and the noise of a busy room. You practise
putting them in, changing domes and charging them until it feels easy.

You come back after two weeks and again after six for adjustments. Most
people need both.

## What it costs

From £1,250 a pair, fitted, including every follow-up visit and free
aftercare for as long as you wear them. You have sixty days to try them;
if they aren't right, bring them back for a full refund.

## Afterwards

Expect your own voice and the rustle of a newspaper to sound loud for the
first week. That settles. Wear them every day, a little longer each day.
