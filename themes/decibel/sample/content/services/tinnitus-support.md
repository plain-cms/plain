Tinnitus is a sound only you can hear: ringing, buzzing, hissing. It is
common, it is rarely a sign of anything serious, and it usually becomes
less noticeable with the right help.

## What happens

Ruth tests your hearing, asks how the tinnitus affects your sleep and your
day, and explains what is going on. Together you make a plan: sound at
bedtime, ways to relax, and sometimes hearing aids with a gentle sound
setting.

£35.
