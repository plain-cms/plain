A hearing test tells us how well you hear each pitch, from deep sounds to
high ones, in each ear. It doesn't hurt, and nothing goes inside your ear
except a pair of soft earphones.

## What happens

We start by talking: where you struggle, and where you don't. Then we look
in your ears to check for wax, and you sit in our quiet room and press a
button each time you hear a beep. We finish with a few words spoken at
different levels, so we know how well you follow speech.

The results go on an audiogram — a chart of each ear, with circles for the
right and crosses for the left. We go through it with you and give you a
printed copy.

## What it costs

£45. If you go on to buy hearing aids from us within three months, the
£45 comes off their price.

## Afterwards

Most people need nothing more than a test every two years. If your results
suggest a problem a doctor should see, we write a letter for you to take to
your surgery.
