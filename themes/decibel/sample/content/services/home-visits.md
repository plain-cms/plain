We visit homes and care homes within ten miles of Ollerby, on Friday
afternoons between 2 pm and 5 pm.

## What happens

Owen brings a portable audiometer and the same equipment we use at the
practice. A test, a wax removal or a repair takes as long as it does in
the shop.

## What it costs

The usual price of the appointment, plus £30 for the visit. Several
residents of one care home share a single visit charge.
