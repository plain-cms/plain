Calder Row Hearing opened in 2011, when Ruth Pennick left a large hearing-aid chain to work the way she thought patients deserved: with time. We are not tied to one manufacturer, so we can recommend whichever hearing aids suit your ears, your hands and your budget — and we'll say so when the answer is "none yet".

## How we work

- **Time first.** A hearing test here takes forty-five minutes. We don't double-book.
- **Prices in writing.** You get the cost before anything happens, and it doesn't change.
- **Looked after for life.** Aids fitted here are cleaned, retubed and checked free for as long as you wear them.
- **Plain words.** We'll explain your results on the chart and give you a copy to take home.

## Who you'll meet

- **Ruth Pennick**, audiologist and owner. Thirty years in hearing care, and still the one who answers when a patient asks "can I just pop in?"
- **Owen Marsh**, audiologist. With us since 2019; he does most of our wax removal, home visits and custom ear plugs.
- **Bev Hollins**, front desk and repairs counter. If your aid has gone quiet, Bev has usually fixed it before the kettle boils.

## Hearing loop and access

The front desk, the waiting area and both rooms have a hearing loop — switch your aids to the T setting. We are happy to write things down, and to send letters in large print.
