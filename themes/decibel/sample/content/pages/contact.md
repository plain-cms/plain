## Book or ask

Call **[01632-960318](tel:01632-960318)** — Bev answers whenever we are open, and she will speak slowly and repeat anything you need. If the phone is difficult for you, write to [hello@calderrowhearing.example](mailto:hello@calderrowhearing.example), or ask a relative to call for you.

## Hearing aid stopped working?

Come to the repairs counter during opening hours; no appointment is needed for new tubes, domes, filters or batteries. If you can't come in, call and we will post you what you need the same day.

We have no out-of-hours line. If you have sudden hearing loss in one ear, pain or a discharge, see your doctor the same day — don't wait for us to open.

| Day | Open |

Home visits are on Friday afternoons, by appointment.

## Where we are

**3 Calder Row, Ollerby** — the green door beside the post office, a minute's walk from Ollerby bus station (routes 2, 7 and 31).

## Parking

Two spaces in front of the shop are kept for patients; ask Bev to hold one when you book. The Market Street car park is three minutes' walk and free for the first two hours.

## Access

- Step-free from the pavement, with a wide door that opens outwards
- Both rooms are on the ground floor, and there is an accessible toilet
- A hearing loop at the desk, in the waiting area and in both rooms
- A chair with arms in the waiting area, and plenty of time to get settled
