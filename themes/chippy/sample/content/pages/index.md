We fry in beef dripping, the way Jack Tanner did when he opened the shop. The cod and haddock are skinned and boned here every morning, and the chips are Maris Pipers from a farm outside Malton, peeled and cut before we open. Nothing goes in the pan until you ask for it. See [the full menu](/menu/), or [how to find us](/visit/).

## This week

Winter hours have started, so we are closed on Mondays until Easter — the [winter hours](/blog/winter-hours/) are on the door and here. The shop also shuts for five days in January while [a new frying range goes in](/blog/new-frying-range/).

## Gluten-free Tuesdays

On Tuesdays the first pan of the day is fresh dripping and a rice-flour batter, and nothing else is fried in it until 14:00. Tell us at the counter and we will do the rest.
