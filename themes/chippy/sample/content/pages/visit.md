The range goes off a quarter of an hour before closing. On bank holidays we keep Sunday hours.

## Find us

9 Pier Road, Staithe Bay — on the harbour side, opposite the lifeboat station and two doors up from the amusements. The 93 bus stops at Harbour Top, three minutes’ walk downhill.

## Parking and access

Pier Road is double yellows all the way down. Use the pay-and-display at Harbour Top, or the free hour on Cliff Street after 18:00. The door is level with the pavement and wide enough for a wheelchair or a pram; there are two benches inside for the wait.

## Collection

Ring [01632 960147](tel:+441632960147) from 11:00 and we will give you a time. There is no minimum order and no charge; pay at the counter by card or cash. We stop taking phone orders half an hour before closing, and we do not deliver.

For a party order of twenty portions or more, email [orders@tannersfishbar.example](mailto:orders@tannersfishbar.example) two days ahead.
