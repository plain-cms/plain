From Monday 2 November the shop is closed on Mondays, and Sunday’s last orders are at 19:00. Tuesday to Saturday stays as it is: lunch from 11:30, tea from 16:30, later on Fridays and Saturdays.

| Day | Open |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | Closed |
| Tuesday to Thursday | 11:30–14:00 and 16:30–21:00 |
| Friday and Saturday | 11:30–21:30 |
| Sunday | 12:00–19:00 |

Summer hours come back on Good Friday, 26 March.
