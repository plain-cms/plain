title: Closed 11–15 January for a new frying range
date: 2026-11-09
description: The old three-pan range comes out after thirty-one years. We shut on Monday 11 January and fry again on Saturday 16 January.
example: true
---

The range Jack Tanner put in during 1995 is coming out. It has fried, by our sums, a little over two million portions of chips, and the fitters say it will take two days to get it through the door.

We close after tea on Sunday 10 January and open again for lunch on Saturday 16 January. The new range has three pans, as before — one kept for fish, one for chips, and one that takes the gluten-free batter on Tuesdays.

Nothing on the board changes, and neither does the dripping.
