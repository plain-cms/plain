## Who we are

Ruth trained at the botanic garden in Sheffield and Colin was a fruit grower's son from the Vale of York. We bought the field in 1994 with one polytunnel and a caravan. Our daughter Nell joined us in 2019 and now runs the propagation house and the fruit.

## How the plants are raised

Nearly everything is grown from our own cuttings, divisions and seed. Perennials are potted in spring, stood outside on gravel beds and sold when the roots have filled the pot, not before. Fruit trees are budded in August onto rootstocks we line out in the bottom field, and sold as two-year-old trees.

We have used peat-free compost since 2011. It is mixed for us from bark, wood fibre and green compost at a yard eight miles away.

## What we do not sell

We do not buy in plants in flower from the Dutch auctions, and we do not sell bedding, houseplants or anything that needs a greenhouse to get through a Yorkshire winter.

> If it is on the bench in April, it stood outside in January.

## The labels

The stock beds beside the car park are labelled as a botanic garden would label them, so that you can see a plant at its full size before you buy it in a pot. Walk round them before you choose.
