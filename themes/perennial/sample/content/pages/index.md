## What we grow

About four hundred perennials, sixty shrubs and thirty kinds of fruit tree, nearly all propagated here from our own stock beds. Nothing is forced under glass. A plant that has stood a winter on this hill will stand one in your garden.

## How to read a label

Every plant is listed under its botanical name, with its height and spread after five years and the months it flowers or crops. The price is for the pot size shown beside it. Where we sell a plant in more than one size, the sizes are listed with the plant.

## In the ground now

Bare-root fruit trees and hedging are lifted from November to March. Order at any time and we ring you the week they come out of the ground. The hellebores are in flower from February, and that is the time to choose one.
