## Finding us

Leave Skelderby by Moor Lane, signed for the moor road, and climb for a mile and a quarter. The nursery is the first gate on the left after the Methodist chapel. The lane is narrow, with passing places; a sat-nav will bring you to the right gate.

The 31 bus from Helmsby stops at the chapel four times a day, Monday to Saturday. It is a five-minute walk from there.

## Parking and paths

There is a gravel car park for twenty cars inside the gate. The sales beds are on level, rolled gravel and a wheelchair or pushchair will manage all of them. The stock beds are on grass and slope gently. Dogs on leads are welcome.

## Paying

Card or cash at the potting shed. We give 10 per cent off twenty or more plants of one kind; ask when you order.

## Delivery and collection

Reserved plants are stood by the gate with your name on them and kept for a week. We load heavy pots and trees into your car for you.

| Where | When | Charge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Within fifteen miles | Thursdays | £8 |
| Fifteen to thirty miles | First Thursday of the month | £15 |
| Bare-root by carrier, mainland | November to March | £14 per parcel |
