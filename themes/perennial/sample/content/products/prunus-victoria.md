One tree is enough: 'Victoria' sets fruit with its own pollen and in a good year needs thinning in June to save the branches.

## To order

Trees are lifted on Mondays from the first week of November. Order by the Friday before and we ring you when yours is out of the ground. A bare-root tree should be planted within three days, or heeled into a spare corner until you are ready.

Prune only in summer, never in winter, to keep silver leaf out.
