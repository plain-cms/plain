Every seedling is different: slate, plum, primrose, white, plain or spotted. The small pots are a year from flowering and sold as they come. The large ones are in flower, and you pick the one you like.

Plant them under deciduous shrubs with leaf mould and leave them alone. Cut the old leaves off at the ground in December so that the flowers show.
