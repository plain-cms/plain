Open with the strongest sentence you have, the one you would be tempted to save for the middle.

Then the situation: a paragraph or two on the concrete thing that started this letter. A Tuesday, a street, a number.

Then the turn, where the letter stops describing and starts to think. What did the situation show you that you did not expect?

Then widen it, carefully: one honest step outward from the small thing to the larger one.

End on the ground, back in the scene you opened with, changed by everything in between.
