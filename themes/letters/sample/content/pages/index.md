Dear reader,

I’m Nell Whitcombe. I edit other people’s sentences for a living, in Duluth, Minnesota, and every few weeks I write a letter of my own and send it to everyone who has asked for one. Each is a single essay — short enough to read with a cup of coffee, long enough to have been worth writing down.

If you’re new, three to start with:

- [Why Letters](/blog/why-letters/) — the first one, and the reason for the name.
- [Three Miles an Hour](/blog/three-miles-an-hour/) — the one people forward.
- [The Reply I Didn’t Send](/blog/the-reply-i-didnt-send/) — the one that gets the most replies.

The whole run is in the [archive](/blog/), set out like a book’s contents and numbered in the order it was written. New letters arrive by [email](/subscribe/) the morning they’re finished, and nothing else ever does.

— Nell
