Rosa's mat Pilates is the timetable's quiet assassin: no equipment, no
jumping, and somehow the class that everyone's physio keeps recommending.

The movements are measured in centimeters and the cues in jokes, and your
core files a formal complaint the next morning. She keeps a running feud
with the word "engage" and teaches what it actually means instead.

Rosa danced professionally for a decade and rebuilt her own back with
Pilates after the career ended. She teaches the mid-morning slots on
purpose — they're the classes her older regulars can make, and they are
fiercely hers.
