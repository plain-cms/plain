Maya opened the studio's very first sunrise class and has taught it, with
two maternity breaks, ever since. Her vinyasa is precise without being
strict: clear cues, long exhales, and one honest arm balance a month.

You arrive half-asleep, you move for fifty minutes without once wondering
what to do next, and you leave having accidentally done your hardest
practice of the week. Tuesday's Slow Flow is the same teacher at half the
speed, and the best first yoga class on the timetable.

She came to yoga as a competitive swimmer with two blown shoulders, which
is why her sequences are kind to shoulders and why she has zero patience
for "no pain, no gain". Hers is the class where someone finally convinces
you to use blocks.
