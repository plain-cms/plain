Imani teaches Barre Basics on Tuesday and Saturday mornings and Prenatal
Yoga on Thursdays. No dance background is needed for the first, and the
second is open from twelve weeks with your midwife's or doctor's say-so.

Barre with her is forty-five minutes of movements too small to look
difficult and a playlist too good to quit on. The prenatal class is
slower: supported shapes, breathing you will use later, and ten minutes at
the end when the room compares notes.

She trained as a doula before she trained as a teacher, and it shows in
how she runs a room — every option is offered twice, and the easier one is
never called the easy one.
