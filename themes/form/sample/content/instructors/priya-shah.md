Priya teaches the classes people come to after the week wins: Yin, the
Saturday Restorative Hour, and the breathwork that regulars describe as
"a nap that fixes things."

Five shapes in sixty minutes, each held long enough for your to-do list to
stop arguing with you. She talks less than any teacher on the timetable
and somehow says the most. Phones live in the cubbies; nobody has ever
missed one.

She found yin during night shifts as an ICU nurse — she still works two a
week — and teaches rest as a skill, not a reward. Ask a regular and they
will quote her: "You don't earn stillness. You practice it."
