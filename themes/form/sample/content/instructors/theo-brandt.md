Theo runs the strength side of the studio: Strength Foundations for people who
have never touched a barbell, and the kettlebell classes for people who now own
opinions about handle diameters.

Groups are small and every rep is watched. He picks your weights until
you've earned the right to choose badly on your own. Loud music is
negotiable; form is not. When someone gets their first pull-up the whole
room stops to applaud, because Theo stops the room.

Before this he was a removals man for nine years, and he teaches lifting
the way movers actually lift — which is why his beginners' backs feel
better after a month, not worse.
