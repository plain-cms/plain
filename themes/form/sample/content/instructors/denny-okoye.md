Denny teaches the classes people come to on a physio's orders and stay in
by choice: lunchtime mobility you can do in work clothes, and the
Wednesday session for bodies coming back from something.

Nothing in his room is glamorous and all of it is specific — hips that sit
in chairs all day, ankles that run on weekends, shoulders that live at a
keyboard. You leave moving better and knowing one new thing about why you
weren't.

He is a physiotherapist by training and a marathon DNF by testimony; the
injury that ended that race is the reason the Wednesday class exists.
Nobody is too broken for his room. That's the room's whole idea.
