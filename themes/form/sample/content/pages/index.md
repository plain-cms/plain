## How it works here

We have one big sprung-floor room, a rack of kettlebells, and a timetable
that covers both halves of a strong body: yoga and Pilates on one side,
strength and mobility on the other. Most of our regulars do both, usually
to their own surprise.

No contracts and no mirrors — you buy a [class pass](/contact/), and you
watch your own body instead of a reflection of it. Classes cap at sixteen
so the [instructors](/instructors/) actually see you; beginners are
announced to no one and adjusted kindly. Mats, blocks, and bells all live
here. Bring yourself and a water bottle.

## New to all of it?

Start with Strength Foundations or Slow Flow — both are built for
first-timers — then ask Denny what your desk has been doing to your hips.
He's been waiting.
