## Your first class is free

Pick anything marked *All levels* or *Beginner* on the [schedule](/schedule/),
come ten minutes early, and say you're new at the desk. That's the entire
process. Wear anything you can move in; mats, blocks, and kettlebells live
here. The only thing worth bringing is a water bottle.

## Prices

| Pass              | Price | Good to know                         |
| ----------------- | ----- | ------------------------------------ |
| First class       | Free  | Any class, once                      |
| Drop-in           | $18   | Pay at the desk, card or cash        |
| 10-class pass     | $150  | Valid six months, shareable          |
| Monthly unlimited | $125  | Cancel any time, no joining fee      |

Students, over-65s, and anyone between jobs pay $12 a class — just say so.

## Where we are

**Ninth Street Movement**, 42 Ninth Street — the blue door between the
bakery and the bike shop. Buses 3 and 11 stop at the corner; bike racks by
the door; the nearest car park is two minutes up on Mill Road.

The desk opens twenty minutes before the first class of the day and closes
after the last one, Monday to Saturday. We're closed on Sundays.
