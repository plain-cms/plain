Doors open twenty minutes before each class. Changes for public holidays
are posted on this page first, and on the studio door second.

## What the levels mean

- **All levels** means it. Every sequence comes with an easier and a
  harder door, and you pick per rep, not per lifetime.
- **Beginner** assumes nothing: never held a kettlebell, never done a
  downward dog. These are the best first classes.
- **Intermediate** assumes about three months of regular classes, here or
  anywhere. Ask at the desk if you're unsure.
