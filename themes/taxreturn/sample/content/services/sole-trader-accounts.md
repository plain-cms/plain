For anyone trading under their own name, from a first year with twelve invoices to a firm of three vans.

## How it runs

Records come in by the end of September. You have draft accounts within four weeks and a meeting, in person or on the phone, to go through them. The return is filed once you have signed.

## How the fee is set

By the state of the records, not by your turnover. Tidy books kept in an app cost £340; a year of loose receipts costs up to £520, and we will tell you which before we start.
