For landlords with one let flat or a dozen, furnished holiday lets and rooms let in your own home.

## What to bring

The agent's annual statement or your own rent record, mortgage interest certificates, and invoices for repairs, insurance and safety checks.

## What it costs

£120 for each property, on top of the fee for the tax return itself. Properties let through one agent on one statement count as one after the third.
