For employers who would rather not learn what a tax code change means at four o'clock on a Friday.

## How it runs

Send the hours or any changes two working days before pay day. You get the payslips, a list of what to pay each person, and the amount due to HMRC.

## What it costs

£6.50 a payslip with a minimum of £26 a month. Setting up a new scheme, or taking one over part-way through a year, is a single fee of £75.
