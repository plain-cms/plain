For owner-run companies with up to about fifteen staff. Larger companies that need an audit are referred on.

## What the monthly fee covers

Everything a company must file in a year, and the directors' personal returns. Bookkeeping, payroll and VAT are separate services and can be added to the same standing order.

| Company | Monthly fee |
| --- | --- |
| One director, no staff | £95 |
| Two directors or up to five staff | £135 |
| Up to fifteen staff | £180 |
