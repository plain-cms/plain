If HMRC has sent you a notice to file, or you have income that is not taxed at source, you need a return. We prepare about two hundred a year.

## What to bring

Your P60 or payslips, a statement of any interest and dividends, pension contributions, gifts to charity, and the letter from HMRC with your ten-digit reference.

## What it costs

| Your return includes | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Employment, pensions and savings only | £180 |
| One self-employment | £260 |
| Capital gains pages | add £90 |

A return brought in after 10 January costs £60 more, because something else has to be moved to fit it in.
