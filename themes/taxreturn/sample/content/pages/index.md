## Who we act for

Sole traders, landlords, partnerships and small limited companies in Ashby Vale, Northcote and the villages along the Wend: builders, childminders, hairdressers, taxi drivers, a few farms. We do not take audit, insolvency or investment work, and will give you the name of someone who does.

## After the first meeting

You get an engagement letter the same week, setting out the work, the fee and the dates we need your records by. Nothing is charged until you have signed and returned it.

## Your records, as they are

A shoebox of receipts, a spreadsheet or the login to a bookkeeping app: bring what you have. We sort it once, then show you a simpler way to keep it.
