## What to bring to a first meeting

- Your Unique Taxpayer Reference, if you have one: ten digits, on any letter from HMRC
- Last year's tax return or accounts, whoever prepared them
- Any letter from HMRC you have not known what to do with
- A rough idea of the year's income and what you spent to earn it

If you have none of these, come anyway. Half of new clients start with a carrier bag.

## Finding the door

Tanner Row runs behind the Corn Exchange. Number 9 is the green door between the bakery and the cycle shop; the office is on the first floor. Ring the bell marked Garside and we will come down.

## Parking and access

The Corn Exchange car park is two minutes away and free for the first hour. There are fourteen stairs and no lift: say so when you ring and we will meet you in the ground-floor room at the library, or at your home.

## Sending records

Post, the letterbox at number 9, or the secure upload link in your engagement letter. Please do not send bank statements by ordinary e-mail.
