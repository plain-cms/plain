## How it started

Helen Garside spent six years in the tax department of a large Leeds firm, preparing returns for people she never met. In 2009 she took the two rooms above the old saddler's on Tanner Row, so that the person who prepares your figures is the person you sit across the table from.

## How we work

Every client has one engagement letter, one fee and one deadline list, renewed each spring. Returns are drafted by November where the records arrive by September, and nothing is filed until you have seen it and signed.

We work in whatever you already use, from paper to cloud bookkeeping, and only suggest changing when it would save you money or a weekend.

## Membership and insurance

The practice holds an ACCA practising certificate and is inspected by the ACCA. It carries professional indemnity insurance of £1 million for any one claim, and is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office for the records it keeps.

## What we do not do

Audit, insolvency, investment advice and mortgages. For those we keep a short list of local firms we trust and take no commission for the introduction.
