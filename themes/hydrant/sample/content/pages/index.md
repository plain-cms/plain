We are volunteers: farmers, a joiner, two teachers, the woman who runs the post office. When the pagers go, whoever is in the village drops what they are doing, and the engine is out of the doors within five minutes. Last year we answered 143 calls, from chimney fires to collisions on the moor road.

Drill night is every Monday and the station doors are open. Come and watch, ask questions or try the hose reel. We always need crew who are in the village during the working day, and nobody starts with experience.

**In an emergency always dial 999.** The station phone is not staffed and nobody watches this site.
