The fire started beside the bridleway at about four in the afternoon and burned twelve hectares of heather before it was held at the wall. Nobody was hurt and no stock was lost.

The beck was too low to pump from. The Metcalfes, the Raws and Jack Horner ran water up from the village in tankers until two in the morning. We could not have held it without them.

The moor is still very dry. Please, no barbecues and no cigarettes up there.
