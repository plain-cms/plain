All three joined after last year’s recruitment evening. Beth farms at Low Ings, Owen is an apprentice joiner and Marta works at the surgery, which gives us two more people in the village on weekdays.

They ride as probationers for a year. If you see them on the engine, be kind: the first few calls are a lot to take in.
