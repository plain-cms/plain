A hydrant plate tells the crew two things in the dark: the size of the main, above the bar of the H, and how many metres away the hydrant lid is, below it. A plate nobody can see costs us minutes.

If the yellow plate outside your house is overgrown, please cut it back. If the lid in the verge is buried or parked on, tell Tom Iveson, the hydrant warden.

Thank you to the six cadets who repainted nineteen lids.
