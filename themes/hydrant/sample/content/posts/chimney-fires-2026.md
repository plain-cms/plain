All four were in chimneys that had not been swept since last winter. None spread to the roof, but one family spent the night with neighbours.

A chimney that burns wood wants sweeping twice a year, and one that burns coal once. Burn only dry logs, and never bank the stove up overnight.

If the chimney does catch, shut the stove doors, get everyone out and dial 999. Do not wait to see whether it burns itself out.
