We start at the Market Cross at 4 pm, cover Kirkgate and the Beck Lane estate first, and reach Thwaite Road by about 7 pm. The route is pinned on the station door from 12 December.

If the pagers go, Santa has to leave in a hurry. We come back and finish the round as soon as we can.
