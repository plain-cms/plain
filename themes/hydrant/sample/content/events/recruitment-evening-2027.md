The evening runs alongside drill night, so you see the crew at work. There is no test and nothing to sign.

- 7 pm: tea, and a look round the engine
- 7.30 pm: watch the drill from the yard
- 8.30 pm: questions with the station officer and two of last year’s recruits

Employers are welcome too. Tell the secretary you are coming if you can: [secretary@marrowbyfire.example](mailto:secretary@marrowbyfire.example).
