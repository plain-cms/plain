Free to come in. The cake stall, the tombola and the hose-the-target game raise money for the moorland unit.

## On the day

- 11 am: doors open
- 12 noon: chip-pan demonstration
- 1 pm: the cadets’ ladder drill
- 2 pm: chip-pan demonstration
- 3 pm: cutting a car apart

Beck Lane is closed to parking. Use the school field on Kirkgate.
