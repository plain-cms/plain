Sallowfield Parish Council first met on 4 December 1894, in the schoolroom, after the Local Government Act of that year gave every rural parish an elected council. It covers the village of Sallowfield and the hamlet of Nether Coombe, about 1,140 people on the electoral roll.

## What the council looks after

- The King George V playing field, its pavilion and the play area
- Twenty-eight allotment plots at Coombe Lane
- Three bus shelters, eleven benches and the noticeboards on the Green and at Nether Coombe
- The war memorial and the churchyard wall
- The defibrillators at the Village Hall and in the old telephone box on Mill Road

It is also consulted on every planning application in the parish, and the planning committee sends its view to the district council.

## Who does what

| Service | Run by |
| --- | --- |
| Playing field, allotments, benches, bus shelters | Sallowfield Parish Council |
| Bins, recycling, planning decisions, council tax | Harnbury District Council |
| Roads, pavements, street lights, schools, libraries | Harnbury County Council |

## How it is paid for

The council raises a precept, a share of the council tax: £31,200 for 2026–27, or £56.40 a year for a Band D home. The accounts are checked by an internal auditor each spring and published here in July with the external auditor’s report.

## Openness

The agenda for each meeting is posted on both noticeboards and here three clear days before. Draft minutes follow within a fortnight and are approved at the next meeting. Councillors’ registers of interests are held by the district council.
