Every council meeting is open to the public and the press, and you need not book. The first fifteen minutes are public question time: each speaker has up to three minutes, and the chair may ask the clerk to reply in writing.

## Writing to the council

Write to the clerk by the Friday before a meeting if you would like a matter put on the agenda. Letters are reported to the next meeting; we do not publish your address.

## Standing for election

The ordinary elections of 2027 are on Thursday 6 May, when all seven seats are elected. You can stand if you are 18 or over, a British, Irish or Commonwealth citizen or a citizen of the EU, and you live, work or own land in the parish or within three miles of it. Councillors are not paid.

| Step | When |
| --- | --- |
| Nomination papers available from the district council | February 2027 |

| Polling day | Thursday 6 May 2027 |

## The vacant seat

One seat has been empty since Councillor Ward moved away in September. The council will co-opt a new member at its January meeting. Send a short letter saying why you would like to serve to the clerk by Friday 18 December.
