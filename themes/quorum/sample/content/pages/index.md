The parish council is the first tier of local government and the one closest to home. It meets in public every month, and every meeting begins with fifteen minutes in which anyone who lives or works in the parish may speak.

Bins, roads and schools are run by the district and county councils. If you are not sure who looks after something, ask the clerk and we will pass it on.
