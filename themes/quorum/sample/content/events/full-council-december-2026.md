Members of the public and the press are welcome. The agenda is posted on both noticeboards on Friday 4 December.

1. Public question time, fifteen minutes
2. Apologies for absence and declarations of interest
3. Minutes of the meeting of 10 November
4. Report from the district councillor
5. Draft budget and precept for 2027–28, first reading
6. Church Street bus shelter: choice of contractor
7. Allotments: water troughs at Coombe Lane
8. Payments for approval
9. Correspondence

Papers can be read at the Parish Office on Wednesday morning, or ask the clerk for a copy by e-mail.
