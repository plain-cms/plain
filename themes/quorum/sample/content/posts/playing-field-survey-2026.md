| Suggestion | Households in favour |
| --- | --- |

| A shelter for older children | 131 |
| Lights on the path from Coombe Lane | 118 |
| A second picnic table | 74 |
| An outdoor gym | 41 |

The council will cost the first three and report at its September meeting. Thank you to everyone who took part.
