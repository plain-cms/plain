No one asked for a by-election in the fourteen days allowed, so the council may fill the seat itself. Anyone who could stand for election may apply: see [Have your say](/join/) for who qualifies.

Write a short letter to the clerk saying who you are and why you would like to serve. Candidates will be invited to speak for three minutes at the January meeting before the vote. The seat is held until the elections on 6 May 2027.
