title: Church Street bus shelter to be replaced
date: 2026-08-18

The shelter is fenced off for now and the bus stop has moved twenty metres towards the Green. The replacement will be oak-framed, to match the shelter at Nether Coombe, and will keep the noticeboard on its side wall.
