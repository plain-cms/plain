In brief, the council:

- agreed to replace the Church Street bus shelter and asked for three quotes
- approved a grant of £350 to the Sallowfield Gardening Club for the planters on the Green
- commented in support of the application for two houses at Orchard End, with a condition on the hedge
- noted the resignation of Councillor Ward, and agreed to fill the seat by co-option

The full draft runs to six pages. Ask the clerk if you would like a copy by e-mail.
