title: Church Street closed for the Remembrance Sunday parade
date: 2026-11-02
description: Church Street will be closed between the Green and Mill Road from 10.30 to 11.30 am on Sunday 8 November for the parade and the two minutes’ silence.
tags:
  - notices
example: true
---

The parade forms up outside the Village Hall at 10.40 am and walks to the war memorial for the service at 10.55 am. The diversion is signed along Mill Road and Coombe Lane.

The council will lay a wreath on behalf of the parish. Anyone who would like to lay their own is welcome to join the line after the service.
