I'm Gwen Rhydderch. I trained for six years at a memorial workshop in Cumbria, came home to Bethesda in 2017 and set up on my own in the old smithy on Lôn Efail. Every letter that leaves here is drawn and cut by me, with a mallet and a chisel; nothing is sandblasted or machine-routed.

## What I cut

- Headstones, ashes tablets and memorial plaques
- House names and numbers
- Foundation stones, dedications and lettering for buildings
- Sundials, garden stones and alphabets
- Recutting and conserving old inscriptions, including gilding

Most work is in blue-grey Welsh slate from the valley. I also cut Portland and Purbeck stone, and Cumbrian green slate when a churchyard asks for it.

## How a commission goes

1. We talk about the wording, the stone and where it will stand. If you can, come to the workshop and see the slates.
2. I draw the lettering full size and send you the drawing. Nothing is cut until you are happy with every word.
3. I cut it — usually four to ten weeks after the drawing is agreed — and fix it, or deliver it ready to fix.

A house name starts at £320 for a slate of up to twelve letters. A headstone starts at £2,800, including the stone, the lettering and fixing within thirty miles. The first drawing costs nothing.

## Who for

Llanfeirion Community Council, the Dyffryn Ogwen Community Library Trust, Plas Gwyrddlas Garden Trust, Aberfelin Inshore Rescue, Cartrefi Ogwen Housing Co-operative, and many families across Gwynedd and Anglesey.
