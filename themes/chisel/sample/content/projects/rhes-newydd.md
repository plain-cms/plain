Ten slates, each 450 × 150 × 25 mm, between six and fourteen letters each, V-cut and painted in off-white.

## The brief

The co-operative had restored the whole of Rhes Newydd and wanted the terrace to look like one street again, while letting every household pick its own name.

## The work

One set of capitals, one size and one slate for all ten, with each name centred on the same line so the row reads level from the road. Two of the names — Tŷ'r Ysgol and Bryn Awel — needed the circumflex and the apostrophe drawn into the letters rather than cut as an afterthought.

## In place

All ten were fixed on one morning in July. A neighbour from the next terrace has since ordered her own.
