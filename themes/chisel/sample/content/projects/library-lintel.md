Four slate panels, each 1,000 × 300 × 50 mm, carrying fifty-two words in Welsh and English, V-cut and left unpainted.

## The brief

The trust had bought Capel Salem for a pound and turned it into a library. They wanted the building to say so from the street, in both languages, without anyone mistaking it for a chapel still.

## The work

The verse was chosen by a vote of the library's readers. I set it out in two lines of capitals, Welsh above and English below, at 70 mm and 45 mm, so the Welsh reads first from across the road. The four panels were cut flat on the banker over seven weeks and fixed on stainless pins in two days from a tower scaffold.

## In place

The panels went up the week before the library opened. Children from the school came to see the last letter cut, and the library now stamps the verse inside every book it lends.
