A round slate dial, 600 mm across and 40 mm thick, on a stone pillar, with hour lines, numerals and a motto cut and gilded.

## The brief

The trust's volunteers had found the pillar of the garden's old sundial under a compost heap. The dial itself was long gone. They wanted a new one that told the right time in that exact spot.

## The work

The hour lines were calculated for the garden's latitude and longitude and drawn out full size before a letter was cut. The motto round the edge — "Amser i hau, amser i fedi", a time to sow, a time to reap — was suggested by the head gardener.

## In place

The dial was set on the old pillar at noon on the day of the garden's reopening, and was four minutes fast. The gnomon was moved by a hair the next morning.
