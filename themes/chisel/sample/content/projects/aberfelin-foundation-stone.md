Portland stone, 750 × 450 × 100 mm, with 142 letters, V-cut and painted in black.

## The brief

The crew had raised the money for a new boathouse themselves over eleven years. The stone had to name the people who did it, stand up to salt spray, and be readable from the slipway.

## The work

I chose Portland for its whiteness against the dark brick of the new building, and cut the letters deeper than usual so that the spray would not round them off. The names of the fundraising committee run in a single line along the foot of the stone.

## In place

The stone was laid on a grey morning in March by Morfudd Price, who joined the crew in 1987.
