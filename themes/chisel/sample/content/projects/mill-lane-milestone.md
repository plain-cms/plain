A slate milestone of about 1825, 1,100 mm high, with the distances to Bangor and Capel Curig.

When the lane was widened the milestone was lifted and reset four metres back from the road. The heritage society asked whether the distances, worn almost flat, could be read again. I recut only what could still be traced — the figures and the two place names — and left the rest of the stone as weather had made it.
