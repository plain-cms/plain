A slate tablet of 1921, 1,500 × 900 mm, with 61 names, fixed inside the porch of Capel Bethel, Llanfeirion.

## The brief

A century of damp had lifted most of the gold, and a local history group had found four men from the village who were never listed. The council wanted them added in a way that did not pretend to be 1921.

## The work

I cleaned the tablet in place, recut the shallowest letters by eye to their original depth, and laid new 23½-carat gold leaf into every letter. The four names went into the space below the last column, in the same capitals but left ungilded, with a single line beneath saying when they were added.

## In place

The tablet was unveiled again on Remembrance Sunday. Relatives of two of the four men came from Liverpool for it.
