Welsh slate, 1,200 × 450 × 40 mm: the twenty-nine letters of the Welsh alphabet, the five English letters it lacks, and the numerals, V-cut and unpainted.

Every commission starts from a drawing, and every drawing starts from the same set of capitals I have refined since my apprenticeship. This stone is that set, cut once, at 40 mm, so that clients can see the letters before anything is drawn for them.

The digraphs — Ch, Dd, Ff, Ng, Ll, Ph, Rh, Th — were the hardest part. Each had to read as one letter and still look like two.

It now hangs on the workshop wall beside the door.
