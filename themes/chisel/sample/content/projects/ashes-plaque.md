Welsh slate, 380 × 280 × 30 mm, with a name, two dates and one line of a hymn, V-cut and painted in grey.

The churchyard had no room left for headstones, but the old boundary wall had been set aside for ashes. The parish asked that every plaque in it be the same size, so the work was all in the lettering: a name long enough to need two lines and a hymn line short enough to need only one.
