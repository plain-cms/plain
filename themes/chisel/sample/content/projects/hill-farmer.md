Welsh slate, 900 × 600 × 75 mm, with a rubbed finish and 96 letters, V-cut and lightly painted in white.

## The brief

The family wanted the farm's name on the stone as large as his own. It was, they said, the thing he would have wanted read first.

## The work

I set his name and the farm's name in capitals of the same height, with his dates in smaller numerals between them, so the stone reads as two names held together by a life. The back of the stone is left riven, as it came from the quarry.

## In place

It stands in the chapel graveyard at Gerlan, looking up the valley towards the farm.
