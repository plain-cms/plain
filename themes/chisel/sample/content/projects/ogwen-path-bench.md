Welsh slate, 300 × 100 × 15 mm, with 38 letters, V-cut and unpainted, let into the backrest of an oak bench.

## The brief

The Friends wanted a plaque that would last outdoors for as long as the bench, and that would be read rather than glanced at.

## The work

Thirty-eight letters is very few, so each one could be large. I cut them at 20 mm, the biggest the slate would take, and left the slate unpainted so that rain would darken it evenly with the oak.

## In place

The bench faces Carnedd Dafydd, at the bend of the path above the river.
