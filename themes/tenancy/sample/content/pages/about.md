## Who we are

**Margaret Lowther** spent twenty years managing council housing in South Lakeland before she started letting the flat above her own shop. **Sam Pike** joined in 2016 from a builder's merchant and does the inspections, the inventories and most of the viewings. There is nobody else, and we like it that way: you will deal with one of us from the first viewing to the day you hand the keys back.

## How we work for tenants

- **No fees.** You pay rent and a deposit, nothing more. A holding deposit of one week's rent keeps a place for you while we take references; it comes off your first month.
- **Deposits** are five weeks' rent and are protected in a government-approved scheme within ten days of you paying it.
- **Viewings** are always with one of us, never a key left under a mat. We do evenings on Tuesdays and Thursdays until 19:00.
- **Repairs** go to us, not to the landlord. Ring the office, or the out-of-hours number we give you at move-in.

## How we work for landlords

| Service | What it covers | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Let only | Advertising, viewings, references, the tenancy agreement and the deposit registration | £450 per tenancy |
| Full management | All of the above, plus rent collection, repairs, two inspections a year and the check-out | 10% of the rent |

We keep a short list of landlords and turn some away. If a flat needs work before it can be let, we will tell you what and why, and wait until it is done.

## The cabinet

Every key we hold hangs in the cabinet behind the counter, on its own numbered hook. A lilac tag means the place is free to let, amber means a tenant has been accepted, grey means it is gone. It is how we run the office, and it is how this website is laid out.
