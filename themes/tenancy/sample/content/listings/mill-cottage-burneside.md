One of the stone cottages built for the paper mill, on the quiet end of Hall Road.

**The facts:** two bedrooms, both doubles; a sitting room with an open fire; a kitchen-diner; a bathroom on the ground floor at the back. Oil-fired central heating, EPC band E. Unfurnished. A small front garden and a parking space beside the house.

**Rent and deposit:** £825 per month; deposit £951. The train to Kendal takes four minutes.

**The drawback:** the bathroom is downstairs, through the kitchen, and the heating runs on oil, which costs more than gas.
