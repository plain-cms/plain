We have one rule, and it does all our marketing: **every listing tells
you the catch.** The downstairs bathroom, the Saturday-morning hum, the
trains you'll hear from the garden — it's in our particulars, in writing,
before you've booked a viewing.

## Buying

[Everything on our books](/listings/) is current to the hour: when a
property goes under offer, its flag changes the same day. Tell us what
you're actually after — the real list, not the portal filters — and
you'll hear about houses before the board goes up.

## Renting

We let a few flats for owners we know, and the rule holds there too: the
listing says what the rent is, what the deposit is, and what is wrong
with the place. A flat flagged To let can still be viewed; Let agreed
means a tenant is on the way in.
