A Victorian two-up two-down at the tannery end of Tanner's Row, reduced
from €265,000 in May. It has been with us ten weeks, and we'd rather tell
you why than have you wonder.

**The facts:** two double bedrooms, bathroom upstairs, sitting room with
the original fireplace, and a kitchen at the back opening to a paved
yard that gets the sun until mid-afternoon. Roof renewed 2021, gas boiler
new last winter, rewired 2016. Freehold.

**The area:** five minutes on foot to the market square, three to the
river path. The corner shop is open until ten, and the 3 bus runs along
Mill Road at the end of the street.

**The catch, honestly:** the kitchen is 1990s and small, and the survey
from a sale that fell through found damp in the rear wall — a failed
gutter, since fixed, with the paperwork to show it. The wall needs
replastering; our builder's quote is €3,400 and you can read it before
you view.
