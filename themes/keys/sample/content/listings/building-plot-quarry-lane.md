The last gap on Quarry Lane: a level plot between two 1950s houses, sold
with full planning permission for a single three-bedroom house.

**The facts:** 610 m², roughly 17 metres of frontage by 36 deep. Permission
granted in March and good for five years, for a two-storey house of 132 m²
with parking for two. Water, mains drainage and electricity are at the
boundary. The architect's drawings and the soil report are included in the
sale, and both are in the office to read before you view.

**The area:** Quarry Lane is a quiet no-through road on the south side of
town. The primary school is eight minutes on foot, and the 11 bus stops at
the bottom of the lane.

**The catch, honestly:** the permission ties you to the approved design,
give or take the windows; a different house means a new application. The
plot slopes gently to the back, so allow for a retaining wall — the soil
report puts a number on it.
