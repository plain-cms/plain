The first floor over the bookshop, with its own door on Chandler Street
and a shop that closes at six for a downstairs neighbour.

**The facts:** two double bedrooms, a bathroom with a bath, and a
kitchen-living room across the front with two sash windows. Gas central
heating, boiler replaced in 2024. To let unfurnished from 1 August on a
twelve-month tenancy; the deposit is one month's rent. The owner lives
two streets away and has let the flat through us for six years.

**The area:** Chandler Street — the bakery opens at 7, the Saturday market
is two minutes on foot, and the 3 and 11 buses stop at the corner.

**The catch, honestly:** no parking and no outside space, and the stairs
are the only way up. The front bedroom hears the street until the cafés
close, around eleven.
