A stone cottage of about 1880 on the lane behind the church, two minutes
on foot from the square and quieter than that sounds.

**The facts:** two double bedrooms, a bathroom upstairs refitted in 2021,
a sitting room with a wood stove, and a kitchen opening to a walled garden
of about 9 metres. Gas boiler serviced every year; roof re-slated in 2018.
Freehold.

**The area:** the bakery and the Saturday market are two minutes away, the
primary school is six, and the 3 bus stops at the end of the lane.

**The catch, honestly:** the stairs are steep and narrow, as they are in
every cottage on the lane, and there is no parking space of its own — the
permit bays on Church Street are a minute's walk.
