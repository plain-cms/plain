Selling a house with us takes four steps, and you can stop after any of
them without owing a cent.

## The valuation

We come to the house for about an hour, look at it the way a buyer's
surveyor will, and give you a price in writing with our reasons. It is
free, and sometimes the advice is to wait.

## The listing

We photograph the house as it is, write the particulars — the catch
included — and put the board up the same week.

## The fee

One and a half per cent of the sale price plus VAT, due on completion.
No sale, no fee.
