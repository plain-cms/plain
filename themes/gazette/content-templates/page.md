Local advertisers keep this paper free to read. An advertisement runs
beside the news for a week at a time, and every booking is confirmed by a
person, not a form.

## Sizes and prices

| Size | Where it runs | Price per week |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Notice | Under the briefs | £25 |
| Quarter | Beside a story | £60 |
| Half | Under the lead story | £110 |

## Deadlines

Copy is due by noon on Wednesday for the week that follows. We can set a
simple advertisement for you at no extra cost.

## The one rule

Advertising buys space in the paper. It does not buy coverage in it.
