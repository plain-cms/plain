## News tips

Seen something? Ring the newsdesk on [01632 960123](tel:+441632960123) or
write to [newsdesk@example.com](mailto:newsdesk@example.com). A reporter
reads everything, usually the same day. If it needs to stay confidential,
say so in the first line and we will take it from there.

## Letters

Letters for publication go to
[letters@example.com](mailto:letters@example.com) and should include your
name and street; the street is all we print. We edit for length, never for
opinion.

## Corrections

We publish corrections at the same prominence as the error. If we got a
fact wrong, tell us plainly:
[corrections@example.com](mailto:corrections@example.com).

## Advertising

Local advertisers keep this paper free to read. For rates and deadlines,
write to [ads@example.com](mailto:ads@example.com) or ring
[01632 960124](tel:+441632960124). Advertising buys space in the paper. It
does not buy coverage in it, and the person who answers cannot change that.

## Find us

on.
