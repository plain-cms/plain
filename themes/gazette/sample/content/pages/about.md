The Westhaven Gazette has covered the town, the harbour and the coast as far
as Pelham Point since 2019. It is written by four reporters from an office
above the chandlery on Chandler Street, and it is free to read.

## Who writes it

- **Rosa Imrie**, editor — the council, the harbour board and the lifeboat
  station.
- **Ana Serrano** — business, housing and the estuary.
- **Dan Okafor** — culture, and whatever the Grouter has tiled this week.
- **Micky Deane** — sport and schools.

## Who pays for it

Readers and local advertisers, in roughly equal parts. The paper is
published by Westhaven Community Press, a society owned by its 640
reader-members, each with one vote. Advertising is taken under one rule:
it buys space in the paper, never coverage in it.

## The rules we report under

- **Names and places are real.** If a source needs anonymity, we say so and
  say why.
- **Corrections run where the error ran.** A front-page mistake gets a
  front-page correction, at the same size.
- **We do not publish what we cannot stand behind.** A story a day late and
  right beats one on time and wrong.
