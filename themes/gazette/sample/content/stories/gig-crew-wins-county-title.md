WESTHAVEN — Westhaven Pilot Gig Club won the county women’s title on Sunday
for the first time in fifteen years, by half a length, in a boat that
belongs to somebody else.

The club’s own gig, *Sorrel*, split a plank on the slipway on Friday
evening. Pelham Point, the defending champions and the crew’s oldest
rivals, offered their second boat within the hour. On Sunday afternoon
Westhaven rowed it past them in the last two hundred metres of a
two-mile course.

“They lent us the boat and we beat them in it,” said coxswain Jo Trevena.
“I have apologised. I did not mean it.”

The six rowers include two sixth-formers, a district nurse and a
harbour-board electrician, and train at 6am three mornings a week. The
average age of the crew is 34; the average age of *Sorrel* is 41.

Pelham Point’s captain, Ruth Abbey, said the loan stood for as long as the
repair took. “We would rather lose to them than win a race they were not
in,” she said. *Sorrel* is due back on the water in a fortnight.
