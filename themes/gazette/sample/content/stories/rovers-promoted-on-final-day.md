WESTHAVEN — Promotion arrived twice on Saturday: once at 4.47pm, when Jonah
Osei rose at the far post to make it 2–1, and again at 5.09pm, when the
radio confirmed that Carden Vale had drawn and 3,100 people who had been
pretending not to listen all screamed at once.

Rovers finish second and go up to the regional premier division for the
first time since 1998. The margin over Vale was a single point, assembled
across a season that included a nine-game unbeaten run in winter and, less
gloriously, March.

Manager Carla Reyes, who took the job in 2023 with the club eighteenth,
declined the champagne on the pitch. “We were part-time when I came. We’re
part-time now. Every one of these players went to work on Monday mornings
and trained on Tuesday nights. Pour it on them.”

Osei, a plasterer, was back on a site in Pelham Point by 8am on Monday. The
club shop had sold out of scarves by Sunday lunchtime.
