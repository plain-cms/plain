When I am home on leave you can ring [01632 960418](tel:+441632960418), any day between nine in the morning and six in the evening.

I live in Lerwick, Shetland, and join ships from Aberdeen, Lerwick or any port the operator flies me to.

Tell me the ship, the trade, the rotation and the date you need someone to join. Copies of my certificates and my discharge book can follow by e-mail.
