Skerry Endeavour carries pipe, containers, fuel, water, drilling mud and brine out to the fields east of Shetland and brings the backload home. Most of the work is done on DP, holding position a few metres from a platform leg while the crane works the deck.

## The deck and the plan

Every load-out starts with a deck plan the night before: what goes where, what comes off first, and what the ship looks like when the weather turns half-way through. I sign off the plan, the stability and the lashing before we sail, and I am on deck for every lift at the installation.

## Handing over a DP watch

A DP watch changes hands in the middle of an operation, so the checklist I wrote for it is short: the reference systems in use, the alarms of the last hour, the worst-case failure and the escape route. The operator adopted it fleet-wide in 2023.
