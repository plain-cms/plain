Noss Head sails from Aberdeen at five in the evening and reaches Lerwick at half past seven the next morning, with a call at Kirkwall in the middle of the night. The ship loads in under two hours, so the deck plan has to be right the first time.

In winter the Pentland Firth and the Fair Isle channel set the timetable as much as the owners do. Lashing for a beam sea on a full freight deck is the part of the job I know best.
