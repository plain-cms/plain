## Hymns at Dusk (2024)

Twelve hymn tunes and two marches, recorded in St Wilfrid's Church on two
winter evenings with the heating off. Start with *Deep Harmony*.

- [The band shop](https://shop.example.com) — download or CD by post; buying here pays the band most
- CDs £10 at every concert, from the table by the door

## Pellow Park (2019)

The bandstand favourites: marches, a waltz, two film themes and the
cornet solo *Shepherd's Song*, played by our principal cornet, Daniel
Okoro. Start with the march *Brackenford*, written for the band's
centenary in 1991.

- [The band shop](https://shop.example.com)
- [Listen online](https://listen.example.com)
