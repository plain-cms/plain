We're Brackenford Silver Band: twenty-eight players aged fourteen to
eighty-one, one band room on Mill Lane, and a march card for every street
in town.

## This Christmas

The Christmas concert at St Wilfrid's is on Saturday 12 December. Tickets
are £12, under-16s £5, from the link on the date or from Hollins
Newsagents on Bridge Street. On Saturday 19 December we play carols round
the tree in the Market Square from four o'clock: bring a voice, the words
are on sheets.

[Every date, with tickets](/shows/)

## Summer on the bandstand

The season opens in Pellow Park on Sunday 2 May. From then until the last
Sunday in August we're on the bandstand most Sunday afternoons, three till
five. The deckchairs are free; the tea hut is not.

## Book the band

Fêtes, galas, weddings, civic services and the odd retirement — a quartet
or the full band, marching or seated. What a booker needs is on the
[press page](/press/), and our two CDs are on the [music page](/music/).

## Come and play

We rehearse on Tuesdays and Thursdays from half past seven in the band
room. Learners start in the Junior Band on Saturday mornings, and we lend
instruments.
