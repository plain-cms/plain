## In one line

Brackenford Silver Band is a twenty-eight-strong community brass band from
Brackenford, playing since 1891.

## In fifty words

Brackenford Silver Band has played the town's parades, bandstand and
Christmas since 1891. Twenty-eight players, aged fourteen to eighty-one,
rehearse twice a week on Mill Lane under musical director Ruth Hallam. The
band competes in the Second Section, runs a Junior Band, and has made two
CDs, *Pellow Park* and *Hymns at Dusk*.

## Photos

Press photos of the band in uniform, seated and marching, are sent by
e-mail on request.

## On the day

- Seated: a space about eight metres by five, or a bandstand, and 28 chairs without arms
- Marching: a route plan and a steward at the front; we march at 112 paces a minute
- Somewhere dry for cases, and parking for the van with the percussion
- We bring our own stands, music and pegs for windy days
