## In one line

Who the band is, how many play, and where it is from.

## In fifty words

Who the band is, when it began, what it plays and where it can be heard. Write only what is true; leave out a length the facts do not fill.

## Photos

Press photos are sent by e-mail on request.

## On the day

- How much space the band needs to sit or stand
- Chairs, cover from rain, a power socket
- Parking for the van or the coach
