## How the firm began

Margaret Pellow set up on her own in 2009 after twelve years in a large practice, where she found that private inventors were being quoted fees meant for multinationals. Daniel Strand joined in 2014 to run trade marks and designs, and the firm moved into the old Ropeworks by the canal two years later.

## How we work

The attorney you meet first is the attorney who drafts your application and answers your calls until it is granted. We write to you in plain English, send a copy of every letter from the patent office with a note on what it means, and quote before we start anything that costs more than half an hour.

## Memberships and insurance

Margaret and Anika are Fellows of the Chartered Institute of Patent Attorneys and European Patent Attorneys; Daniel is a Fellow of the Chartered Institute of Trade Mark Attorneys. The firm holds professional indemnity insurance of £5 million for each claim.

Our complaints procedure and terms of business are sent with every engagement letter, and on request.
