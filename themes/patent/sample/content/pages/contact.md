## What to bring

Whatever shows how the invention works: sketches, a prototype, photographs, a parts list or a page of notes. For a trade mark, bring the name or logo as you use it now and a list of the goods you sell or plan to sell. Nothing you show us leaves the meeting room without your agreement.

## Finding us

The Ropeworks is the long brick building on the towpath side of Canal Street, five minutes' walk from Wrexley station. Our door is at the far end, under the clock; ring the bell marked Unit 6.

## Parking and access

There are four visitor spaces in the yard in front of the building, and a pay-and-display car park on Mill Lane. Our office is on the ground floor with level access throughout and an accessible toilet.

## Meeting by video

If you would rather not travel, the first meeting can be held by video. We send a link with the appointment and ask you to post or e-mail sketches beforehand.
