## Who we act for

Private inventors, university spin-outs, start-ups and manufacturers with fewer than two hundred staff, from Wrexley, Prestholme and the towns of the Lune valley. Most of our clients are engineers; many have never met a patent attorney before.

## Before you show anyone

Keep an invention to yourself until it is filed. A demonstration at a trade show, a crowdfunding page or a video online can stop it being patented at all. If you have to show it to a manufacturer or an investor first, ask us for a confidentiality agreement.

## What we do not take on

Court litigation, and patents for medicines and biotechnology. When those come up we give you the name of a firm that does them well.

## After the first meeting

You get a letter within five working days: what can be protected, what we suggest doing first, our fee for it and the official fees on top. Nothing is charged until you have agreed it in writing.
