The application is the one document that decides what a patent covers, so most of the fee is the attorney's time spent understanding the invention and writing claims that a competitor cannot easily design around.

## How it runs

Two meetings, a first draft within three weeks, a second after your comments, then filing. From the filing date you have twelve months to decide whether to go abroad.
