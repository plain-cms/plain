Most people come to us with a working prototype, a sketch or a nagging idea that someone must have thought of it already. All three are good starting points.

## What happens

We ask how the invention works, what problem it solves and whether you have shown it to anyone. You leave knowing whether a patent is likely to be worth having, or whether a design registration or a trade mark would serve you better.
