---
title: Registering a product design
description: The shape and look of a product registered in the UK for up to twenty-five years, often the quickest protection a manufacturer can get.
fee: From £380
points:
  - Advice on whether a design registration fits the product
  - Representations prepared from your photographs or CAD views
  - Filing in the UK, and in the EU where you sell
order: 80
example: true
---

A registered design protects how a product looks, not how it works. It is cheap, quick and often granted within weeks, which makes it a good companion to a patent application or a stand-in where a patent is out of reach.
