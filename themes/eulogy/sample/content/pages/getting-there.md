St Petroc’s stands at the top of Church Street, in the middle of Otterbrook. The village hall, where the tea is, is five minutes’ walk downhill on Mill Lane.

## By train

Tiverford Parkway is the nearest station, six miles away. Trains from Bristol and London stop there about once an hour. The last train towards Bristol leaves at 21.12.

From the station, Otterbrook Cars meet trains if you book ahead on [01632 960275](tel:+441632960275); the fare to the church is about £16. The 373 bus leaves the station forecourt at twenty past each hour and stops at the church gate.

## By car

Please park at the village hall on Mill Lane, where there is room for sixty cars, and walk up to the church. The few spaces beside the church are kept for anyone who finds walking hard; there is a steward on the gate from one o’clock.

## Without steps

The south door has a ramp and the church is level inside. The village hall is level throughout, with an accessible toilet. If you would like a lift from the hall to the church, tell us when you reply.
