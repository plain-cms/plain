Everyone is welcome at the service; you do not need to be asked. We only ask you to tell us three things: whether you are coming to the tea, how many of you there will be, and whether you need one of the parking spaces by the church.

Rather talk? Ring Tom, Ruth’s son, on [01632 960418](tel:+441632960418), any evening.

## Cards

Cards can be left at the back of the church on the day, or sent to Tom Ellacott, Holly Cottage, Mill Lane, Otterbrook EX16 9RX.

## Memories of Ruth

We are gathering stories of Ruth for her great-grandchildren. If you have one, or a photograph, or a school report she wrote, bring it on the day: there is a book at the back of the church and a table for pictures in the hall.

## Giving in her memory

In place of flowers, Ruth asked for gifts to the Otterbrook School library. There is a retiring collection at the service, and cheques can be made out to Otterbrook School Parents’ Association and sent to Tom.
