Ruth taught at Otterbrook Primary School for thirty-four years, swam in the sea every Sunday until she was eighty-three, and grew more runner beans than the village could eat. She died at home on Saturday 24 October 2026, with her family around her.

Everyone who knew her is welcome. Some of you are coming a long way: [Getting there](/getting-there/) has the trains, the parking and places to stay, and [Join us](/join/) says how to let us know you are coming, so that there is tea enough for all.
