The service lasts about fifty minutes. The church is open from a quarter past one, and the family will be at the south door from half past.

## Flowers

Family flowers only, please. If you would like to give something, there is a retiring collection for the Otterbrook School library, which Ruth started in 1971 with a shelf of her own books.

## Children

Children are very welcome, at the service and at the tea. There are crayons and paper at the back of the church, and no one will mind a little noise.

## If you cannot be there

The service is being recorded. Ask Tom for a copy and he will send it to you, with the sheet of hymns and readings.
