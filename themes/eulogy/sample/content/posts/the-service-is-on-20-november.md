Thank you for all your cards, calls and kindness since Ruth died. The service of thanksgiving is at St Petroc’s at 2 pm on Friday 20 November 2026, led by the Revd Philippa Gale.

Afterwards there is tea in the village hall. Ruth chose the hymns herself some years ago, and the [order of service](/programme/) has them.
