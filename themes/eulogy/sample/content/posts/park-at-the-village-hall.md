St Petroc’s has room for only eight cars. Please leave them for those who find walking hard, and park at the village hall on Mill Lane, where the tea is afterwards. It is five minutes’ walk up Church Street to the church.

If you need one of the spaces by the church, say so when you reply and a steward will keep one for you.
