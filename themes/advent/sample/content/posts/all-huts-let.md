The last hut went on Wednesday, to a cheesemaker from Low Fold Farm. That makes forty-two, the most the Market Place will hold with room for the choirs at the cross.

Nine of this year’s stallholders are new to Keldwick, among them a glassblower, two potters and a family who make mustard. Thorpe’s Toffee keeps its hut beside the cross, as it has since the first market in 1994.

If you missed out this year, the waiting list for next year is open at the market office.
