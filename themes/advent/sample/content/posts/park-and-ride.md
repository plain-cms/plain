The Market Place is closed to cars on every market day, so this year all visitors’ parking is at the auction mart on Moor Road.

It is £3 a car, paid at the barrier, and the bus into town is free. It runs every fifteen minutes and drops you at the Market Cross. [Getting there](/getting-there/) has the times and the step-free way round the cobbles.
