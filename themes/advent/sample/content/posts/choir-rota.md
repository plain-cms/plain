Six choirs will take turns on the Market Cross steps this year, twenty minutes on the hour from 11 am until the huts close.

On the last afternoon, Sunday 20 December, all six sing together at four o’clock. Song sheets are at the information hut, so bring your voice.

The [programme](/programme/) lists every choir. The rota for each day goes up at the information hut on the morning.
