The huts are open every Saturday from 10 am to 7 pm and every Sunday from 11 am to 5 pm, from 28 November to 20 December. Times at the cross can move by a few minutes when a choir is late off the bus.

## The choirs

A different choir sings on the hour, every hour, for twenty minutes. Between them they cover the four weekends, and the rota for the day is pinned up at the information hut by the cross.

- Keldwick Community Choir
- St Oswald’s Church Choir
- Swale Valley Male Voice Choir
- Keldwick Primary School Choir, Saturdays only
- The Wednesday Singers
- Dales Ladies’ Choir

If your choir would like a turn, see [Join us](/join/).

## If it snows

The market opens in snow; the huts have roofs and the Council grits the Market Place by eight. In a gale or on ice the huts stay shut, and a notice goes on the Market Cross and at the Corn Exchange door by nine that morning.

## Father Christmas

He is in the Old Corn Exchange from 12 noon to 4 pm on every market day. It is £5 a child, with a small present, and there is no booking: queue at the Church Street door. The grotto is on the ground floor and has a step-free way in.
