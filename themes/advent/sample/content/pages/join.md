Nobody needs a ticket to come to the market. To take part in it, talk to Helen Garbutt at the market office in the Town Hall: [market@keldwick.example](mailto:market@keldwick.example) or [01632 960384](tel:+441632960384), weekdays from 9 till 4.

## Have a hut

All forty-two huts for this year are let. Bookings for next year open on 1 March, and the waiting list is kept at the market office.

| Hut | One weekend | All four weekends |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Makers and growers | £180 | £560 |
| Hot food and drink | £260 | £840 |
| Charities and town groups | £40 | £120 |

Every hut has a light, two sockets and a counter. You bring the stock, the float and something warm to stand on.

## Bring a choir

Each choir sings for twenty minutes from the Market Cross steps. We give every singer a cup of mulled wine or spiced apple afterwards. Tell Helen which weekend suits you and how many you will be.

## Lend a hand

We need stewards on the Moor Road barrier and at the information hut, in two-hour turns. A steward’s coat, a hot drink and a mince pie are provided.
