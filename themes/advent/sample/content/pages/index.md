Every Advent since 1994 the Market Place has filled with wooden huts, and the town has come out to buy its toffee, its wreaths and its presents from the people who make them. The market is run by Keldwick Town Council and the Chamber of Trade, and the huts are let to makers and growers from the dales.

Come for an hour or for the afternoon. The choirs sing from the Market Cross steps on the hour, every hour, and the lights stay on until seven on Saturdays. [Getting there](/getting-there/) has the park and ride and the buses.
