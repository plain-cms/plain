The Market Place is closed to cars on market days, from 8 am until the huts are packed away. Please do not try to park in the town: there is room for no more than the residents.

## Park and ride

Park at Keldwick Auction Mart on Moor Road, signed from the A-road at both ends of the town. It costs £3 a car, paid as you come in, and the bus to the Market Cross is free. Buses run every fifteen minutes, from 9:30 am to 7:30 pm on Saturdays and from 10:30 am to 5:30 pm on Sundays.

## By bus

The 156 from Ashby Moor station stops in Church Street, two minutes from the cross. It runs every hour, at twenty past, and the last bus back leaves Church Street at 7:20 pm on Saturdays and 5:20 pm on Sundays.

## Without steps

The Market Place is cobbled. For a level way round, keep to the flagstone pavement on the north side, by the Corn Exchange, and come into the market down Church Street. Four blue-badge spaces are kept at the top of Church Street; tell the steward at the Moor Road barrier.

Toilets, one of them accessible, are in the Old Corn Exchange. There is a changing table there too.
