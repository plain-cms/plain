## A heading for each question people ask

*Short paragraphs. Where there are sums (what a gift buys, where the money
went) use a table with the figure in the first column, 12 characters or
fewer; the theme sets that column as large figures.*

*For the donate page, add `donateCta: true` above: once `donateUrl` is set
in `data/impact.json`, the page opens with a Give online button. Always give
an e-mail address or a phone number as a link, so there is a way to reach
you without the button.*
