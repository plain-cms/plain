title: Bring the 14 bus back to every half hour
description: After six in the evening the 14 now runs once an hour. Shift workers at the harbour and the hospital are the ones left waiting.
example: true
---

In 2023 the borough cut its support for the evening 14 and the operator
halved the service. The last bus to Saltings now leaves the hospital at
22:10.

**What it changes:** a half-hourly 14 until eleven puts the harbour, the
hospital and the station within reach of a night shift without a car.

**What happens next:** the support contract comes up for renewal in the
autumn of 2027. I will ask for the evening journeys to be priced back in
before it is signed, not after.

**What it needs:** about £64,000 a year from the borough's transport
budget, a little under what it spent on consultants for the new car park.
