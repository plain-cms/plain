title: Deliver a leaflet round
description: One bag, one street, about an hour. Forty volunteers have walked Saltings since March 2026; four streets still have nobody.
example: true
---

Every home in Saltings gets three leaflets before polling day. That is
12,300 letterboxes, and every one of them is walked.

**What it changes:** a household that never sees a candidate still reads
what each pledge would cost and who decides it.

**What happens next:** rounds go out from 9 Ropewalk every Saturday at ten
and every Wednesday at six. Anjali gives you a bag and a map with your
street marked.

**What it needs:** an hour at a walk. Quay Street, Chandler's Row, Mast
Lane and the top of Ropewalk still need someone.
