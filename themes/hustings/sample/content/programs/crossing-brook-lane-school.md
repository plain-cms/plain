title: A crossing outside Brook Lane School
description: A zebra crossing where 340 children cross the coast road twice a day, after eleven years of the council saying it will look at it.
example: true
---

Brook Lane School sits on the coast road, where the limit is 30 and the
traffic does 40. A lollipop warden covers the morning only.

**What it changes:** 340 children, and the parents walking them, cross on
a zebra with beacons instead of waiting for a gap.

**What happens next:** the county highways team has already surveyed the
site twice. A ward councillor can put it on the area committee's list for
the next budget round, which is where it has never been.

**What it needs:** about £38,000 from the county's road safety budget,
and a councillor who asks for it at every meeting until it is built.
