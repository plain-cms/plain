description: Each ward gets £15,000 a year to spend locally. Saltings has never been told where its share went. I will publish every line, every quarter.
example: true

Every ward councillor in Wrenmouth controls a small local budget for
grants and repairs. Nobody in Saltings could tell me what it was spent on.

**What it changes:** you can see every grant and every repair paid for in
this ward, and ask about any of it.

**What happens next:** a page in the library and on this site, updated
every quarter, starting the first month after the election.

**What it needs:** an afternoon every three months, and nothing from the
budget itself.
