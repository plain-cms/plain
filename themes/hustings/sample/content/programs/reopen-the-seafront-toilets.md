title: Reopen the seafront toilets at Saltings Steps
description: The only public toilets on the front closed in 2023 to save £9,000 a year. Reopened, run with the beach café, from Easter to October.
example: true
---

The toilets at Saltings Steps closed to save money. Since then the beach
café has had a queue at its door and a sign that says customers only.

**What it changes:** families, older walkers and anyone with a medical
condition can spend a day on the front again.

**What happens next:** the beach café has offered to open and clean them
in return for a reduced rent. The borough has never answered the offer.

**What it needs:** about £4,500 a season once the café's offer is taken,
half of what the closure saved.
