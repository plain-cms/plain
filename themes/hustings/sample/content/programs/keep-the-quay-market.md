---
title: Keep the Thursday market on the quay
description: The borough wants the Thursday market in the new car park. Twenty-two traders and the cafés behind them want it to stay where it is.
example: true
---

The Thursday market has stood on the quay since 1887. The borough's plan
moves it to the top deck of the new car park, out of sight of the harbour.

**What it changes:** twenty-two stalls stay where people walk past them,
and the cafés on Quay Street keep their busiest day.

**What happens next:** the move goes to the planning committee in June
2027. I will speak against it there and ask for the traders to be heard
first.

**What it needs:** no money at all, which is the point. Only a vote.
