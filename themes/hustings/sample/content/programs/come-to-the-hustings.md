A hustings is the one evening in a campaign when every candidate stands in
the same room and answers the same questions. Ours is at St Brendan's Hall,
Quay Street, on Thursday 22 April 2027, from 7pm to 9pm.

**What it changes:** you hear every candidate on your street's problem,
not only the one who knocked on your door.

**What happens next:** write your question on a card at the door or send
it before the night. The chair, from the Wrenmouth Civic Society, picks the
order.

**What it needs:** a full hall. Doors open at 6.30, the hall is step-free,
and there is tea.
