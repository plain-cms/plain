---
title: Put a board in your window or garden
description: A violet correx board in a front window tells a street that someone here is voting. Thirty-one are up and nine are waiting for a window.
example: true
---

Boards win nothing on their own, but a street with three of them is a
street where people start to ask who the candidate is.

**What it changes:** your neighbours see that this is a real contest, not
a seat that was decided before anyone voted.

**What happens next:** e-mail your address and Peter brings a board and a
stake, or collect one from the Thursday stall on the quay.

**What it needs:** a window or a patch of front garden, until the day
after polling day, when Peter collects it again.
