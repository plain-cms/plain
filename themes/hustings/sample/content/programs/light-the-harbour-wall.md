---
title: Light the harbour wall again
description: Fourteen lamps on the harbour wall have been dark since a storm in 2022. The walk home along the water should not be in the dark.
example: true
---

The lamps went out in the storm of February 2022 and the borough's own
survey says the cabling needs replacing. Nothing has happened since.

**What it changes:** the half-mile path from the station to Saltings Steps
is lit after dark again, for the first time since 2022.

**What happens next:** the work is already costed in the survey. I will
ask for it in the capital programme, where it can be paid for in one year.

**What it needs:** £27,500 for fourteen lamps and new cable, quoted in the
borough's 2024 survey.
