Saltings has returned the same party's candidate at every borough election
since 2003, usually on a turnout under a quarter. Nobody campaigns here
because nobody needs to.

I am standing as an independent because the things this ward has lost
were never put to it: the toilets, the lights, the evening buses. I will
spend the next fourteen months finding out what else, one door at a time.
