By the end of September we had knocked on 2,000 doors. I keep a tally on
the back of every walk sheet.

| Times | What people raised first |
| --- | --- |
| 412 | The evening 14 bus |
| 288 | The seafront toilets |
| 196 | Speeding on the coast road |
| 141 | The market moving off the quay |

The bus surprised me. It is now [a pledge of its
own](/programs/bring-back-the-14-bus/).
