I asked the borough for the survey under the freedom of information rules
and it arrived in nineteen days. It is short and clear: the cable is
corroded from the second lamp onwards, the columns are sound, and the whole
job is £27,500.

> The installation is not safe to re-energise without full recabling.

That sentence has sat in a drawer for two years. It is now on [the
programme](/programs/light-the-harbour-wall/).
