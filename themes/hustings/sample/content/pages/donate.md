## How to give

**By cheque:** payable to "Graham Pye, agent for Tessa Marlow" and posted to
9 Ropewalk, Saltings, Wrenmouth, or handed in at the Thursday stall on the
quay.

**By bank transfer:** e-mail
[tessa@tessamarlow.example](mailto:tessa@tessamarlow.example) or call
[01632 960 418](tel:+441632960418) and Graham sends the details. By law we
need your name and address for any gift over £50.

## If you would rather give an hour

A round is one street, one bag of about 180 leaflets and an hour at a walk,
with no knocking and no talking unless you want to. Rounds go out from
9 Ropewalk every Saturday at ten and every Wednesday at six. Write to
[rounds@tessamarlow.example](mailto:rounds@tessamarlow.example) with the
streets you know and Anjali will match you to a round near home, or read
[how a leaflet round works](/programs/deliver-a-leaflet-round/).
