I came to Saltings in 2000 to pilot ships into Wrenmouth harbour and never
left. Since 2019 I have chaired the Ropewalk Residents' Association, which
is how I learned how little of what this ward is promised ever arrives.

I am standing because the seat has been safe for so long that nobody has
knocked on a door here in living memory. I want to be the councillor you
can find: at the Thursday market, at a monthly surgery, on the phone.

polling day, as the law requires. The legal spending limit for Saltings
