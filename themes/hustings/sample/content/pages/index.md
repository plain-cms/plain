## What has gone

In four years Saltings has lost the seafront toilets at Saltings Steps, the
lights on the harbour wall and every other 14 bus after six in the evening.
None of it went to a vote in this ward. Most of it was decided in a budget
paper nobody here was asked about.

## What I will do

I have lived on Ropewalk for twenty-six years and piloted ships into this
harbour for nineteen of them. I am not a member of any party, so no whip
tells me how to vote. My six pledges are costed and short, and every one of
them is on [the programme](/programs/) with what it needs and who decides.

## How you can help

A ward campaign is won on foot. If you can give one evening, [take a leaflet
round](/programs/deliver-a-leaflet-round/): a bag of 180 leaflets, one
street, about an hour. If you can't walk it, [£20 pays for the
printing](/donate/) of one round.
