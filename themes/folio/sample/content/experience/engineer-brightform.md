Brightform makes forms that large companies embed in their own sites. My
team owned the renderer, the code that runs on customers' pages, where every
kilobyte and every focus ring is somebody else's problem if you get it
wrong.

Two years there taught me that performance and accessibility are both
architecture problems. The bundle got small when the renderer stopped
shipping a framework, and the screen-reader experience got good when state
stopped living in the DOM. The audit that followed opened the door to two
government contracts.
