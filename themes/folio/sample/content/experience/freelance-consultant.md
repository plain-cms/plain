A year of short engagements between jobs, most of them the same shape:
arrive, measure, delete JavaScript, and leave a dashboard behind so the
numbers stay honest afterwards.

The furniture retailer's checkout was the largest. It went from 4.2 seconds
to 2.0 on mid-range phones, by cutting things rather than by being clever.
The report from that job became a template I still use, where every
recommendation is paired with the metric it should move and a date to check
it.
