Stadslab places adults who are retraining as developers with small Rotterdam
companies, and pairs each of them with a working engineer for the first
year. I take two people every January.

We meet for an hour every other week. Most of it is code review on whatever
they shipped that fortnight, and the rest is the part nobody teaches: how to
ask for help, how to read a codebase that predates you, and how to say that
an estimate was wrong.
