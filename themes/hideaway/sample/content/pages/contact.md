## Ring or write

Call [01632-960418](tel:01632-960418) or write to
[stay@craigmuie.example](mailto:stay@craigmuie.example). If we are out on the
hill, leave a message and we will ring back the same evening.

| Day | Phone answered |
| --- | --- |
| Sunday to Friday | 9:00–20:00 |
| Saturday | 10:00–16:00 (changeover) |

## Booking a week

Tell us the cottage, the Saturday you would like to arrive, how many of you
there are and whether a dog is coming. We will tell you straight away if the
week is free, hold it for three days, and send our bank details for the
deposit. Nothing is booked until the deposit arrives.

## Finding us

Craigmuie Farm, Moniaive, Thornhill DG3 4HP. From the village cross, take the
Dalwhat road north for three miles; the farm lane is on the left after the
second cattle grid, with our name on the milk-churn stand. The village
cottages each have their own directions, sent with the key number.
