Every cottage here is one we clean, stock and answer for ourselves: the same
linen, the same box of eggs and oatcakes on the table, the same phone number
if the stove sulks. We take bookings by phone and e-mail, not through a booking
site, so the price you see is the price you pay.

[See every cottage, with prices by season](/listings/).

- [The Steading](/listings/the-steading/) sleeps six, for families and two
  households sharing
- [Smiddy Cottage](/listings/smiddy-cottage/) is on the High Street, a step
  from the pub and the bakery
- [The Byre](/listings/the-byre/) is on the farm, for walkers and their dogs
