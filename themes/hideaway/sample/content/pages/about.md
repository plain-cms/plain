Craigmuie is a hill farm of about 400 hectares, three miles up the Dalwhat
glen from Moniaive. Ewan's family has farmed it since 1958; Morag came for a
summer in 1994 and is still here. We keep Blackface ewes on the hill and a
few suckler cows on the low ground, and in 2018 we turned the old byre and the
shepherd's bothy into cottages rather than watch the roofs fall in.

The Steading followed in 2021. Since then three neighbours have asked us to
let their cottages for them, Smiddy Cottage, Dalwhat Cottage and the Old
Schoolhouse at Tynron, and we run them exactly as we run our own.

## What a stay is like

You arrive from 16:00 on a Saturday and leave by 10:00 the next. The key is
in a box by the door and we send the number the week before. One of us looks
in on the first evening, unless you would rather we didn't. There is no
mobile signal at the farm cottages, but there is Wi-Fi in each, and a
landline in the Steading.
