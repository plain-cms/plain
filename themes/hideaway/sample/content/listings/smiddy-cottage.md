The forge itself is now a store for the cottage's bicycles; the house beside
it is where the smith lived. Tom Hastie, whose grandfather shod horses here,
owns it, and we let it for him.

**Sleeps four:** one double, one twin, a bathroom with a walk-in shower, a
kitchen at the back opening to a small walled yard.

From November to March we also take short breaks: Friday to Monday, or Monday
to Friday, for £290. One dog is welcome.

**Worth knowing:** the bedrooms face the street. Moniaive is quiet, but on a
folk-festival weekend in May it is not.
