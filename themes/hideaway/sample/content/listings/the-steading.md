The arches of the cart shed are glazed now and make one long kitchen and
sitting room facing south up the Dalwhat glen. The granary above holds the
bedrooms.

**Sleeps six:** two doubles and a twin; one bathroom with a bath, one shower
room downstairs. Underfloor heating, a wood stove, a landline.

| November to March, except Christmas and New Year | £780 |

| July, August, Christmas and New Year | £1,150 |

Minimum stay one week in July and August; short breaks of three or four
nights the rest of the year for £480. Up to two dogs, and an enclosed garden
for them.

**Worth knowing:** it is a working farm. In April there are lambs in the
field below, and a quad bike past the window at six in the morning.
