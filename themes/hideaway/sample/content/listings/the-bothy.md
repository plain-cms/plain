The bothy sits on its own a quarter of a mile up the farm track. It was the
shepherd's shelter at lambing; now it is one room with a box bed, a kitchen
along one wall, a stove, and a shower room built into the old peat store.

**Booked:** every week up to the end of December 2027 is taken. We open the
weeks of 2028 to bookings on 1 February 2027; ring or write to be told first.

| Season | Price per week |
| --- | --- |
| November to March, except Christmas and New Year | £395 |
| April to June, September and October | £450 |
| July, August, Christmas and New Year | £520 |

Sleeps two. No dogs, no children under twelve: the track is steep and the
burn is close.
