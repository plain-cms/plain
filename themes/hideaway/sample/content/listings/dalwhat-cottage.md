Dalwhat Cottage belongs to Jean Gibson, who lived here for fifty years and
now lives in the village. It is a long, low house of three bedrooms, with the
river at the foot of the garden.

**Booked:** every week up to the end of January 2027 is taken, the fortnight
over Christmas and New Year by Jean's own family. We will open the
weeks from February 2027 to bookings once Jean has decided her prices for the
year; ring us if you would like to hear first.

**Sleeps five:** a double, a twin and a single, one bathroom with a shower
over the bath. Oil-fired heating and an open fire.
