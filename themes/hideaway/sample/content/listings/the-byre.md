The byre kept twelve dairy cows until 1979. The stalls are gone; the long
stone wall and the stone flags are not.

**Sleeps four:** a double and a twin, a bathroom with a bath and a shower, a
kitchen and sitting room in one with a wood stove. A boot room by the door has
a hose, a drying rack and a dog bed.

Short breaks from November to March: Friday to Monday, or Monday to Friday,

**Worth knowing:** the Southern Upland Way crosses the hill behind the farm,
and walkers sometimes knock to ask the way. We are happy to tell them.
