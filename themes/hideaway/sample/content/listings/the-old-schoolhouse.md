Tynron school closed in 1971. Isla and Rob Kerr bought it in 2009 and made a
family house of it; now they live in Edinburgh and we look after it for them.

**Sleeps eight:** two double bedrooms, one twin, and a bunk room for children.
One bathroom with a bath, one shower room. The schoolroom has a wood stove
and a long table that seats ten.
