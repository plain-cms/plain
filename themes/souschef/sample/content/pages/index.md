**Looking for my first head chef role from March 2027.** Sheffield, Leeds or
the Peak District.

I have cooked professionally since 2013, the last six years as a sous chef: in
a brasserie, a hotel, a tasting-menu room, a village pub and a wood-grill
kitchen, with brigades of three to fourteen.

What owners bring me in for:

- **Whole animals, used whole.** Carcass buying, butchery, and a menu written
  around what is in the walk-in.
- **A pass that stays calm.** Clear calls, one prep list everyone can read,
  and a service that finishes on time.
- **Margins you can see.** Every dish costed before it goes on, and the gross
  profit checked every week.

The dates and the numbers are on the [CV](/experience/), which prints on a
sheet of A4. If it sounds like your kitchen, [get in touch](/contact/).
