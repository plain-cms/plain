Hollin & Rye cooks over a wood grill and buys whole lambs and pigs from two
farms in the Hope Valley. Nothing arrives in portions, so the menu is written
around what is hanging in the walk-in, and the butchery is mine.

## How the kitchen runs

Service is Wednesday to Sunday, with a brigade of nine across two shifts. I
call the pass on four of those nights and write the order sheets for the
farms on Mondays.

## What I changed

When I started, each section kept its own prep list and the gaps were found
at two in the afternoon. Now there is one list on the wall, written the night
before and ticked off by whoever does the job, and the kitchen is set by four.
