The Wheatsheaf is a stone pub on the main road through Hathersage, full of
walkers at lunch and families on a Sunday. Grouse, partridge and venison came
in feather and fur from the estates on the moor, and I learned to hang and
break them down there.

The gross profit work was mostly unglamorous: weighing the Sunday roasts,
putting the carcasses to use in pies and terrines, and a weekly stock-take the
owners could read.
