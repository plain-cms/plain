Over Pinhaw Beacon early, and from the top the first sight of the Dales: paler hills, greener fields, and a white scar of limestone on the skyline.

At East Marton the Way joins the Leeds and Liverpool Canal for a mile, and passes under the double-arched bridge — one arch built on top of another when the road was raised. Narrowboats going by at walking pace, which is the right pace for everything.

Then the Aire. From Gargrave to Malham the Way follows the river upstream through fields, and it is gentle and flat and full of meadow flowers, and I walked it far too slowly.

Fifteen miles. Malham was full of people eating ice cream. I joined them.

— Ruth
