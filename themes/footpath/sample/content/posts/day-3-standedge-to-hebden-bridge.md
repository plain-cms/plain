Back at the Standedge stile at nine, four weeks after leaving it.

Blackstone Edge is gritstone boulders on a ridge, with a stretch of old paved road crossing it that may be Roman and may not; the experts still argue. Then the strangest moment of the Way so far: a long, narrow footbridge high over the M62, six lanes of lorries under your boots and a moor on either side.

## The reservoir drains

Then miles of easy level walking beside the drains that feed a chain of reservoirs, with the Pike in view the whole time and never seeming to come any closer. Stoodley Pike was built for the end of the war with Napoleon, fell down, and was built again. There is a dark stair inside to a balcony; I went up it and the wind nearly took my hat.

Steeply down through woods to the canal in the Calder valley, and along the towpath into Hebden Bridge. Fourteen miles.
