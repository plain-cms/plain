Clear and cold, the opposite of yesterday. Laddow Rocks in the early sun, with the whole of Longdendale laid out behind.

Black Hill used to be famous as the worst bog on the Way. The old accounts are full of people sinking to the thigh and losing a boot. Now there is a line of stone flags across it, flown in by helicopter from demolished mills, and the moss is growing back green where twenty years ago there was bare black peat. I walked across dry-shod and felt slightly cheated.

Down past the Wessenden reservoirs, one after another like steps, to the road at Standedge. Eleven miles, done by two o'clock, and the bus to Marsden station came within five minutes. The knee said nothing at all.

First trip done: twenty-seven miles. Two hundred and forty-one to go.
