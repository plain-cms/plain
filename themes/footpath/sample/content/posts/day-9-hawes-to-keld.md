Across the valley to Hardraw first, then up. Great Shunner Fell is five miles of climbing from the road, gradual enough that you never quite notice it, steep enough that you never stop noticing it.

The summit shelter is a stone cross of walls, so there is always one corner out of the wind. I ate lunch in the north-east corner with a family from Leeds doing it the other way, who told me the hard bit was still to come.

Down to Thwaite, then round the side of Kisdon Hill on a ledge path above the Swale, with the river loud below. Keld is a handful of houses and three waterfalls within ten minutes' walk.

Thirteen miles. The knee complained for the first time on the way down, and was quiet again by supper.
