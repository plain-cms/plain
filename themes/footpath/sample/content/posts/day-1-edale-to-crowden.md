Off the 07:48 from Sheffield at Edale with six other people in boots. Two of them were starting the Way as well; we wished each other luck outside the pub where the Way begins and never saw each other again.

Up Jacob's Ladder in fine drizzle, and then the cloud came down to meet me and stayed for six hours. Kinder plateau in cloud is a world about ten yards across: peat, a slab, another slab, a sheep.

## The Downfall

At Kinder Downfall the wind was blowing straight up the gully, and the stream was going over the edge and coming back up as spray. I stood in it for a minute, getting wetter, because you do not often see a waterfall go the wrong way.

Across the Snake Pass road, then the long flagged causeway up to Bleaklow Head, where the cairn appeared out of nothing. The descent down Torside Clough was the first time all day I saw further than a field.

Crowden at half past five. Sixteen miles, one blister, wet to the knee.

— Ruth
