A steep pull out of the Calder valley through Colden, where the fields are so small and the walls so many it is like crossing a chessboard.

Up on the moor by the Walshaw Dean reservoirs a curlew followed me for half a mile, calling the whole way. Then the climb to Top Withins: a roofless farmhouse under two sycamores, with a plaque explaining, quite firmly, that it bears no resemblance to the house in the novel. There were eleven people taking photographs of it anyway, and a signpost that gives the way back to Haworth in Japanese as well as English.

Down to Ponden Reservoir and the guest house above it at four. Eleven miles. Home tomorrow on the bus to Keighley and the train from there.

That is two trips. Fifty-two miles in, and the knee is holding.
