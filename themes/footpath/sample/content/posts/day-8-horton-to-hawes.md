A walled lane out of Horton, past the pothole at Sell Gill, where the stream drops into the ground and you can hear it falling a long way below.

Then the deep wooded gorge of Ling Gill, and an old packhorse bridge with a stone in it asking travellers to remember the people who repaired it in 1765. I did.

## Cam High Road

The best walking of the Way so far is Cam High Road: a Roman road along the top of the fells, straight as a rule, green and dry and wide enough for a cart. For two hours I saw no one but a farmer on a quad bike with two dogs on the back.

Down Dodd Fell into Wensleydale and Hawes at four. Fourteen miles, done without a single wet foot.

— Ruth
