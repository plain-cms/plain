Twenty-four degrees by ten in the morning, which on Ickornshaw Moor is unheard of. The shooting huts on the top shimmered.

Down at Cowling, a farm gate with a cool box on a chair and a hand-written card: *Water, squash, flapjack. £1 each. Honesty box.* I had two bottles of water and a flapjack and put four pounds in the tin, because it felt like the kind of place that should be overpaid.

## A short day on purpose

Only ten miles today, by design: the knee does better if the first day of a trip is the shorter one. In Lothersdale by two, with time to sit outside with a pot of tea and watch the cricket on the field by the mill chimney. Nobody I asked knew who was winning, including, I think, the players.
