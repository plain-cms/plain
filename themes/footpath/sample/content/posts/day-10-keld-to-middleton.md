The longest day so far, and planned with care: early start, small pack, sandwiches made the night before.

Four miles of moor to the inn at Tan Hill, standing on its own at the top of the world. It was not yet open; the sheep in the car park did not seem to mind.

Sleightholme Moor after that is wet in all weathers, and it was wet today. Then God's Bridge, a single slab of limestone the river has worn a way under, and a tunnel beneath the A66 where you can hear the traffic over your head.

Baldersdale, then a string of reservoirs, and the last miles down into Teesdale with the evening light along the river. Middleton-in-Teesdale at seven, with twenty miles on the map and, I think, about twenty-two in the legs.

Five trips done, a hundred and thirty-nine miles walked. The stations run out from here.

— Ruth
