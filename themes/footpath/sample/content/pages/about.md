I'm Ruth Ashby. I live in Sheffield, an hour on the train from Edale, and I taught geography at the same comprehensive for thirty-one years. I have walked bits of the Pennine Way for most of my life. This is the first time I have set out to walk all of it, in order, from the Peak District to Kirk Yetholm.

## How the walk works

I walk two days at a time, usually a Saturday and a Sunday, about once a month. At the end of each pair I find the nearest bus or station and go home; next time I go back to the exact stile I left. It is slower than doing the 268 miles in three weeks, and much kinder to a left knee that has had one operation already.

## How the entries work

- One entry for each walking day, numbered by the day of the walk.
- Written the same evening, in a guest house, a bunkhouse or a tent. Tidied a little the next morning, never rewritten.
- Distances are from the map, not a watch, and rounded to the nearest mile.
- No photographs. The phone stays in the bottom of the rucksack in case of emergencies.

## Writing back

Every entry takes replies. Corrections from people who know a stretch better than I do are especially welcome. Write to [ruth@northbound.example](mailto:ruth@northbound.example).
