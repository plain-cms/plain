I'm Ruth Ashby. I taught geography in Sheffield for thirty-one years, and in 2026 I set out to walk the Pennine Way from Edale to the Scottish border — not in one go, but two days at a time, taking the train home in between and back out to wherever I stopped. Every walking day gets one entry, written up that evening wherever I'm sleeping.

Three to start with:

- [Why northbound, and why in pieces](/blog/why-northbound/) — the plan, and the knee that made it.
- [Day 1: Edale to Crowden, mostly inside a cloud](/blog/day-1-edale-to-crowden/) — sixteen miles across Kinder and Bleaklow, seen from about ten yards away.
- [Day 7: Malham Cove, Fountains Fell and Pen-y-ghent](/blog/day-7-malham-to-horton/) — three climbs, one limestone cove, and the day the weather turned.

Every stage is in the [archive](/blog/), newest first. To have each one by e-mail the evening it is written, see [Subscribe](/subscribe/).

— Ruth
