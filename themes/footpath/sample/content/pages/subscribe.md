Each walking day arrives as one e-mail, sent the evening I write it up: usually two in a weekend, then nothing until the next trip about a month later. No digests, no kit reviews, no announcements.

To be added to the list, write to [ruth@northbound.example](mailto:ruth@northbound.example) with the word *subscribe*. I add every name by hand, so allow a day or two. To leave, reply to any entry with the word *stop*.

Prefer a feed reader? The [RSS feed](/blog/rss.xml) carries every new stage as soon as it is published.
