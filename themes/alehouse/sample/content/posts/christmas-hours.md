The bar is open on Christmas Day from 11 until 2, drinks only, as it has been for as long as anyone can remember. The kitchen closes after lunch on Christmas Eve and opens again on Saturday 2 January.

| Day | Bar | Kitchen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Christmas Eve | 12–11 | 12–2 |
| Christmas Day | 11–2 | Closed |
| Boxing Day | 12–6 | Closed |
| 27 to 30 December | 12–11 | Closed |
| New Year’s Eve | 12 until late | Closed |
| New Year’s Day | 12–6 | Closed |

## Christmas lunches

From Tuesday 1 December the kitchen cooks a three-course Christmas lunch for tables of six or more, at £29 a head. It needs ordering a week ahead: put your name on the list on the bar, or ring [01632 960147](tel:+441632960147).

## New Year’s Eve

No tickets and no set menu. The fire will be lit, the guest ale will be something strong, and Alan has promised to bring the accordion.
