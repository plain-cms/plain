After a summer off, the quiz returns on Thursday 8 October and runs every week until Easter. Questions start at eight sharp; the kitchen takes orders until half past, so eat first or eat between rounds.

- Teams of up to six
- A pound a head, and the winning team takes the pot
- Six rounds, one of them always on the village
- No telephones on the table

The team that comes last gets the jar of pickled eggs. It has not been won twice by the same team yet.
