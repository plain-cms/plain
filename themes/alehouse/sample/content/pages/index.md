The beer travels eight miles, from Thornholme Brewery up the dale, and is kept the old way: in the cellar, on the stillage, pulled by hand. The kitchen is two people and one oven, so the menu is short — a pie, a fish, a ploughman’s, and a roast on Sundays that is usually gone by three. See [the full menu](/menu/), or [how to find us](/visit/).

## This week

On the pumps: Skelbeck Best and three guests — this week Thornholme Pale, a dark mild and a porter. The [Thursday quiz](/blog/quiz-night/) starts at eight — teams of up to six, a pound a head, winner takes the pot. The [Christmas and New Year hours](/blog/christmas-hours/) are up, and the Christmas lunch list is on the bar.

## Dogs, boots and children

All welcome in the bar. Muddy boots stay in the porch, there are water bowls by the fire and a jar of biscuits behind the pumps. Children are welcome until eight o’clock.
