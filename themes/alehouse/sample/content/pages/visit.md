Last orders at the bar are twenty minutes before closing. On bank holiday Mondays we keep Sunday hours, without the roast.

## Find us

The Plough & Harrow, 2 The Green, Skelbeck, North Yorkshire. Skelbeck sits at the end of its own lane, four miles off the main road — follow the brown sign for the church and keep going until the road runs out.

There is room for a dozen cars in the yard behind the pub, and more along the green. The 128 bus stops at the green three times a day; the last one back leaves at 17:40. The front door has two steps, so come in through the yard for a level way to the bar.

## The back room

The room behind the bar seats twenty-four and has its own fire. It is free to use for wakes, christenings, parish meetings and the darts team; ask at the bar and we will put your name in the diary.
