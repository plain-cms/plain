The shop is on the corner of Sheep Street and Tanners Row, opposite the Buttercross, with the galvanised baths hanging outside. The 64 bus from Shrewsbury stops at the Buttercross on the hour.

## Parking and access

There is twenty minutes of free parking on Sheep Street and a pay-and-display car park behind the Corn Exchange, three minutes away on foot. For anything heavy, pull into the yard off Tanners Row and we will load it for you.

There is one step at the front door. The yard door is level and wide enough for a wheelchair or a sack truck; ring the bell and we will open it.

## Paying

Cash, card and contactless. Tradesmen and farms can run a monthly account; ask Dilys.

## Collecting

Orders are put by under your surname on the shelf behind the till and kept for a fortnight. Paraffin is drawn while you wait, so bring your can.

## The van

The van goes out on Tuesday and Friday afternoons to anywhere within eight miles of the Buttercross. Delivery is £4 a drop, or free on orders over £60. Ring by noon on the day on [01632 960482](tel:01632960482).
