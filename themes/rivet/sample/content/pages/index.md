## Behind the counter

Four hundred and twelve oak drawers hold the loose stock: woodscrews, nails, washers, cup hooks and split pins. Bring in the old one, or the thing it has to hold, and we will count out what you need, whether that is one screw or a gross.

## Things that are hard to find

Paraffin from the pump in the yard, lamp wicks and mantles, sash cord cut from the reel, linseed oil paint mixed to the shade of your front door, and the odd size of stove bolt that nobody else keeps.

## Through the winter

Rock salt, coal hods and draught strip are by the door from November. Knives, shears and mower blades are sharpened in the back shop on Thursdays.
