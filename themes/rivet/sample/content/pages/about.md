Edwin Tolley and Harold Marsh came back from the Shropshire Light Infantry in 1919, worked two years for a wholesaler in Shrewsbury, and took the lease on 14 Sheep Street in 1921. The drawers behind the counter are the ones they had built that winter by a chapel joiner, and the brass label holders are still the originals.

The shop is run today by Ruth Marsh, Harold's granddaughter, with Colin Pryce at the counter and Dilys Tolley in the office on Mondays and Fridays.

## What we stock, and where it is made

We buy from makers before we buy from importers, and we say so on the label.

| Stock | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| Woodscrews, bolts and nails | A fastener works in the Black Country |
| Hammers, chisels and saws | Three forges and a saw works in Sheffield |
| Linseed oil paint | Mixed here from Lincolnshire oil and dry pigment |
| Buckets, hods and watering cans | A galvaniser in the West Midlands |
| Brushes and brooms | A brush works in Somerset |

## Loose, not in packets

Almost everything small is sold loose. You pay for the six screws the job needs and not for a blister pack of forty, and you can check the thread against the old one before you leave.

## If we do not have it

We will say so, tell you who does, or order it in. Most things from the wholesaler arrive on the Thursday van.
