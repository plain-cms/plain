Bring a colour card, a painted offcut or a flake from the door itself. We mix on Tuesdays and Fridays, and the tin is ready the next working day with the recipe written on the lid, so the shade can be made again.

## What it is for

Bare or previously oil-painted wood, and primed iron. It goes on thin, dries slowly and does not peel.
