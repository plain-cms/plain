The price is for No. 6 by ¾ in, the size most hinges and door furniture take. Longer and heavier screws cost more; the figure is typed on each drawer.

## Before you drive them

Brass is soft. Drill a pilot hole, run a steel screw of the same size in and out first, and put a rub of candle wax on the thread.
