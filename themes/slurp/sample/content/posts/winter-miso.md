The spicy miso ramen is back on the machine from today. Same recipe as last winter: a red and white miso blend from a brewer in Nagano, minced pork fried hard in the wok, sweetcorn, beansprouts and a knob of butter that you should stir in before anything else.

It is hot, but not a dare. If you want it milder, say so when you hand over the ticket and we will leave out the chilli oil.

It stays on until the clocks go forward at the end of March.
