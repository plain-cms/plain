Our last service of 2026 is lunch on Thursday 24 December, with the last ticket at 14:30. We open again at 11:30 on Tuesday 5 January.

The stockpots get their yearly descale while we are away, so the first tonkotsu of the year will be ready for dinner on the 5th, not lunch. Everything else is on from the moment the door opens.
