When the broth runs out we close early and say so on the door.

## Find us

Arch 7, Viaduct Street, Aldermoor. From the station, leave by the Viaduct Street exit and turn left; we are the seventh arch along, two minutes on foot. Buses 4 and 11 stop at Corn Exchange. The nearest car park is on Wharf Lane.

The door is level with the pavement. Three places at the low end of the counter have chairs instead of stools — tell us at the door and we will keep one for you.

## Queues and groups

We do not take bookings. For six or more, ring [01632 960417](tel:+441632960417) before 11:00 and we will tell you when the counter is quiet. For anything else, write to [hello@twelvestools.example](mailto:hello@twelvestools.example).

## Collection

Every bowl can go home with you: broth in one tub, noodles in another. Say “to take away” when you hand over your ticket, and wait by the hatch. We do not deliver.
