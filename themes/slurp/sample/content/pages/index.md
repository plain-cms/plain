Press a key, pay, and hand your ticket over the counter: no bookings, no bill at the end. The broth goes on the night before, the noodles are cut upstairs every morning, and a bowl is in front of you about six minutes after you sit down. See [the full menu](/menu/), or [how to find us](/visit/).

## On the machine

The [winter miso](/blog/winter-miso/) is on until the clocks change, and the lunch set runs Tuesday to Saturday until 15:00. We shut over the New Year — [the dates are here](/blog/new-year-closing/).

## How the machine works

Choose on the machine by the door and pay by card or cash; it prints one ticket for each thing you pressed. Hand them to whoever is behind the counter and take any free stool. Still hungry? Leave some broth in the bowl and buy a kaedama ticket.
