Joe bought 14 Ashton Terrace in 2009 to pay his way through a doctorate and
never sold it. The other four houses followed, the last in 2021. We live at
40 Ashton Terrace, so none of the houses is more than eight minutes' walk
from our kitchen.

## The tenancy

Each room has its own contract for 48 weeks, from Saturday 4 September 2027
to Saturday 5 August 2028. The rent is per week, paid monthly or in three
termly instalments, whichever suits your loan.

| What | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Bills | Gas, electricity, water, broadband and contents insurance are in the rent |
| Deposit | £250, held in a government-approved deposit scheme and returned within ten days of the end |
| Guarantor | Usually a parent; with no guarantor, we take the first term's rent in advance |
| Fees | None, for anything |

## The houses

Every house has a licence from the city council for shared housing, fire
doors on every room, a cleaner for the kitchen and bathrooms every fortnight,
and an entry phone at the front door with a numbered push for each room, so
your visitors ring you and not the whole house.

## Repairs

Ring or write. No heating, no hot water, a leak or a lock that will not shut:
one of us comes the same day, evenings and Sundays included. Anything else
within three working days, and we tell you which day before we let ourselves
in.
