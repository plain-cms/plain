Booking for Paper & Parchment Forum 2027 opened this morning. The prices have not changed since last year: £185 for both days, £110 for one and £60 for students and retired members.

Each workshop takes twelve people, and last year both were full by June. If you want one, book early and choose it on the ticket page.

Everything about prices, bursaries and invoices is on [Book a place](/join/).
