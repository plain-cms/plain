The Forum is the yearly meeting of the Northern Guild of Book and Paper Conservators, and it is open to anyone who looks after old paper: bench conservators, archivists, librarians, students and the binders who come to argue about paste. About 160 of us fill the Cloth Hall each September.

This year the flood comes first: ten years after the water reached the borough strongroom, the people who dried 4,000 boxes of records tell us what survived. [Getting there](/getting-there/) has the trains and the hotels near the hall.
