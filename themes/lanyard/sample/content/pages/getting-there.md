The Cloth Hall is in the middle of Halbridge, at the top of Market Street, six minutes’ walk from the station.

## By train

Halbridge is on the line between Leeds and Manchester, with two trains an hour each way. From the station, turn left up Station Road, cross at the lights and Market Street is ahead of you. The hall is the stone building with the clock.

## By car

The Market Street car park, opposite the hall, is £6 a day and open from 7 am to 10 pm. It fills by nine on weekdays; the Riverside car park, eight minutes’ walk down the hill, rarely does.

## Step-free

The main door on Weavers Yard has a ramp, and a lift goes to the studios on the first floor. There are accessible toilets on both floors. Two blue-badge spaces are kept in Weavers Yard; ask for one when you book.
