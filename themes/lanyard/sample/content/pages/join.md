The Forum is open to everyone who works with books, archives or paper, whether or not you belong to the Guild. Booking is on the ticket page; you will be asked which workshop you want and anything you cannot eat.

| Ticket | Price | Includes |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Both days | £185 | Every talk, one workshop, both lunches and coffee |
| One day | £110 | Talks, lunch and coffee on the day you choose; a workshop place if you choose Thursday |
| Students and retired members, both days | £60 | The same as both days |
| Conference dinner, Thursday | £42 | Three courses at the Old Dye Works and the coach there and back |

## Bursaries

The Guild pays the full fee for six students and two people in their first year of work. Write a few lines about what you do and why you want to come, by Friday 30 July 2027.

## Questions

Sarah Pickles, the Guild’s secretary, answers everything else: [forum@nbpcguild.example](mailto:forum@nbpcguild.example) or [01632 960318](tel:+441632960318), weekdays from 9 to 4. Invoices for employers are no trouble; ask her before you book.
