The hall opens at 8.30 on both days, with coffee in the foyer. Talks run to time; questions are taken at the end of each one, not during.

## Workshops

Both workshops run on Thursday afternoon, in the conservation studios on the first floor, and each takes twelve people. You choose one when you book. Bring an apron; tools, tissue and leather are provided.

## Short papers

Five projects from members, fifteen minutes each:

1. Re-housing a parish chest of 300 deeds
2. A cheap humidity chamber made from a fish tank
3. Repairing a tithe map in four pieces
4. Testing ten archival boxes in a damp store
5. What we learned from the first year of the apprenticeship

## Posters

Posters go up in the Long Room on Thursday morning and stay up until the close. Boards are A0, portrait. The prize is a year’s Guild membership.

## With thanks

The coffee, the poster boards and the coach to the dinner are paid for by Halbridge Archival Supplies, Thornby Bookcloth and the Wolds County Record Office.
