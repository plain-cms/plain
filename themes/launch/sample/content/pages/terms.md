Last updated 2 March 2026.

## The service

Pulse checks the endpoints you give it and alerts the people you name. We
aim for 99.99% availability of the checker itself; on the Scale plan that
figure is a commitment with service credits behind it.

## Your side

Only monitor systems you own or have permission to test. Don't use Pulse
to load-test someone else's site — 30-second checks from six regions add
up.

## Paying and leaving

Plans bill monthly or yearly in advance. Cancel whenever you like from the
billing page; yearly plans are refunded to the day. Your check history can
be exported as JSON for 30 days after you leave.

## The limits

Monitoring reduces the cost of an outage; it cannot prevent one. Our
liability is limited to the fees you paid in the previous twelve months.
