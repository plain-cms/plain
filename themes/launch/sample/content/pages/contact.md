## Sales and getting started

Starting a plan, moving off another monitor, or checking that Pulse fits
before you commit: [hello@pulsemonitor.example](mailto:hello@pulsemonitor.example).
Tell us roughly how many endpoints you watch and who carries the pager, and
we'll reply with a plan and a setup link — no call required.

Prefer to talk? Ring [+1 (415) 555-0134](tel:+14155550134), weekdays
9am–5pm Pacific. An engineer picks up.

## Support

Customers on every plan, including the free one, write to
[support@pulsemonitor.example](mailto:support@pulsemonitor.example). Include
the monitor name and the time something looked wrong; we'll have the check
history open before we answer.

## Security reports

Found a vulnerability? [security@pulsemonitor.example](mailto:security@pulsemonitor.example)
reaches the two people who can fix it. We acknowledge within 24 hours and
credit reporters in the [changelog](/changelog/). More on the
[security page](/security/).
