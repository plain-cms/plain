**Every plan includes every region.** We don't sell geography; the free
plan checks from the same six regions as Scale.

**Annual pays for two months.** Any plan, minus 17%, cancel whenever —
prorated to the day, by design, because exit friction is a smell.

**Nonprofits and open-source projects:** the Team plan is free —
[email us](mailto:hello@pulsemonitor.example) from the project's domain and
it's done in one reply. No form, no renewal theater.

**Not sure which plan fits?** [Tell us what you run](/contact/) and we'll
say honestly — including when the answer is the free one.
