Last updated 2 March 2026.

## What we collect

An email address and password to sign in; names, phone numbers and email
addresses for the people on your rotations; billing details, which go
straight to our payment processor and never touch our servers.

## What we don't

No advertising trackers, no third-party analytics and no cookies beyond
the one that keeps you signed in. This website loads nothing from anyone
else's servers.

## Status page subscribers

People who subscribe to your status page are your subscribers, not ours.
We send them the updates you post and nothing else, and we don't let
anyone — including you — export the list.

## Your rights

Ask for a copy of your data or for all of it to be deleted and we'll do it
within 30 days: [privacy@pulsemonitor.example](mailto:privacy@pulsemonitor.example).
