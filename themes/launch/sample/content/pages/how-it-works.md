## 1. Add an endpoint

Paste a URL or an IP and port. Pulse starts checking it every 30 seconds
from six regions on independent networks. There is no agent to install and
nothing to open in your firewall.

## 2. Say what healthy means

A 200 is not the same as working. Add assertions — a status code, a header,
a phrase the response must contain, a latency ceiling at p95 — and Pulse
evaluates them in every region on every check.

## 3. Decide who gets woken

Build a rotation, set quiet hours and choose the threshold that counts as
customer-facing. One incident pages one person, with the diff of what
changed attached. If they don't acknowledge, it escalates.

## 4. Tell your customers the truth

Your status page posts the incident automatically with real timestamps,
emails subscribers and keeps a history nobody can quietly edit. When it is
over, export the timeline as a postmortem skeleton.
