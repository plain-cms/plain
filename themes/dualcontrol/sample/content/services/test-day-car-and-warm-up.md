An examiner will only test you in a car that is taxed, insured for the test and fitted with an extra mirror. The simplest answer is the one you have had every lesson in.

## How the day goes

Dawn collects you an hour and a half before the test. You drive the routes once, park up at the centre with ten minutes in hand, and she waits inside while you are out. If you would like her to sit in the back, ask the examiner. It is allowed.

## What it costs

A fixed price for about two and a half hours. If the DVSA cancels the test on the day, which happens in fog and snow, you pay only for the warm-up hour.
