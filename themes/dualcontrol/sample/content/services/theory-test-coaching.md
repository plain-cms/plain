The theory test has two halves: fifty multiple-choice questions and fourteen video clips. More people fail the clips than the questions, usually by clicking too early.

## How the hour goes

Bring whatever app or book you have been using. We sit a practice paper against the clock, go back over the ones you guessed, and then watch clips together until the scoring makes sense.

## What it costs

A fixed price for the hour. Dawn's own learners get the first session free with their second block of lessons.
