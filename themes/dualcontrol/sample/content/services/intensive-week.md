A week is enough for someone who has driven before or has had ten or so lessons. A complete beginner needs longer, and the assessment drive will tell us which you are.

## How the week goes

Five hours a day in two sittings, with lunch between. Monday and Tuesday are spent on what you find hardest. Wednesday is a mock test, Thursday puts right whatever it showed, and Friday is a warm-up hour and then the real thing.

## What it costs

The from-price covers the manual car, the test fee and the assessment drive. In the automatic it is £1,040. You need to have passed your theory test before the week can be booked, because the practical test cannot be reserved without it.
