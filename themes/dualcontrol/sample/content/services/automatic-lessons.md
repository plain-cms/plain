Most learners in an automatic need about a quarter fewer hours than in a manual, because the feet have less to do. The licence you get covers automatic cars only.

## How the lessons go

The first is on the quiet roads behind the retail park: moving off, stopping, steering. By the fifth or sixth you are on the ring road, and the last few are spent on the test routes out of Station Approach.

## What it costs

The from-price is for one hour, paid on the day. Most people book ninety minutes at a time, at one and a half times the hourly price. A block of ten brings it down to £40.
