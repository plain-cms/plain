The national average is forty-five hours with an instructor before the test. Dawn's learners take about thirty-eight, mostly because they practise with a parent in between.

## How the lessons go

The first is clutch control in the car park at Coldwell sports ground, where there is nothing to hit. Junctions and roundabouts follow, then the dual carriageway, then the manoeuvres. The last six hours or so are spent on the roads the examiners use.

## What it costs

The from-price is for one hour, paid on the day. Ninety minutes is the most useful length once you are past the basics. A block of ten brings the hour down to £36.
