The first time most people drive in silence with someone marking a clipboard is the test itself. It is far better for that to happen a fortnight early.

## How it goes

Dawn meets you at the test centre car park and behaves as an examiner would: the eyesight check, the show-me and tell-me questions, twenty minutes following the sat nav, one manoeuvre. She says nothing until it is over.

## What it costs

A fixed price for about seventy minutes in all. You do not need to be one of Dawn's own learners, and you can bring your own car if it is insured for you.
