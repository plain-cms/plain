Pass Plus is the government's own course for new drivers. There is no test at the end. You are signed off on each module when you reach the standard.

## The six modules

1. Driving in town
2. Driving in all weathers
3. Driving out of town, on rural roads
4. Driving at night
5. Dual carriageways
6. Motorways

## What it costs

The from-price covers all six hours in Dawn's car and the certificate. Some insurers knock more than that off a first-year premium, so ring yours before you book.
