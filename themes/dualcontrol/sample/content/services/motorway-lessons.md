The driving test never goes near a motorway, so a new licence comes with no practice on the fastest roads there are. Learners can now take a motorway lesson before the test, in a dual-control car with an approved instructor.

## How the lesson goes

We drive out to the junction at Harrow Cross, join southbound, and use the next three junctions to practise slip roads until they are boring. The way back is in heavier traffic, on purpose.

## What it costs

A fixed price for the two hours. A second session in the dark or in rain is worth having, and costs the same.
