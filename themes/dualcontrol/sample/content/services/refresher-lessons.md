About one learner in five already has a licence. Some passed at seventeen and never owned a car. Some have moved here from a country that drives on the right. Some had a fright.

## How the lessons go

There is no syllabus. The first hour is a drive somewhere unthreatening while Dawn works out what has gone rusty, and after that you choose: the big roundabout by the hospital, the multi-storey, the school run.

## What it costs

The from-price is by the hour. Three or four hours is usual, and nobody is sold a block.
