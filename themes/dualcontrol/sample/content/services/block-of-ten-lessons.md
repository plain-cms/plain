Nobody is asked to buy a block before the first lesson. Have one, see whether we get on, and decide after.

## How it works

You pay by bank transfer or card before the first hour of the block. Each lesson is ticked off on a card you keep in your licence wallet, so there is never an argument about how many are left.

## What it costs

£360 is for ten hours in the manual car. In the automatic it is £400. If you pass, move away or change your mind, the unused hours are paid back in full.
