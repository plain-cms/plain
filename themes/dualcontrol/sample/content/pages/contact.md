## What to have ready

- Your provisional licence number, or the date you applied for it
- Where you would like to be picked up
- Manual or automatic, if you already know
- Any lessons you have had before, and roughly how many
- Your theory test date, if it is booked

## Where we drive

Pick-up from any address in Aldermere, Coldwell and Marsh Green. There is no office to visit: the car comes to you.
