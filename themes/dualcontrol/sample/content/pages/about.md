## Fifteen years in the passenger seat

Dawn Whitlock qualified as an Approved Driving Instructor in 2011, after twelve years driving the 47 bus between Aldermere and Coldwell. She left a national driving school in 2014 to work for herself, because she wanted to teach one person properly rather than fill a diary.

She teaches about twenty-five learners at a time. The youngest is seventeen and the oldest so far was seventy-one.

## Two cars

- A manual five-door hatchback, 2024, petrol
- An automatic city car, 2023

Both have dual controls, a second mirror for the instructor and a dashcam that records the road and not you. Both are cleaned out every evening.

## What you can count on

- The same instructor and the same car every week
- A lesson that starts on time and lasts the full hour
- A written note after each lesson: what went well, what comes next
- An honest answer when you ask whether you are ready for the test
- No block booking until you have had a first lesson and liked it

> My daughter is not a confident person and came home from every lesson smiling. I didn't expect that.
>
> Priya S., Coldwell
