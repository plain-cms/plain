## Where we drive

Pick-up anywhere in Aldermere, Coldwell and Marsh Green, and the villages along the Stour road. Tests are taken at Aldermere test centre on Station Approach, so its roundabouts, its one-way system and the hill start on Tanner's Rise are where your later lessons will be.
