You no longer need a separate test to tow a trailer, which means nobody shows you how. Two or three hours is enough for most drivers.

## How the lesson goes

We start somewhere quiet with the hitch and the safety checks, then reverse between cones until it stops feeling backwards. The second half is on the road: junctions, roundabouts and a stretch of dual carriageway.

## What it costs

The from-price is for one hour with your own car and trailer. If you would like to use our trailer, say so when you book and the figure is agreed before the day.
