We collect from home, college or work anywhere within about eight miles of the test centre. Inside that circle there is no travel charge, and the lesson starts when you get in the car.

## Usual waiting times

| Area | Next free slot |
|:--|:--|
| Town centre and the estates | About a week |
| The villages to the north | Two weeks |
| Evenings and Saturdays | Three weeks |

## A little further out

For an intensive course we will travel up to fifteen miles. For weekly lessons that far out you are better served by someone closer, and we will happily give you a name.
