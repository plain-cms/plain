Show a current student card at the till. The offer runs Tuesday to Thursday, all day, and you get the same barbers and the same cut as everyone else.

## What happens

Take a seat on the bench and you are called in turn. Tell your barber the guard number you usually have, or show a photo. Most cuts take twenty minutes.

## What it costs

The from-price covers a clipper cut or a scissor cut. A skin fade is a few pounds more, because it takes longer. On Fridays and Saturdays the usual prices apply.
