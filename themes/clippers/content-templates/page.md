We close on bank holiday Mondays and open as usual for the rest of the week. The Saturday before is the busiest day of the month, so come early or come on Thursday.

## The weekend

| Day | Hours |
|:--|:--|
| Friday | 9am to 6pm |
| Saturday | 8am to 4pm |
| Sunday and Monday | Closed |
| Tuesday | 9am to 6pm |

## How long is the wait?

Ring the number at the top of this page and whoever picks up will count the heads on the bench for you.
