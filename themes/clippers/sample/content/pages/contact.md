The last customer is seated fifteen minutes before closing.

## Before you come

- You don’t need an appointment. Walk in and take a seat on the bench.
- If you know your guard numbers, say them. If you don’t, bring a photo.
- Cash or card. There is a cash machine at the market entrance.
- The quietest hours are Tuesday and Wednesday afternoons, until the schools come out at three.

## Where to find us

Dario’s Barbers, 17 Fenwick Street, Northmere NM1 4QD. We are between the covered market and the bus station, on the same side as the bakery. Buses 4, 11 and 27 stop at the end of the street.
