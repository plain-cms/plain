Dario Conti learned the trade in his uncle’s shop in Bari and came to Northmere in 1991. He cut hair in other people’s shops for seven years, then took the lease on number 17 when the cobbler retired. The first chair is the one he bought that winter, re-covered twice since.

Luca, his nephew, swept the floor here on Saturdays as a boy and qualified in 2011. Yusuf joined in 2019 from a shop in the city and brought the fades with him. Ask any of us for any cut. We all do all of them.

## What we promise

- **No appointments, and no queue-jumping.** You are seen in the order you walked in. If you want a particular barber, say so and wait for his chair.
- **The price on the wall.** No charge for a wash you didn’t ask for, and nothing added at the till.
- **We ask before we cut.** Thirty seconds of talk at the start saves six weeks of growing it out.
- **If it isn’t right, come back.** Within a week we will put it right for nothing.

## Training and insurance

- **NVQ Level 3 in barbering**, all three of us.
- **Registered barbers.** The certificates hang by the till.
- **Public liability insurance** of £2 million.
- **Fresh every time.** Clippers are sprayed and brushed between customers, razor blades are used once, and every neck gets a clean strip.

## The shop

Three chairs, a bench that seats six, the football on the radio and yesterday’s paper. There is a step at the door. Knock on the window and one of us will bring the ramp out.
