## Who’s cutting

Dario Conti opened the shop in 1998 with one chair and a second-hand pole. His nephew Luca took the second chair in 2011 and Yusuf the third in 2019. Between us we do fades, flat tops, scissor cuts and proper wet shaves, and we would rather tell you a cut won’t suit you than take your money for it.

## What it costs

The prices are fixed and they are on the wall before you sit down. Cash or card, no extras at the till.

## Where we are

17 Fenwick Street, Northmere, between the covered market and the bus station. Look for the pole. There is an hour’s free parking on Market Place, two minutes’ walk away.

## How long is the wait?

Ten to twenty minutes on most days, longer on a Saturday morning. Ring [01632-960155](tel:01632-960155) and whoever picks up will count the heads on the bench for you.
