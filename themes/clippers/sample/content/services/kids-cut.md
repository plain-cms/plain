First haircuts are welcome, and so are wrigglers. Luca has two of his own and has not yet lost an ear.

## When to come

First thing on Saturday, or before three on a weekday if they are not at school yet. The shop is quieter and so are the children.

## What it costs

The price is for under-twelves. Older than that and it is a grown-up’s cut at the grown-up’s price.
