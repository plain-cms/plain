A fade is all in the blend. From the skin at the neck to an inch at the crown there should be no step, only a shadow that gets darker.

## What happens

We set the line where the fade starts, work up through the guards half a number at a time, and blend out with the lever and the corner of the blade. Then the top. Allow half an hour.

## Keeping it sharp

A skin fade looks its best for about two weeks. Most regulars come every three.
