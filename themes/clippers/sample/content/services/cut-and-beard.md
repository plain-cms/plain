The join between the haircut and the beard is where most trims go wrong. Doing both at once means the sideburns fade into the beard and don’t stop at a line.

## What happens

Hair first, then the beard, then the two are blended at the temple. Allow thirty-five minutes.

## What it costs

The price is for a clipper or scissor cut with the beard. With a skin fade it is £27.
