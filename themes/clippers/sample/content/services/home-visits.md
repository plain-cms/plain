The shop is shut on Mondays, and that is when Dario goes out. He has been cutting some of the same heads for twenty-five years, and a few of them can’t manage the step any more.

## Where we go

Houses and flats in Northmere, the wards at St Aldhelm’s with the sister’s say-so, and the care homes on Priory Road and Tannery Lane.

## What it costs

It depends on how far we travel and how many heads there are when we arrive. Ring [01632-960155](tel:01632-960155) and you will have a figure before you put the phone down.
