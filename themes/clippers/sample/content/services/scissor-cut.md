Clippers cut to one length. Scissors follow the head. If your hair is longer than a number eight, or you want it to sit properly as it grows out, this is the cut.

## What happens

We damp the hair down, cut the back and sides over a comb, then take the top down a section at a time. It takes about twenty-five minutes.

## What it costs

The price covers hair down to the collar. For a restyle, where a lot comes off and you are not sure what you want, allow more time and come on a weekday afternoon.
