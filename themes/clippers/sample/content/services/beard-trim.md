A beard looks after itself for about three weeks. After that the neck line creeps up, the cheeks go fuzzy and one side is longer than the other. Ten minutes in the chair puts it back.

## What happens

We comb it out dry and ask how short you want it. The bulk comes down with a guard or over a comb, the lines are cut in with the trimmer, and the neck is finished with lather and an open razor.

## What it costs

The price covers any beard, from stubble to a full one. If it hasn’t been cut for a year and needs washing first, we will say so and add nothing for it.
