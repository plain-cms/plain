Nobody needs one. That is rather the point. It is the closest shave you will have, and the only half hour of the week when nobody can ask you anything.

## What happens

The chair goes back. A hot towel softens the beard while the lather is worked up. We shave once with the grain and once across it, then finish with a cold towel and an unscented balm.

## Good to know

Come with at least two days of growth. If you are shaving for a wedding or an interview, have your first one a week before so that the skin has time to settle.
