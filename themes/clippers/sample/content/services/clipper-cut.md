The quickest cut in the shop, and the one most regulars have. If you say “two on the sides, four on top”, that is all we need to know.

## Not sure of your number?

A number one leaves three millimetres and a number eight leaves an inch. We start a guard longer than you think you want, and go shorter if you ask. It can’t be put back.

## What it costs

The price covers up to two guard lengths, blended where they meet. A fade down to the skin is a [skin fade](/services/skin-fade/), which takes twice as long.
