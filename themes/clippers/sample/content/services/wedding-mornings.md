Six men, three chairs and one deadline. We have done it often enough to know that the best man is always late.

## How it works

We open at seven and lock the door behind you. Three chairs take six heads in ninety minutes. Bring the buttonholes and you can leave from here.

## What it costs

The price is for each head and covers a cut and a shave or beard trim. The smallest party is four. Write to [chair@dariosbarbers.example](mailto:chair@dariosbarbers.example) with the date at least a month ahead.
