A new door is rarely the size of the frame it is going into. Hanging one well is an hour and a half of planing, chiselling and trying it again.

## How the job runs

You buy the doors, or we supply them at cost. We hang five or six in a day. Fire doors take longer, because the gaps, hinges and seals all have to match the maker’s certificate.

## What decides the price

The from-price is per door, for a standard door into a sound frame, when there are three or more to do. One door on its own, new linings and fire doors are priced when we have seen them.
