A staircase is the largest piece of furniture in the house, and the only one the building inspector checks. We have been making them since 1989.

## What we make

Straight flights, quarter and half landings, kite winders for tight corners, and loft stairs that have to find headroom where there is none. Softwood for paint, or oak and ash to be seen.

## How the job runs

The flight is built complete in the workshop and comes to you in one piece where it will go through the door, or in two where it will not. You are without stairs for one day. We tell you which.

## What decides the price

The from-price is a straight softwood flight of thirteen risers with a painted balustrade. Turns, hardwood and glass add to it.
