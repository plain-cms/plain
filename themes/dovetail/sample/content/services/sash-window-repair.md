A sash window that rattles, sticks or will not stay up is nearly always worth mending. The frame has usually lasted a century and will last another.

## How the job runs

We do two or three windows a day, working from inside the room. Rotten sills and bottom rails are cut back to sound wood and new timber is spliced in.

## What decides the price

The from-price is an overhaul and draught-proofing of one window in sound condition. New sills, new sashes and double-glazed units in the old frame are priced window by window.
