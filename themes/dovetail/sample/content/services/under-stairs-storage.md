The triangle under the stairs is deep, dark and awkward. Three pull-out units make all of it reachable: coats in the tall one, shoes in the middle, the vacuum cleaner at the low end.

## How the job runs

We check what is behind the plaster first, as meters and pipes often live there. Making takes three weeks and fitting takes two days.

## What decides the price

The from-price is three pull-outs under a straight flight, painted. Push-to-open catches, oak fronts and moving a meter cupboard add to it.
