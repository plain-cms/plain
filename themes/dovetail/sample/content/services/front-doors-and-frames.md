Old houses have door openings that no catalogue lists. We make the door for the hole, in the pattern the house had when it was built.

## What we make

Four and six panel doors, boarded and ledged cottage doors, and half-glazed doors with toughened or leaded lights. Oak, or sapele where the door faces the weather.

## How the job runs

We measure the opening and take a template of the old mouldings. The new door is hung in a day, and the house is never left open overnight.

## What decides the price

The from-price is a four-panel door in a new frame, primed. Glazing, fanlights, side panels and ironmongery of your choosing are priced on the drawing.
