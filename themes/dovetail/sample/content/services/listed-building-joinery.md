In a listed building the rule is to repair, not replace. That suits us. A two-hundred-year-old shutter is made of better pine than anyone can buy now.

## What we take on

Panelled doors and shutters, box sashes, window seats, wall panelling, staircases and the odd church pew. We work to the conservation officer’s conditions and are used to their questions.

## How it is priced

No two jobs are alike, so there is no from-price. We survey, write down what each piece needs, and give you a fixed figure for the lot.
