A timber worktop moves with the seasons. Fixed down hard it splits. We fit it so that it can move and still stay flat.

## How the job runs

Your kitchen units need to be in and level first. We template, cut and fit in a day for most kitchens, and the oil is dry enough to use by the next morning.

## What decides the price

The from-price is fitting up to three metres of your own worktop with one joint and one cut-out. Supplying the timber, drainer grooves and curved ends add to it.
