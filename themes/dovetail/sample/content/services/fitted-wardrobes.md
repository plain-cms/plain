A fitted wardrobe holds about a third more than a freestanding one in the same wall, because it uses the corners and goes to the ceiling.

## How the job runs

We ask what you own: long coats, how many shoes, whether you fold or hang. The inside is drawn around the answer. Making takes three to four weeks, and fitting takes two or three days.

## What decides the price

The from-price is a run of 2.4 metres with four doors, painted, under a flat ceiling. Attic slopes, drawers with dovetailed sides and oak interiors add to it.
