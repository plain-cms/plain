The alcoves either side of a chimney breast are never the same width, and neither is square. A bought cupboard leaves a gap that someone fills with a strip. Ours are cut to the wall.

## How the job runs

Ruth measures each alcove in six places and draws both to scale. Once you have signed the drawing, making takes about three weeks. Fitting a pair takes two days, and the room is usable each evening.

## What decides the price

The from-price is one pair of alcoves: a cupboard below and three shelves above, painted. Oak tops, glazed doors, wiring for lamps and a television shelf add to it, and each is priced on the drawing.
