## Call us

[01632-960117](tel:01632-960117)

If the machines are running we will not hear the phone. Leave a message and one of us rings back the same day.

## Write to us

[workshop@pennockjoinery.example](mailto:workshop@pennockjoinery.example)

Photos help. A picture of the wall, the stairwell or the door tells us most of what we need before we visit.

## What to have ready

- Rough sizes: the width of the alcove, the height of the ceiling, the number of doors.
- What the room is used for, and what has to go inside the cupboards.
- Whether you want it painted or in a timber you can see.
- A date, if there is one: a baby, a sale, a kitchen arriving.

## Where to find us

The Old Wheelwright’s, 9 Tanner Row, Skelby. There is room for two cars in the yard. Come and see what is on the benches, but ring first, because some days all three of us are out fitting.

We work in Skelby and about twenty-five miles around it.
