## Who turns up

Alan Pennock has been making stairs and doors in Skelby since 1989. His daughter Ruth joined him at the bench in 2011, and Dev finished his apprenticeship with us in 2025. Whoever measures your alcove is the person who cuts the timber for it, and the same three of us carry it through your front door.

## Where we work

The workshop is the old wheelwright’s shop on Tanner Row. We fit in Skelby, Ashby Moor, Kettlewell Green and anywhere else within about twenty-five miles. Staircases and front doors we will take further, because they travel flat on the trailer.

## Got a wall that isn’t square?

Most are not. Ring [01632-960117](tel:01632-960117) on a weekday between 8am and 5pm, and we will book a time to come and measure up. The visit and the drawing cost nothing.
