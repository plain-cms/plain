Butter Row is the covered lane between the Market Square and Castle Street. We are the fourth shop on the left coming from the square, with the model railway running round the window.

## Parking and buses

The Castle Street car park is two minutes away and free for the first hour. Buses 14 and 292 stop in the Market Square.

## Getting in

There is one low step at the door and a ramp behind the counter; knock on the window and we will put it down. The ground floor is level. The club room and the second-hand shelf are up fourteen stairs, and we are glad to bring boxes down.

## Paying

Cash, card and contactless. Pocket money is counted out on the counter with no hurry at all.

## Collecting and posting

Anything put aside waits under your name for a week. Kits ordered in arrive on Thursday afternoon and we ring when they are here, or you can ring us on [01632 960418](tel:01632960418).

| --- | --- | --- |
| Brushes, tools, small parts | Royal Mail, second class | £3.95 |
| Kits and games | Tracked parcel | £5.50 |

Orders over £60 are posted free.
