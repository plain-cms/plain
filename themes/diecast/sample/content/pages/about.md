Ron Tappenden opened on Butter Row in March 1971 with a tray of Wrenby kits in polythene bags and a rack of Linnet tinlets. His daughter Gail took over the counter in 1994, and her son Joe now does the ordering and runs the club night.

## Who you will meet

Gail is in from Tuesday to Friday and knows every board game on the back wall. Joe builds armour in 1:35 and will talk about weathering for as long as you let him. On Saturdays there is usually a Tappenden of some age at the pocket-money counter.

## Where the stock comes from

Kits, paints and tools come direct from Wrenby, Linnet and Velmar and from two British hobby wholesalers, with a delivery every Thursday. Anything in their catalogues that is not on our shelves can be here the week after you ask.

## Second-hand kits

We buy unstarted kits and sell them from the shelf by the stairs. Every box is opened and the sprues counted against the instructions before it goes out; if a part is missing, the label says which.

## What we do not sell

Nothing with a screen, and nothing that needs batteries to be any fun.
