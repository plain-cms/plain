## On the shelves

About four hundred kits from Wrenby, Ridgeway and Velmar, the full Linnet enamel range in its rack by the till, die-cast in 1:76 for the railway modellers, and a back wall of board games. The pocket-money counter is at a height a six-year-old can see over.

## Ask before you buy

Tell us who the kit is for and what they have built before. We would rather sell a nine-year-old a Spitfire that gets finished than a Lancaster that goes back in the box, and we will say which glue, which brush and which three paints it needs.

## Club night

The Ludbrook Model Club meets upstairs on the first Thursday of the month at seven. Bring whatever is on your bench. Under-sixteens come free with an adult, and the kettle is on.
