Two evenings' work for a patient nine-year-old, or one wet Saturday with a grown-up to hold the wings while the glue sets.

## What else it needs

A pair of nail scissors or side cutters to take the parts off the sprue, and a saucer of water for the decals. Do not twist the parts off; the gates take a bite out of them.
