## One line

Marlow & the Tide are a four-piece soul band from a river town: horn-driven, harmony-heavy, and better live.

## Fifty words

Marlow & the Tide write soul songs about night shifts and last buses: four voices, two horns and a rhythm section that never hurries. Formed in 2019, they play around sixty shows a year and released their second album, *Night Ferry*, in 2027.

## Two hundred words

Marlow & the Tide started in 2019 as a duo playing covers in a harbour pub, and became a band the night the landlord asked for something he had not heard before. June Marlow writes the songs; Dev Asante (bass), Ruth Kowal (drums) and Sam Achterberg (keys, trumpet) turn them into arrangements.

The first album, *Low Water* (2022), was recorded live in two days and sounds like it: fast, loud and a little out of breath. *Night Ferry* (2027) took six nights and leaves more room — songs about night shifts, last buses and towns that empty at six, with a two-piece horn section on half the tracks.

The live show is where the band make their case. Sets run seventy-five minutes without a break, the harmonies are sung rather than played back, and the last song usually ends in the crowd. They have played around sixty shows a year since 2022, from ninety-capacity basements to festival main stages, and have sold out their hometown show four years running.

Marlow & the Tide are unsigned, self-managed and book their own tours.
