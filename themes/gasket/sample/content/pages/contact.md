## Ring us

## Write to us

[bookings@tanneryyard.example](mailto:bookings@tanneryyard.example)

Good for booking an MOT or a service a week or two ahead. If the car will not start today, ring.

Last MOT of the day goes on the ramp at 4.15pm.

## What to have ready

- The registration number, so we can look up the right parts
- The mileage, and when it was last serviced if you know
- What it is doing, and when: cold mornings, braking, over bumps, above fifty
- Whether a warning light is on, and what colour it is

## Finding the yard

Tannery Yard, Mill Road, Wetherfold. Turn in beside the old tannery chimney and we are the second unit on the left, with the blue roller door. There is room for six cars outside. Out of hours, the key box is by the office door.
