## Who's under the bonnet

Ray Pennock opened the workshop in 1994. His daughter Jo has been an MOT tester since 2016 and runs the second ramp. Between them they service, test and repair about thirty cars a week, petrol, diesel and hybrid, and one of them answers the phone.

## What it costs

You get one figure, parts, labour and VAT together, before any work starts. If we find something else once the car is up, we ring you before we touch it.

## Where to find us

Tannery Yard is off Mill Road, behind the old tannery chimney, five minutes' walk from Wetherfold station. Drop the car before work and we will ring when it is ready.

## Light on the dashboard?

Ring [01632-960337](tel:01632-960337) and tell us what it is doing. We can usually look at it the same week, and the same day if it is not safe to drive.
