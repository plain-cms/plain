Ray Pennock served his time at the main dealer on Bridge Street and took the unit in Tannery Yard when it closed. Jo grew up sweeping the floor here, went away to train on hybrids, and came back in 2014. Callum, our apprentice, is in his second year at Wetherfold College and does the tea, the tyres and more of the servicing every month.

## What we promise

- **One price, agreed first.** Parts, labour and VAT in a single figure before work starts.
- **A phone call before anything extra.** If the car needs more than we quoted for, you decide, not us.
- **Your old parts back.** They go in a box in the boot, so you can see what was worn.
- **Twelve months or 12,000 miles** on every repair, parts and labour, in writing.

## Testing and qualifications

- **DVSA authorised MOT station** for class 4 vehicles: cars, taxis and small vans up to 3,000 kg.
- **Two MOT testers.** Ray and Jo both hold the tester's qualification and take the annual assessment.
- **Level 3 in hybrid and electric vehicles.** Jo is qualified to work on high-voltage systems.
- **F-gas certified** for air-conditioning work.
- **Public liability and road-risk insurance**, so your car is covered on the ramp and on a road test.

## The workshop

Two ramps, one of them the MOT bay, a brake roller, a headlamp aligner and a diagnostic machine that reads every make sold here since 2001. There is a waiting room with a window onto the test bay, a kettle and a radiator that works.
