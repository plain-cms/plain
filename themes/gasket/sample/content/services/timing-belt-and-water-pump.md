A timing belt gives no warning. Makers set a mileage or an age for changing it, often five or six years, and the age matters as much as the miles.

## Not sure when yours is due?

Ring with the registration and the mileage. We look up the interval and check the service history for the last change. If the car has a chain instead of a belt, we tell you and you keep your money.

## What decides the price

The from-price is a four-cylinder petrol engine with easy access. Engines where a mount or a wheel-arch liner has to come out take longer. The price you are given includes the pump and the coolant.
