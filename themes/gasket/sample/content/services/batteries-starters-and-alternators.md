Half the batteries we are asked to replace are fine. The fault is an alternator not charging, a poor earth, or something left awake overnight draining it.

## What we do

A load test takes ten minutes and tells us which it is. If it is the battery, most are in stock and fitted while you wait. Cars with stop-start need a particular type, and the car has to be told it has a new one.

## What decides the price

The from-price is a standard battery for a small petrol car, fitted. Stop-start batteries start at £140. Starter motors and alternators are priced per car, with a reconditioned unit offered where a good one exists.
