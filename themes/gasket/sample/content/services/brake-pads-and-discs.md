If the pedal has changed, do not wait for the MOT. A set of pads caught in time costs a third of what pads and scored discs do.

## What we check

Wheels off, pads and discs measured, hoses and pipes looked at for cracks and rust, and the handbrake tested on the rollers. You see the measurements.

## What decides the price

The from-price is front pads on a small car. Discs and pads together on one axle start at £185. Rear brakes with an electric handbrake take longer. Every price is for the axle, fitted, and agreed before work starts.
