Air conditioning loses about a tenth of its gas a year through the seals. After three or four years it stops feeling cold, and the windscreen takes longer to clear.

## What we do

The machine draws out what is left, holds the system under vacuum to show any leak, and puts back the exact weight the maker specifies. It takes forty-five minutes and you can wait.

## What decides the price

The from-price is for the older R134a gas, used in most cars built before 2017. Newer cars take R1234yf, which costs us far more, and that regas is £119. If the vacuum test shows a leak we stop, show you, and charge for the test only.
