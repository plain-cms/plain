A code is a clue, not an answer. "Oxygen sensor" on the reader is as often a split pipe or a tired plug as it is the sensor.

## What we do

We read the stored codes, then test the part before we blame it. That is half an hour for most lights. An amber light can wait a day or two. A red one, or one that flashes, should not be driven on, so ring and we will tell you whether to come in or call recovery.

## What decides the price

The from-price is the first half hour, which settles most faults. Intermittent ones, the kind that only happen on a wet Tuesday, are charged by the half hour after that, and we stop and ring you before each one.
