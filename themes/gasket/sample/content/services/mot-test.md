Fixed price. You can book the test up to a month, less a day, before the old certificate runs out and keep the same renewal date.

## How the test goes

Bring the car in at your slot and take a seat by the window onto the test bay. Jo or Ray works through lights, steering, suspension, brakes on the rollers, tyres, seat belts, emissions and the body underneath. It takes about fifty minutes.

## If it fails

You get the list and a price for each item before we do anything. Small things, a bulb, a wiper blade, a number plate lamp, we can usually fit on the spot and pass the car the same visit. You are free to have the work done anywhere. If the car is back within ten working days, the retest of those items is free.
