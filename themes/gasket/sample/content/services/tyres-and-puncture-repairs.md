A nail in the middle of the tread can nearly always be repaired. One in the shoulder or the sidewall cannot, and nobody honest will try.

## Puncture repairs

The from-price is one repair, balanced. If the tyre has been driven on flat the inside may be damaged, and we show you before deciding.

## New tyres

We do not keep a wall of stock. Tell us the size on the sidewall and what you want to spend, and we order budget, mid-range or premium for the next morning. The price includes fitting, a new valve, balancing and disposal of the old tyre.
