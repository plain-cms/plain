A clutch gives warning: the revs rise and the car does not, or the bite point climbs to the top of the pedal. Book it in then and you choose the day.

## How the job goes

The gearbox comes out, which is most of the labour. We fit the plate, cover and release bearing together, because doing one at a time means paying for the gearbox to come out twice. Most cars are ready the same evening.

## What decides the price

The from-price is a small petrol car with a solid flywheel. A dual-mass flywheel that has worn adds between £250 and £450. We cannot see it until the gearbox is off, so we give you both figures before we begin.
