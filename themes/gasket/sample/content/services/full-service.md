Once a year or every 12,000 miles, whichever comes first. We follow the schedule the manufacturer wrote for your engine, not a one-size list.

## What we do

Everything in an interim service, and then the wheels come off. Pads and discs are measured, not glanced at. Filters are changed, the plugs or the fuel filter go in when they are due, and we road test the car before and after. Allow half a day.

## Under warranty?

A service here keeps a new-car warranty valid, as long as the parts match the maker's specification and the record is kept. Ours do and it is.

## What decides the price

The from-price is a petrol hatchback. Diesels, long-life spark plugs and engines that take more than five litres of oil add to it. You hear the full figure before you book.
