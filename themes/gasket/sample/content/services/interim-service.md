An interim service sits halfway between two full ones. It suits a car that does mostly town miles, where the oil works hardest, or one that covers more than 12,000 miles a year.

## What we do

The old oil comes out warm and the filter is changed. With the car on the ramp we look at the brake pads through the wheels, check the tyres for wear and age, and go over the steering and suspension joints. It takes about an hour.

## What decides the price

The from-price covers a petrol car taking up to four and a half litres of oil. Bigger engines and oils to a special specification cost a little more, and we tell you the figure when you book.
