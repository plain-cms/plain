A louder car is usually a small hole or a failed joint, and it rarely needs the whole system. We put it on the ramp, show you where it is blowing, and replace only the section that has gone.

## What we fit

Back boxes, centre sections, flexi pipes and catalytic converters for most makes, from the motor factor in town. Parts ordered before eleven are on the car that afternoon.

## What decides the price

The from-price is a back box fitted to a small hatchback. A catalytic converter costs more, and how much depends on the car. You hear the figure, parts and labour together, before we order anything.
