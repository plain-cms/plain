A service takes half a day and a clutch takes a whole one. You do not have to spend it in our waiting room.

## The options

| What | Cost |
|:--|--:|
| Lift home or to the station, within three miles | Free |
| Collection and return of your car, within five miles | £10 |
| Courtesy car for the day | £15 |

## Booking the courtesy car

We have one, a small manual hatchback, so ask for it when you book the work. Bring your driving licence. It goes out with a quarter of a tank and we ask for it back the same way.
