Open with the moment the film earned or lost you: one scene, described plainly.

Then the facts a reader needs: who made it, how long it runs, where and when it is showing.

Then the argument. What is the film trying to do, and how close does it get?

Then the verdict, in a sentence a reader could repeat at the box office.
