Each Friday morning the new review arrives by e-mail: one film, or one double bill, with where and when it is showing in York. There are no digests, no offers and no round-ups of the year.

To be added to the list, write to [ruth@secondfeatures.example](mailto:ruth@secondfeatures.example?subject=Subscribe) with the word *subscribe*. I add every name by hand, so the first one may take a day or two. To leave, reply to any review with the word *stop*.

Prefer a feed reader? The [RSS feed](/blog/rss.xml) carries every review the moment it is published.
