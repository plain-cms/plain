I’m Ruth Ashdown. I reviewed films for a York paper for nineteen years, and when it dropped its arts page I carried on here. Every Friday there is one review: a new release at the Bootham Electric one week, a repertory screening at the Fossgate Picture House the next, and now and then a double bill.

Three to start with:

- [A Matter of Life and Death, on 35mm](/blog/a-matter-of-life-and-death-on-35mm/) — the print with the scratch, and the quietest room I have ever sat in.
- [The Salt Year, by Agnieszka Wrona](/blog/the-salt-year/) — a winter in a salt mine’s café, and two hours I would sit through again.
- [Why I sit in row F](/blog/why-i-sit-in-row-f/) — the one readers argue with.

Every review is in the [archive](/blog/), newest first. New ones arrive by [e-mail](/subscribe/) on Friday mornings.
