I’m Ruth Ashdown. I have lived in York since 1998 and reviewed films for the *Ouse Valley Press* from 2005 until its arts page closed in 2024. *Second Features* is what I did next: the same job, without the word count.

I see almost everything at two places. The **Bootham Electric** on Bootham Row has three screens and shows new releases, subtitled ones included. The **Fossgate Picture House** on Fossgate is a single screen run by its film society, with a 35mm projector it keeps going out of stubbornness and love; its repertory programme comes out on the first of every month.

## The rules

- I pay for my own ticket, every time.
- No stars. The verdict is in the first line under the title, and the reasons are in the review.
- If I leave before the end, I say so, and I don’t review what I didn’t see.
- Repertory reviews name the print or the copy: 35mm, a restoration, or a plain digital file.

Replies are welcome, and the good ones change my mind more often than you would think. Write to [ruth@secondfeatures.example](mailto:ruth@secondfeatures.example).

— Ruth
