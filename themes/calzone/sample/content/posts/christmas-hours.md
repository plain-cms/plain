Our last night of the year is Wednesday 23 December, open until 22:00 as usual. The shop is then closed from Christmas Eve to New Year’s Day, and Tonino lights the oven again on Saturday 2 January at 16:30.

- Monday 21 December: closed, as every Monday
- Tuesday 22 and Wednesday 23 December: 16:30–22:00
- Thursday 24 December to Friday 1 January: closed
- Saturday 2 January: 16:30–23:00

Party orders for the week before Christmas need two days’ notice instead of one. Email [orders@toninospizza.example](mailto:orders@toninospizza.example) or ask at the counter.
