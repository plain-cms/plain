The phone is answered from 16:00. Last orders are a quarter of an hour before closing.

## Find us

61 Chapel Road, Fernhill — the green door between the launderette and Fernhill Cycles, opposite the 27 bus stop. There is a bench inside for six and a shelf to lean on; we have no tables.

## Parking and access

Two fifteen-minute bays are right outside, and Chapel Road is free to park on after 18:00. The door is level with the pavement. If the step at the counter is in your way, wave and we will bring the box to you.

## Collection

Ring [01632 960358](tel:+441632960358), tell us what you want and give a name. A pizza is ready in about fifteen minutes on a weeknight and twenty-five on Friday and Saturday; a calzone takes four minutes more. Pay at the counter by card or cash.

## Delivery

We deliver to Fernhill, Oakfield and Lower Marsh, about two miles from the shop. The charge is £2 and the smallest order we deliver is £15. Delivery orders are taken from 16:30 until half an hour before closing, and paid by card over the phone or cash at the door.

For ten pizzas or more — an office, a team, a birthday — email [orders@toninospizza.example](mailto:orders@toninospizza.example) the day before.
