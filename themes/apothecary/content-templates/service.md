Say in two or three sentences what this is and who it helps, and whether
people need to book or can simply walk in.

## What happens

Describe the visit from the customer's side: where it happens, who they see,
and how long it takes.

## What it costs

The price as you would say it at the counter, or who gets it free. Leave this
section out if you have no price to give.

## Afterwards

What is normal afterwards, and when to come back or call.
