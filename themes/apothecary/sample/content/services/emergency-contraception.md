The sooner it is taken, the better it works, so come in as soon as you can. Ask at the counter for “a word with the pharmacist” — you don’t have to say why.

## What happens

In the consultation room, with the door shut, the pharmacist asks a few questions about timing and any medicines you take, then explains which option suits you. It takes about ten minutes.

## What it costs

Free for anyone eligible under the local scheme; otherwise £25. We tell you which applies before anything else.
