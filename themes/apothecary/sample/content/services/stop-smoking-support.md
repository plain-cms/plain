You are about three times more likely to stop for good with support than on willpower alone.

## What happens

A first visit of about twenty minutes to plan your quit date and choose patches, gum or tablets. Then ten minutes once a week, when we take a breath reading so you can see the change.

## What it costs

Free, including the patches or gum, for the full twelve weeks.
