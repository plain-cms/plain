We vaccinate from the start of the flu season until our stock runs out. Daniel runs most sessions; book a time or walk in and wait a few minutes.

## What happens

A short form, a few questions, then the injection in your upper arm. We ask you to stay for fifteen minutes afterwards, just in case.

## What it costs

Free for people over 65, pregnant women and anyone with a long-term condition that qualifies. Otherwise £16. We send a note to your GP either way.

## Afterwards

A sore arm for a day or two is normal. Paracetamol helps. If you feel unwell for longer than that, call us.
