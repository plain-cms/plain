People who take several medicines often have one or two that no longer earn their place, or two that don’t get on. A review sorts that out.

## What happens

We go through each medicine with you: what it is for, how you take it, whether it causes trouble. If something should change, we write to your GP with a suggestion — we never stop or swap a medicine ourselves.
