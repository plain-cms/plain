High blood pressure rarely feels like anything, which is why it is worth checking. Walk in or call to book a time.

## What happens

You sit for five minutes, then we take three readings with a cuff on your upper arm and use the average. We tell you what the number means in plain words and write it on a card for you.

## What it costs

| Who | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Adults over 40 | Free |
| Under 40 | £5 |

## Afterwards

If the reading is high we may lend you a monitor to wear at home for a day, and send the results to your GP with your agreement.
