Some vaccinations need two or three doses weeks apart, so the earlier you book the better. Call or ask at the counter.

## What happens

The first appointment takes about twenty minutes: we go through where you are going, what you will be doing, and your health, then agree what you need. Most people have their first jabs at the same visit.

## What it costs

The consultation is free. Vaccinations are priced per dose:

| Vaccination | Doses | Price per dose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hepatitis A | 1 (booster later) | £55 |
| Typhoid | 1 | £40 |
| Hepatitis B | 3 | £50 |
| Rabies | 3 | £75 |

## Afterwards

We give you a record card. Keep it with your passport.
