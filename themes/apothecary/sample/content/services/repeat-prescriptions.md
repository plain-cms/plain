Hand your repeat slip in at the counter, drop it through the letterbox, or call. We send it to the surgery and collect the signed prescription for you.

## What happens

Kerry checks each prescription against what you had last time and calls you if anything looks different. It is ready three working days after you order, bagged with your name on it.

## What it costs

Ordering and collecting is free. You pay the usual prescription charge at the counter unless you are exempt.

## Afterwards

If you run out before it’s ready, tell us. For many medicines we can give you an emergency supply of a few days.
