A pharmacist is on duty every minute we are open. Ask at the counter and one will come to you, usually within a couple of minutes.

## What happens

We ask a few questions — how long, how bad, what you have tried, what else you take. Then we tell you what will help, whether you need to buy anything at all, and when it would be worth seeing a doctor instead. If you would rather not talk at the counter, ask for the consultation room.

## What it costs

Nothing. You only pay if you choose to buy something, and we will often suggest the cheapest version that does the same job.

## Afterwards

If things haven’t improved in the time we said, come back or call. We note what we suggested, so you don’t have to explain it all again.
