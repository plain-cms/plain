Arif delivers in Millbeck, Calder Rise and Hebble Bottom on Tuesday and Friday afternoons.

## What happens

We ring the day before to say it’s coming. Arif needs a signature for some medicines and will check your name and address at the door.

## What it costs

Free for anyone housebound, recovering from an operation, or caring for someone who is. For everyone else, £2.50 a delivery.
