If remembering which tablet goes when has become hard work, a tray takes the guessing out of it. Each compartment is marked with the day and the time — morning, midday, teatime, bedtime.

## What happens

Ask us, or your GP, whether trays suit your medicines; a few can’t go in them. Kerry makes up each week’s tray and checks it with the pharmacist before it is sealed.

## What it costs

Free for anyone who needs one to manage their medicines. Collect each week, or have it delivered on a Tuesday or Friday.
