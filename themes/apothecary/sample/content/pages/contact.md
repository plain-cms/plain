## Call in, ring or write

No appointment is needed for most things — just come to the counter while we are open. To check whether your prescription is ready, or to book a vaccination, call **[01632-960418](tel:01632-960418)**; Joan or Kerry will answer. You can also write to [counter@pennockspharmacy.example](mailto:counter@pennockspharmacy.example) — we reply the same working day.

**Unwell when we’re closed?** Call **[111](tel:111)** for advice at any hour, day or night. In an emergency — chest pain, difficulty breathing, a severe allergic reaction — call **[999](tel:999)**.

## Opening hours

| Day | Open |
| --- | --- |
| Monday–Friday | 8.30am – 6.30pm |
| Saturday | 9am – 5pm |
| Sunday | Closed |

There is a pharmacist on duty at all of these times.

## Where we are

**9 Weavers Row, Millbeck HX7 4QD** — the green shopfront opposite the war memorial, two doors up from the post office. The 590 and 592 buses stop outside the memorial.

## Parking

Two short-stay bays outside the shop, free for thirty minutes. The Mill Lane car park is a three-minute walk downhill and free after 3pm.

## Access

- Step-free entrance at the front, with a bell beside the door if you would rather we came out to you
- The consultation room is on the ground floor and takes a wheelchair
- A hearing loop at the counter
- Large-print labels on any prescription — ask once and we note it for every one after
