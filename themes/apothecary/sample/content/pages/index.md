Most of what we do needs no appointment. Bring your prescription, ask at the counter for the pharmacist, or ask for a quiet word in the consultation room at the back — it has a door that shuts.

## Most asked for

- [Repeat prescriptions](/care/repeat-prescriptions/) — we collect them from Millbeck Health Centre; ready in three working days
- [Advice at the counter](/care/advice-at-the-counter/) — coughs, rashes, earache and “is this normal?”
- [Flu vaccination](/care/flu-vaccination/) — ten minutes, walk in or book
- [Blood pressure checks](/care/blood-pressure-checks/) — free, fifteen minutes, in the consultation room
- [Travel vaccinations](/care/travel-vaccinations/) — book at least six weeks before you travel

[Everything we do, and what it costs](/care/)

## Coming in for the first time

You don’t need to register with us. Hand your prescription over the counter, or ask the surgery to send it to Pennock’s, and we’ll have it ready. If anything about a medicine worries you — a side effect, a clash with something else you take, a dose that seems odd — ask. That is what the pharmacist is for, and it costs nothing.

## Where we are

9 Weavers Row, Millbeck — the green shopfront opposite the war memorial, two doors up from the post office. There’s a step-free entrance and a bell by the door if you’d rather we came out to you.
