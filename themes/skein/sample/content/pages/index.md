## What is on the shelves

Every hank of wool in the shop was dyed by Ruth in the room behind the counter, ten hanks to a pot. Beside the wool there are needles and hooks in every size, a wall of button drawers, and patterns written for our own yarn.

## Matching a dye lot

No two pots come out quite the same, so each band carries a dye lot number. Buy enough from one lot for the whole garment; if you run short, bring the band and we will find the nearest match or dye you another.

## This season

The woad vat is running again after the summer, which means the deep blues are back. First Frost, the palest of them, is on the shelf until the end of January.
