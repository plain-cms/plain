The shop is the one with the blue door, halfway down Finkle Street between the bakery and the Methodist chapel. The 31 bus from Malton stops in the Market Place, two minutes away on foot.

## Parking and access

The Market Place has free parking for two hours, except on Wednesdays when the market is on; use the Cattle Mart car park behind the church instead. There is one shallow step at the door and a ramp behind the counter that we are glad to put out. The table at the back has four chairs and a kettle, and nobody minds how long you sit.

## Paying

Cash, card and contactless. Gift tokens are sold in any amount and do not run out.

## Putting aside and collecting

We keep yarn aside under your surname for a week. For a jumper's worth, ring first on [01632 960174](tel:01632960174) so that Ruth can check there is enough in one dye lot.

## Posting

Parcels go out on Tuesdays and Fridays in a paper bag inside a card box.

| Where | Cost | Usually arrives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| United Kingdom | £3.60 | In two to three days |
| Ireland and the rest of Europe | £9.50 | In about a week |
| Everywhere else | Ask us | In two to three weeks |

## Winding

Hanks have to be wound into a ball before you knit from them. We wind anything bought in the shop free of charge while you wait.
