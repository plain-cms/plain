We work within an easy drive of the yard. Inside that area there is no travel charge.

## How soon

| Area | Usual wait |
|:--|:--|
| In town | Two or three days |
| The valley villages | Within the week |
| Further out | By arrangement |

## Storms

After a gale the phone does not stop. Anything letting water in is made safe first, wherever it is, and the lasting repair is booked once the weather settles.
