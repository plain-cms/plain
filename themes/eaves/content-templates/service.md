A roof window is a hole in a sound roof, so the flashing matters more than the glass. We fit the kit the maker supplies for your roof covering and nothing improvised.

## How the job goes

We measure from inside, mark the rafters, and agree the position with you before anything is cut. One window is a day's work from a tower scaffold. The room below is sheeted and the opening is never left uncovered overnight.

## What it costs

The from-price covers one window up to 78 by 98 centimetres in a slate or plain-tile roof, with the scaffold tower. Bigger windows, pairs and conservation types are priced on the survey, in writing.
