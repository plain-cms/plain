## Call us

[01632-960417](tel:01632-960417)

If we are on a roof we cannot answer safely. Leave your name and street and we ring back at dinner time or at five.

## Write to us

[office@pughandparry.example](mailto:office@pughandparry.example)

Best for surveys, quotations and anything with photographs. We answer in the evening.

## What to have ready

- Slate or tile, if you know, and how many storeys
- Where the water shows indoors, and since when
- What you can see from the ground: slipped slates, a leaning pot, a gutter hanging
- A photograph from the garden or across the road, texted to 07700-900417

## Where we work

Abermoel and about twenty miles around it, from Pentre Uchaf at the top of the valley to Porth Tegid on the coast.

The yard is at the Old Smithy, Quarry Road, Abermoel. One of us is there from 7am to 7.45am most mornings. After that we are out, so ring before you call round.
