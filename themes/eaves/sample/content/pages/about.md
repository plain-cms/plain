Gwilym Pugh learned slating from his uncle on the chapel roofs of the valley and went out on his own in 1998. Carys Parry came to him as an apprentice in 2009, took to leadwork, and became a partner ten years later. Tomos joined as an apprentice in 2024 and is working towards his NVQ. Most of our work comes from people whose roofs we have been on before, or from the house next door.

## What we promise

- **A written price before we start.** The figure in the letter is the figure on the invoice. If we lift the slates and find rotten timber, we stop, show you, and agree the extra before going on.
- **Photographs of everything.** You cannot see your own roof. You see what we saw before any work is done, and what we did afterwards.
- **We turn up, or we ring.** Roofing waits on the weather. If rain moves your job you hear from us by eight that morning.
- **A tidy site.** Magnet round the garden for nails, gutters emptied of our dust, old slate and lead taken away.
- **Ten years on our work, in writing.** Twenty-five on a full re-slate.

## Trade and insurance

- **NVQ Level 3** in roof slating and tiling, both partners.
- **Leadwork certificates** in bossing and welding for Carys.
- **Members of the roofing trade federation**, inspected every three years.
- **£5 million public liability insurance.** We will send the certificate with the quotation if you ask.
- **Trained on the tower scaffold**, with working-at-height tickets renewed every three years.

## The yard

The old smithy on Quarry Road holds about four thousand reclaimed slates, sorted by size and thickness, along with ridge tiles, chimney pots and lead. If your roof is Welsh slate, the chances are that the match for it is already on our racks.
