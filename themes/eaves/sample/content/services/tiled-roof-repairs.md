Tiles crack under frost, aerial fitters and fallen branches. The tile is cheap. Finding one that matches a roof laid in 1962 is the skill, and we keep a stack of the old profiles for that reason.

## Tiles

Plain clay, pantiles, handmade clay and the concrete interlocking tiles on most post-war houses. Where a tile is no longer made, we take a sound one from the back slope for the front and put the near match where nobody looks.

## Ridges and hips

Mortar under a ridge lasts about thirty years, and then the tiles sit there by weight alone until a gale tests them. We re-bed them in fresh mortar, or fit a dry-ridge system that is screwed down and needs no mortar at all.

## What it costs

The from-price covers up to a dozen tiles, or three metres of ridge re-bedded, on a two-storey house. Bigger repairs are priced from the photographs before we start.
