One slipped slate lets the rain reach the batten, and a wet batten lets go of its neighbours. A repair done in the week you notice it is a small job. Left until spring, it rarely is.

## Matching the slate

We keep reclaimed Welsh slate in the yard in the common sizes, sorted by thickness, so a repair does not show from the road. A new slate is slid up into the course and held on a copper strap, not a strip of lead that uncurls in three winters.

## When a repair is not the answer

If slates are slipping all over the roof, the nails have rusted through and a patch will not hold. We will say so, show you the nails, and price a [strip and re-slate](/services/strip-and-re-slate/) so that you can compare the two.

## What it costs

The from-price covers up to ten slates on a two-storey house, reached from a roof ladder. Higher roofs and awkward ones need the tower or a scaffold, and we price that before we start.
