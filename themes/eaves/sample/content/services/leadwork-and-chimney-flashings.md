Most leaks blamed on slates start where the roof meets something else: a chimney, a dormer, a neighbour's wall. Lead is what keeps that joint dry, and it lasts a hundred years when it is fitted in short lengths that can move in the sun.

## What we renew

Chimney aprons, back gutters and step flashings, lead valleys, dormer cheeks and the soakers hidden under the slates. Cement fillets and flashing tape are taken off and done properly in lead.

## Chimneys while we are up there

A stack is repointed in lime mortar, the pots re-bedded and the flaunching renewed in the same visit. It costs far less than putting the scaffold up twice.

## What it costs

The from-price covers one length of step flashing or a chimney apron reached from a roof ladder. A full set of chimney leadwork with scaffold is usually between £650 and £900, and you have the figure in writing first.
