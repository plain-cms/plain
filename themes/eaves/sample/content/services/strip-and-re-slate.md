Welsh slate lasts two hundred years. The iron nails holding it last about eighty. When slates start slipping every winter the roof is "nail sick", and the cure is to take it off and hang it again properly.

## How the job goes

We strip one slope at a time, so the house is never open to the sky overnight. Every slate is rung with the hammer: the sound ones go back on the roof, the soft ones go in the skip. Most roofs give back seven slates in ten.

## How long it takes

A terraced house takes about two weeks, front and back, with the scaffold up for three. You get a start date in writing and a call if the weather moves it.

## What it costs

The from-price is per square metre of roof, re-using your slates, with scaffold. A typical terrace of 70 square metres comes to about £6,400. All-new Welsh slate costs more, and we price both so that you can choose.
