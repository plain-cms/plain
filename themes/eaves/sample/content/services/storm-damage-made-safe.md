After a gale, the first job is to stop the water and stop anything else coming down. The lasting repair can wait for a dry day. A wet ceiling cannot.

## What to do before we arrive

Keep everyone away from the wall below the damage. Put a bucket under the drip and move what you can. Do not go up yourself. Then ring, and tell us what you can see from the ground.

## What we do

We take down anything loose, cover the opening with a heavy sheet fixed under the slates above and battened to the roof, and photograph everything. If the wind is still too strong to work safely we will tell you when we can.

## What it costs

The from-price covers the visit and a temporary cover up to two metres square on a two-storey house. Most buildings policies pay for making safe, and our note and photographs are what the insurer asks for.
