A gutter that overflows sends the rain down the wall instead of down the pipe, and the damp patch indoors turns up a winter later. Most houses want clearing once a year, after the leaves are down. Under trees, twice.

## How the visit goes

We work from ladders with a stand-off, so nothing leans on the gutter itself. Moss and silt come out by hand, the downpipes are rodded, and the run is flushed with a hose to see that it falls the right way. A terraced house takes about an hour.

## Repairs and new lengths

Cast iron is worth keeping. We re-seal the joints, replace rusted bolts and paint the inside with bitumen. Cracked plastic is swapped length for length. New aluminium or cast-iron guttering costs from £38 a metre fitted.

## What it costs

The from-price covers a two-storey terraced house, front and back. Three storeys, conservatory roofs and anything that needs the tower are priced before we start.
