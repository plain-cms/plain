A surveyor's report says "roof coverings should be inspected by a roofing contractor". This is that inspection. It is for anyone buying a house, selling one, or lying awake wondering about a stain on the bedroom ceiling.

## What we look at

Slates or tiles, fixings, ridges and hips, leadwork, chimneys, gutters and the fascia behind them. From inside the loft we check the underside of the slates, the felt, the timbers and the ventilation.

## What you get

A report of four or five pages within two working days: what is sound, what wants doing this year, and what can wait five. Each item has a photograph and a price. Solicitors and lenders accept it.

## What it costs

The from-price covers a house of up to three bedrooms within ten miles of Abermoel. If we go on to do the repairs, the cost of the survey comes off the bill.
