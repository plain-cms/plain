## Finding each pitch

- **Station forecourt** — beside the taxi rank, facing the main entrance. Two minutes from platform 1.
- **Quay Road business park** — in the visitors’ car park behind Unit 4. Free parking for half an hour.
- **Market Square** — the north side, between the flower stall and the clock. The square is closed to cars on market days; use Cooper Street car park.
- **Old Tannery** — through the brick arch on Tanners Row. The yard is cobbled but level, and there are covered benches.
- **Riverside market** — on the towpath end of the car park, by the footbridge.

The hatch is at standing height. If you would rather not queue at the kerb, wave and one of us will come round to take your order.

## Ordering ahead

There is nothing to book: join the queue and we cook when you order. For ten wraps or more, ring the truck before eleven on [01632 960 471](tel:+441632960471) or email [hatch@bigbluesouvlaki.example](mailto:hatch@bigbluesouvlaki.example) the day before. Cards and phones only.

## Hire the truck

We take the truck to weddings, works parties and street parties on Sundays and Mondays, from forty guests, within twenty miles of Tidewell. Email with the date and the place and we will reply the same day.
