Niko grills, Maria wraps, and nothing is cooked until you order it — allow four minutes at the hatch. The pitta comes from Kostas’ bakery on Quay Road every morning and the lamb from Dunmore’s on Market Square. Read [the full menu](/menu/), or see [where we park each day](/visit/).
