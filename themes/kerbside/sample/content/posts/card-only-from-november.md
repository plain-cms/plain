From Monday 2 November we take cards and phones only.

Nine orders in ten are already paid that way, and counting a float in a truck at midnight is nobody’s favourite job. It also means the hands that take your money are never the hands that wrap your food.

If you only carry cash, tell us at the hatch. Until the end of the year we will take it and sort the change — we would rather feed you than turn you away.
