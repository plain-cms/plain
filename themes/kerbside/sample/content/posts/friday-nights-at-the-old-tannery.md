title: Friday nights at the Old Tannery are back
date: 2026-10-16
description: The night market runs every Friday until 18 December. We serve from five until half past nine.

The Old Tannery night market is back for the winter, every Friday from 16 October to 18 December. We are in our usual corner by the brick arch, serving from 17:00 to 21:30.

The whole menu is on, and the loukoumades fryer comes with us — it stays at home on weekday lunchtimes. There are covered benches, a fire pit and a bar run by the brewery next door.

The Friday lunchtime pitch at the station is off until the new year: one truck cannot be in two places.
