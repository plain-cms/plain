Thigh meat, marinated overnight in lemon, garlic and oregano, then grilled on skewers over lumpwood charcoal while you wait — about four minutes. The chips go inside the wrap, the way they do in Athens.

Allergens: wheat, milk. Ask for it without tzatziki and it is dairy-free.
