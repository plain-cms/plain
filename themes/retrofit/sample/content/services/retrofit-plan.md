A plan stops the common mistakes: new windows fitted before the walls are insulated, a heat pump sized for a house that is about to lose half its heat loss, insulation that leaves nowhere for the damp to go.

## How it runs

The coordinator reads the assessment, models each stage and writes the plan. You get it within ten working days of the assessment, with a summary page you can hand to an installer for a quote.
