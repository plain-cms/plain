title: Energy certificate for a home you are selling
description: An energy performance certificate for a house or flat going on the market, visited within five working days and lodged the next day.
fee: From £85
points:
  - A visit of about an hour, at a time that suits you or your agent
  - The certificate lodged on the national register the next working day
  - A copy by e-mail to you and, if you wish, to your estate agent
  - The recommendations explained in plain words on the phone
order: 10
example: true
---

A home needs a valid certificate before it is marketed, and a certificate lasts ten years. If yours was done since the last time you changed the boiler, the windows or the insulation, ring first: you may not need a new one.

## What it costs

| Property | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Flat or maisonette | £85 |
| House, up to three bedrooms | £95 |
| House, four or five bedrooms | £120 |
| Six bedrooms or more | By quotation |

Prices include VAT and the register's lodgement fee.
