---
title: Thermal imaging survey
description: A camera survey on a cold evening that shows where heat leaves the house, from missing insulation and draughty joints to cold bridges.
fee: £240
points:
  - Carried out between November and March, when the outside air is cold enough
  - Images of every outside wall and the main rooms inside
  - A short report marking each fault and what is likely to cause it
order: 60
example: true
---

The camera needs a difference of at least ten degrees between inside and out, so surveys run in the winter months and are booked a few weeks ahead. Keep the heating on through the afternoon before the visit.
