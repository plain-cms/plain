Most grant-funded insulation, and many installers, need a retrofit assessment before work starts. It records the house as it is, so that the work that follows is designed around it rather than around a standard detail.

## Who it is for

Owners planning wall insulation, a new heating system or a deep retrofit; landlords with a home below band E; anyone who has been told by an installer that their house "needs" something and would like a second opinion.

## What it costs

From £395 for a house of up to four bedrooms within the district. Larger or listed houses are quoted on the phone.
