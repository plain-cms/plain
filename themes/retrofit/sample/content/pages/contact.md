## Before the visit

The assessor needs to see every room, the loft, the boiler and its controls, the hot water cylinder if there is one, and the electricity meter. If the loft hatch is in a cupboard, clear a way to it.

Keep any paperwork that shows what cannot be seen: a guarantee for cavity wall insulation, the certificate for replacement windows, the commissioning sheet for a boiler or heat pump. Without it, the certificate has to assume the worst.

## Finding the office

Tannery Yard is behind the station car park, through the brick archway beside the cycle shop. Unit 3 is the green door at the far end, on the ground floor with no steps. There are two visitor spaces in the yard; the station car park is free for the first hour.

## Paying

By bank transfer or card, when the certificate is lodged. Retrofit work is invoiced half at booking and half when the plan is sent. Estate agents and letting agents with an account are invoiced monthly.
