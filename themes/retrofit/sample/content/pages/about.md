## How it started

Ruth Holloway trained as a building surveyor and spent eight years on damp and timber reports before energy certificates arrived. She set up on her own in 2011, lodging certificates for the estate agents on Bridge Street, and moved the practice to Tannery Yard in 2016. Retrofit work began in 2019 and is now half of what we do.

## How a visit runs

A certificate visit takes about an hour for a three-bedroom house. We measure every room, look in the loft and at the boiler and heating controls, check the windows and the walls, and photograph what we find for the record. Nothing is drilled, lifted or moved.

A retrofit assessment takes most of a morning. It covers everything a certificate does, plus the condition of the house, its ventilation and how you use it, because insulation that suits the walls of a 1930s semi can trap damp in a stone cottage.

## Accreditation and insurance

Both assessors are accredited under an approved scheme and audited on a sample of their work every year. The practice carries professional indemnity insurance of £2 million for any one claim and is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office for the records it keeps.
