Freya bought the top floor of a Victorian villa in Clifton and moved in with a cello, a music stand and one chair.

## The challenge

A bare room with a wooden floor rings, and practice was unbearable for her and for the neighbour below. She did not want acoustic panels on her walls.

## The approach

Soft furnishing does the work: a wool rug over felt underlay, lined curtains in a spot-printed cotton, a deep sofa and a wall of open bookshelves that breaks up the sound. Everything else came from salerooms within a morning's drive, including an armless chair at exactly the height she plays.

## The outcome

Freya says she can hear her own bowing again. The neighbour sent flowers.
