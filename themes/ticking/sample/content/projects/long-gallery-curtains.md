The long gallery at Hatherleigh Court is 27 metres long with seven windows down one side, and its curtains had hung there, fading, since 1956.

## The challenge

Each drop is 4.2 metres, the walls are panelled oak that could not take a fixing, and the family lives in the house and wanted the room open at Easter.

## The approach

The curtains are a heavy wool-linen in bottle green, interlined with bump, hand-pleated and hung from iron poles fixed into the window frames instead of the panelling. The workroom made all fourteen curtains on one long table over nine weeks.

## The outcome

They went up in two days in the week before Easter. The room is two degrees warmer, and the family says it is quiet enough now to read in.
