The attic of this cottage had been a store room for thirty years. Two brothers, fourteen and sixteen, were about to share a bedroom downstairs, and their parents asked us to find them a room each.

## The challenge

The ceilings slope to 90 centimetres at the eaves, and there was full standing height in only a strip down the middle of each room.

## The approach

We put the beds under the slopes, built low cupboards into the eaves, and set each desk in the dormer where the ceiling is highest. Trellis wallpaper runs over the walls and the slope together, so the rooms read as one surface instead of a run of awkward angles.

## The outcome

Both boys moved upstairs in the half-term holiday. The younger one has asked for a lock.
