Coombe Farm has milked cows above Witham Friary since 1911, and its kitchen had last been fitted in 1978. Sunday lunch is twelve people, and on the day we first visited, four of them were eating standing up.

## The challenge

The room is long and low, with one deep window to the yard and a back door that the whole farm walks through in boots. Anything fitted had to take a knock from a feed bucket and be scrubbed down by hand.

## The approach

We moved the range to the end wall and gave the middle of the room to one oak table, 3.6 metres long, made by a joiner in Nunney from boards the family had stored in the barn. The cupboards are painted tongue-and-groove with a larder behind a pair of doors, and the blinds are in a heavy indigo ticking that will wash.

## The outcome

Twelve chairs fit with room to pass behind them, and the family says the table has not been cleared since it arrived.
