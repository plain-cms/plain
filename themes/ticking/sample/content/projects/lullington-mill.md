The Marshes bought the mill house in 2022 with a newborn, a dog and the contents of a London flat. The building had been converted in the 1980s and nothing in it had been touched since.

## The challenge

Four storeys, steep stairs and thirty-one windows, almost none of them a standard size. The family wanted the house to feel like one place and not eleven separate decisions.

## The approach

We gave the house a short palette: three paint colours, two wood finishes and a single family of fabrics — ticking, check and plain linen — used throughout at different scales. Every window was measured twice and dressed by our own workroom: roman blinds in the deep stone reveals, curtains where there was room to stack them back. The lighting plan was drawn before the electrician arrived, so that no lamp in the house sits more than a cable's length from a socket.

## The outcome

The work took fourteen months and was finished in time for the baby's second birthday, which was held in the old grain loft.
