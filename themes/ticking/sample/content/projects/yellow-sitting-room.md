The Pascoes' first-floor sitting room is the best-proportioned room in the house and the coldest, with two tall windows that face north onto the street.

## The challenge

The room had been painted a pale grey that turned blue in winter light, and the family stopped using it from October to March.

## The approach

We painted the walls a deep straw yellow and the woodwork two shades darker, so the room glows even on a grey afternoon. The sofa they already owned was rebuilt and covered in a red-and-cream gingham linen, and the curtains are interlined, full-length and hung from poles above the architraves, so they keep the draught off the floor.

## The outcome

The family moved the television out and the piano in. They tell us the room is in use every evening of the year.
