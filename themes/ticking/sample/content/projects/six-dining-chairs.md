Mrs Hollis inherited the chairs from her grandmother, with seats that had been covered and recovered on top of one another since about 1910.

We stripped all six to the frame, reglued two loose joints, and rebuilt the seats traditionally with webbing, springs and horsehair. They are covered in a narrow blue ticking, close-nailed in antique brass. Six chairs took the workroom three weeks.
