The old scullery at the back of the rectory was where coats, wet dogs and the washing machine all ended up together.

## The challenge

Two spaniels, three children and a long muddy lane: the room had to be hosed down, and it had to be the first thing anyone saw coming in from the garden.

## The approach

A raised tiled bath with a hand shower sits under the window at a height that saves the owners' backs. Above the boot benches is a run of pegs at two heights for children and adults, and the laundry is behind sliding painted doors so it can be shut away.

## The outcome

The dogs are bathed in four minutes instead of twenty, and the rest of the house stays clean.
