Rosie and Callum Fairbairn were expecting their first child and asked for a nursery that would not need redoing in five years.

## The approach

We kept the expensive things grown-up: a plain painted wardrobe with adjustable shelves, a blackout roman blind in a red gingham, and a wallpaper with a small trellis that suits a baby and a twelve-year-old equally. The cot, changing table and animal prints are the only things that will have to go.

## The outcome

The cot has already been swapped for a bed, and nothing else in the room needed to change.
