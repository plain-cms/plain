Agnes Whitlock trained as a curtain maker in a Bath workroom before she ever drew a floor plan. She opened the studio in Frome in 2017, and it is still small on purpose: Agnes, her assistant Priya Sandhu, and a sample library of some four thousand cuttings on the shelves behind them.

## How we work

We take on six or seven houses a year, so that every client is met by the person who will be standing in their room when the curtains go up. A project begins with a visit and a measured survey, and then a morning at the studio with the cloth on the table — nobody chooses a fabric from a screen here.

We would rather recover the chair your grandmother left you than sell you a new one, and most schemes keep more furniture than they replace.

## What we do

- Space planning and measured surveys
- Colour schemes and paint schedules
- Curtains, blinds and pelmets, made in our own workroom
- Upholstery and loose covers
- Kitchens, boot rooms and fitted joinery
- Lighting plans
- Sourcing antiques and second-hand furniture

## Who for

Families in farmhouses, rectories, mill houses and terraced cottages between Bath and Bruton, and a few further away: a cellist's flat in Bristol, a long gallery near Shaftesbury.

## Start a project

Write a few lines about the house and the rooms you have in mind, and send two or three phone pictures if you have them. A first visit within thirty miles of Frome is free; further afield we charge travel at cost.

Write to [studio@agneswhitlock.example](mailto:studio@agneswhitlock.example) or ring Agnes on [01632 960 418](tel:+441632960418).
