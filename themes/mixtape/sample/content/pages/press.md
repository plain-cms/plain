## In one line

Kemi Lowe is a rapper from Pitsmoor, Sheffield, who puts her music out on
cassette and plays it live with DJ Fennel.

## In fifty words

Kemi Lowe raps about Pitsmoor, night buses and the people on them, over
beats she builds with her DJ, Rory Fenn. She has released three tapes and
a freestyle EP since 2022, hosts the monthly Tape Swap night at the Ruskin
Social, and tours *Side Roads*, her new tape, from November 2026.

## In two hundred words

Kemi Lowe started rapping on the top deck of the number 52 bus, recording
freestyles on her phone between Pitsmoor and the city centre. Those
recordings became her first release, *Freestyles from the 52* (2022).

She met Rory Fenn — DJ Fennel — at an open mic the same year. Since then
the two have made every record together: *Pitsmoor Tapes Vol. 1* (2023) on
a four-track in a kitchen, *Vol. 2* (2024) in a rehearsal room under a
railway arch, and *Side Roads* (2026) in Rory's box room over one winter.
Every release comes out on cassette first, in short runs, and on
streaming services after.

Live, it is the two of them: Kemi on the microphone, Rory on the decks
and a sampler. Sets run from twenty minutes at a showcase to an hour at a
headline show. They have opened for Tamsin Hale and Okoro Grant, and the
*Side Roads* tour takes them from Bristol to Scarborough between November
2026 and May 2027.

At home, Kemi hosts Tape Swap at the Ruskin Social on the first Friday of
every month: an open mic, a DJ and a table of cassettes to swap.

## Technical needs

- Two vocal microphones (one for Kemi, one spare), wireless if the room
  has one
- A sturdy table at hip height for two CDJs and a sampler, with two
  stereo inputs and a mains socket
- Monitor wedge for the vocal, and a booth monitor for the DJ
