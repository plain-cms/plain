I'm Kemi Lowe, a rapper from Pitsmoor in Sheffield. I make tapes with my
DJ, Rory Fenn — DJ Fennel on the flyers — and we play anywhere with a
microphone and a decent pair of speakers: club nights, support slots,
showcases and the odd festival tent.

## The new tape

*Side Roads* is ten tracks about the long way home. It's out on cassette
(a run of 300, £8 at shows) and on every streaming service. Start with
*Ring Road Hymn*. Everything else I've put out is on the [music
page](/music/).

## On the road

Headline dates in Birmingham, Glasgow, Leeds and Sheffield, two support
slots, two showcases, a festival afternoon and a Christmas Tape Swap.
London, Sheffield and Manchester have sold out. Every date, with tickets, is on the [shows page](/shows/).

## Tape Swap

On the first Friday of every month I host Tape Swap at the Ruskin Social:
an open mic from 21:00, DJ Fennel until 02:00, and a table of cassettes to
swap. Free, over-18s. December's moves to the 18th for a Christmas special.
