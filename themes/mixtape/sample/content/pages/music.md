## Side Roads (2026)

Ten tracks about the long way home, produced with DJ Fennel in his box
room over one winter. Start with *Ring Road Hymn*, then *Last 52*.

- [Bandcamp](https://example.bandcamp.com) — the cassette, and the
  download that pays us most
- [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com) · [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com)
- Cassettes £8 at every show

## Pitsmoor Tapes Vol. 2 (2024)

- [Bandcamp](https://example.bandcamp.com) ·
  [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com) · [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com)
