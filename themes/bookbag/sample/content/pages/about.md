Wrenfield Board School opened in January 1898 with two classrooms, a bell tower and 96 pupils from the mill cottages. The bell is still there, and Year 6 still ring it on the last day of the summer term.

## The school today

We are a community primary school run by the county council, with 210 children in seven classes from Reception to Year 6, and a nursery of 26 places for children aged 3 and 4. Classes are named after the birds that nest in the hedge along the field: Wrens, Finches, Robins, Thrushes, Kestrels, Swallows and Owls, from the youngest up.

For any question about your child, start with the class teacher at the classroom door at the end of the day. For anything the teacher cannot answer, the office and the staff on this page are glad to help.

## The building

The Victorian school is now the hall, the library and the four junior classrooms. The infant wing was added in 1972 and the nursery, with its own door on Brook Street, in 2015. The reading garden behind the hall opened in October 2026.

## Policies and reports

Printed copies of the school prospectus, our policies and the latest inspection report are kept in the office. Ask at the window or write to [office@wrenfield.example](mailto:office@wrenfield.example) and we will send them by e-mail.
