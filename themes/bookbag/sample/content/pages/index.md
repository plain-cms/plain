We have taught the children of Wrenfield since 1898, in the same stone building at the top of Mill Brow. Every class has a teacher and a teaching assistant, and every child is known by name in the dinner queue.

Families are welcome at assemblies, fairs and open mornings all through the year. Letters home are under [News](/blog/), and the office is glad to answer anything else: ring before 4 pm.
