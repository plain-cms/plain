Most children join us in the nursery in the term after they turn 3, or in Reception in the September after they turn 4. Children join other year groups whenever we have a place.

## Come and look round

Visits are on Tuesday mornings, when you will see the school as it really is. Ring the office on [01632 960384](tel:01632960384) to book a time; you are welcome to bring your child.

## Applying for a place

1. **Nursery:** fill in our nursery form, from the office, at any time. We offer places a term ahead.
2. **Reception:** apply to the county council by 15 January for a place that September. A nursery place does not carry on into Reception, so every family applies.
3. **Any other year:** ring the office. If we have a place, the county council will confirm it within ten school days.

## What it costs

| What | When | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nursery, 15 hours a week | Mornings, 8.45 to 11.45 am | Free for all 3 and 4 year olds |
| Nursery, extra hours | Afternoons until 3.15 pm | £5.40 an hour |
| Breakfast club | From 7.45 am | £3 a morning |
| After-school club | Monday to Thursday until 5.30 pm | £8 a session |
| School lunch | Reception to Year 2 | Free |
| School lunch | Year 3 to Year 6 | £2.60 a day |

Help with lunch and club costs is available for families who need it. Ask the office in confidence.

## Uniform

A royal-blue jumper or cardigan, a white or yellow polo shirt, and grey or black trousers, skirt or pinafore. The Friends of Wrenfield sell good second-hand uniform for £1 an item after the Friday assembly.
