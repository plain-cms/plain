Every family applies, including families whose children are already in our nursery. Applications are made online on the county council's website, where you can list up to three schools.

Come and see us first at the open morning on Tuesday 24 November, 9.30 to 11 am, or book a Tuesday visit with the office on [01632 960384](tel:01632960384).

Places are offered on 16 April 2027.
