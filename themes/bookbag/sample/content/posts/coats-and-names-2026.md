Children go outside at every playtime unless it is pouring, so a waterproof coat is needed every day from now until Easter. Hats and gloves are welcome too.

The lost property box by the office holds 31 unnamed jumpers. Anything left at Christmas goes to the Friends of Wrenfield second-hand rail, so come and look before Friday 18 December.
