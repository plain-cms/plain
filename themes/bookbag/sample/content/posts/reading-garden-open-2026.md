Thank you to the twenty-three parents and grandparents who dug, laid paths and built the benches, and to Wrenfield Builders' Merchants for the timber.

Each class has a reading garden afternoon every week. On Wednesdays from 3.15 to 3.45 pm families are welcome to sit and read with their children before going home.
