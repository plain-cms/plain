This winter’s speakers:

- 10 November: Ruth Gillard, the withy cutters of the Levels
- 8 December: Dr Helen Garside, reading the 1841 tithe map
- 12 January: Peter Lyle, schools and schooling in Haverleigh
- 9 February: bell-founders of the West Country
- 9 March: annual general meeting and members’ slides

Talks start at 7.30 pm in the village hall. Members are free and visitors pay £3 at the door.
