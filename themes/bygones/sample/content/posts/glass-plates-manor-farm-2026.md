The box turned up in a loft at Manor Farm in September, wrapped in a 1931 copy of the county paper. Robert Coombes has cleaned and scanned the first sixty plates: haymaking, the threshing gang, a wedding at St Bridget’s and the 1924 flood on Mill Street.

Each plate now has its own record card. If you recognise a face, a field or a building, the archive room is open on Wednesday mornings.

Some of the prints will be shown at the lantern-slide evening on 5 December.
