title: Who was Ellen Tapp? An appeal for help
date: 2026-08-30
description: A sampler stitched by Ellen Tapp, aged eleven, in 1838 has come back to the village. We would like to know who she was and where she lived.
example: true
---

The sampler was bought at a sale in Taunton and given to the museum by its new owner. It names Ellen, her age and "Haverleigh School", but she is not in the baptism registers.

If Tapp is a name in your family tree, please write to [archive@haverleighhistory.example](mailto:archive@haverleighhistory.example).
