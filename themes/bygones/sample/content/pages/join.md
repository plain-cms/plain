## What it costs

| Membership | A year | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single | £12 | Every winter talk, Haverleigh Notes |
| Household | £18 | The same for two adults at one address |
| Under 18s and students | Free | Every winter talk |
| Visitors at a talk | £3 at the door | That evening’s talk |

The year runs from October to September. Pay at any talk, at the museum in season, or by bank transfer: the membership secretary sends the details.

## How to join

1. Come to a winter talk and ask at the door, or write to the membership secretary.
2. Fill in the one-page form. We never share your details.
3. Your first Haverleigh Notes comes with your card.

## Ways to help

The museum needs stewards for one weekend afternoon a month. The archive needs indexers, who type and file cards on Wednesday mornings. The oral history project needs interviewers and typists. None of these needs experience, and every one is a good way to meet the village.
