We are about 160 members who are curious about the parish: its farms and withy beds, its families, its chapel and its school. Some of us research, some steward the museum, and most simply come to the talks.

You do not need to be a member to come to a talk, visit the museum or ask the archive a question. Bring a name, a house or an old photograph and we will look it up.

Read [about the society](/about/), or see [how to join](/join/).
