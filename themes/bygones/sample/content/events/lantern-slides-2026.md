Arthur Penrose was vicar of Haverleigh from 1898 to 1921 and photographed almost everyone in the parish. His 212 slides came to the society in 1987; forty of the best are shown with the lantern he bought for the Sunday school.

Tickets are £4, children free, from Susan Tilley on [01632 960377](tel:01632960377) or at the door.
