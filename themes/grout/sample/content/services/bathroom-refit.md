Most bathrooms that fail do so behind the tiles: a weeping joint, plasterboard where there should be cement board, a shower tray bedded on nothing. We do the plumbing and the tiling ourselves, so nobody can blame the other trade.

## How the job runs

A standard family bathroom takes ten to twelve working days. The first two are strip-out and first-fix plumbing, then boarding and tanking, then tile, then the suite. We agree the dates before we start and tell you the afternoon the water will be off.

## What it costs

The from-price is labour and setting materials for a room up to five square metres with the layout left as it is. Your suite and tiles are on top, bought by you or by us at trade price. Moving the soil pipe or a wall is priced separately, before we begin.
