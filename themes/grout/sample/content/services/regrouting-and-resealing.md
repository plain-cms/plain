Tiles outlast their grout by decades. If the tiles are sound, new joints and new sealant make a bathroom look five years younger in a day.

## What it costs

The from-price covers a shower enclosure or the walls round a bath. A fully tiled bathroom is usually a day and a half.

## Afterwards

Leave the shower off for twenty-four hours. The sealant needs that long to cure, and it is the part that keeps water out of the wall.
