One cracked tile does not mean a new floor. If you kept a few spares we use those. If not, bring us a broken piece and we will find the nearest match.

## What we look for

A tile cracks for a reason: a joist that moves, a pipe that was notched in too shallow, a patch of adhesive that never took. We tell you which, so the new tile does not go the same way.
