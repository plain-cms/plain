Tile and grout are not waterproof. In a wet room the membrane beneath them does that job, and it is the one part of the room nobody will ever see again.

## How it is done

The drain and former go in first. Board joints and corners are taped, then two coats of membrane cover the floor and the walls of the shower area. We plug the drain, flood the floor and leave it overnight. Only then does the tile go on.

## What it costs

The from-price covers tanking a shower area of up to two square metres, floor and walls. A whole room is priced by the square metre after we have measured.
