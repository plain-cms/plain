Taking the bath out is the commonest job we do. It usually takes five days, and the rest of the bathroom stays as it is.

## Tray or level floor

A low stone-resin tray is the simpler choice and suits most timber floors. A level-access floor, with the tile running straight into the shower, needs the joists to allow a fall to the drain. We check that on the first visit.

## What it costs

The from-price covers labour and setting materials for a shower in the space the bath leaves. Tray, screen, valve and tiles are on top.
