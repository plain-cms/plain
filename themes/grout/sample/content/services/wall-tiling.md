Good wall tiling is decided before a tile is stuck. We set out from the centre of the wall, or from the feature you will look at most, so that nothing thin ends up in a corner.

## What decides the price

Tile size and the number of cuts. A wall of 30 by 60 porcelain goes up faster than the same wall in small metro tiles laid herringbone. Windows, niches and boxed-in pipes add time.

## What we will not tile over

Loose plaster, old tiles that sound hollow, and plasterboard in a shower. We will tell you, and price putting it right.
