A heating mat adds about a centimetre to the floor and very little to the bill if the floor is coming up anyway. It suits bathrooms, kitchens and conservatories.

## What it costs

The from-price is for mat, insulation and laying, on top of the floor tiling. The thermostat and the electrician's connection are a fixed £240 per room.
