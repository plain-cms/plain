A tiled floor is only as good as what is under it. Timber floors are overboarded or decoupled so the tiles do not crack along the joists; concrete is primed and levelled where it dips.

## What decides the price

The size of the tile and the state of the floor. Large-format porcelain needs a flatter base and two people to lay. Levelling compound, overboarding and matting are priced by the square metre once we have seen the floor.

## Walking on it

Keep off for twenty-four hours after laying and another twelve after grouting. We leave a plank route to the kitchen if we have to.
