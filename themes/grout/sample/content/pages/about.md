Declan Garrity served his time with a flooring firm in 1994 and went out on his own in 2003. For years he tiled bathrooms that somebody else had plumbed, and spent too many mornings putting right what was behind the wall before he could start. In 2016 his daughter Niamh finished her plumbing apprenticeship and joined him. Since then the two of them have done the whole room, bar the wiring.

## What we promise

- **One price, in writing.** We measure first. If we lift a floor and find rot, we stop and show you before going on.
- **We set out before we stick.** Every wall is dry-laid on paper so cuts fall evenly at both ends and no sliver ends up beside the door.
- **Everything behind the tile is done properly.** Cement board, not plasterboard, in wet areas. Tanking on every shower wall, whether or not you can see it.
- **A room you can use.** We tell you the day the bathroom is back in service, and a toilet is working every night in between.
- **Two years on workmanship.** On paper, with the invoice.

## Qualifications and insurance

- **NVQ Level 3 in wall and floor tiling**, Declan.
- **NVQ Level 3 in plumbing and domestic heating**, Niamh, who is also approved for work on the mains water supply.
- **Members of the national tiling trade association**, and we work to BS 5385, the tiling standard.
- **£2 million public liability insurance.** Ask and we will email the certificate.

Electrical work in bathrooms is done by Imran Sheikh, a Part P registered electrician who has wired every one of our rooms since 2017.

## The workshop
