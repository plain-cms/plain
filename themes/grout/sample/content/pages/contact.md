## What to have ready

- The size of the room, or of the wall or floor to be tiled
- What is there now: old tile, bare plaster, floorboards, concrete
- Whether you have chosen a tile, and its size if you have
- When you would like the room back

The workshop is at Unit 6, Tannery Yard, Mill Street, Dunmere. We are on site most days, so ring before you come.
