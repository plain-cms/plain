A niche is the detail people point at when the bathroom is finished. It has to be planned before the walls are boarded, so tell us early.

## What we build

Single and double niches in stud walls, tiled shelves across a boxed-in pipe run, and corner ledges in showers where the wall is solid and cannot be cut.

## How the visit goes

We set the niche out against the tile you have chosen, so its edges land on joints rather than through the middle of a tile. Framing and tanking take a morning; the tiling is done with the rest of the wall.

## What it costs

The from-price covers one niche up to 30 by 60 centimetres in a stud wall we are already tiling. A niche cut into a finished wall, or into brick, is priced when we have seen it.
