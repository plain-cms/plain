Glazed tile needs very little. The joints between the tiles need a bit more, and the bead of sealant round the bath needs the most.

## Week to week

Warm water, a drop of washing-up liquid and a soft cloth. Keep bleach and limescale remover off the grout: both eat it.

## Year to year

| What | How often |
|:--|:--|
| Wipe down the shower screen seals | Monthly |
| Re-seal natural stone | Every two years |
| Replace the silicone round the bath | Every five years, or when it goes black |

## When to ring us

A tile that sounds hollow when tapped, a crack that follows a joint, or grout that stays dark after the shower has dried. Ring us and we will come and look.
