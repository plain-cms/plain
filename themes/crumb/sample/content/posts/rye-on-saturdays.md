We stop baking rye in summer: the dough ferments too fast in a warm bakery and the loaves come out sour and flat. The mornings have turned, so from this Saturday it is back.

It is a dense loaf — seventy per cent wholemeal rye from Harcastle Mill, with sunflower, linseed and pumpkin seeds soaked overnight — and it is better on the second day than the first. Slice it thin.

There are forty loaves each Saturday and they are usually gone by eleven. Call [01632 960417](tel:+441632960417) on Friday if you would like one kept.
