Most weekdays the bread lasts until about two o’clock and the pastries until noon. On Saturdays the queue starts before we open.

## Find us

12 Wren Street, Hexley Bridge HB3 2WR — the old saddler’s shop with the green door, halfway between the Market Place and the river.

- **On foot:** four minutes from the Market Place; turn down beside the Corn Exchange.
- **By bus:** the 41 and 43 stop at Bridge End, two minutes away.
- **By car:** Wren Street is too narrow to park in. Mill Yard car park is round the corner, and the first half hour is free.
- **By bike:** there are hoops outside the Corn Exchange.

There is one shallow step at the door and we keep a ramp behind the counter — knock on the window and we will bring it out. Dogs are welcome to wait in the yard, where there is a water bowl.
