Everything on [the menu](/menu/) is made in the room behind the counter, from flour milled at Harcastle Mill eleven miles up the valley. We make what one oven can bake in a night; when it is gone, we close. If you are coming far, ring before ten and we will put a loaf by with your name on it. [How to find us](/visit/).

## This week

The seeded rye is back on Saturdays — forty loaves, out of the oven at about nine. Cardamom buns are on every day this week, and the filter coffee is a washed Ethiopian from Tiller Coffee across the bridge.

## Coming up

The Christmas order book is open: stollen, mince pies and bread for the day itself. [Christmas orders and closing dates](/blog/christmas-orders/).
