Holloway & Sons opened in 1985 and still sells nails by weight. Their old website was a phone number and a photograph of the shopfront taken in the snow.

## The challenge

People were driving past to the retail park because they assumed a small shop would be dear and would not have the thing. It usually had the thing, and it was usually cheaper.

## The approach

I spent two Saturdays behind the counter and wrote down what people asked for. The website became those questions, answered in Graham Holloway’s own words, with the stock list under each. In the shop, the hand-written aisle cards became typed ones that say what is there and what it is for.

## The outcome

The site went live in February. Graham says the question he hears most now is “I saw on your website that you…”, which he takes as a good sign.
