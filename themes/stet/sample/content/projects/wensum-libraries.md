The Wensum Libraries Trust runs five libraries in market towns west of Norwich. Visits were holding up; new members were not.

## The challenge

Every library campaign says the same three things: free, warm, books. People already know. The Trust needed a reason to walk in for the first time, on a budget of posters and a few weeks of social posts.

## The approach

Staff kept a notebook at each desk for a month and wrote down things people said. The campaign printed the best of them, word for word, with the name of the library underneath.

> “Is there a book about the thing my dad used to whistle?” — Dereham

## The outcome

Over the summer the five libraries signed up 1,140 new members, against 610 the summer before. Two of the posters are now framed behind the desks.
