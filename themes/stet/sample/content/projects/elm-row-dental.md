Elm Row is a four-chair NHS and private practice near the Cathedral. Its old site promised a “state-of-the-art patient journey”, which is not what a frightened person wants to read at eleven at night.

## The challenge

Two in five people who began an online booking gave up before the last step. The practice manager thought the form was too long. It was — but the words around it were the bigger problem.

## The approach

I rewrote every page in the voice the receptionists use on the phone: short sentences, the price before the procedure, and a plain admission that most people dislike the dentist. The booking form now says how long each step takes and what happens next. A four-page guide keeps new staff writing the same way.

## The outcome

Half as many people now leave the booking form before the end, and the practice added a Tuesday-evening list for nervous patients after the page about it filled in a fortnight.
