Two former lifeboat crew set up a distillery in a barn behind the dunes at Waxham, with a list of forty possible names and no favourite among them.

## The challenge

Every name on the list was already a gin, a boat or a holiday cottage. Whatever we chose had to sound like the coast without a gull, an anchor or the word “sea” in it.

## The approach

Marram is the grass that holds the dunes together, and the distillers pick its seed heads for the botanical blend. The tagline came from them too: “Grown in sand, bottled by hand.” The back label tells the story in sixty words, which is as many as anyone reads standing in a shop.

## The outcome

The first batch of 600 bottles sold out in five weeks, mostly to people who had picked one up and turned it over.
