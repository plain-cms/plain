Oakes has run buses out of Long Stratton since 1952. Its timetables were printed tables of four-figure times with footnotes in three kinds of asterisk.

## The challenge

Most passengers are over seventy or under eighteen, and neither group was reading the footnotes. Drivers spent part of every shift explaining which buses ran on school days only.

## The approach

I wrote each route as a short list of sentences — “Leaves Tasburgh at 7.42 on school days, 8.15 in the holidays” — and set the exceptions where they happen instead of at the bottom. The same wording went onto the stop signs and into the app, so a journey reads the same everywhere.

## The outcome

Calls to the office about times fell by half in the first three months, and the county council now asks its other operators to follow the same pattern.
