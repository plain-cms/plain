Pottergate Shoe Repairs is one man, one bench and a machine older than he is. Dev Mistry took the shop over from his uncle and wanted people to know it was still open.

## The challenge

The shopfront said “Shoe Repairs, Keys Cut, Watch Batteries” in letters that had faded to pink. It said nothing about how quickly any of it happened.

## The approach

Most jobs take Dev less time than people expect, so the line said exactly that: “Most heels mended while the kettle boils.” The leaflet went through 3,000 doors in the streets around the Lanes, with a list of prices and a map.

## The outcome

Dev repainted the fascia himself with the new line. Walk-in repairs doubled over the summer, and he has taken on a Saturday apprentice.
