A summer project of my own. Pocket booklet, 32 pages, risograph on yellow stock, edition of 200, given away at the Norwich Zine Fair.

## The challenge

I wanted to know whether the terms everyone agrees to — a gym, a phone contract, a parking app, a library card — could be written so that someone would actually read them.

## The approach

I took twelve real sets of terms, kept every obligation and right in them, and threw out everything else. The longest went from 9,000 words to 640. Each rewrite sits opposite a count of what was cut.

## The outcome

All 200 copies went in an afternoon. Three of the people who took one have since hired me to do the same for their own small print.
