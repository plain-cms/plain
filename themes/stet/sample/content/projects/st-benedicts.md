St Benedict’s houses 212 people across Norwich, most of them after a spell of homelessness. Its annual report was thorough, accurate and read by almost nobody outside the board.

## The challenge

The report had to satisfy the regulator and the funders, and also be something a tenant would recognise their own life in.

## The approach

The figures moved to the back, in one clear table. The front became six tenants, each telling one ordinary day in their own words, edited for length and nothing else. Every number in the report appears once, with what it means beside it.

## The outcome

The report went from 48 pages to 20. The Trust printed 400 copies and ran out, and two of its funders asked for the tenants’ pages to use in their own reports.
