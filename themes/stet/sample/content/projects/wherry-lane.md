Wherry Lane has hired out day boats from the same staithe at Horning since 1974. Bookings were steady in August and thin everywhere else, and their posters still listed the engine size of every boat.

## The challenge

The yard wanted families from Norwich and Ipswich to book a weekday in April or May — people who think of a day off as something to fill. The posters had to work on a station platform, read in three seconds.

## The approach

Nobody hires a boat for its engine. I wrote about the speed limit instead: four miles an hour, which is the whole point. The line ran on six posters, each with one thing you only notice slowly — a heron, a pub garden, a child asleep in a life jacket.

> Four miles an hour. Cross at your own pace.

The thirty-second radio script was a single held note of birdsong and one sentence.

## The outcome

Weekday bookings for April and May were up by a third on the year before, and the yard has kept the line on its booking page.
