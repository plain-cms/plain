I have written copy for a living since 2011: six years at an agency on Prince of Wales Road, then on my own from a desk above a print shop in Colegate. Every job is written by me, start to finish, and nothing goes out until it has been read aloud.

## How I work

I start by listening — to you, to your customers, and to the words you already use without noticing. Then I write too much, cut it in half, and send you the half that stands up. You get two rounds of changes, and I mark every change so you can see what moved and why.

I would rather write one line that works than a page that fills the space.

## What I write

- Taglines and names
- Websites and product pages
- Campaigns — posters, radio, social posts
- Signage, timetables and app text
- Annual reports and editing
- Tone-of-voice guides

## Who for

Wherry Lane Boat Hire, Holloway & Sons Hardware, Elm Row Dental Practice, Marram Distilling Co., Wensum Libraries Trust, Oakes Rural Buses, St Benedict’s Housing Trust and Pottergate Shoe Repairs.

## Rates

A tagline starts at £450. A five-page website starts at £1,800. Anything else gets a fixed quote after a first conversation, and the quote does not change unless the job does.
