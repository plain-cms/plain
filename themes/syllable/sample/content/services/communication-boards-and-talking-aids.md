A communication board is a sheet of words and pictures that someone points to. It never runs out of battery and it gives everyone around them the same words to use. We make boards for toddlers who are not talking yet, for adults with aphasia, and for anyone whose speech is hard to understand for a while.

## What happens

We spend a session finding the words that matter most: the people, the food, the feelings, and core words like *more*, *stop* and *help*. Mina then designs the board, colour-coded so the same kind of word is always in the same place, and we teach the whole family to use it.

## What it costs

Sessions are £85 for 50 minutes. Each printed, laminated board is £25, and we update it as often as words are added.

## Afterwards

If a board is not enough, we help you try simple talking devices and apps before you spend money on one.
