If you are worried about how your child is talking or understanding, an assessment gives you a clear answer and a plan. Most children never notice they are being assessed.

## What happens

Owen or Priya plays with your child on the floor while you sit with them and chat. We listen to how your child talks, see what they understand, and ask you what a normal day sounds like at home. At the end we tell you what we found, in plain words, and whether therapy would help.

## What it costs

The assessment is £150, including a short written summary within ten working days. A full report for nursery or school, if you need one, is £220.

## Afterwards

Some children need no more than a few ideas for home. Others start weekly sessions of 50 minutes at £85 each.
