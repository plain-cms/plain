Aphasia means a stroke has damaged the parts of the brain that handle language. Thinking is usually untouched; it is finding and using the words that has become hard. Therapy can help for months and years after a stroke, not only in the first weeks.

## What happens

The first appointment is a 90-minute assessment with Ruth, at a table, with plenty of breaks. We look at understanding, talking, reading and writing, and we ask what you want to get back to: a phone call to a grandchild, ordering at the café, the crossword. After that, sessions are 50 minutes, usually once a week for a block of eight.
