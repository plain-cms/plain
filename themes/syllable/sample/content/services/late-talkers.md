Some toddlers start talking late and catch up on their own; some need a hand. If your two-year-old has fewer than fifty words or is not yet putting two words together, it is worth talking to us.

## What happens

After the first assessment, Owen runs six weekly sessions of 50 minutes where you and your child play together and he coaches you, so the practice carries on at home all week. Saturday mornings are kept for children, so working parents can come.

## What it costs

The first assessment is £150. Each coaching session is £85.

## Afterwards

We meet again three months later to see how far your child has come and whether anything more is needed.
