Home visits are mostly for adults after a stroke and for families with a new baby or a child who finds new places hard. Being at home often helps: we practise with the real kettle, the real phone and the real family.

## What happens

Ruth or Owen arrives at the time agreed and stays for 50 minutes. The session is the same as one at the clinic, only with your own things to hand.

## What it costs

A home session is £105 within ten miles of the Old Reading Room, travel included.
