Swallowing problems are called dysphagia. After a stroke they are common and they can be serious, because food or drink can go down the wrong way. If someone is choking at every meal or has a chest infection now, call their GP first.

## What happens

Ruth watches you eat and drink, at the clinic or at home, and checks how your mouth and throat are moving. She then suggests changes: how to sit, how much to take, which foods are easier, and exercises to strengthen the swallow.

## What it costs

The assessment is £160 and takes about 90 minutes. Review visits are £85, or £105 at home.
