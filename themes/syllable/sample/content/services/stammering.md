Stammering is common, and it is nobody's fault. Priya works with young children and their parents, with teenagers on their own if they prefer, and with adults who want to feel easier at work or on the phone.

## What happens

The first session is 75 minutes for a child and 90 for an adult. We talk about what stammering is like for you, and you choose the goals. Sessions after that are 50 minutes and can be weekly or every other week.

## What it costs

The first session is £150 for a child and £160 for an adult. Each session after that is £85.
