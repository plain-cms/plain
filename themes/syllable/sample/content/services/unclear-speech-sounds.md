Lots of young children say "tat" for "cat" for a while. When the mix-ups carry on past four, or strangers cannot follow your child, a little therapy usually makes a big difference.

## What happens

After the first assessment, sessions are 50 minutes of games built around the sounds your child finds hard, with a short practice sheet to take home each week.

## What it costs

The first assessment is £150 and each session is £85.
