Dysarthria is speech that has become slurred, quiet, slow or nasal because the muscles of the lips, tongue, voice or breathing are weaker. The words are all there; getting them out clearly is the problem.

## What happens

Ruth assesses your speech, voice and breathing in a 90-minute first appointment and agrees with you what to work on. Sessions after that are 50 minutes and practical: pacing, volume, and tricks to be understood on the phone.

The first assessment is £160 and each session is £85 at the clinic or £105 at home.
