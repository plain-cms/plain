Ruth Hallam opened the practice in 2014 after sixteen years as a speech and language therapist on the county stroke unit. She wanted a place where therapy could carry on after people went home, and where a family could bring a quiet toddler without waiting a year. All three therapists are state-registered.

## How we work

We start from what someone can already do, not from a list of what is missing. Sessions are short and practical, and every one ends with one thing to try at home before the next. Families and partners are welcome in the room: the people someone talks to every day matter more than we do.

We use pictures, signs, communication boards and plain play alongside talking. Nobody has to speak to take part.

## The people you will meet

- **Ruth Hallam**, speech and language therapist — adults after a stroke, aphasia, slurred speech and swallowing
- **Owen Petrakis**, speech and language therapist — late talkers, speech sounds and the Saturday children's mornings
- **Priya Lennox**, speech and language therapist — stammering, for children, teenagers and adults
- **Mina Choudhury**, therapy assistant — makes our communication boards and runs practice sessions between appointments
- **Jo Whitley** — answers the phone, keeps the diary and will find you a time
