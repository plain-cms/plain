## Booking or asking

Call **[01632-960418](tel:01632-960418)** while we are open and Jo will answer, or write to [hello@penwickspeech.example](mailto:hello@penwickspeech.example) and we reply within one working day. Most people start with a free fifteen-minute phone chat; you do not need a letter from a doctor or a school.

## If speech changes suddenly

We are not an emergency service. If someone's speech becomes slurred or muddled all at once, or their face droops or an arm goes weak, it may be a stroke: call **[999](tel:999)** straight away, even if it seems to pass.

## Where we are

**The Old Reading Room, 7 Cotton Lane, Penwick PN3 4HL** — the red-brick building with the round window, opposite the library car park. The number 14 bus stops on Cotton Lane, two doors down.

## Parking and access

There are two marked spaces for our visitors at the side of the building, one of them a wide space for wheelchair users. The library car park opposite is free for the first two hours.

The entrance is step-free with a power-assisted door, the therapy room and the toilet are on the ground floor, and there is a hearing loop at the front desk. Tell us if you need anything else and we will have it ready.
