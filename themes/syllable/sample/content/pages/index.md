We are three speech and language therapists, a therapy assistant and a very patient receptionist in the Old Reading Room on Cotton Lane. We see toddlers who are slow to start talking, school children whose speech is hard to follow, and adults who have lost words, speech or swallowing after a stroke. Every appointment is one person, one therapist, and as much time as the words need.

## Where most people start

- [Late talkers](/care/late-talkers/) — for children of 18 months to 3 years who have few words yet
- [Aphasia therapy after a stroke](/care/aphasia-therapy-after-a-stroke/) — finding words again, at the clinic or at home
- [Unclear speech sounds](/care/unclear-speech-sounds/) — when family understand your child but other people don't
- [Stammering](/care/stammering/) — for children, teenagers and adults
- [Communication boards and talking aids](/care/communication-boards-and-talking-aids/) — for anyone who needs another way to say it

## Your first visit

Call us first for a free fifteen-minute chat on the phone, so we can tell you honestly whether we are the right people to help. The first appointment is an assessment: for a child it takes 75 minutes and is mostly play on the floor; for an adult it takes 90 minutes at a table, with someone you trust beside you if you like. You go home with a plan in plain words and, within ten working days, a short written summary.
