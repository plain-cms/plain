## Finding the door

We are the black-painted door with the bell beside it, between the library and the florist, three minutes' walk from the market square. The 14 and 22 buses stop outside the library.

## Parking and access

There are four parking spaces at the back of the building, reached through the archway into Mill Yard. The back door is step-free, and the chapel of rest and the arranging room are on the ground floor.

## What to bring to the first meeting

- The certificate for burial or cremation, if the registrar has given it to you
- Any written wishes the person left, or a funeral plan if they had one
- The names of the people who will want to be told

Bring nothing else if you do not have it to hand. We can work out the rest together.
