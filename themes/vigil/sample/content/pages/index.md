## Where we work

Kelsby, Thornby Cross, Upper Aldwick and the villages of the Seld valley. We travel further for families who have moved away, and we bring people home from anywhere in the world.

## When someone has died

If someone has died at home or in a care home, ring us and tell us their name and where they are; two of us will come, whatever the time. If they died in hospital there is no need to hurry: the hospital looks after them until you are ready, and we collect the paperwork from there.

## What we do not do

We do not sell funeral plans, we do not take payment at the first meeting, and we never add an item to the bill that you did not choose. After we have met, you receive a written estimate the same day.
