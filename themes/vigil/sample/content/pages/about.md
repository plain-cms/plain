## How it began

Arthur Ruddock was a joiner who made coffins for the Kelsby undertaker on Mill Yard. When the undertaker retired in 1979, Arthur took over the business and moved it to the old chapel on Chantry Street, where we still are. The night bell beside the door is the one he fitted that year.

Ellen and Kate grew up above the office and took the firm over in 2011. Arthur still helps on Saturdays.

## How we work

The person you meet first stays with you to the end: the same funeral director answers your calls, leads the funeral and brings the ashes back to you. We care for everyone in our own mortuary and chapel of rest, and we use our own hearse and limousine, driven by people you will have met.

## Memberships and insurance

We are members of a national association of funeral directors and follow its code of practice, which includes an independent complaints scheme. We hold public liability insurance of £5 million.
