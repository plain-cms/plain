title: Bringing someone into our care
description: We come at any hour to the home, care home or hospital, and look after the person who has died in our own chapel of rest.
fee: Included in every funeral
points:
  - Two of us attend, in a private vehicle without markings
  - Care in our own mortuary on Chantry Street
  - Washing, dressing and preparing for visiting
  - Collecting the paperwork from the hospital or the doctor
order: 10
example: true

When you ring, we ask only for the person's name, where they are and who we should speak to. Everything else can wait until the morning.

## If you are not ready

Some families want to sit with the person for a while before we come. Tell us, and we will come at the time you choose.
