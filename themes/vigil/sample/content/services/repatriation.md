description: We arrange the documents, the flight and the receiving funeral director when someone dies abroad or wishes to rest in another country.
fee: By estimate
points:
  - Liaising with the hospital, the police and the consulate abroad
  - Translation and legalisation of the death certificate
  - Embalming and the sealed coffin that airlines require
  - The flight, and collection from the airport at this end
order: 50
example: true

Ellen arranges every repatriation herself and speaks French. Most journeys within Europe take five to ten days; further afield, two to three weeks. If the person had travel insurance, ring the insurer first and give them our name: they usually pay us directly.

The estimate depends on the country and the airline, so we write it for you once we know where the person is.
