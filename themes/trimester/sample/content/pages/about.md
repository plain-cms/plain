I trained in Leeds and spent eleven years in hospital and community teams, on labour wards, in birth centres and on the district. I loved the work and hated handing people over at the end of a shift. In 2019 I set up on my own so that I could stay with a family from beginning to end.

## How it works

I take on no more than four families due in any one month. That is what lets me answer the phone myself, stay for as long as a visit needs, and be rested when you call at night.

I am registered with the Nursing and Midwifery Council and carry full professional indemnity insurance for home and hospital work. I work alongside your GP and the local maternity unit, and if you or your baby need hospital care at any point, I come with you and hand over in person.

## The people you will meet

- **Anna Holroyd** — independent midwife; your midwife from booking to discharge
- **Jo Whiteley** — midwife; second midwife at every home birth, and covers when Anna is on leave
- **Maggie Dyson** — answers the phone on weekday mornings, books visits and sends invoices

Jo and I meet every family together at least once before 36 weeks, so whoever arrives when labour starts is a face you already know.

## Leave and cover

I take two weeks off each summer and one week at New Year, and I tell you the dates at the booking visit. I don't take on families due during my leave.
