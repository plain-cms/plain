## Book or ask

Call **[01632-960418](tel:01632-960418)** during the hours below — Maggie or I will answer — or write to [anna@holroydmidwifery.example](mailto:anna@holroydmidwifery.example) and I reply within a working day. The first half-hour call costs nothing.

## In labour, or worried?

If you are booked with me, call the on-call line, **[07700-900417](tel:07700-900417)**, day or night. It rings on my phone and, when I'm at a birth, on Jo's. Call about bleeding, waters breaking, reduced movements, or simply because something feels wrong. You will never be told off for ringing.

If you are not booked with me, call your own maternity unit or 111. In an emergency, call 999.

## The Weavers' Room

**The Weavers' Room, 7 Mill Fold, Marsden HD7 6AB** — the green door at the top of the cobbled yard behind the bakery on Peel Street, two minutes' walk from the station. There are two parking spaces in the yard; ring when you arrive and I'll come down.

The room is on the ground floor, with one low step at the door and a ramp on request. If getting out is hard, I come to you instead — most of my visits are at home anyway.

## Where I visit

Homes within about twelve miles of Marsden: Slaithwaite, Linthwaite, Golcar, Meltham, Holmfirth, Honley, Diggle, Delph and the villages between. Further afield, ask — I can sometimes say yes.
