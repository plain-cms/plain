I'm Anna, a registered midwife for eighteen years and independent since 2019. I look after a small number of families at a time, so the midwife at your booking visit is the one who comes when labour starts and the one who weighs your baby a week later.

## How I can help

- [Full pregnancy care](/care/full-pregnancy-care/) — antenatal visits, your birth and six weeks afterwards, one fee
- [Home birth](/care/home-birth/) — two midwives with you, on call from 37 weeks
- [Antenatal appointments](/care/antenatal-appointments/) — an unhurried hour at your kitchen table
- [Postnatal visits](/care/postnatal-visits/) — feeding, stitches, sleep and the questions that come at 3 am
- [Birth reflections](/care/birth-reflections/) — talking through a birth that still sits heavy

[Everything I offer, and what it costs](/care/)

## Your first visit

We start with a free half-hour phone call, so you can ask anything before deciding. If we go ahead, the booking visit takes about ninety minutes, usually at your home: your health, your history, your hopes for the birth, and a plan for the weeks ahead. You keep your own notes, and nothing goes in them that we haven't talked about.

## Where I work

I visit homes within about twelve miles of Marsden — Slaithwaite, Meltham, Holmfirth, Golcar, Diggle and the villages in between. Antenatal appointments can also be at the Weavers' Room, 7 Mill Fold, Marsden, two minutes up the hill from the station.
