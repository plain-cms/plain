Every baby is offered a full physical examination in the first three days. I am trained to do it at home, so you don't need a trip to hospital with a newborn.

## What happens

I check your baby's eyes, heart, hips, and — for boys — testes, and look over the whole body from the fontanelle down to the toes. It takes about forty minutes and you hold your baby for most of it. I explain each step and anything I find.

## What it costs

£150, or included in [full pregnancy care](/care/full-pregnancy-care/).

## Afterwards

The results go in your baby's red book and to your GP. If anything needs a second look, I arrange the referral the same day.
