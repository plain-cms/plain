The weeks after birth are when many families feel most alone. Postnatal visits mean someone who knows you checks in, often daily at first, then less as you find your feet.

## What happens

I check your baby's weight, colour, feeding and cord, and offer the heel-prick test on day five. I check your blood pressure, tummy, bleeding and any stitches, and ask how you are sleeping and feeling. Partners are welcome to every visit and their questions count too.

## Afterwards

At about six weeks I do a final check for you and your baby and hand over to your health visitor.
