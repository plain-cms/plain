These sessions are for anyone expecting a baby, whether or not you are in my care. Groups are small, so there is time for every question.

## What happens

The two evenings run on consecutive Wednesdays from 5.30 pm to 7.30 pm. The first covers how labour starts and progresses, positions, breathing and the choices of pain relief. The second covers the first hours and days: feeding, sleep, recovery and when to call for help. Birth partners get their own half hour on what is actually useful.

## What it costs

£180 per couple for both evenings. Families in my full care come free.

## Afterwards

You go home with a short handout and my number. Most groups keep a chat going long after the babies arrive.
