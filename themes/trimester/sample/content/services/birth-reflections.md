Some births stay with us. You may have questions nobody had time to answer, or gaps in what you remember, or a fear of going through it again. Talking it through with a midwife who has time can help.

## What happens

We meet at your home or the Weavers' Room. If you have your notes, we read them together and I explain what the entries mean and why decisions were made. You talk; I listen and answer what I can, honestly. There is no right way to do this and you can stop at any point.

## What it costs

£70 for one session of ninety minutes. A second session, if you want one, is £50.

## Afterwards

Many people find one session enough. If you are planning another pregnancy, we can use what came up to write a birth plan that takes it into account.
