Feeding is the thing new parents call me about most. It is often painful or confusing in the first weeks, and it nearly always gets easier with the right help.

## What happens

I come to your home, ask how feeds have been going and watch one from start to finish. We look at latch, positioning and your baby's mouth, and check your baby's weight and nappies. I can also help with expressing, bottles alongside breastfeeding, or stopping when you are ready to.

## What it costs

£80 for a visit of up to ninety minutes. A follow-up call the next day is included.

## Afterwards

I leave you with a short written plan. If feeding is still hurting after two or three days, call and I come back.
