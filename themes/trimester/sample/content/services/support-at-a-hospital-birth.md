Not everyone wants a home birth, and that is fine. Many families want the hospital and a midwife they know.

## What happens

When labour starts, I come to you at home and stay through the early hours, when most people would otherwise be on their own. When it is time, we travel in together. In hospital the unit's midwives are responsible for your care; I stay with you as your companion, help you say what you want, and explain what is happening.

## What it costs

Part of [full pregnancy care](/care/full-pregnancy-care/) with a planned hospital birth. On its own, with two antenatal visits to get to know each other first, it is £1,200.

## Afterwards

I visit you at home the day after you come out of hospital, and postnatal care carries on from there.
