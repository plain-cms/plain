Antenatal visits are where we get to know each other. They follow the usual schedule — every four weeks at first, every two from 28 weeks, weekly from 36 — and each one lasts as long as it needs, usually about an hour.

## What happens

I check your blood pressure and urine, feel your bump and listen to your baby's heartbeat, and you can listen too. Then we talk: how you are sleeping, how you are feeling, what is worrying you, what you would like for the birth. I arrange your scans and blood tests through your GP surgery and go through every result with you.

## Afterwards

You keep your notes. If anything changes between visits, call — that is what the phone is for.
