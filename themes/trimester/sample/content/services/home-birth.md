A home birth means your labour happens where you live, among your own things, with two midwives who already know you.

## What happens

From 37 weeks I am on call for you around the clock. When labour starts, you ring the on-call line and I come out, bringing everything we need, including gas and air. Jo joins us for the birth. We stay at least two hours afterwards, until you are fed, washed and in bed.

If you or your baby need a hospital at any point, we transfer together and I stay until you are handed over.

## What it costs

Home birth is part of [full pregnancy care](/care/full-pregnancy-care/). It is not booked on its own, because a safe home birth needs the antenatal visits before it.

## Afterwards

I visit the next day and on most days of the first week. Jo or I are on the end of the on-call line throughout.
