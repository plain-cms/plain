The booking visit sets up the rest of your pregnancy. It is the longest appointment we have, so we do it at your home where you are comfortable.

## What happens

We go through your health and family history, past pregnancies, and how this one has been so far. I take your blood pressure, height and weight, and offer the routine blood tests. We talk about the dating and anomaly scans and arrange them. By the end you have your notes, a plan for the next visits, and my numbers.

## What it costs

£120 on its own, or included in [full pregnancy care](/care/full-pregnancy-care/) and antenatal care from booking.

## Afterwards

Results of blood tests come to me within about a week. I call you with every one, normal or not.
