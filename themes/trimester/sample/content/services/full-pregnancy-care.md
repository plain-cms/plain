This is the care most families choose. You have one midwife — me — from early pregnancy until you are both well and settled, with Jo as the second midwife you also get to know.

## What happens

- The booking visit and every antenatal appointment, at home or in the Weavers' Room
- Two birth-planning visits, one with Jo
- On call for you, day and night, from 37 weeks until your baby arrives
- Your birth at home, or me with you in hospital as your birth companion
- The newborn examination
- Postnatal visits for 28 days, then a six-week check for you both

## What it costs

| Care | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Full pregnancy care with a planned home birth | £4,350 |
| Full pregnancy care with a planned hospital birth | £3,600 |

A deposit of £500 holds your place; the rest can be paid in instalments up to 36 weeks. If you move into hospital care for a medical reason, nothing more is charged and the unused part of the fee is refunded.

## Afterwards

At the six-week visit we close your notes together and I hand your care back to your GP and health visitor.
