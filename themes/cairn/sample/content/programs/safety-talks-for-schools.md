The best call-out is the one that never happens. Most of our casualties set
off with no torch, no map and no warm layer.

**Who it serves:** primary and secondary schools in the valley, Scout and
Guide groups, walking clubs and expedition award groups.

**A typical week:** one or two visits a month, in term time. Children get
inside a survival bag, learn the distress signal and try to lift the
stretcher.

**What it costs us:** £40 a visit in fuel and printed route cards. It is
free to schools and clubs.
