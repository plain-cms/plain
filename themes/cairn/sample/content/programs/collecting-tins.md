title: Collecting tins in the valley pubs
description: Thirty-eight orange tins on bars and shop counters raise a sixth of everything the team spends. Look after one in yours.
example: true
---

Most of the people we carry off the hill had a pint in the valley the night
before. The collecting tin on the bar is where most of them say thank you.

**Who it serves:** the whole team. In 2025 the tins raised £11,400, a
sixth of what it costs to keep us ready.

**A typical week:** Sue Halliwell and a rota of volunteers do a tin round
once a month. Each tin is sealed, emptied by two people, counted at the base
and banked the same week. If you run a pub, café or shop, we bring the tin,
a poster and a sticker for the door.

**What it costs us:** £6 for each tin, and the diesel for the round. A full
tin from a busy bar holds about £85.
