Most of our call-outs are walkers with a broken ankle or a twisted knee,
miles from the road, on ground no ambulance can reach. Some are people lost
in cloud, and a few are the worst day of a family’s life.

**Who it serves:** anyone hurt, lost or stuck on the fells above
Kettlethwaite, Harrowdale Bridge and Stenning: walkers, climbers, fell
runners, shepherds and their dogs. It is free to everyone, always.

**A typical week:** one or two call-outs, more in summer and at half-term.
The police page the team leader; the first team vehicle leaves Station Yard
within fifteen minutes; a hill party walks in with a stretcher, a casualty
bag, oxygen and pain relief, and carries the casualty down to the road or to
a helicopter.

**What it costs us:** £310 on average for one call-out, in diesel, medical
supplies and wear on kit. In an emergency, dial 999 and ask for Police, then
Mountain Rescue.
