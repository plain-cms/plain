example: true

Not every search is on the fell. An older man with dementia who walked out
of his front door, a teenager who did not come home: the police ask us
because we can put thirty trained searchers on the ground within the hour.

**Who it serves:** missing people and their families, anywhere in the
valley, at the request of the police.

**A typical week:** five or six of these searches a year. Team members are
trained in search planning and work in lines through woodland and fields,
with Moss, our search dog, ahead of them.

**What it costs us:** about £240 a search, mostly diesel, batteries and
food for a long night.
