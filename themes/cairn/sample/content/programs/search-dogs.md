A dog works on air scent: she finds people, not tracks, and cloud and dark
make no difference to her nose.

**Who it serves:** lost and missing people on the fell and in the valley,
and neighbouring teams who borrow her on big searches.

**A typical week:** Ellie Pickthall trains with Moss three evenings a week
and goes out on assessments twice a year. Moss passed her final assessment
in 2024.

**What it costs us:** about £4,000 over the three years it takes to train
a search dog, in food, vet bills and travel to assessments. Ellie gives her
time free.
