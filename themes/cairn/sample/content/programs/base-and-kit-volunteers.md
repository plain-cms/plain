A call-out on a winter night can run to twelve hours. While the hill party
is out, someone has to be at the base: logging every radio message, putting
the kettle on for the first team back down, and ringing the hospital.

**Who it serves:** every hill party on every call-out, and the families who
ring the base waiting for news.

**A typical week:** a kit check on Monday evenings at Station Yard: torches
charged, oxygen tested, survival bags counted, first-aid packs restocked. On
a call-out, two base volunteers are paged with the hill team.

**What it costs us:** £4,200 a year to heat and light the base. The work
itself is given free. Write to us if you can give a Monday evening a month.
