The same rain that fills the becks on the fell floods the lanes in the
valley bottom. Twice in the last five winters, houses in Harrowdale Bridge
have had water to the windowsills.

**Who it serves:** people trapped in flooded homes and cars in the valley,
working alongside the fire service and the police.

**A typical week:** most weeks, none. In a wet winter, our swiftwater
technicians train once a month on the Harrow Beck below Stenning weir, and
stay on standby whenever the river warnings go out.

**What it costs us:** £850 for each drysuit, and £1,900 every five years to
retrain each technician.
