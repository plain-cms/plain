We take on four or five new hill members a year. You need to be at home on
steep ground in bad weather, live or work within twenty minutes of Station
Yard, and be willing to be woken at three in the morning.

**Who it serves:** the casualties of the next twenty years. Every hill
member we train is someone who can carry a stretcher.

**A typical week:** training every Wednesday at 7.30 pm, at the base or on
the hill: navigation at night, rope work, casualty care, radio. Trainees do a
year of training and a casualty care course before they go on the call-out
list.

**What it costs us:** about £1,200 to train and equip one new hill member in
their first two years.
