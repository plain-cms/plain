Winter on the Harrowdale fells starts in November and lasts until Easter.
Short days catch out more walkers than snow does.

**Who it serves:** anyone who walks the fells, from first-timers to people
who have done it for forty years.

**A typical week:** on the first Thursday of the month from December to
March, at 7.30 pm in the back room of The Drovers Arms in Kettlethwaite. No
booking, no charge. Bring a head torch.

**What it costs us:** about £20 an evening for printed maps and tea. The
Drovers lends us the room.
