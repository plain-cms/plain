## What we do

In 2025 the team was called out 74 times: walkers with broken ankles on
Scar Fell, a fell runner benighted below Kettle Crag, a family lost in cloud
on Harrow Pike. Every one of those call-outs was answered by a volunteer who
left their work, their tea or their bed to go. We are not paid, and nobody we
carry down is ever asked for a penny.

## How a call-out works

In an emergency, dial **999**, ask for **Police**, then **Mountain Rescue**.
The police page our team leader, who calls you back. Within fifteen minutes
the first team vehicle leaves the base at Station Yard, and a hill party is
walking in with a stretcher, a casualty bag, oxygen and a radio. See [our
programs](/programs/) for everything else we do, from flood response to
winter skills evenings in the pub.

## Where the money goes

It costs about £68,000 a year to keep the team ready: two rescue vehicles,
the base, radios, medical kit and training. We get no government money for
it. All of it is given: in the collecting tins on the bars of the valley’s
pubs, and by people we once carried down. The [donate page](/donate/)
shows what each gift buys.
