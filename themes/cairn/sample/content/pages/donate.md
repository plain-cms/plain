Every hour on the hill is given free. Your money buys everything a volunteer
carries up it.

| Gift | What it buys |
| --- | --- |
| £3 | One orange survival bag, given to a casualty to keep |
| £25 | Diesel for a call-out to the head of the valley |
| £60 | A head torch that lasts a twelve-hour night search |
| £310 | The average cost of one call-out, start to finish |
| £850 | A drysuit for one of our swiftwater technicians |
| £4,800 | A casualty stretcher that splits in two for carrying |

## How to give

**In the pub:** there is a team collecting tin on the bar of The Drovers
Arms in Kettlethwaite, The Packhorse Inn at Harrowdale Bridge, The Black Bull
in Stenning and The Woolpack at Scarside, and in 34 other pubs, cafés and
shops in the valley. Every tin is emptied and counted by two volunteers.

**By cheque:** made out to Harrowdale Mountain Rescue Team and posted to Team
base, Station Yard, Kettlethwaite, Harrowdale, Cumbria.

**By bank transfer:** e-mail
[treasurer@harrowdalemrt.example](mailto:treasurer@harrowdalemrt.example) or
call [01632 960418](tel:+441632960418) and Megan Lowther, our treasurer,
sends the details.

If you pay UK income tax, tell us and we can claim Gift Aid: 25p on every
pound, at no cost to you.

## Other ways to help

- Look after a collecting tin in your own pub, café or shop:
  [collecting tins in the valley pubs](/programs/collecting-tins/).
- Give your evenings at the base: [base and kit
  volunteers](/programs/base-and-kit-volunteers/).
- Carry a survival bag, a torch and a whistle, and know the distress signal.
