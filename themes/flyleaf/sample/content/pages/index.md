## On the shelves

Four of us work here and every new book is in the shop because one of us read it and wanted to press it on somebody. Fiction and nature writing downstairs, poetry on the landing, and the children's room up three steps at the back.

## Priced in pencil

We buy second-hand books over the counter on Tuesdays and from house clearances most weeks. Each one is priced in pencil on the flyleaf, and the better finds are listed here before they reach the shelf.

## Signed at the till

Writers from up and down the coast sign their stock when they call in. Some will dedicate a copy to order if you give us a week.
