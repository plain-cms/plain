We are on the harbour side of Fish Street, between the chandler and the bakery. Look for the blue lamp over the door and the trolley of £1 paperbacks outside when it is dry.

## Getting here

The Quay Road car park is three minutes' walk and free after four o'clock. Buses 6 and 44 stop at the Custom House, at the bottom of the street. There are two bicycle hoops outside the bakery.

## Steps and access

The front room is level from the pavement and the door is wide enough for a wheelchair or a pushchair. Poetry is up eleven stairs and the children's room up three; tell us what you are after and we will bring a pile down to the armchair.

## Paying

Cash, card and book tokens. We keep accounts for schools and libraries.

## Post and collection

| How | Cost | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Collect at the till | Free | Held for a week |
| Post, one parcel | £3.50 | Sent the next working day |
| By bicycle in Aldermouth | Free | Fridays after closing |

## Selling us books

Bring up to two boxes on a Tuesday and we will make an offer while you wait. For a whole house, ring [01632 960418](tel:01632960418) and Sam will call round.
