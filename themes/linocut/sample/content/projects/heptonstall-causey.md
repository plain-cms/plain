Reduction linocut in five colours, 38 × 50 cm on 300 gsm cotton paper, edition of 30, £240 unframed.

The causey is the line of worn flagstones the packhorses climbed from the valley to Heptonstall. I drew from the same stone every morning for a week in February, when the trees are bare enough to see the church tower through them.

## The approach

A reduction print is cut from one block: print the palest colour on every sheet, cut away what should stay that colour, print the next, and so on down to the darkest. Five colours meant five rounds of cutting and five days of printing, with the sheets drying on a line across the studio between each.

## The outcome

Twenty-six of the thirty sold in the first month, most of them to people who walk the causey themselves. The block now holds only the umber of the last colour, and it will not print again.
