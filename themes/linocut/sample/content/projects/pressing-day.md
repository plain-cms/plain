Lumb Lane presses about 4,000 bottles a year from their own orchard and two neighbours’ trees. Until 2024 the bottles carried a printed sticker with the farm’s address in a word-processor font.

## The approach

One apple block, one lettering block per blend, and four inks: a russet for the dry, a gold for the medium, a green for the perry and a deep red for the oak-aged. The labels are printed on the farm’s own wet-strength paper in runs of 500, so a new blend is a new ink, not a new design.

## The outcome

The cider is now stocked in nine shops and pubs along the valley, up from two, and the farm prints a short run of each label itself from blocks kept in the barn.
