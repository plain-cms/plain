Woodcut and wood type, 50 × 70 cm, printed in umber and red on a cream poster paper, 150 copies.

The traders wanted something that belonged in the hall rather than on a bus shelter. We took the Albion down to the market for the anniversary Saturday and printed the posters between the cheese stall and the haberdasher, one every four minutes, from nine until the bell.

## The outcome

Every stallholder took one for their pitch. The remaining copies were sold at £20 for the traders’ fund for repairs to the clock, and the block hangs in the hall office.
