Woodcut from three cherry blocks, 30 × 40 cm on kozo paper, edition of 20, £160 unframed.

Beech bark in March is grey, green and silver at once, and lino could not hold it. Cherry has a grain that prints, so the grain does the work of the bark: one block for the pale ground, one for the trunks, one for the moss on the north side of each.

The paper is thin enough that the ink comes through to the back, and each sheet is burnished with a wooden spoon rather than run through the press.
