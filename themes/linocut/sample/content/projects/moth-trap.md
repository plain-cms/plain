Linocut in two colours, 20 × 25 cm, edition of 40, £85 unframed.

My neighbour runs a moth trap on the first warm night of every month and lets them go at dawn. On the night in June I drew this, there were thirty-eight kinds in the egg boxes. Nine made it onto the block: the elephant hawk-moth, the buff-tip, the peppered moth and six smaller ones, each at the size it was.
