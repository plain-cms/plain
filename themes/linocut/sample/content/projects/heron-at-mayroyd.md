Linocut, 30 × 40 cm, black on a warm white paper, edition of 30, £120 unframed.

This was the first block I printed after the Albion came to the studio, and the heron is still there most mornings. One colour, no tricks: the water is the marks the gouge left, and the bird is what I did not cut.
