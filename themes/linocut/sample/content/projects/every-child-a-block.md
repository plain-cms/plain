Five mornings with one class of thirty-one children, ten safety cutters, a box of soft-cut lino and a tabletop press.

Each child drew and cut a self-portrait block 10 cm square. On the Friday we printed all thirty-one in a row along a single roll of paper, nine metres long, in one blue ink.

The frieze has hung along the back wall of the hall since that summer, and the school now runs the week for each new Year 5.
