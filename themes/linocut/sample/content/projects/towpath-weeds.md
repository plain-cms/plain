Twelve linocuts, each 15 × 15 cm, printed in black-green on a cream wove paper and bound as a 2026 wall calendar in an edition of 300.

The volunteers clear the towpath between Lock 9 and the aqueduct every second Sunday. They wanted a calendar to sell at the lock-keeper’s cottage, and they wanted it to be about the plants they spend all year pulling up and the ones they leave alone.

## The approach

One plant a month, drawn on the towpath with a volunteer who knew its name: coltsfoot in February, Himalayan balsam in August, teasel in December. Each block carries the plant’s name cut by hand in the same capitals, so the twelve sheets read as one set.

## The outcome

All 300 calendars sold before the end of November and paid for a year of tools, gloves and skip hire. The volunteers have asked for a second set of twelve.
